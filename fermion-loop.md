# Fermion loop

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md)

A closed chain of [Dirac propagators](dirac-propagator.md) is a [fermion loop](fermion-loop.md). Its [closed fermion loop sign](closed-fermion-loop-sign.md) distinguishes it from a bosonic loop. Tracing spinor indices produces [gamma matrix](gamma-matrices.md) traces, and the high-momentum powers of the propagators determine possible [ultraviolet divergences](ultraviolet-divergence.md).
