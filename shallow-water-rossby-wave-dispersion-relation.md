# Shallow-water Rossby-wave dispersion relation

↑ **Parent:** [Linear Rossby-wave equation](linear-rossby-wave-equation.md)

For a resting midlatitude layer, the slow [Rossby wave](rossby-wave.md) equation is $(\nabla_h^2-a^2)\eta_t=-\beta\eta_x$, where $a=f_0/\sqrt{gH}$. With the [plane wave](plane-wave.md) convention $e^{i(kx+ly-\omega t)}$,

$$
\omega=-\frac{\beta k}{k^2+l^2+a^2}.
$$

The zonal [phase velocity](phase-velocity.md) is westward for $\beta>0$, but the zonal [group velocity](group-velocity.md) changes sign at $k^2=l^2+a^2$.
