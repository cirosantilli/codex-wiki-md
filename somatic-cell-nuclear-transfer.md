# Somatic cell nuclear transfer

↑ **Parent:** [Cellular reprogramming](cellular-reprogramming.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somatic_cell_nuclear_transfer)

[Somatic cell nuclear transfer](somatic-cell-nuclear-transfer.md) introduces a body-cell [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) into an [ovum](ovum.md) whose own nuclear contribution has been removed or inactivated. The donor nuclear [genome](genome.md) and recipient [cytoplasm](cytoplasm.md) have different origins; successful development does not show that the donor [cell nucleus](cell-nucleus.md) acts independently of the recipient's machinery.
