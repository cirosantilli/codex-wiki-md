# Focal sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Focal mechanism](focal-mechanism.md)

An imagined [sphere](sphere.md) around a seismic point source on which source-to-station ray takeoff directions are plotted. A lower-hemisphere equal-area projection commonly displays first-motion polarities and nodal planes.
