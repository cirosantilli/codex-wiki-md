# Relational algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Database query language](database-query-language.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relational_algebra)

An algebra of operations on [database relations](database-relation.md). Classical [relational algebra](relational-algebra.md) has [relational selection](relational-selection.md), [relational projection](relational-projection.md), [relational join](relational-join.md), [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md), union and [relational difference](relational-difference.md); [database aggregation](database-aggregation.md) requires an extension.
