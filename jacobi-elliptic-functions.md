# Jacobi elliptic functions

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptic function](elliptic-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_functions)

The Jacobi elliptic functions include $\operatorname{sn}(u,k)$, $\operatorname{cn}(u,k)$ and $\operatorname{dn}(u,k)$, with $\operatorname{sn}^2+\operatorname{cn}^2=1$ and $\operatorname{dn}^2+k^2\operatorname{sn}^2=1$. Their ratios give the other standard members. The [Jacobi elliptic sine](jacobi-elliptic-sine.md) is the inverse of the [elliptic integral of the first kind](elliptic-integral-of-the-first-kind.md).
