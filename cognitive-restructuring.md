# Cognitive restructuring

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive behavioral therapy](cognitive-behavioral-therapy.md)

Examining and revising interpretations or assumptions that influence [emotion](emotion.md) and action, typically with explicit alternatives and tests rather than unsupported reassurance. Its contribution to a [treatment](treatment.md) package requires component-level evidence.
