# Symmetric stable distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Stable distribution](stable-distribution.md)

A symmetric stable distribution has this [characteristic function](characteristic-function.md), with $c>0$ and location $\mu$. At location zero it is a [strictly stable distribution](strictly-stable-distribution.md): weighted sums of [independent](independent-random-variables.md) copies have scale factor $(\sum_j|a_j|^\alpha)^{1/\alpha}$. The index $\alpha$ need not permit a finite [mean](expected-value.md) or [variance](variance-split.md). The scale in this convention is $c^{1/\alpha}$, so the coefficient of $|t|^\alpha$ and the scale parameter should not be confused.
