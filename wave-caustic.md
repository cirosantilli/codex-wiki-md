# Wave caustic

↑ **Parent:** [Geometrical optics](geometrical-optics-split.md)

A [wave caustic](wave-caustic.md) is a locus where the projection of a family of rays or wavefronts becomes singular. Several ray paths may coalesce there, and the geometrical approximation can predict divergent intensity. Finite-wavelength [diffraction](diffraction.md) replaces that ideal divergence by a resolved wave pattern. The construction applies to acoustic as well as optical waves.
