# Group rings of poly-(cyclic or finite) groups are Noetherian

↑ **Parent:** [Group ring](group-ring.md)

If $A$ is a [left Noetherian ring](left-noetherian-ring.md) and a [right Noetherian ring](right-noetherian-ring.md), and $G$ is a [poly-(cyclic or finite) group](poly-cyclic-or-finite-group.md), then $A[G]$ satisfies both chain conditions. Induct along the [subnormal series](subnormal-series.md). A finite factor gives a finite free extension on each side, handled by [finite-module extension preserves Noetherianity](finite-module-extension-preserves-noetherianity.md). An infinite cyclic factor gives a [skew Laurent polynomial ring](skew-laurent-polynomial-ring.md), with the twisting [automorphism](automorphism.md) induced by conjugation by a lift of the cyclic generator. The [noncommutative Hilbert basis theorem](noncommutative-hilbert-basis-theorem.md) and clearing negative powers prove the required chain conditions at that step. In particular $\mathbb Z[G]$ is [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md).
