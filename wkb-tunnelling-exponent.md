# WKB tunnelling exponent

↑ **Parent:** [WKB approximation](wkb-approximation.md)

For an opaque barrier between simple [turning points](turning-point.md), the leading [transmission probability](transmission-probability.md) is $T\simeq e^{-2S_B/\hbar}$, where $S_B=\int\sqrt{2m[V(x)-E]}\,\mathrm dx$ over the forbidden interval. The exponent for [probability](probability.md) is twice that for amplitude. Matching determines prefactors and the domain where this approximation applies.
