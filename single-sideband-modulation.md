# Single-sideband modulation

↑ **Parent:** [Amplitude modulation](amplitude-modulation.md)

[Single-sideband modulation](single-sideband-modulation.md) transmits one of the two translated message bands generated by [amplitude modulation](amplitude-modulation.md). For a real signal, the transmitted negative-frequency band is the complex conjugate of the positive-frequency band. Removing the redundant band reduces the required [signal bandwidth](signal-bandwidth.md). The [carrier wave](carrier-wave.md) may also be suppressed; its retention or removal must be specified separately.
