# Rational homotopy groups of the connected sum of two complex projective planes

↑ **Parent:** [Rational homotopy group](rational-homotopy-group.md)

The [Sullivan model of the connected sum of two complex projective planes](sullivan-model-of-the-connected-sum-of-two-complex-projective-planes.md) has two generators in each of degrees two and three, and none elsewhere. Its rational [homotopy groups](homotopy-group.md) are therefore exactly the displayed two copies of $\mathbb Q^2$, with all other positive degrees zero.
