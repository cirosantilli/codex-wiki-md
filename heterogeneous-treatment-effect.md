# Heterogeneous treatment effect

↑ **Parent:** [Average treatment effect](average-treatment-effect.md)

A heterogeneous treatment effect varies across units or covariate values rather than being constant throughout the population. The [conditional average treatment effect](conditional-average-treatment-effect.md) describes one common form of this heterogeneity.
