# Growth-time form of cosmological dust equations

↑ **Parent:** [Linear matter perturbation growth equation](linear-matter-perturbation-growth-equation.md)

Use a monotone [linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md) $b(t)$ as time, $\mathbf u=\dot{\mathbf x}/\dot b$, and $\psi=a\varphi/(Cb)$ with $C=3H_0^2\Omega_{m0}/2$ and $a_0=1$. The [cosmological Poisson equation](cosmological-poisson-equation.md) becomes $\nabla^2\psi=\delta/b$; the [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) becomes $\partial_b\delta+\nabla\cdot[(1+\delta)\mathbf u]=0$. The material derivative is $D_b=\partial_b+\mathbf u\cdot\nabla$. Substitution into the pressureless [cosmological Euler equation](cosmological-euler-equation.md), followed by the growth equation and $\dot b=Hbf$, proves the displayed motion equation, with $f=d\ln b/d\ln a$. The density derivative in the conservative continuity equation is Eulerian, not material.
