# Degree of a holomorphic vector bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md)

On a [compact Riemann surface](compact-riemann-surface.md), the degree of a [holomorphic vector bundle](holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) is the [degree of a line bundle](degree-of-a-line-bundle.md) of its [determinant line bundle](determinant-line-bundle.md). A [Hermitian metric on a holomorphic vector bundle](hermitian-metric-on-a-holomorphic-vector-bundle.md) and its [Chern connection](chern-connection.md) express this degree by the trace-curvature integral. The value is independent of the metric because the normalized trace curvature represents the [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md).
