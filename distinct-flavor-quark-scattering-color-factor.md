# Distinct-flavor quark scattering color factor

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum chromodynamics](quantum-chromodynamics.md)

For massless distinguishable [quarks](quark.md) exchanging one [gluon](gluon.md), summing the squared generator contraction gives two with $\operatorname{Tr}(T^aT^b)=\delta^{ab}/2$. Averaging nine initial colors and four initial spins leaves the coefficient $1/18$ multiplying the two Dirac [traces](matrix-trace.md). Their contraction is $8(s^2+u^2)$, yielding the displayed tree-level result. Identical [quarks](quark.md) require an additional exchange amplitude and its interference.
