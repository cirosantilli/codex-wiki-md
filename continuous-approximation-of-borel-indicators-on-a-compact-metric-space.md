# Continuous approximation of Borel indicators on a compact metric space

↑ **Parent:** [Borel probability measure](borel-probability-measure.md)

For a [Borel probability measure](borel-probability-measure.md) on a [compact metric space](compact-metric-space.md), [inner regularity](inner-regular-measure.md) and [outer regularity](outer-regular-measure.md) give $K\subseteq B\subseteq U$ with $K$ compact, $U$ open and $\mu(U\setminus K)<\epsilon$. The [Urysohn lemma](urysohn-s-lemma.md) supplies a [continuous function](continuous-function.md) $\varphi$ equal to one on $K$ and zero outside $U$, with values in $[0,1]$. Its difference from the [indicator function](indicator-function.md) of $B$ is supported in $U\setminus K$ and bounded by one, proving the displayed estimate. This transfers identities established for continuous test functions to measurable sets.
