<h1 id="affine-invariance-of-hotelling-s-statistic">Affine invariance of Hotelling's statistic</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hotelling's T-squared statistic](hotelling-s-t-squared-statistic.md)

Under a nonsingular affine transformation $Y=AX+b$, transform the null mean by the same map. Then the centered sample mean becomes $A(\bar X-\mu_0)$ and the [sample covariance matrix](sample-covariance-matrix.md) becomes $ASA^T$. Since $(ASA^T)^{-1}=A^{-T}S^{-1}A^{-1}$, the quadratic form in [Hotelling's T-squared statistic](hotelling-s-t-squared-statistic.md) is unchanged. Its value and test decision are consequently invariant under changes of units, origin and nonsingular coordinates.
