# Complete-linkage clustering

↑ **Parent:** [Agglomerative hierarchical clustering](agglomerative-hierarchical-clustering.md)

The farthest cross-pair distance determines the next merge. It favors groups with small diameter and reduces the chaining effect of [single-linkage clustering](single-linkage-clustering.md), but can be sensitive to isolated extreme observations. Ties can affect intermediate partitions.
