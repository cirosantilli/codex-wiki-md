# Quantum differential cross-section from a scattering amplitude

↑ **Parent:** [Differential scattering cross-section](differential-scattering-cross-section.md)

If

$$
\psi\sim e^{ikz}+f(\theta)\frac{e^{ikr}}r,
$$

then the ratio of scattered radial [probability current](probability-current.md) through $r^2d\Omega$ to the incident probability flux is

$$
\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}=|f(\theta)|^2.
$$
