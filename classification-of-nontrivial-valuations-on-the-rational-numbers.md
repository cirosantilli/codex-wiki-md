# Classification of nontrivial valuations on the rational numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Non-Archimedean valuation](non-archimedean-valuation.md)

An additive nontrivial [valuation](valuation.md) on the rational field is equivalent to exactly one [P-adic valuation](p-adic-valuation.md). Integers have nonnegative values; some prime must have positive value, and [Bézout's identity](bezout-identity.md) prevents two distinct primes from doing so. Unique factorization then gives the formula, with $c>0$ in the real-valued case. The trivial [valuation](valuation.md) is a separate possibility if admitted by the definition.
