# Transition probability for a rotated two-level observable

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum state](quantum-state.md)

Let orthonormal energy [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) be $\Psi_1,\Psi_2$, and let a measured [observable](observable.md) have [eigenvectors](eigenvector.md) $v_+=\cos\alpha\,\Psi_1+\sin\alpha\,\Psi_2$ and $v_-=-\sin\alpha\,\Psi_1+\cos\alpha\,\Psi_2$. If the initial [quantum state](quantum-state.md) is $v_-$, [unitary time evolution](unitary-time-evolution.md) multiplies each energy component by $e^{-itE_j/\hbar}$. Its amplitude in $v_+$ is $\sin\alpha\cos\alpha(e^{-itE_2/\hbar}-e^{-itE_1/\hbar})$. The [Born rule](born-rule.md) gives the displayed [probability](probability.md). The energy gap fixes the oscillation [frequency](frequency.md), while the angle between the measurement and energy bases fixes its amplitude. Energy degeneracy or coinciding bases makes the transition [probability](probability.md) vanish.
