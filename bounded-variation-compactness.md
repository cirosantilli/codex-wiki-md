# Bounded-variation compactness

↑ **Parent:** [Function of bounded variation on a domain](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md)

On a bounded [Lipschitz domain](lipschitz-domain.md), a sequence with bounded $L^1$ [norms](norm.md) and bounded [total variation seminorm](total-variation-seminorm-on-a-domain.md) has a subsequence converging strongly in $L^1$. The limit is in the [BV space](function-of-bounded-variation-on-a-domain.md), and its variation does not exceed the limit inferior. This supplies the [compactness](compact-space.md) step of the [direct method in the calculus of variations](direct-method-in-the-calculus-of-variations.md) for linearly growing [gradient](gradient.md) energies.
