# Ionization parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Photoionization](photoionization.md)

The [ionization parameter](ionization-parameter.md) compares the local density of hydrogen-ionizing photons with the hydrogen number density. For an unattenuated isotropic source the displayed form uses its ionizing photon production rate $Q_H$. Ionic fractions depend on $U$ together with the radiation spectrum, temperature, recombination rates and shielding, so $U$ alone does not determine every emission-line ratio.
