# Pooled sample variance

↑ **Parent:** [Sample variance](sample-variance.md)

For two independent normal samples of sizes $m,n$ with a common unknown variance, pooling their within-sample sums of squares gives the [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md) $S_p^2=(S_{XX}+S_{YY})/(m+n-2)$. The numerator divided by the common population variance has a [chi-squared distribution](chi-squared-distribution.md) with $m+n-2$ degrees of freedom and is independent of both sample means. Therefore $(\bar X-\bar Y-(\mu_X-\mu_Y))/(S_p\sqrt{1/m+1/n})$ has a [Student t-distribution](student-s-t-distribution.md). This exact construction requires a positive residual number of degrees of freedom and the common-variance assumption.
