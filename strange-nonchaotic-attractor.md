# Strange nonchaotic attractor

↑ **Parent:** [Strange attractor](strange-attractor.md)

A geometrically irregular [attractor](attractor.md) need not have chaotic exponential separation of nearby trajectories. Quasiperiodically forced systems provide primary examples in which strangeness and a positive largest [Lyapunov exponent](lyapunov-exponent.md) do not coincide. Consequently a fractal-looking numerical plot alone cannot establish [chaos](chaos.md). [Strange attractors that are not chaotic, Physica D 13 (1984), pages 261–268](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-2789(84)90282-3)
