# Rotational spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Rotational spectroscopy](rotational-spectroscopy.md)

A set of [spectral lines](spectral-line.md) associated with transitions between molecular [rotational energy](rotational-energy.md) levels. A polar [linear rigid rotor](linear-rigid-rotor.md) has electric-dipole transitions with $\Delta J=\pm1$; in the ideal model adjacent absorption frequencies are separated by twice the [rotational constant](rotational-constant.md) in frequency units.
