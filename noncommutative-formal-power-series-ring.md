# Noncommutative formal power series ring

↑ **Parent:** [Free associative algebra](free-associative-algebra.md)

For a [field](field.md) $k$ and finitely many generators, this [ring](ring.md) consists of arbitrary coefficient families indexed by finite [words](string.md) in the generators. Addition is coefficientwise and multiplication uses concatenation: the coefficient of $w$ in $FG$ is $\sum_{uv=w}F_uG_v$, a finite sum. Finite series form the [free associative algebra](free-associative-algebra.md). If $F,G\ne0$, their lowest nonzero homogeneous parts multiply to a nonzero polynomial, since a [word](string.md) of fixed length has a unique splitting at a specified position. Thus the ring is a [noncommutative domain](noncommutative-domain.md).
