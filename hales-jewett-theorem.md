# Hales-Jewett theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorial line](combinatorial-line.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hales–Jewett_theorem)

For every finite alphabet $X$ and every positive integer $k$, there is $n$ such that every $k$-coloring of $X^n$ contains a monochromatic [combinatorial line](combinatorial-line.md).
