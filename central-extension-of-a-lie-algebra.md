# Central extension of a Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)

A central extension is a [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) surjection whose [kernel](kernel-of-a-linear-map-split.md) commutes with every element of the larger algebra. The [Virasoro central extension](virasoro-central-extension.md) adds a central generator to the [Witt algebra](witt-algebra.md).
