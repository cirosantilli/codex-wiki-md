# Silver chloride

↑ **Parent:** [Optical material](optical-material.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_chloride)

[Silver chloride](silver-chloride.md) is a [chemical compound](chemical-compound.md) with formula $\mathrm{AgCl}$. Its darkening under illumination supplied the chemical readout in the historical [ultraviolet](ultraviolet.md) discovery described above. That response depends on the incident [optical spectrum](optical-spectrum.md) and the sample's condition; it does not provide an uncalibrated direct measurement of incident [power](power.md). [Caltech/IPAC, Ritter's silver-chloride detector](https://coolcosmos.ipac.caltech.edu/page/lesson_ritter_experiment).
