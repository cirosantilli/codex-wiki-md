# Extremal types theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Extreme value theory](extreme-value-theory.md)

Every non-degenerate limit in [convergence in distribution](convergence-in-distribution.md) of positively scaled and centered [sample maxima](sample-maximum.md) is a [max-stable distribution](max-stable-distribution.md). Up to positive affine transformations its law is the [Gumbel distribution](gumbel-distribution.md), a [Fréchet distribution](frechet-distribution.md) with positive shape parameter, or a [negative Weibull distribution](negative-weibull-distribution.md) with positive shape parameter. This classifies possible limits, without asserting that every [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) admits a limiting normalization.
