# Elastic membrane

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

An elastic membrane is a thin deformable sheet whose transverse restoring force is supplied by in-plane tension. For uniform tension $T$ and mass per area $m$, a two-dimensional small-displacement model has $m\eta_{tt}=T\eta_{xx}-[p]$, where the pressure jump is defined from the lower fluid to the upper fluid. Fluid loading modifies its traveling modes through the [acoustic wave on a tensioned massive membrane](acoustic-wave-on-a-tensioned-massive-membrane.md).
