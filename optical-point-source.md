# Optical point source

↑ **Parent:** [Point spread function](point-spread-function.md)

An [optical point source](optical-point-source.md) has spatial extent negligible relative to the imaging scale being tested. The [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md) is its idealization; a finite bead or unresolved [star](star.md) only approximates it.
