# Compact perturbation invariance of Fredholm operators

↑ **Parent:** [Fredholm operator](fredholm-operator.md)

For a [Fredholm operator](fredholm-operator.md) $L$ on a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), invert its restriction from the kernel complement to its closed range, extending by zero on the range complement. The resulting bounded $B$ has $BL=I-P$ and $LB=I-Q$ with finite-rank projections. Adding a [compact operator](compact-operator-split.md) $K$ gives two-sided inverses modulo compact errors. Such a parametrix forces finite kernel, a lower bound on the kernel complement, closed range and finite adjoint kernel; the latter is the [cokernel](cokernel.md). Repeating with $-K$ proves the equivalence without assuming self-adjointness.
