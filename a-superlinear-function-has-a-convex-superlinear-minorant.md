# A superlinear function has a convex superlinear minorant

↑ **Parent:** [De la Vallée-Poussin criterion](de-la-vallee-poussin-criterion.md)

Choose increasing $R_m\to\infty$ so $H(t)\geq m^2|t|$ whenever $|t|\geq R_m$. Put $\Psi(s)=\sum_{m\geq1}(s-R_m)_+$ for $s\geq0$. This is nonnegative, increasing and convex. If $m$ terms are active, then $\Psi(s)\leq ms\leq H(t)$ for $s=|t|$. For every fixed $N$, $s\geq2R_N$ gives $\Psi(s)\geq Ns/2$, proving superlinearity. Thus a nonconvex superlinear energy lower bound still supplies the [convex function](convex-function.md) needed in the [De la Vallée-Poussin criterion](de-la-vallee-poussin-criterion.md). A continuous superlinear [function](function-split.md) not initially nonnegative can first have its finite [global minimum](global-minimum.md) subtracted.
