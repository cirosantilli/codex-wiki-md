# Autocatalytic enzyme clearance with a saddle threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Michaelis-Menten kinetics](michaelis-menten-kinetics.md)

[Substrate](substrate-biochemistry.md) replication competes with saturating [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) clearance in this fast-slow model, where $K>\lambda>0$ and $\epsilon\ll1$. The physical strip $u\geq0$, $0\leq v\leq1$ is invariant. If $0<\eta<\lambda/K$, the origin is attracting and a positive saddle exists at $u_e=\lambda/\eta-K$, $v_e=1-\eta K/\lambda$. Eliminating the fast complex fraction gives $u'\simeq\eta u(u-u_e)/(u+K)$, separating extinction from runaway. For initially zero complexes, the stable-manifold threshold is $u_*=u_e+\epsilon(1-\eta)v_e+O(\epsilon^2)$, away from singular [parameter](parameter.md) limits. To derive the correction, write the [stable manifold](stable-manifold.md) as $u=u_e+\epsilon\phi(v)$; its invariance equation gives $\phi'=-(1-\eta)$ and $\phi(v_e)=0$. A Dulac factor $1/u$ has strictly negative weighted divergence, excluding a periodic alternative to the two basins. Saturated clearance and autocatalytic growth can therefore create an unstable [concentration](concentration.md) threshold rather than a stable nonzero balance.
