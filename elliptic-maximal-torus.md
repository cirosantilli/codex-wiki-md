# Elliptic maximal torus

↑ **Parent:** [Rational maximal torus](rational-maximal-torus.md)

A [rational maximal torus](rational-maximal-torus.md) is elliptic when it is contained in no proper rational [parabolic subgroup](parabolic-subgroup.md). This definition allows the split central torus of the ambient group; an absolute absence of every split subtorus is not the right condition when the centre is nontrivial.
