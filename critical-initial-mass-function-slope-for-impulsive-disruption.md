# Critical initial mass function slope for impulsive disruption

↑ **Parent:** [Impulsive disruption by stellar mass loss](impulsive-disruption-by-stellar-mass-loss.md)

For an [initial mass function](initial-mass-function.md) $dN\propto m^{-\alpha}dm$ on $0.1<m/M_\odot<100$, complete loss of material initially above $10M_\odot$ removes fraction

$$
F(\alpha)=\frac{100^{2-\alpha}-10^{2-\alpha}}{100^{2-\alpha}-0.1^{2-\alpha}},\qquad F(2)=\frac13.
$$

The [impulsive disruption by stellar mass loss](impulsive-disruption-by-stellar-mass-loss.md) threshold is $F=1/2$, with unique finite root $\alpha\simeq1.79101236$. Decreasing $\alpha$ shifts mass to larger stars and increases $F$, so the idealized system is globally unbound for $\alpha<\alpha_{\rm crit}$. The derivation requires rapid, well-mixed loss and absence of a confining external potential.
