# Supermodularity of graph component count

↑ **Parent:** [Component (graph theory)](component-graph-theory.md)

For open-edge sets $S,T$ in a fixed finite graph, including isolated [graph vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) in $k$, one has $k(S\cap T)+k(S\cup T)\ge k(S)+k(T)$. Incidence-vector spans have rank $r(S)=|V|-k(S)$; their union span is the sum of the two spans, while the intersection-edge span is contained in the intersection of spans. The vector-space [dimension formula for a sum of subspaces](dimension-formula-for-a-sum-of-subspaces.md) proves submodularity of $r$, equivalently supermodularity of $k$.
