# Stellar potential-energy tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Tensor virial theorem](tensor-virial-theorem.md)

This force-moment tensor enters the [tensor virial theorem](tensor-virial-theorem.md). For an isolated self-gravitating population it is symmetric by pairwise interchange of the Newtonian interaction; for a tracer in an external field use the force-moment definition directly, with the appropriate symmetric combination in a time-dependent inertia identity.
