# Poisson branching process

↑ **Parent:** [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process.md)

A [Galton-Watson process](galton-watson-process.md) with a [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) of offspring of mean $\mu$ has this [probability generating function](probability-generating-function.md). For $\mu=1+\varepsilon>1$ its [branching survival probability](survival-probability-of-a-branching-process.md) $r$ satisfies $-\log(1-r)=(1+\varepsilon)r$, and $2\varepsilon/(1+2\varepsilon)\leq r\leq2\varepsilon$. Indeed $\varepsilon=\sum_{j\geq1}r^j/(j+1)$ lies between $r/2$ and $r/[2(1-r)]$. These exact inequalities belong to the [Poisson branching process](poisson-branching-process.md), not to every finite [binomial branching process](binomial-branching-process.md).
