# Normality is a local property

↑ **Parent:** [Normal family](normal-family.md)

Choose a countable cover by [connected](connected-space.md) disks on which a family is normal. From any sequence extract successive subsequences converging on each disk and use the diagonal sequence. The limits agree on overlaps; a finite holomorphic limit and infinity cannot agree on a nonempty overlap. Connectedness therefore gives one global type of limit, and finite [compact](compact-space.md) subcovers give [locally uniform convergence](locally-uniform-convergence.md). This proves normality of the family on the whole domain from normality near every point.
