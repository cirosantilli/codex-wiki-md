# Primer dimer

↑ **Parent:** [PCR specificity](pcr-specificity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primer_dimer)

A [primer dimer](primer-dimer.md) is an amplification artifact in which [PCR primers](pcr-primer.md) provide extendible partners for one another. Its accumulation consumes substrates and may produce a signal even without the intended template. It is not evidence that the intended [amplicon](amplicon.md) was present.[FAQ: primer structures as DNA-polymerase substrates, and carryover PCR-product contamination](why-do-i-see-amplification-curves-in-my-ntc-samples-by-new-england-biolabs.md)
