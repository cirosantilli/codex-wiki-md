# Microlensing optical depth

↑ **Parent:** [Gravitational microlensing](gravitational-microlensing.md)

The [microlensing optical depth](microlensing-optical-depth.md) is the probability, when small, that a source lies within one [Einstein radius](einstein-radius.md) of a foreground lens. For point lenses and compact-object [mass density](density.md) $\rho_l$ in nearby Euclidean geometry,

$$
\tau=\frac{4\pi G}{c^2}\int_0^{D_s}\rho_l(D_l)\frac{D_l(D_s-D_l)}{D_s}\,dD_l.
$$

The individual lens mass cancels because $n_l=\rho_l/m$ and $R_E^2\propto m$. Detection efficiencies and event durations still depend on individual masses and survey cadence.
