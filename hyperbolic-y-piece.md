# Hyperbolic Y-piece

↑ **Parent:** [Pair of pants (mathematics)](pair-of-pants-mathematics.md)

A [Y-piece](hyperbolic-y-piece.md) is a compact curvature-$-1$ [hyperbolic surface](hyperbolic-surface.md) homeomorphic to a [pair of pants](pair-of-pants-mathematics.md), with three geodesic boundary curves. Its labelled boundary lengths $\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3>0$ determine it up to [isometry](isometry.md). Cutting along the three mutually perpendicular seams produces two congruent [right-angled hyperbolic hexagons](right-angled-hyperbolic-hexagon.md) with alternating sides $\ell_i/2$. Conversely, doubling the hexagon along its other three sides constructs the [Y-piece](hyperbolic-y-piece.md) for arbitrary positive lengths.
