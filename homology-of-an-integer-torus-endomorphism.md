# Homology of an integer torus endomorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Torus](torus.md)

On the [torus](torus.md) $\mathbb R^d/\mathbb Z^d$, an integer [matrix](matrix.md) $A$ defines an [endomorphism](endomorphism.md). The coordinate circles give a basis of the [first homology group](first-homology.md), and the induced map on that group is $A$. The [Künneth theorem](kunneth-theorem.md) identifies higher [integral homology](integral-homology.md) with the [exterior powers](exterior-power.md) of $\mathbb Z^d$, so the degree-$q$ map is $\bigwedge^q A$. In top degree it is multiplication by $\det A$. The map is a [homeomorphism](homeomorphism.md) precisely when $A$ is a [unimodular matrix](unimodular-matrix.md); a merely nonsingular integer [matrix](matrix.md) need not have an integer inverse.
