# Nuclear magnetic dipole moment

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear spin](nuclear-spin.md)

The [magnetic dipole moment](magnetic-dipole-moment.md) associated with [nuclear spin](nuclear-spin.md), using dimensionless [angular momentum operators](angular-momentum-operator-split.md) and the nuclear magneton $\mu_N$. The quoted scalar moment is its expectation in the stretched [quantum state](quantum-state.md), $\mu_I=g_I\mu_N I$. In the same electronic [energy level](energy-level.md), the leading magnetic-dipole [hyperfine structure](hyperfine-structure.md) constant is proportional to $\mu_I/I$, apart from isotope-dependent electronic and nuclear-size corrections.
