# Gravitational potential evolution of a barotropic fluid

↑ **Parent:** [Newtonian gauge](newtonian-gauge.md)

For a flat background dominated by one constant-$w$ [barotropic equation of state](barotropic-equation-of-state.md), assume $\delta P=w\delta\rho$ and no [scalar anisotropic stress](scalar-anisotropic-stress.md). The [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations.md) then reduce the common Newtonian-gauge potential to the stated [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) equation, with $\mathcal H=2/[(1+3w)\tau]$. For radiation the regular solution is $3\Phi_0(\sin x-x\cos x)/x^3$, $x=k\tau/\sqrt3$, and oscillates with a decaying envelope after sound-horizon entry. For pressureless matter the two solutions are a constant and $\tau^{-5}$. A non-adiabatic pressure perturbation would supply an additional source, so constant background $w$ alone does not justify the homogeneous equation.
