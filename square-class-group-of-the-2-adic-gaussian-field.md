# Square-class group of the 2-adic Gaussian field

↑ **Parent:** [Square-class group of a field](square-class-group-of-a-field.md)

The [valuation](valuation.md) contributes one factor $C_2$. The [unit decomposition of the 2-adic Gaussian field](unit-decomposition-of-the-2-adic-gaussian-field.md) contributes $\mu_4/\mu_4^2\cong C_2$ and the additive quotient $\mathbb Z_2[i]/2\mathbb Z_2[i]\cong C_2^2$. Thus there are sixteen [square classes](square-class.md) and fifteen [quadratic extensions](quadratic-extension.md) over this field.
