# Full-faithfulness criterion for presheaf geometric embeddings

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric morphism induced by a functor](geometric-morphism-induced-by-a-functor.md)

The [geometric morphism](geometric-morphism.md) induced by $T:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ is a [geometric embedding](geometric-embedding.md) exactly when $T$ is [full and faithful](full-and-faithful-functor.md). Its right adjoint has $(f_*H)(d)=\operatorname{Nat}(\mathcal D(T-,d),H)$. The counit at $c$ is precomposition with $\mathcal C(-,c)\to\mathcal D(T-,Tc)$. It is invertible for all $H$ precisely when that comparison presheaf is an isomorphism, by the [Yoneda lemma](yoneda-lemma.md), which is precisely full faithfulness of $T$.
