# Prime filtration

↑ **Parent:** [Noetherian module](noetherian-module.md)

A prime filtration of a [module](module-mathematics.md) over a commutative [ring](ring.md) is a finite increasing chain from zero to the module with each nonzero successive [quotient module](quotient-module.md) isomorphic to $R/P$ for a [prime ideal](prime-ideal.md) $P$. Every [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) over a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md) has one. In each nonzero quotient, a [maximal annihilator of a module element is prime](maximal-annihilator-of-a-module-element-is-prime.md) supplies a cyclic submodule $R/P$; lift it and repeat. The [ascending chain condition](ascending-chain-condition.md) for a [Noetherian module](noetherian-module.md) makes the process terminate. This is not necessarily a composition series: the prime ideals need not be maximal.
