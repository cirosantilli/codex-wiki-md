# Surface accretion Eddington rate

↑ **Parent:** [Eddington accretion rate](eddington-accretion-rate.md)

For radiatively efficient release of Newtonian surface binding [energy](energy-split.md), $L\sim GM\dot M/R_*$ and $L_E=4\pi GMc/\kappa$ imply the displayed rate. Its efficiency is $\eta\sim GM/(R_*c^2)$, so at fixed [opacity](opacity.md) the maximum rate scales with [stellar radius](stellar-radius.md). This differs from defining an Eddington supply rate with an arbitrarily fixed reference efficiency. Geometry, advection and [mass loss](mass-loss-astrophysics.md) can modify the simple limit.
