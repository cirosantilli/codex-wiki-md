# Ring inversion

↑ **Parent:** [Conformation](conformation.md)

[Ring inversion](ring-inversion.md) interconverts ring [conformations](conformation.md) while preserving covalent connectivity and configurational [stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md). In a cyclohexane chair flip, axial and equatorial labels exchange, while the up/down face of a given substituent is retained.
