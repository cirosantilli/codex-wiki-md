# Jones polynomial of a connected sum

↑ **Parent:** [Jones polynomial](jones-polynomial.md)

For [knots](knot.md), matching [bracket smoothing states](bracket-smoothing-state.md) across a [connected sum of knots](connected-sum-of-knots.md) joins one state [circle](circle.md) from each side. Their total [circle](circle.md) count is therefore the sum of the two counts minus one. The reduced [Kauffman bracket](kauffman-bracket.md) state weights multiply, and [writhe of a link diagram](writhe-of-a-link-diagram.md) adds, proving the formula.
