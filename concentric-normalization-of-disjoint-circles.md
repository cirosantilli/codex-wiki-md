# Concentric normalization of disjoint circles

↑ **Parent:** [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md)

Two disjoint ordinary [circle](circle.md) boundaries can be taken to distinct concentric [circles](circle.md) by a [Möbius transformation](mobius-transformation.md). After their centres are put at $0,d$ with radii $a,b$, the map $(z-s)/(z-t)$ works when $st=a^2$ and $s+t=(d^2+a^2-b^2)/d$. Disjointness makes the quadratic have distinct real roots. Already concentric [circles](circle.md) require no transformation.
