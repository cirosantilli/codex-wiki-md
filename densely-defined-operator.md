# Densely defined operator

↑ **Parent:** [Operator domain](operator-domain.md)

A [linear operator](linear-operator.md) $T$ on a [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md) $H$ is densely defined when its [operator domain](operator-domain.md) is a [dense subset](dense-set.md) of $H$. Density makes the [adjoint operator](adjoint-operator.md) unique wherever its defining inner-product identity has a solution.
