# Symmetric product criterion for symmetric matrices

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md)

If $A^T=A$ and $B^T=B$, then $(AB)^T=B^TA^T=BA$. Hence their product is a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md) exactly when they commute. For example, $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ and $B=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ are symmetric but have nonsymmetric product $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$. This criterion differs from asking whether an arbitrary matrix can be factored as a [product of symmetric matrices](product-of-symmetric-matrices.md).
