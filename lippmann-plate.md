# Lippmann plate

↑ **Parent:** [Optics](optics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lippmann_plate)

A [Lippmann plate](lippmann-plate.md) records colour as a depth-dependent optical structure in a [photographic emulsion](photographic-emulsion.md). A [standing wave](standing-wave.md) writes a spacing; [wave interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) during later illumination selects the reflected [wavelengths](wavelength.md). This makes a [photographic plate](photographic-plate.md) a spatial record of an [optical spectrum](optical-spectrum.md). The striking idea is that information about colour can live in geometry.

[Gabriel Lippmann](gabriel-lippmann.md) presented his colour process in 1891 and received the 1908 [Nobel Prize in Physics](nobel-prize-in-physics-split.md). His career belongs principally to [physics](physics-split.md): he held chairs in mathematical and experimental [physics](physics-split.md).[Institutional biography, paragraphs on the 1883 and 1886 physics appointments and 1891 presentation](a-propos-de-gabriel-lippmann-by-centre-de-recherche-public-gabriel-lippmann.md) [Nobel nomination archive: Gabriel Lippmann, awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics 1908](gabriel-lippmann-nomination-physics-1907-9-0-by-nobel-prize-outreach.md)
