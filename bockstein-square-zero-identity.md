# Bockstein square-zero identity

↑ **Parent:** [Bockstein factorization through integral cohomology](bockstein-factorization-through-integral-cohomology.md)

Exactness of the integral coefficient sequence gives $\widehat\beta\rho=0$. The [Bockstein factorization through integral cohomology](bockstein-factorization-through-integral-cohomology.md) therefore gives $\beta^2=\rho\widehat\beta\rho\widehat\beta=0$, for every modulus $n\geq2$, including composite moduli.
