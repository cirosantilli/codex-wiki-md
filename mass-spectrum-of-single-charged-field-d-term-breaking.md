# Mass spectrum of single charged-field D-term breaking

↑ **Parent:** [Single charged-field D-term breaking](single-charged-field-d-term-breaking.md)

For canonical kinetic terms, vanishing [superpotential](superpotential.md), $g>0$ and $q\xi>0$, the [D-term scalar potential](d-term-scalar-potential.md) $V=\tfrac12(\xi+gq|\phi|^2)^2$ has its minimum at $\phi=0$. Both real scalar modes have squared mass $gq\xi$, while the chiral Weyl fermion, photon and [gaugino](gaugino.md) are massless; the gaugino is the [goldstino](goldstino.md). The scalar-to-fermion squared-mass splitting is $gq\xi$. On the other branch $q\xi<0$, $v^2=-\xi/(gq)$ cancels $D$ and preserves [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md); the Higgsed vector, remaining real scalar and Dirac fermion have common squared mass $2g^2q^2v^2$. A single nonzero-charge chiral fermion has a [gauge anomaly](gauge-anomaly.md), so a quantum realization requires an anomaly-cancelling completion; these are the stated classical vacuum spectra.
