# Surjectivity of special orthogonal stabilization below the sphere dimension

↑ **Parent:** [Special orthogonal sphere fibration](special-orthogonal-sphere-fibration.md)

Project a based map $\alpha:S^r\to SO(s+1)$ to $S^s$. Since $r<s$, the resulting [homotopy class](homotopy-class.md) vanishes. Lift a based null-homotopy by the [special orthogonal sphere fibration](special-orthogonal-sphere-fibration.md). Its endpoint lies in the fiber $SO(s)$. If the lift moves its base point, right-multiply at time $t$ by the inverse of its base-point value, which lies in the fiber. This keeps the projected homotopy unchanged and makes the lift based. Thus $\alpha$ is based-homotopic to a map into $SO(s)$, proving the asserted surjectivity.
