# Ringel form

↑ **Parent:** [Representation of a quiver](representation-of-a-quiver.md)

This bilinear form is $\langle\mathbf n,\mathbf m\rangle_Q=\sum_i n_im_i-\sum_{\rho:i\to j}n_im_j$. The [standard projective resolution of a quiver representation](standard-projective-resolution-of-a-quiver-representation.md) gives its value as $\dim\operatorname{Hom}_Q(X,Y)-\dim\operatorname{Ext}^1_Q(X,Y)$ for the dimension vectors of $X,Y$. Its diagonal is the [Tits form of a quiver](tits-form-of-a-quiver.md).
