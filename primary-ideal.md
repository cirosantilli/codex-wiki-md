# Primary ideal

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal](ideal.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_ideal)

An ideal $I$ is primary when $ab\in I$ and $a\notin I$ imply $b^n\in I$ for some positive integer $n$.
