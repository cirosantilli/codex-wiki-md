# Contractibility of compatible almost complex structures

↑ **Parent:** [Compatible almost complex structure](compatible-almost-complex-structure.md)

The [metric construction of a compatible almost complex structure](metric-construction-of-a-compatible-almost-complex-structure.md) is continuous in the auxiliary [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md), and recovers $J$ when applied to $g_J=\omega(\cdot,J\cdot)$. Interpolating $g_J$ linearly to a fixed auxiliary metric therefore contracts the space of [compatible almost complex structures](compatible-almost-complex-structure.md). In particular it is nonempty and path connected.
