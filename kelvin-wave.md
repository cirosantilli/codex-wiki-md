# Kelvin wave

↑ **Parent:** [Geostrophic adjustment](geostrophic-adjustment.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelvin_wave)

A coastal Kelvin wave is trapped within a [Rossby deformation radius](rossby-deformation-radius.md) of a boundary. It propagates with the boundary on its right in the Northern Hemisphere and on its left in the Southern Hemisphere, and its linear shallow-water potential-vorticity anomaly vanishes.
