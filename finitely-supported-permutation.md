# Finitely supported permutation

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation](permutation.md)

A finitely supported [permutation](permutation.md) fixes every element outside some finite [subset](subset.md). The finitely supported [permutations](permutation.md) of $\mathbb N$ are the union of the finite [symmetric groups](symmetric-group.md) acting on $\{1,\ldots,k\}$, and hence form a [countable set](countable-set.md). All [permutations](permutation.md) of $\mathbb N$ form an [uncountable set](uncountable-set.md): independently swapping or fixing each pair $(2j-1,2j)$ injects the [power set](power-set.md) of $\mathbb N$ into this larger [set](set-split.md).
