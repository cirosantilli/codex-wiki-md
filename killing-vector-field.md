# Killing vector field

↑ **Parent:** [General relativity](general-relativity-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killing_vector_field)

A Killing vector field $K$ generates a local one-parameter family of isometries. Equivalently,

$$
\mathcal L_Kg=0
\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad
\nabla_{(\mu}K_{\nu)}=0.
$$
