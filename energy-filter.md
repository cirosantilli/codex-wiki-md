# Energy filter

↑ **Parent:** [Electron microscope](electron-microscope.md)

An [energy filter](energy-filter.md) selects electrons according to energy. In [cryo-electron microscopy](cryo-electron-microscopy.md), rejecting energy-loss electrons can reduce background, but filtering cannot reverse chemical damage or remove every source of scattering noise.
