# Super Proton-Antiproton Synchrotron

↑ **Parent:** [Particle accelerator](particle-accelerator-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Proton–Antiproton_Synchrotron)

The [Super Proton-Antiproton Synchrotron](super-proton-antiproton-synchrotron.md) used the converted SPS ring for counterrotating [proton](proton.md) and [antiproton](antiproton.md) beams. In the discovery running, two $270\,\mathrm{GeV}$ beams provided $\sqrt{s}=540\,\mathrm{GeV}$, unlike a stationary-target collision at the same incident [energy](energy-split.md). Antiproton production, accumulation and [stochastic cooling](stochastic-cooling.md) supplied the collisions used in the [W and Z discovery experiments](w-and-z-discovery-experiments.md).[Section 3, printed p.247 and Fig.5: counterrotating beams, accumulation and SPS conversion](experimental-observation-of-the-intermediate-vector-bosons-w-plus-w-minus-and-z0-by-carlo-rubbia.md)

Why can opposite [electric charges](electric-charge-split.md) share the bending magnets? Reversing both $q$ and [velocity](velocity.md) $\mathbf v$ leaves the [Lorentz force](lorentz-force.md) $q\mathbf v\times\mathbf B$ unchanged at a given point. Both circulating species can therefore bend toward the same ring center in the same [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), with equal [magnetic rigidity](magnetic-rigidity.md) magnitudes. This argument explains the bending geometry; actual injection, focusing, timed acceleration and collisions still require a complete [particle accelerator](particle-accelerator-split.md) system.
