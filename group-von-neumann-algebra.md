# Group von Neumann algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Von Neumann algebra](von-neumann-algebra.md)

For a discrete [group](group-split.md), its [group von Neumann algebra](group-von-neumann-algebra.md) is generated on $\ell^2(\Gamma)$ by left translations $\lambda(g)\delta_h=\delta_{gh}$. The [vector](vector.md) $\delta_e$ is cyclic and separating, and its [vector](vector.md) functional is tracial. Its [modular conjugation](modular-conjugation.md) acts by $J\delta_g=\delta_{g^{-1}}$ with conjugated scalar coefficients. The commutant is the algebra generated by right translations $\rho(g)\delta_h=\delta_{hg^{-1}}$.
