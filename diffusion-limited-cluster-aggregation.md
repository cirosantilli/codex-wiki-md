# Diffusion-limited cluster aggregation

↑ **Parent:** [Particle aggregation](particle-aggregation.md)

In diffusion-limited cluster aggregation, mobile clusters undergoing [Brownian motion](brownian-motion-split.md) attach with probability close to one when they encounter another cluster. Irreversible first-contact sticking tends to trap particles in exposed outer branches, producing open aggregates. In the common three-dimensional isotropic cluster model, the [mass fractal dimension](mass-fractal-dimension.md) is roughly $1.8$. This is different from depositing individual random walkers onto one fixed cluster. Rotational motion, anisotropic attraction and bond restructuring can change the morphology.
