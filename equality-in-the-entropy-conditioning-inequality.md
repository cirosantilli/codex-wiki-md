# Equality in the entropy conditioning inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Conditional entropy](conditional-entropy.md)

For [discrete random variables](discrete-random-variable.md) with finite [information entropy](information-entropy.md) $H(X)$, [Gibbs inequality](gibbs-inequality.md) gives

$$
H(X)-H(X\mid Y)=\sum_y p_Y(y)D(p_{X\mid y}\Vert p_X)\geq0.
$$

Equality holds exactly when $X$ and $Y$ are [independent random variables](independent-random-variables.md). Finiteness matters: if $A$ has infinite [information entropy](information-entropy.md) and is independent of a fair bit $B$, then $X=(A,B)$ and $Y=B$ are dependent but $H(X)=H(X\mid Y)=+\infty$.
