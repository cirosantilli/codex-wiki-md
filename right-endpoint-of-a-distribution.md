# Right endpoint of a distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md)

The [right endpoint of a distribution](right-endpoint-of-a-distribution.md) with [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) $F$ is $x_F=\sup\{x:F(x)<1\}$, possibly infinite. The [survival function](survival-function.md) is positive below $x_F$.
