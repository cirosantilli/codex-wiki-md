# Tait conjectures

↑ **Parent:** [Knot diagram](knot-diagram.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tait_conjectures)

The Tait conjectures concern reduced [alternating knot diagrams](alternating-knot-diagram.md): they minimize the [crossing number of a knot](crossing-number-of-a-knot.md), have an invariant diagram [writhe](writhe.md), and are related by flypes. These formerly conjectural assertions are now theorems; the first is the [Tait crossing-number theorem](tait-crossing-number-theorem.md).
