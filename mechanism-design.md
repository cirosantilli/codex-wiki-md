# Mechanism design

↑ **Parent:** [Game theory](game-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanism_design)

Mechanism design chooses allocation and payment rules while accounting for how agents' information and incentives affect their reports. [Social choice theory](social-choice-theory.md) studies collective choice from preferences, while [auction](auction.md) design also uses monetary transfers.
