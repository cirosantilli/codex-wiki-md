# Tightness of a probability measure

↑ **Parent:** [Probability measure](probability-measure.md)

A [probability measure](probability-measure.md) is tight if compact sets carry arbitrarily nearly all its mass. Every [Borel probability measure](borel-probability-measure.md) on a [Polish space](polish-space.md) is tight. The stronger family condition is [uniform tightness](uniform-tightness.md).
