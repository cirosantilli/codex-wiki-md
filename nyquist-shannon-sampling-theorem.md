<h1 id="nyquist-shannon-sampling-theorem">Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Fourier analysis](fourier-analysis-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyquist–Shannon_sampling_theorem)

A suitably band-limited function can be reconstructed from equally spaced samples when the sampling rate exceeds twice its largest ordinary frequency. Sampling more slowly aliases distinct [frequencies](frequency.md) in the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md). For spatial sampling, the same statement relates sample pitch to the shortest resolvable wavelength.
