# Area form

↑ **Parent:** [Volume form](volume-form.md)

The oriented metric [volume form](volume-form.md) on a two-dimensional surface. On the unit [sphere](sphere.md) with standard orientation, $\omega=\sin\theta\,d\theta\wedge d\varphi$ and $\int\omega=4\pi$. Its [pullback of a differential form](pullback-of-a-differential-form.md) under a map measures signed local area.
