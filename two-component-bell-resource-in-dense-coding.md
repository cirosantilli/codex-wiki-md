# Two-component Bell resource in dense coding

↑ **Parent:** [Superdense coding](superdense-coding.md)

With phase/parity labels $(u,v)$ encoded by $X^vZ^u$, a resource $a|\Phi^+\rangle+b|\Phi^-\rangle$ gives intended-label probability $|a|^2$ and phase-label-flip probability $|b|^2$ under a [Bell-basis measurement](bell-basis-measurement.md). This follows because the local [Pauli operator](pauli-operator.md) maps the two orthogonal components into the two Bell states whose phase label differs. The resource is a coherent [pure state](pure-state.md), even though its measured label statistics form a classical noisy channel.
