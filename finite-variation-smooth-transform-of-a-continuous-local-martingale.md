# Finite-variation smooth transform of a continuous local martingale

↑ **Parent:** [Continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md)

If $M$ is a [continuous local martingale](continuous-local-martingale.md) and $f\in C^2(\mathbb R)$, then [finite variation](total-variation-of-a-function.md) of $f(M)$ forces $f(M_t)=f(M_0)$. The [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) and the [quadratic variation of a stochastic integral](quadratic-variation-of-a-stochastic-integral.md) give $\int_0^t f'(M_s)^2d[M]_s=0$. The [occupation-times formula](occupation-times-formula.md) implies that $L_t^a(M)=0$ almost everywhere where $f'(a)\ne0$. On the zero set of $f'$, its derivative $f''$ vanishes almost everywhere: zeros at which $f''\ne0$ are isolated and hence countable. Thus $\int_0^t|f''(M_s)|d[M]_s=\int |f''(a)|L_t^a(M)da=0$. Both terms in the [Itô formula](ito-s-lemma.md) vanish.
