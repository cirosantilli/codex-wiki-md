# Pure-state negative conditional entropy criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum conditional entropy](quantum-conditional-entropy.md)

For a bipartite [pure state](pure-state.md) with finite [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md), the joint [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md) is zero, so its [quantum conditional entropy](quantum-conditional-entropy.md) is $-S(\rho_A)$. The [Schmidt decomposition](schmidt-decomposition.md) makes the nonzero [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of $\rho_A$ the squared [Schmidt coefficients](schmidt-coefficient.md). Their [Shannon entropy](information-entropy.md) vanishes exactly when there is one coefficient, equivalently the joint [pure state](pure-state.md) is a [product state](product-state.md). Thus negative [quantum conditional entropy](quantum-conditional-entropy.md) detects exactly the [entangled](entangled-state.md) bipartite [pure states](pure-state.md); it is only a sufficient criterion for [entanglement](entangled-state.md) of general mixed [density operators](density-matrix.md).
