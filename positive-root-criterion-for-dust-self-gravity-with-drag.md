# Positive-root criterion for dust self-gravity with drag

↑ **Parent:** [Dust gravitational dispersion relation with gas drag](dust-gravitational-dispersion-relation-with-gas-drag.md)

For any nonzero positive drag, the cubic [dust gravitational dispersion relation with gas drag](dust-gravitational-dispersion-relation-with-gas-drag.md) is negative at $s=0$ if $\omega^2<\Omega^2$ and positive at sufficiently large positive $s$. The [intermediate value theorem](intermediate-value-theorem.md) therefore guarantees a growing real root. This existence argument is independent of the weak-drag expansion and includes the dynamically unstable $\omega^2<0$ regime.
