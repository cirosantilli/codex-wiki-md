# Bulk-viscous limit of a material-density-damped condensate

↑ **Parent:** [Material-density damping of a Gross–Pitaevskii condensate](material-density-damping-of-a-gross-pitaevskii-condensate.md)

Write $\psi=\sqrt\rho e^{i\theta}$ away from zeros, and $\mathbf v_s=\nabla\theta$. Its [Madelung equations](madelung-equations.md) give $\rho_t+\nabla\cdot(\rho\mathbf v_s)=0$ and $\theta_t+|\mathbf v_s|^2/2=1-\rho+\nabla^2\sqrt\rho/(2\sqrt\rho)-\zeta D_n\rho$. For an incompressible [normal fluid](normal-component-of-a-superfluid.md), $D_n\rho=-\nabla\cdot[\rho(\mathbf v_s-\mathbf v_n)]$. Taking a gradient proves the displayed equation when each space-time derivative of the slowly varying amplitudes is $O(\epsilon)$: the [quantum potential](quantum-potential.md) force is $O(\epsilon^3)$, while the bulk term is $O(\epsilon^2)$.
