# Weighted collective oscillator frequency

↑ **Parent:** [Phase reduction](phase-reduction.md)

Linearizing weakly coupled locked phases gives $0=\xi-\Omega\mathbf1-kL\theta$, where $L$ is the directed [graph Laplacian](laplacian-matrix.md). Multiplying by a normalized left null [vector](vector.md) $\pi^{\mathsf T}$ eliminates coupling and gives the displayed weighted [frequency](frequency.md). In a balanced network, including symmetric reciprocal coupling, $\pi=\mathbf1/N$ and the [frequency](frequency.md) is the [arithmetic mean](arithmetic-mean.md). A zero diagonal in the coupling [matrix](matrix.md) alone does not imply balance. For two directed weights $p,q>0$, the weights are $(q,p)/(p+q)$.
