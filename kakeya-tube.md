# Kakeya tube

↑ **Parent:** [Kakeya set](kakeya-set.md)

A Kakeya tube is a neighborhood of transverse radius $\delta$ of a line segment of fixed unit length. Round cross-sections and comparable rectangular cross-sections are interchangeable up to fixed constants. The tube has [Lebesgue measure](lebesgue-measure.md) comparable to $\delta^{n-1}$ in $\mathbb R^n$.
