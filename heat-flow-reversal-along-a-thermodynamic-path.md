# Heat-flow reversal along a thermodynamic path

↑ **Parent:** [First law of thermodynamics](first-law-of-thermodynamics-split.md)

For a quasistatic simple compressible system with only pressure-volume [work](work.md), the direction of [heat](heat-split.md) transfer is determined by $dU+p\,dV$. A reversal occurs where this vanishes and changes sign. It need not coincide with a maximum or minimum of [temperature](temperature-split.md), because the substance can still do [work](work.md) when its internal [energy](energy-split.md) is stationary.
