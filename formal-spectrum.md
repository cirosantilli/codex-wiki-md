# Formal spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Formal scheme](formal-scheme.md)

For a complete [ring](ring.md) with an [adic topology](adic-topology.md) defined by $I$, the [formal spectrum](formal-spectrum.md) has the prime ideals containing $I$ as its points and structure sheaf $\varprojlim_n\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Spec}(A/I^{n+1})}$. On a [principal open subset](principal-open-subscheme.md) $D(f)$ its sections form the [completed localization of an adic ring](completed-localization-of-an-adic-ring.md), not generally the ordinary localization $A_f$.
