# Flexural-gravity wave

↑ **Parent:** [Flexural wave](flexural-wave.md)

A flexural-gravity wave bends a floating [elastic plate](elastic-plate.md) while displacing the underlying water. For a uniform plate with areal mass $m$ and [bending stiffness](bending-stiffness.md) $D$ above inviscid deep water of [mass density](density.md) $\rho_w$, put $\alpha=m/\rho_w$ and $\beta=D/\rho_w$. Combining the plate equation with the water's [kinematic boundary condition](kinematic-boundary-condition.md) gives the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). Both [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) and plate bending restore displacement; water and plate inertia resist acceleration. It assumes small amplitude, no prestress and negligible shear deformation of the plate.
