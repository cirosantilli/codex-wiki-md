# Quasistatic gyrotactic balance with half-rate reorientation

↑ **Parent:** [Bottom-heavy spherical-cell orientation dynamics](bottom-heavy-spherical-cell-orientation-dynamics.md)

This convention defines the [gyrotactic reorientation time](gyrotactic-reorientation-time.md) by an aligning rate $1/(2B)$. Multiplying the [torque](torque.md) balance by $2B$ and perturbing about the upward direction gives $\mathbf p_\perp=B\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf e_z$. For an [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), its horizontal [divergence](divergence.md) is $-B\nabla^2w$, because $\nabla\times\boldsymbol\omega=-\nabla^2\mathbf u$. The parameter $B$ is half the reorientation parameter used in the convention with aligning rate $1/B$; writing the governing equation prevents a factor-of-two ambiguity.
