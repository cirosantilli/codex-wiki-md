# Ideal magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation

↑ **Parent:** [Ideal magnetohydrodynamics](ideal-magnetohydrodynamics.md)

The inviscid [magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation](magnetohydrodynamic-momentum-equation.md) balances inertia, [pressure](pressure.md), [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) and [Lorentz force density](lorentz-force-density.md). The magnetic force separates into [magnetic tension](magnetic-tension.md) and [magnetic pressure](magnetic-pressure.md). It is coupled to the [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) and the [ideal magnetohydrodynamic induction equation](ideal-magnetohydrodynamic-induction-equation.md).
