# Proton drip line

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear drip line](nuclear-drip-line.md)

The [proton drip line](proton-drip-line.md) is the proton-rich boundary at which one- or two-proton emission becomes energetically possible. It is governed by [proton separation energies](proton-separation-energy.md), rather than the energetics of [beta decay](beta-decay.md) alone.
