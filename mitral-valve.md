# Mitral valve

↑ **Parent:** [Heart valve](heart-valve.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitral_valve)

The [mitral valve](mitral-valve.md) separates the left atrium from the [left ventricle](left-ventricle.md). In a reservoir model with the atrium incorporated into the venous filling compartment, ideal one-way flow is $Q_v=[p_v-p_{LV}]_+/R_v$, where $[x]_+=\max(x,0)$ and $R_v$ is open-valve [hydraulic resistance](hydraulic-resistance.md). The positive-part rule prevents reverse flow in this idealization.
