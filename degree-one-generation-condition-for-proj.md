# Degree-one generation condition for Proj

↑ **Parent:** [Proj construction](proj-construction.md)

If a nonnegatively graded ring $S$ is generated by $S_1$ as an $S_0$-algebra, the [standard affine opens of Proj](standard-affine-open-of-proj.md) $D_+(f)$ with $f\in S_1$ cover $\operatorname{Proj}S$. Indeed a homogeneous prime containing every degree-one element would contain the [irrelevant ideal of a graded ring](irrelevant-ideal-of-a-graded-ring.md). This condition makes all [twisting sheaves on Proj](twisting-sheaf-on-proj.md) invertible and makes graded sheafification compatible with [tensor products of sheaves](tensor-product-of-sheaves.md).
