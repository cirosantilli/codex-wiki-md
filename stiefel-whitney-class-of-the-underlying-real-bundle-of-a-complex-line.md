<h1 id="stiefel-whitney-class-of-the-underlying-real-bundle-of-a-complex-line">Stiefel–Whitney class of the underlying real bundle of a complex line</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Stiefel–Whitney class](stiefel-whitney-class.md)

A [complex line bundle](complex-line-bundle.md) has a canonically oriented underlying real [vector bundle](vector-bundle.md) of rank two. Its first [Stiefel–Whitney class](stiefel-whitney-class.md) vanishes, and its second [Stiefel–Whitney class](stiefel-whitney-class.md) is the mod-two reduction of its [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md).
