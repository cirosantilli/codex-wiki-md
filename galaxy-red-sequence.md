# Galaxy red sequence

↑ **Parent:** [Galaxy evolution](galaxy-evolution.md)

A galaxy red sequence is the relatively narrow red locus in a galaxy colour-magnitude diagram, populated largely by systems with little recent [star formation](star-formation.md). Its colour, abundance, and [galaxy stellar mass](galaxy-stellar-mass.md) evolve. Dust and selection must be distinguished from old stellar ages; growth can involve quenching and later assembly of existing stars.
