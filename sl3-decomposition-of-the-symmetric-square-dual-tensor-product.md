# sl3 decomposition of the symmetric-square dual tensor product

↑ **Parent:** [Symmetric powers of the defining sln representation](symmetric-powers-of-the-defining-sln-representation.md)

For the defining three-dimensional [special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md) representation $V$, contraction $(uv)\otimes f\mapsto f(u)v+f(v)u$ maps $(S^2V)\otimes V^*$ onto $V$. The kernel contains the [highest-weight vector](highest-weight-vector.md) $e_1^2\otimes e_3^*$ of [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) $(2,1)$. The [Weyl dimension formula](weyl-dimension-formula.md) gives its irreducible module dimension $15$, the entire kernel dimension; the [Weyl complete reducibility theorem](weyl-complete-reducibility-theorem.md) supplies the displayed splitting.
