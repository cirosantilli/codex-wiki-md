# Hexamethylborazine

↑ **Parent:** [Borazine](borazine.md)

[Hexamethylborazine](hexamethylborazine.md) is $\mathrm{B_3N_3Me_6}$, with one methyl on every ring atom. Its [nitrogen](nitrogen.md) atoms can form [Lewis acid-base adducts](lewis-acid-base-adduct.md), while [nucleophiles](nucleophile.md) can attack its boron atoms.
