# White phosphorus

↑ **Parent:** [Phosphorus](phosphorus.md)

A molecular allotrope consisting of tetrahedral $\mathrm P_4$ molecules. Every vertex has three P–P bonds and one [lone pair](lone-pair.md); its $60^\circ$ P–P–P angles produce substantial angle strain.
