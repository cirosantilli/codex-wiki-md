# Strict concavity

↑ **Parent:** [Strictly concave function](strictly-concave-function.md)

Strict concavity is the displayed property for distinct $x,y$ in a [convex set](convex-set.md) and $0<t<1$. It makes a stationary point of a [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md) its unique global maximum, whenever such a stationary point exists.
