# Integral bounds for a logarithmic factorial

↑ **Parent:** [Stirling formula](stirling-formula.md)

For integer $N\geq2$, write $\ln(N!)=\sum_{k=2}^N\ln k$. Monotonicity gives $\int_{k-1}^k\ln x\,dx\leq\ln k\leq\int_k^{k+1}\ln x\,dx$. Summing and evaluating the endpoint integrals proves the displayed bounds. They also hold with equality when $N=1$. The lower integral starts at one and the upper at two; confusing those endpoints loses the constant in the upper bound. The bounds imply $\ln(N!)=N\ln N-N+O(\ln N)$, while [Stirling's formula](stirling-formula.md) determines the logarithmic and constant corrections.
