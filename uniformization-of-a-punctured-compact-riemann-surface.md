# Uniformization of a punctured compact Riemann surface

↑ **Parent:** [Uniformization theorem](uniformization-theorem.md)

Let $R$ be a compact [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md) of [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) $g$, and remove $n$ distinct points. The punctured surface is uniformized by the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) exactly when $2g-2+n>0$. It is uniformized by the [complex plane](complex-plane.md) exactly when $2g-2+n$ is $-1$ or $0$, corresponding respectively to $\mathbb C$, and to $\mathbb C^*$ or a [complex torus](complex-torus.md). The sole spherical case is $g=n=0$.
