# Bogoliubov transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical transformation of mode operators](canonical-transformation-of-mode-operators.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogoliubov_transformation)

A [Bogoliubov transformation](bogoliubov-transformation.md) is a [canonical transformation of mode operators](canonical-transformation-of-mode-operators.md) mixing [creation operators](creation-operator.md) and [annihilation operators](annihilation-operator.md). It diagonalizes quadratic [Hamiltonians](hamiltonian.md) or relates different vacuum descriptions. Bosonic and fermionic canonical bracket constraints differ. A nonzero creation-mixing coefficient makes its vacuum contain particles relative to the original mode decomposition.
