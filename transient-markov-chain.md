# Transient Markov chain

↑ **Parent:** [Markov chain](markov-chain-split.md)

An [irreducible Markov chain](irreducible-markov-chain.md) is transient when its states are [transient states](transient-state.md). Starting at any one state, there is a positive probability of never returning, and the number of visits to any fixed state is finite [almost surely](almost-sure-convergence.md).
