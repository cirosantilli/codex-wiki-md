# Integral Hermitian forms on the square complex torus

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann form on a complex torus](riemann-form-on-a-complex-torus.md)

On $\mathbb C/(\mathbb Z+\mathbb Zi)$, a [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md) linear in its first argument has integral imaginary part on the [period lattice](period-lattice.md) exactly when its real scalar coefficient is integral. Positivity is equivalent to $t>0$. The associated [holomorphic line bundle](holomorphic-line-bundle.md) has degree $t$ because its [First Chern class](first-chern-class.md) is represented by $(it/2)dz\wedge d\overline z$. In particular the oriented Chern pairing is minus the imaginary part under this convention.
