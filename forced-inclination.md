# Forced inclination

↑ **Parent:** [Complex inclination](complex-inclination.md)

The particular, forced part of a [complex inclination](complex-inclination.md) solution. For a fixed, circular, inclined inner [planet](planet.md) and an exterior test ring, linear [Laplace-Lagrange secular theory](laplace-lagrange-secular-theory.md) gives $\dot w=-i\nu(w-w_p)$, so $w_f=w_p$ and $w=w_p+(w(0)-w_p)e^{-i\nu t}$. The ring describes a circle about the planetary tilt in an [Argand diagram](complex-plane.md); relative to the planet, its [mutual inclination](mutual-inclination.md) stays constant while its [ascending node](ascending-node.md) precesses.
