# Stokes operator

↑ **Parent:** [Navier-Stokes equation](navier-stokes-equation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stokes_operator)

The Stokes operator is $A=-P_\sigma\Delta$ on divergence-free vector fields, where $P_\sigma$ is the [Leray-Helmholtz projection](leray-helmholtz-projection.md). On a periodic domain it equals $-\Delta$ on its domain, and its inverse gains two [Sobolev derivatives](sobolev-space-split.md).
