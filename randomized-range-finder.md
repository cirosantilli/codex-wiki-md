# Randomized range finder

↑ **Parent:** [Low-rank approximation](low-rank-approximation.md)

Choose a random sampling [matrix](matrix.md) $X$, form $Y=AX$, and find an [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) $Q$ for the sampled [column space](column-space.md) by a thin [QR decomposition](qr-decomposition.md). The [low-rank approximation](low-rank-approximation.md) $\widehat A=QQ^TA$ keeps the component of $A$ in that space. Since $QQ^TAX=AX$, it reproduces the sampled action exactly. If $Y$ has deficient [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md), one can use only a [basis](basis.md) of its [column space](column-space.md); alternatively an [orthonormal](orthonormal-set.md) completion must be specified.
