# Saint-Venant strain compatibility and differential identities

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

For a [infinitesimal strain tensor](infinitesimal-strain-tensor.md) $e=(\nabla u+\nabla u^T)/2$, commuting [displacement](displacement.md) [derivatives](derivative.md) gives the symmetric incompatibility [tensor](tensor.md) $C=0$. Its six component equations are the [Saint-Venant compatibility equations](saint-venant-strain-compatibility-and-differential-identities.md). They satisfy three differential identities because $\partial_i\partial_k$ is symmetric while $\epsilon_{ik\ell}$ is antisymmetric. Thus there are three independent differential constraints, although keeping all six equations is a coordinate-symmetric way to state them. At a nonzero Fourier [wavevector](wavevector.md) along the third axis the constrained components are $e_{11},e_{22},e_{12}$, while $e_{13},e_{23},e_{33}$ arise from the three [displacement](displacement.md) components. On a [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) region compatibility gives local integrability up to a rigid motion; on a [multiply connected region](multiply-connected-region.md) [circulation](circulation-physics.md) conditions must also be imposed.
