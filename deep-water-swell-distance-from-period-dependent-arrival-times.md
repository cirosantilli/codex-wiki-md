# Deep-water swell distance from period-dependent arrival times

↑ **Parent:** [Deep-water gravity wave](deep-water-gravity-wave.md)

For two wave periods emitted together at one location, deep-water [group velocity](group-velocity.md) is $gT/(4\pi)$. A distant observer sees the shorter period $T_2<T_1$ later, with $\Delta t=(4\pi D/g)(1/T_2-1/T_1)$. This yields the displayed range estimate. Different emission times, a moving storm, finite-depth dispersion, currents and refraction are possible confounders; the propagation calculation alone does not establish a unique source range without these assumptions.
