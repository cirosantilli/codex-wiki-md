# Empirical misclassification risk

↑ **Parent:** [Misclassification risk](misclassification-risk.md)

On labelled examples $Z_1,\ldots,Z_n$, the empirical misclassification risk is

$$
\widehat R(h)=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\ell_{0-1}(h,Z_i).
$$

An [empirical risk minimizer](empirical-risk-minimization.md) chooses a [classifier](classifier.md) attaining the smallest value over its [hypothesis class](concept-class.md).
