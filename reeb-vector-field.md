# Reeb vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Contact form](contact-form.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reeb_vector_field)

The Reeb vector field of a contact form is uniquely characterized by

$$
\alpha(R_\alpha)=1,
\qquad
\iota_{R_\alpha}d\alpha=0.
$$
