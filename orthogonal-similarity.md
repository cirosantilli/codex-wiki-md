# Orthogonal similarity

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix similarity](matrix-similarity.md)

An orthogonal [matrix similarity](matrix-similarity.md) changes coordinates by an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md). It preserves [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md), [singular values](singular-value.md) and Euclidean geometry; it also preserves symmetry when applied to a [symmetric matrix](symmetric-matrix.md). A sequence of [Householder transformations](householder-transformation.md) can use this freedom to produce a [Hessenberg reduction](hessenberg-reduction.md), while a symmetric matrix can be reduced further to tridiagonal form.
