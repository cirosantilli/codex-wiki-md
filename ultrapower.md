# Ultrapower

↑ **Parent:** [Ultraproduct](ultraproduct.md)

An ultrapower is an [ultraproduct](ultraproduct.md) whose factors are all one structure $M$. Its elements are equivalence classes $[f]$ of functions $f:I\to M$, where $f\sim g$ exactly when $\{i\in I:f(i)=g(i)\}$ belongs to the chosen [ultrafilter](ultrafilter.md) $\mathcal U$.
