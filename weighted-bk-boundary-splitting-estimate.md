# Weighted BK boundary-splitting estimate

↑ **Parent:** [One-arm probability](one-arm-probability.md)

For independent [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) on the [cubic lattice](cubic-lattice.md), define $\tau_p(x,y)=\mathbb P_p(x\leftrightarrow y)$ and $g_n=\mathbb P_p(0\leftrightarrow\partial\Lambda_n)$, with $g_0=1$. Split an open [self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md) at its first radius-$m$ boundary vertex $y$. The remaining segment reaches distance at least $n-m$ from $y$, using disjoint edges. The [BK inequality](van-den-berg-kesten-inequality.md), translation invariance and the [union bound](boole-s-inequality.md) yield the displayed estimate. Keeping the individual connection weights can be stronger than replacing their sum by $|\partial\Lambda_m|g_m$.
