# Resonance channel threshold conversion

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear reaction Q-value](nuclear-reaction-q-value.md)

The same compound-state [energy](energy-split.md) is measured from different rest-energy thresholds in different entrance channels. Subtracting those thresholds converts the required centre-of-momentum [kinetic energies](kinetic-energy.md). For a stationary target and nonrelativistic projectile, the laboratory [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) is $T_{\rm lab}=T^*(m_{\rm projectile}+m_{\rm target})/m_{\rm target}$. A negative converted $T^*$ means that entrance channel is closed at that resonance [energy](energy-split.md).
