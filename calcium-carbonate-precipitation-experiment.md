# Calcium carbonate precipitation experiment

↑ **Parent:** [Precipitation (chemistry)](precipitation-chemistry.md)

The University of Utah's teaching module [Carbon Captured Chalk by Tony Butterfield](carbon-captured-chalk-by-tony-butterfield.md) describes a supervised demonstration. Its Materials and Methods uses a balance, weighing boats, spatula, separate centrifuge tubes, a volume-measuring vessel and filter paper. [Sodium carbonate](sodium-carbonate.md) and [calcium chloride](calcium-chloride.md) are dissolved separately in [water](water.md) before mixing. The reported readout is an initially opaque mixture followed by a milky dispersion of fine [solid](solid.md) particles; filtration retains the chalk-like product, which is then dried. The module also proposes observing drops together on a slide using an [optical microscope](optical-microscope.md).[Carbon Captured Chalk, Materials and Methods, steps 1–13; Optional Activities and Extensions, microscope slide](carbon-captured-chalk-by-tony-butterfield.md)

The handout does not report blank trials. Useful additional controls would retain each starting [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) separately, mix each with [water](water.md), and pass [water](water.md) alone through the same filter. A new [solid](solid.md) only in the combined sample would support a mixing-dependent [chemical reaction](chemical-reaction-split.md); these are proposed comparisons, not reported results. Residue from a filter blank would instead flag contamination.

Cloudiness plus a retained [solid](solid.md) supports phase separation; appearance alone cannot establish composition, a particular [crystal polymorph](crystal-polymorph.md), quantitative yield or the [solubility product](solubility-product.md). Fine [solid](solid.md) particles may pass through a filter, and retained liquid can leave dissolved [salts](salt-chemistry.md) on drying. Identifying [calcium carbonate](calcium-carbonate.md) needs additional chemical or structural evidence. The teaching source supplies no measured [ion products](ion-product.md), instrument-calibrated cloudiness series or equilibration test. Its concentrated demonstration therefore illustrates [precipitation](precipitation-chemistry.md) without validating the toy model's conditional constant or proving complete removal of dissolved [calcium](calcium.md).
