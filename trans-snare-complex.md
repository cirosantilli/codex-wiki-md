# Trans-SNARE complex

↑ **Parent:** [SNARE complex](snare-complex.md)

A [trans-SNARE complex](trans-snare-complex.md) links [SNARE proteins](snare-protein.md) anchored in different [lipid bilayers](lipid-bilayer.md). Its formation can bring membranes together before complete [membrane fusion](membrane-fusion.md).
