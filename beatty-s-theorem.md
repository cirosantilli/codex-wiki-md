<h1 id="beatty-s-theorem">Beatty's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Beatty sequence](beatty-sequence.md)

If $\alpha,\beta>1$ are [irrational numbers](irrational-number.md) with $1/\alpha+1/\beta=1$, the [Beatty sequences](beatty-sequence.md) $\lfloor n\alpha\rfloor$ and $\lfloor n\beta\rfloor$ partition the [positive integers](positive-integer.md).
