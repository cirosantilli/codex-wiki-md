# Fermi motion

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear Fermi gas model](nuclear-fermi-gas-model.md)

The motion of particles occupying the [momentum](momentum-split.md) sea of a [Fermi gas](fermi-gas.md), especially [nucleons](nucleon.md) in an [atomic nucleus](atomic-nucleus-split.md). It persists at zero [temperature](temperature-split.md) and broadens scattering kinematics relative to a stationary-nucleon approximation.
