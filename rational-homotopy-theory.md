# Rational homotopy theory

↑ **Parent:** [Homotopy](homotopy.md)

Study [homotopy](homotopy.md) after killing torsion, especially for [simply connected](simply-connected-space.md) spaces of finite type. The [rational Whitehead theorem](rational-whitehead-theorem.md) detects rational [homotopy](homotopy.md) equivalences by rational [homology](homology-split.md), while [Sullivan minimal models](sullivan-minimal-model.md) encode rational [homotopy](homotopy.md) generators and cohomological relations.
