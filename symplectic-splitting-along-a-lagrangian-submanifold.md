# Symplectic splitting along a Lagrangian submanifold

↑ **Parent:** [Weinstein neighborhood theorem](weinstein-neighborhood-theorem.md)

A [Lagrangian complement](lagrangian-complement.md) to $TX$ in $TM|_X$ is identified with $T^*X$ by $v\mapsto\omega(\,\cdot\,,v)|_{TX}$. This produces a [vector bundle isomorphism](vector-bundle-isomorphism.md) $TX\oplus T^*X\to TM|_X$ that is the identity on $TX$ and carries the canonical pairing $\zeta(u)-\eta(v)$ to $\omega$. A suitably chosen [tubular neighborhood](tubular-neighborhood.md) map therefore matches the ambient two-forms along $X$, enabling the [relative Moser theorem](relative-moser-theorem.md).
