# Diamond cubic structure

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal structure](crystal-structure.md)

An FCC [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) with a two-atom basis at fractional positions $(0,0,0)$ and $(1/4,1/4,1/4)$. Its conventional cubic cell contains eight atoms. Equal scattering amplitudes at the two basis sites produce additional [systematic absences](systematic-absence.md) beyond the FCC parity selection rule.
