# Skolem expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Skolem function](skolem-function.md)

A [Skolem expansion](skolem-expansion.md) adjoins a [Skolem function](skolem-function.md) for every existential [first-order formula](first-order-formula.md). Iterating through the successively enlarged languages, and taking their union, gives a language with witness [functions](function-split.md) even for [first-order formulas](first-order-formula.md) involving previously added [functions](function-split.md). Starting from a language of size $\kappa$, the resulting language has size at most $\kappa+\aleph_0$. A hull closed under its [functions](function-split.md) is a [Skolem hull](skolem-hull.md) and is elementary in the expanded structure by the [Tarski-Vaught test](tarski-vaught-test.md).
