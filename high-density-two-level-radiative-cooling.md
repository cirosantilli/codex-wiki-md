# High-density two-level radiative cooling

↑ **Parent:** [Isochoric thermal stability of a collisionally excited line](isochoric-thermal-stability-of-a-collisionally-excited-line.md)

When collisions thermalize an optically thin two-level atom, the upper fraction is $f_u=g e^{-\theta/T}/(1+g e^{-\theta/T})$ and the emitted power per atom is $A_{21}\Delta E f_u$. Its logarithmic [temperature](temperature-split.md) slope is $(\theta/T)(1-f_u)>0$. [Radiative cooling](radiative-cooling.md) still occurs, but collision frequency no longer sets the emitted power as in the low-[density](density.md) limit.
