# Edge-triangle symmetrization

↑ **Parent:** [Zykov symmetrization](zykov-symmetrization.md)

For each real $c$, some maximizer of $e(G)-c k_3(G)$ among [graphs](graph-split.md) on $n$ [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) is [complete multipartite graph](complete-multipartite-graph.md). Among maximizers maximize $\sum_v d(v)^2$. Cloning either of two nonadjacent [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) preserves the objective, since maximality forces their local contributions $d(v)-c e(G[N(v)])$ to agree. If their [vertex neighbourhoods](vertex-neighbourhood.md) differ, the sum of the two changes in the squared-[degree of a vertex](degree-graph-theory.md) objective is twice the size of their symmetric difference, a contradiction. Nonadjacency therefore partitions the [vertices](vertex-graph-theory.md) into classes with identical [vertex neighbourhoods](vertex-neighbourhood.md).
