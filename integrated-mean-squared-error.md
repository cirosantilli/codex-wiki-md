# Integrated mean squared error

↑ **Parent:** [Mean squared error](mean-squared-error.md)

The [integrated mean squared error](integrated-mean-squared-error.md) is $\mathbb E\int_D(\widehat m(x)-m(x))^2\,dx$ over a specified domain $D$. The [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) permits integration of the pointwise [bias-variance decomposition of mean squared error](bias-variance-decomposition-of-mean-squared-error.md).
