# Prime-exponent parametrization of smooth numbers

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth number](smooth-number.md)

For distinct [primes](prime-number-split.md) $p_1,\ldots,p_r$, let $M$ consist of the positive [integers](integer-split.md) having no other [prime factors](prime-factor.md), including $1$. The map from a number to its exponent tuple is a [bijection](bijection.md) from $M$ to $\mathbb N^r$ when $0\in\mathbb N$. The [fundamental theorem of arithmetic](fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic.md) proves that the tuple is uniquely defined and that equality of tuples implies equality of numbers. Every nonnegative tuple gives the allowed number $p_1^{e_1}\cdots p_r^{e_r}$, proving [surjectivity](surjective-function.md). Multiplication of numbers corresponds to coordinatewise addition of tuples, with $1$ corresponding to the zero tuple.
