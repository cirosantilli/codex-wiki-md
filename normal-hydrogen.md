# Normal hydrogen

↑ **Parent:** [Spin isomers of hydrogen](spin-isomers-of-hydrogen.md)

[Normal hydrogen](normal-hydrogen.md) denotes the $3:1$ [orthohydrogen](orthohydrogen.md)-to-[parahydrogen](parahydrogen.md) mixture, the high-[temperature](temperature-split.md) equilibrium limit. Near room [temperature](temperature-split.md) the equilibrium ratio is already close to this limit. Cooling an isolated sample need not change its composition on the measurement timescale: ordinary collisions can redistribute rotational populations within each spin species much faster than they interconvert the spin species. A kinetically frozen composition can therefore coexist with rotational [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md) separately within each species.

The distinction was historically decisive. The institutional account [Die Entdeckung des para-Wasserstoffs](https://www.mpinat.mpg.de/607569/para-Wasserstoff), in its discussion of the rotational [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md), explains that assuming an equilibrating composition missed the observed behavior, whereas freezing the high-[temperature](temperature-split.md) ratio reproduced it. It also records [Karl Friedrich Bonhoeffer](karl-friedrich-bonhoeffer.md) and [Paul Harteck](paul-harteck.md)'s 1929 preparation using a [catalyst](catalyst.md) and [thermal conductivity](thermal-conductivity.md) detection.[paragraphs on frozen high-temperature composition and the 1929 charcoal/thermal-conductivity discovery](die-entdeckung-des-para-wasserstoffs.md)
