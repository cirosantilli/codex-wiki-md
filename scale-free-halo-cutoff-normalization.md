# Scale-free halo cutoff normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Peak-height calibration from an exponential mass-function cutoff](peak-height-calibration-from-an-exponential-mass-function-cutoff.md)

For $\sigma(M,0)=SM^{-q}$ with $q=(n+3)/6>0$, matching the [Press-Schechter halo mass function](press-schechter-halo-mass-function.md) to $AM^{q-2}\exp[-(M/M_*)^{2q}]$ requires $\sigma(M_*,0)=\delta_c(z)/\sqrt2$. The comoving normalization is $A=2q\bar\rho_{m,0}M_*^{-q}/\sqrt\pi$. Consequently both $A$ and $M_*$ evolve with the [linear growth factor](linear-growth-factor.md); $M_*\propto D^{1/q}$.
