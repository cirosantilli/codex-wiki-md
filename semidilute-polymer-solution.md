# Semidilute polymer solution

↑ **Parent:** [Polymer solution](polymer-solution.md)

A solution above the [polymer overlap concentration](polymer-overlap-concentration.md) but below the concentrated regime, with interpenetrating chains. Local polymer structure is characterized by a [correlation length](correlation-length.md) or concentration-blob size. Scaling formulas describe a broad semidilute regime most reliably when the concentration greatly exceeds overlap while remaining small compared with one in [volume fraction](volume-fraction.md).
