# Tumour spheroid

↑ **Parent:** [3D cell culture](3d-cell-culture.md)

A [tumour spheroid](tumour-spheroid.md) is a three-dimensional aggregate of tumour cells used to study growth, transport and interactions with surrounding material. It need not reproduce the organized cellular diversity of an [organoid](organoid.md).
