# Metric quotient of a pseudometric

↑ **Parent:** [Pseudometric](pseudometric.md)

Zero distance in a [pseudometric](pseudometric.md) defines an [equivalence relation](equivalence-relation.md): the triangle inequality proves transitivity. If $x$ and $x'$ have zero distance, as do $y$ and $y'$, the triangle inequality in both directions gives $d(x,y)=d(x',y')$. Thus distance descends to the quotient and becomes a [metric](metric.md), since zero quotient distance means the classes coincide.
