# One-dimensional acoustic phonon heat capacity

↑ **Parent:** [Phonon density of states](phonon-density-of-states.md)

A single acoustic branch $\omega=v|k|$ has thermal energy per length $U_T/\ell=\int dk\,\hbar v|k|/[2\pi(e^{\beta\hbar v|k|}-1)]=\pi(k_BT)^2/(6\hbar v)$. Differentiating gives the displayed linear [heat capacity](heat-capacity.md). The regime requires [temperatures](temperature-split.md) below the microscopic [phonon](phonon.md) cutoff but above the finite-size level spacing, with the translational [zero mode](zero-mode.md) treated separately.
