<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For every $\chi$ on a hyperplane $\langle\chi,\alpha^\vee\rangle=k$, with $k\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$, part (b) embeds one [Verma module](../../../../verma-module.md) into the other. A central element acts on the nonzero image by both scalars, so $j_z(\chi)=j_z(s_\alpha\chi)$ there.

The difference of these two expressions is a [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) on $\mathfrak h^*$. Fix all coordinates parallel to those hyperplanes and vary the remaining coordinate. It vanishes at every nonnegative integer, so as a [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) in that coordinate it is identically zero. Its coefficient polynomials therefore vanish as well. Consequently the equality holds for all $\chi$. Since the simple reflections generate the [Weyl group](../../../../weyl-group.md),

$$
\boxed{j_z(w\chi)=j_z(\chi)\qquad(w\in W).}
$$
