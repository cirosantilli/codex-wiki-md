<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The centre commutes with $\mathfrak h$, so every nonzero [representation weight](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) component of the common [eigenvector](../../../../eigenvector.md) $m$ has the same [central character](../../../../central-character-of-an-associative-algebra-module.md) $\theta$. Pick such a component $m_\lambda$. In [Category O](../../../../category-o.md), its $U(\mathfrak n_+)$-span is finite-dimensional and has finitely many weights. Choose a maximal [representation weight](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) in that span and a nonzero vector $v$ of that [representation weight](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md), say $\nu$. Every [positive root](../../../../positive-root.md) operator kills $v$, since it would raise the [representation weight](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) within the same finite span. Centrality ensures $zv=\theta(z)v$.

The universal property supplies a nonzero map $M^{\nu+\rho}\to M$ taking its highest vector to $v$. The centre acts on that image both by $\theta$ and by $\theta_{\nu+\rho}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{\theta=\theta_\xi,\qquad \xi=\nu+\rho,\quad\nu\in\operatorname{wt}(M).}
$$
