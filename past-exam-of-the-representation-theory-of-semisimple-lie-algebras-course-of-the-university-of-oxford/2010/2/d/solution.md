<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

In the shifted normalization of part (a), the [Harish-Chandra isomorphism](../../../../harish-chandra-isomorphism.md) is the algebra isomorphism

$$
\boxed{Z(U(\mathfrak g))\overset\sim\longrightarrow S(\mathfrak h)^W,\qquad z\longmapsto j_z.}
$$

Thus the centre is identified with the Weyl-invariant polynomials on $\mathfrak h^*$. Central characters satisfy $\theta_\chi=\theta_\xi$ exactly when $\chi$ and $\xi$ belong to the same Weyl orbit. The $\rho$ shift is already incorporated in $M^\chi$ having [highest weight](../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) $\chi-\rho$; without that shift the corresponding Weyl action is the dot action.
