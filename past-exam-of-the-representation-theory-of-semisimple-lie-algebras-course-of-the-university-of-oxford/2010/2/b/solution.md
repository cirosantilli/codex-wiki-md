<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write $h_\alpha$ for the simple [coroot](../../../../coroot.md) and choose the corresponding normalized $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ triple. The order condition means

$$
\chi-s_\alpha\chi=k\alpha\in Q_+,\qquad
k=\langle\chi,\alpha^\vee\rangle\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}.
$$

The highest vector has $h_\alpha$-[representation weight](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) $\langle\chi-\rho,\alpha^\vee\rangle=k-1$. The raising-lowering identity gives

$$
E_\alpha E_{-\alpha}^km_\chi
=k((k-1)-k+1)E_{-\alpha}^{k-1}m_\chi=0.
$$

Other simple [positive root](../../../../positive-root.md) vectors commute with $E_{-\alpha}$ and also kill this vector. Thus it is a highest vector of [representation weight](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md)

$$
\chi-\rho-k\alpha=s_\alpha\chi-\rho.
$$

The universal property of the [Verma module](../../../../verma-module.md) gives the required homomorphism. It is injective: under the [Poincaré-Birkhoff-Witt theorem](../../../../poincare-birkhoff-witt-theorem.md) identification of each [Verma module](../../../../verma-module.md) with the free rank-one $U(\mathfrak n_-)$ [module](../../../../module-mathematics.md), it is right multiplication by $E_{-\alpha}^k$. The algebra $U(\mathfrak n_-)$ has no zero divisors, because its associated graded algebra is the [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) domain $S(\mathfrak n_-)$. Hence

$$
\boxed{M^{s_\alpha\chi}\hookrightarrow M^\chi,\qquad
m_{s_\alpha\chi}\mapsto E_{-\alpha}^km_\chi.}
$$

For $k=0$ this is the identity map, so the equality case of the order is covered.
