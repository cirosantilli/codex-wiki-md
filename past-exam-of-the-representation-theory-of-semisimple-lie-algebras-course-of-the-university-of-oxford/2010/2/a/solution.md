<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use a [triangular decomposition of a Lie algebra](../../../../triangular-decomposition-of-a-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak g=\mathfrak n_-\oplus\mathfrak h\oplus\mathfrak n_+$. The [highest weight](../../../../highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) space of $M^\chi$ has [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md) one. If $z$ is central, $zm_\chi$ is still of [representation weight](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) $\chi-\rho$ and is killed by $\mathfrak n_+$, so

$$
zm_\chi=\theta_\chi(z)m_\chi.
$$

Since the [module](../../../../module-mathematics.md) is generated by $m_\chi$ and $z$ commutes with $U(\mathfrak g)$, the same scalar acts on every vector. Addition, multiplication and the unit show that $z\mapsto\theta_\chi(z)$ is an [algebra homomorphism](../../../../algebra-homomorphism-over-a-field.md) to $\mathbb C$.

For the [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) assertion, the relevant variable for an element of $S(\mathfrak h)$ is $\chi$. The dependence on $z$ itself is linear, as already follows from the scalar action. Apply the [Poincaré-Birkhoff-Witt theorem](../../../../poincare-birkhoff-witt-theorem.md) ordering $U(\mathfrak n_-)U(\mathfrak h)U(\mathfrak n_+)$. Because $z$ has adjoint [representation weight](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) zero, its surviving action on the highest vector is precisely the pure $U(\mathfrak h)$ component $p_z$; terms with a [positive root](../../../../positive-root.md) factor kill that vector, and a term with only a nontrivial negative [root](../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) factor could not have [representation weight](../../../../weight-of-a-representation.md) zero. Since $U(\mathfrak h)=S(\mathfrak h)$,

$$
\boxed{\theta_\chi(z)=p_z(\chi-\rho),\qquad j_z(\chi):=p_z(\chi-\rho)\in S(\mathfrak h).}
$$

This specifies the intended [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) despite the printed variable mismatch. The unadorned [module](../../../../module-mathematics.md) symbol in that sentence is understood as $M^\chi$.
