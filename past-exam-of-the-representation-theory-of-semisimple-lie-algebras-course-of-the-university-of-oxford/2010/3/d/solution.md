<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Convolve the identity of part (c) with the inverse denominator $K*\delta_{-\rho}$ found in part (a). Associativity gives

$$
\pi_{A^\phi}=\sum_{w\in W}(\det w)\,K*\delta_{w(\phi+\rho)-\rho}.
$$

Translation by a [Kronecker delta](../../../../kronecker-delta.md) obeys $(K*\delta_\lambda)(\psi)=K(\psi-\lambda)$. Taking the coefficient at $\psi$ therefore yields

$$
\boxed{\pi_{A^\phi}(\psi)=\sum_{w\in W}(\det w)
K\bigl(\psi+\rho-w(\phi+\rho)\bigr).}
$$

This is the [Kostant multiplicity formula](../../../../kostant-multiplicity-formula.md) in the paper's convention that $K$ counts negative [root](../../../../root-of-a-root-system.md) partitions. With a partition function counting [positive root](../../../../positive-root.md) sums, each argument would have the opposite sign.
