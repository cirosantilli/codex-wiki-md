<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a finite-support [formal character](../../../../formal-character-of-a-weight-module.md), use the same pullback rule $(wu)(\xi)=u(w^{-1}\xi)$; equivalently $w(e^\lambda)=e^{w\lambda}$. This specifies the action on the denominator, which is a finite delta sum rather than a [polynomial](../../../../polynomial-split.md) function. The whole infinite-support completion need not be Weyl-stable, so no such claim is required.

A simple [reflection](../../../../reflection-mathematics.md) $s_\alpha$ permutes the positive roots other than $\alpha$ and sends $\alpha$ to $-\alpha$. In the product defining $D$, all factors are therefore permuted except the $\alpha$ factor, whose sign reverses. Thus $s_\alpha D=-D$. Simple reflections generate $W$, and each has [determinant](../../../../determinant.md) $-1$, so

$$
\boxed{wD=(\det w)D.}
$$
