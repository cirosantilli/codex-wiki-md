# 3

↑ **Parent:** [2010](../2010-split.md)

For $f,g\in\mathcal E$, define their [convolution](../../convolution.md) by

$$
(f*g)(\lambda)=\sum_{\mu+\nu=\lambda}f(\mu)g(\nu).
$$

The support restrictions make each sum finite: for fixed upper-cone representatives, the [simple root](../../simple-root.md) coefficients in the two downward displacements are bounded nonnegative integers. This also proves closure in $\mathcal E$ and associativity by finite reordering at each coefficient. The unit is $\delta_0$, and $\delta_\mu*\delta_\nu=\delta_{\mu+\nu}$.
