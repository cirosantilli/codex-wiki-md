# Quantum depolarizing channel

↑ **Parent:** [Pauli channel](pauli-channel.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_depolarizing_channel)

A qubit depolarizing channel shrinks every [Bloch vector](bloch-vector.md) by the same factor: $\Lambda_p(\rho)=p\rho+(1-p)I/2$. In this retention-parameter convention it is completely positive for $-1/3\leq p\leq1$; the usual mixture of unchanged and fully randomized states uses $0\leq p\leq1$.
