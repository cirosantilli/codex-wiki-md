# Conditional maximum likelihood

↑ **Parent:** [Likelihood function](likelihood-function.md)

A conditional [likelihood function](likelihood-function.md) conditions on an initial observation or another specified statistic. In an [autoregressive model](autoregressive-model.md), conditioning on the first observed state avoids modeling its initial density. For Gaussian transitions, maximizing this likelihood is a regression problem; treating an autoregressive coefficient as known changes the exact distribution of an estimated mean.
