# Skin depth

↑ **Parent:** [Skin effect](skin-effect.md)

The [skin depth](skin-depth.md) is the distance over which a field amplitude falls by $e^{-1}$ inside a conductor. In the local good-conductor limit, $\delta=\sqrt{2/(\mu\sigma\omega)}$. Intensity then falls by $e^{-2}$ over one [skin depth](skin-depth.md); amplitude and intensity penetration lengths differ by a factor two.
