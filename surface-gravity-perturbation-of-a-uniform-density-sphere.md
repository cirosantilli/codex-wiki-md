# Surface gravity perturbation of a uniform-density sphere

↑ **Parent:** [Incompressible stellar surface mode](incompressible-stellar-surface-mode.md)

For a uniform-density sphere with [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md) of radius $R$ with one surface [spherical harmonic](spherical-harmonic.md) displacement $\xi_r(R)=lA R^{l-1}Y_l^m$, the bulk density perturbation vanishes but the surface contributes $\delta\rho=\rho\xi_r(R)\delta(r-R)$. Continuity of the [Newtonian gravitational potential](newtonian-gravitational-potential.md) and the [Poisson equation](poisson-equation.md) give $[\partial_r\delta\Phi]_{\mathrm{out}-\mathrm{in}}=4\pi G\rho\xi_r(R)$. The regular interior and decaying exterior solutions are

$$
\delta\Phi_{\mathrm{in}}=C r^lY_l^m,\qquad
\delta\Phi_{\mathrm{out}}=C R^{2l+1}r^{-l-1}Y_l^m,\qquad
C=-\frac{4\pi G\rho l A}{2l+1}.
$$

The coefficient follows because the derivative jump is $-(2l+1)C R^{l-1}Y_l^m$. This surface source is why neglecting the bulk density perturbation does not justify neglecting the perturbed gravity.
