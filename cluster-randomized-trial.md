# Cluster-randomized trial

↑ **Parent:** [Randomized controlled trial](randomized-controlled-trial.md)

A cluster-randomized trial assigns whole groups, such as institutions, to an intervention rather than assigning individuals separately. Positive [intraclass correlation](intraclass-correlation-coefficient.md) reduces independent information: the equal-size exchangeable approximation has [design effect](design-effect.md) $1+(m-1)\rho$ for cluster size $m$. The number of independent randomized clusters also matters for inference. Cluster assignment does not itself remove requirements for [informed consent](informed-consent.md) or justify dropping outcome ascertainment in the control arm.
