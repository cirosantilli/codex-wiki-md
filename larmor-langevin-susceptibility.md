# Larmor-Langevin susceptibility

↑ **Parent:** [Diamagnetism](diamagnetism.md)

The dimensionless SI [volume magnetic susceptibility](volume-magnetic-susceptibility.md) of number [density](density.md) $n$ of independent spherical bound [electrons](electron.md) with [mass](mass.md) $m$ and electron-weighted [mean-square orbital radius](mean-square-orbital-radius.md). In a uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md), the quadratic minimal-coupling [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) gives $\Delta E=e^2B^2\langle x^2+y^2\rangle/(8m)$. Spherical symmetry gives $\langle x^2+y^2\rangle=2\langle r^2\rangle/3$; differentiating twice with respect to $B$ gives the displayed negative response. Atomic orbitals use the bare [electron mass](electron-mass.md), whereas a shallow donor envelope within an [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md) approximation uses that envelope's [effective mass](band-effective-mass.md).
