# Continuous functions on the p-adic integers

↑ **Parent:** [Non-Archimedean analysis](non-archimedean-analysis.md)

The [continuous functions](continuous-function.md) from the [p-adic integers](p-adic-integer.md) to the [P-adic numbers](p-adic-number.md) form a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) over $\mathbb Q_p$ with [supremum norm](supremum-norm.md) $\|f\|_\infty=\sup_{x\in\mathbb Z_p}|f(x)|_p$. Compactness of the domain bounds the norm, and [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) in the complete codomain proves completeness. Translation and the [forward difference operator](forward-difference-operator.md) $\Delta f(x)=f(x+1)-f(x)$ are bounded operators, with $\|\Delta f\|_\infty\leq\|f\|_\infty$.
