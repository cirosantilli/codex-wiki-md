# Measurable additive function is continuous

↑ **Parent:** [Additive function](additive-function.md)

Every Lebesgue-measurable additive function $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ is continuous. Small-value preimages are measurable; one of their integer dilates has positive measure, so the [Steinhaus theorem](steinhaus-theorem.md) puts a neighbourhood of zero in their difference set. Additivity then makes $f$ small on that neighbourhood.
