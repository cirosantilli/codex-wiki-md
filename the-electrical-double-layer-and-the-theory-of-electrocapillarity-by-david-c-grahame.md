# The Electrical Double Layer and the Theory of Electrocapillarity by David C. Grahame

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry bibliography](chemistry-bibliography-split.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Work by David C. Grahame](work-by-david-c-grahame.md)

Chemical Reviews 41(3), 441–501. Publisher: [https://doi.org/10.1021/cr60130a002](https://doi.org/10.1021/cr60130a002)

Electrochemical Society primary-paper archive: [https://knowledge.electrochem.org/estir/hist/hist-66-Grahame.pdf](https://knowledge.electrochem.org/estir/hist/hist-66-Grahame.pdf)

Section VI, printed pp. 470–474, derives the diffuse-layer theory from Poisson and Boltzmann equations. Equations (43)–(48), printed p. 474, distinguish integrated charge, integral [capacitance](capacitance.md) and differential [capacitance](capacitance.md). The archived PDF includes two preliminary pages before printed p. 441.
