# Amino acid composition

↑ **Parent:** [Protein primary structure](protein-primary-structure.md)

The [amino acid composition](amino-acid-composition.md) lists amounts or proportions of the different [amino acid residues](amino-acid-residue.md) in a [protein](protein.md) or specified [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md), without giving their order. The toy MAGAMG and MMAAGG each have counts M:2, A:2, G:2, or a proportion $1/3$ of each. Knowing composition therefore does not supply [protein primary structure](protein-primary-structure.md). Sanger's account begins by distinguishing the already available composition measurements from the largely unknown residue order. See [Sanger's authored 1985 account of the move from composition to terminal-residue analysis](https://garfield.library.upenn.edu/classics1985/A1985ADE5900002.pdf).
