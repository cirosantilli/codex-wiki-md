# Buoyant thermal

↑ **Parent:** [Turbulent plume](turbulent-plume-split.md)

A buoyant thermal is a localized rising volume of fluid whose [mass density](density.md) is lower than its surroundings. It can form the head of a [starting plume](starting-plume.md). A spherical mixed thermal of radius $R$ has volume $V=4\pi R^3/3$ and [reduced gravity](reduced-gravity-split.md) $g_t=g(\rho_0-\rho_t)/\rho_0$ in the [Boussinesq approximation](boussinesq-approximation.md).
