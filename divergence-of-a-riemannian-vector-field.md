# Divergence of a Riemannian vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence](divergence.md)

For the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md) of a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md), the divergence of $X$ is $\operatorname{div}_gX=\operatorname{tr}(Y\mapsto\nabla_YX)$. In coordinates it equals $(\det g)^{-1/2}\partial_i((\det g)^{1/2}X^i)$. On an oriented manifold it satisfies $\mathcal L_X\omega_g=(\operatorname{div}_gX)\omega_g$. The [codifferential](codifferential.md) on a [one-form](one-form.md) satisfies $\delta\theta=-\operatorname{div}_g(\theta^\sharp)$, where $\sharp$ is the [musical isomorphism](musical-isomorphism.md); this follows by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) or the [Hodge star](hodge-star-operator.md) formula.
