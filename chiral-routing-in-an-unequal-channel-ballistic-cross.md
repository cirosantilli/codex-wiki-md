# Chiral routing in an unequal-channel ballistic cross

↑ **Parent:** [Bend resistance](bend-resistance.md)

For left-deflected injection in the left 1,top 2,right 3,bottom 4 geometry, use 1 to 2 with $M$ [modes](mode-statistics.md), 2 to 3 with $M$, 3 to 4 with $N$, and 4 to 1 with $M$ plus 4 to 3 with $N-M$. The zero-current probes give $V_2=V_1$ and $V_3=MV_1/N$, hence $R_{14,23}=(h/e^2)(1/M-1/N)$. Reversing the transmission matrix gives equal probe potentials and zero [bend resistance](bend-resistance.md). Fixed-lead resistance need not be even in field; reciprocity also exchanges current and [voltage](voltage.md) pairs.
