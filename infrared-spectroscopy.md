# Infrared spectroscopy

↑ **Parent:** [Chemistry](chemistry-split.md)

[Infrared spectroscopy](infrared-spectroscopy.md) measures a sample's response to [infrared](infrared.md) [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation.md). Absorption associated with stretching or bending motions of [chemical bonds](chemical-bond.md) helps identify groups and their environments. Peak positions are commonly reported as reciprocal [wavelength](wavelength.md), in $\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$; a lower value means lower [frequency](frequency.md). [McMurry: infrared spectroscopy and its wavelength convention](https://openstax.org/books/organic-chemistry/pages/12-6-infrared-spectroscopy)
