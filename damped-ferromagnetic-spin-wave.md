# Damped ferromagnetic spin wave

↑ **Parent:** [Ferromagnetic magnon](ferromagnetic-magnon.md)

A damped ferromagnetic spin wave has a complex pole $\omega=\mathord\pm\omega_k-i\Gamma_k$. Both poles of its [retarded Green function](retarded-green-function.md) must lie in the lower half of the complex frequency plane so that the response respects [causality](causality.md).
