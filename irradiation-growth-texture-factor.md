# Irradiation growth texture factor

↑ **Parent:** [Texture averaging of irradiation growth](texture-averaging-of-irradiation-growth.md)

In an ideal axisymmetric volume-conserving [crystal grain](grain-in-a-polycrystal.md) model, directional growth is proportional to $1-3\langle\cos^2\theta\rangle$, where $\theta$ is the angle between that direction and the crystal c-axis. Random [crystallographic texture](crystallographic-texture.md) has $\langle\cos^2\theta\rangle=1/3$.
