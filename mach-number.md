# Mach number

↑ **Parent:** [Compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mach_number)

The [Mach number](mach-number.md) is the ratio of [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md) speed to local [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md).

More precisely, the [Mach number](mach-number.md) is the [dimensionless quantity](dimensionless-quantity.md)

$$
M=\frac{|\mathbf v_{\mathrm{body}}-\mathbf u_{\mathrm{gas}}|}{c}=\frac{U}{c},
$$

where $U$ is the magnitude of the body's [relative velocity](relative-velocity.md) through the surrounding [gas](gas.md), and $c>0$ is the local [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) measured in the [gas](gas.md) rest [reference frame](reference-frame.md). For a [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md) past a stationary body, the same [Mach number](mach-number.md) uses the local [fluid](fluid.md) speed in the body's rest [reference frame](reference-frame.md). A [Mach number](mach-number.md) below, equal to, or above one means [subsonic flow](subsonic-flow.md), [sonic flow](sonic-flow.md), or [supersonic flow](supersonic-flow.md), respectively. The denominator is a property of the local [gas](gas.md): its [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) depends on [temperature](temperature-split.md) and composition.[Mach Number, paragraphs defining the speed ratio and dependence on gas type and temperature; flight-regime list](mach-number-nasa-glenn-research-center.md)

The [relative velocity](relative-velocity.md) matters even when the body is at rest in a [wind tunnel](wind-tunnel.md). A uniform change of [reference frame](reference-frame.md) subtracts the same [velocity](velocity.md) from body and [gas](gas.md), so their [relative velocity](relative-velocity.md) and the [Mach number](mach-number.md) stay unchanged under a [Galilean transformation](galilean-transformation.md). Ground speed alone cannot supply $U$ when there is wind. For an original collinear example, a body moving at $680\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ over the ground through [gas](gas.md) moving in the same direction at $170\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ has $U=510\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. With a stipulated [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md) $c=340\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$, its [Mach number](mach-number.md) is $1.5$, rather than $2$. A moving observer changes apparent [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md) arrival times, as in the [radial Mach number](radial-mach-number.md), without changing the body's [Mach number](mach-number.md) relative to the [gas](gas.md).

Why is the threshold one so useful? [Acoustic waves](acoustic-wave.md) carry small [pressure](pressure-split.md) disturbances at the [sound speed](speed-of-sound.md). In [subsonic flow](subsonic-flow.md), such information can travel upstream relative to the body; in a uniform [supersonic flow](supersonic-flow.md), it is swept downstream. The [Mach cone](mach-cone.md) turns this difference in communication into visible geometry. Matching [Mach number](mach-number.md) between a [wind tunnel](wind-tunnel.md) and flight is therefore essential to matching [compressible flow](compressible-flow-split.md) effects.[Mach Number, paragraphs on downstream propagation and matching wind-tunnel and flight Mach numbers](mach-number-nasa-glenn-research-center.md)

An actual [supersonic wind tunnel](supersonic-wind-tunnel.md) experiment makes the distinction between [wavefronts](wavefront.md) and [shock waves](shock-wave.md) concrete. In NASA Glenn's 1-by-1-foot [wind tunnel](wind-tunnel.md), interchangeable converging-diverging [nozzle](nozzle.md) blocks set the [Mach number](mach-number.md). A strut-mounted exhaust-[nozzle](nozzle.md) model had a nosecone, an air plenum and transparent sidewalls for [schlieren photography](schlieren-photography.md). Before installing the model, the researchers photographed the empty test section: two [shock wave](shock-wave.md) patterns originated at a wall joint after the [nozzle](nozzle.md) block. This control guided model placement to keep reflected [shock waves](shock-wave.md) away from the plume-study region. The rig included internal [total pressure](stagnation-pressure.md) and [static pressure](static-pressure.md) sensing and a traversing external [static pressure](static-pressure.md) probe. A baseline [nozzle](nozzle.md) and previous [pressure](pressure-split.md) data supplied a planned validation comparison; the report distinguishes this plan from the observed empty-tunnel control. This establishes an apparatus origin for those visible [shock waves](shock-wave.md), not a measurement of the [Mach cone](mach-cone.md) from an infinitesimal source. The reported finite [shock wave](shock-wave.md) angle cannot simply be inserted into the [Mach angle](mach-angle.md) formula to calibrate $M$: [shock wave](shock-wave.md) strength and geometry also matter.[Wind Tunnel Model Design for the Study of Plume Effects on Sonic Boom for Isolated Exhaust Nozzles, NASA/TM-2010-216259, §II printed p. 2; §§IV–V printed pp. 8–9; Figs. 7–10 and concluding remarks printed p. 10](wind-tunnel-model-design-for-the-study-of-plume-effects-on-sonic-boom-for-isolated-exhaust-nozzles-by-raymond-s-castner.md)

In conventional [schlieren photography](schlieren-photography.md), a bright slit source and two concave mirrors send parallel [light rays](light-ray.md) through the [wind tunnel](wind-tunnel.md) and focus them at a knife edge before an [optical camera](optical-camera.md). Spatial changes in [mass density](density.md) cause [refraction](refraction.md); the knife edge converts the deflection into image contrast, making [shock waves](shock-wave.md) visible. This is the general optical principle, rather than a specification of every component in the preceding rig. A photograph projects a three-dimensional [fluid flow](fluid-flow.md) into two dimensions; contrast alone does not provide a calibrated [pressure](pressure-split.md) jump or [acoustic wave](acoustic-wave.md) amplitude.[Schlieren Flow Visualization, paragraphs describing the two-mirror slit-source and knife-edge system, density-gradient contrast, and two-dimensional recording of three-dimensional shocks](schlieren-flow-visualization-nasa-glenn-research-center.md)
