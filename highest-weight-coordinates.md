# Highest-weight coordinates

↑ **Parent:** [Highest weight of a representation](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md)

The coordinates of a [highest weight](highest-weight-of-a-representation.md) record its eigenvalues on a chosen basis of a [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md). They depend on that basis. [Dynkin labels](dynkin-label.md) instead use the simple [coroots](coroot.md); orthonormal Cartesian coordinates and Dynkin labels must be converted before applying a dimension or [Casimir eigenvalue](casimir-eigenvalue.md) formula.
