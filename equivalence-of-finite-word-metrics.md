# Equivalence of finite word metrics

↑ **Parent:** [Word metric](word-metric.md)

For two finite symmetric choices of [generating set of a group](generating-set-of-a-group.md) $S,T$, let $K=\max(\{1\}\cup\{|s|_T:s\in S\})$ and $L=\max(\{1\}\cup\{|t|_S:t\in T\})$. Replacing letters by their fixed words gives $|g|_T\leq K|g|_S$ and $|g|_S\leq L|g|_T$. Thus the identity is a [bilipschitz equivalence](bilipschitz-equivalence.md) between the [word metrics](word-metric.md). Finiteness is essential for these maxima; an infinite generating set can collapse whole subgroups to diameter one.
