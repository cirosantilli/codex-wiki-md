# Idelic congruence subgroup

↑ **Parent:** [Idèle class group](idele-class-group.md)

The ideles congruent to one modulo $\mathfrak m$ have finite components in $1+\mathfrak p^{n_\mathfrak p}$ at primes in the finite modulus and positive components at selected real places. Their image in $C_K$ is the idelic congruence subgroup.
