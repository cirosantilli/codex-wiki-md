# Rational torsion in the family x times x plus one times x plus m squared

↑ **Parent:** [Torsion point of an elliptic curve](torsion-point-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

For integral $m\geq2$, suppose $E:y^2=x(x+1)(x+m^2)$ has [good reduction](good-reduction-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) at five or seven. At five, $m^2\equiv4$; at seven, $m^2\equiv2$ or $4$. Each resulting reduced [elliptic curve](elliptic-curve.md) has eight points. These restrictions follow from $\Delta=16m^4(m^2-1)^2$ and $c_4=16(m^4-m^2+1)$: if an odd [prime](prime-number.md) divides $m(m^2-1)$, the [j-invariant of an elliptic curve](j-invariant-of-an-elliptic-curve.md) has negative [valuation](valuation.md). The [torsion-freeness of the formal group over Qp for odd p](torsion-freeness-of-the-formal-group-over-qp-for-odd-p.md) bounds the whole rational torsion by eight. The point $(m,m(m+1))$ doubles to $(0,0)$, and $(-1,0)$ is an independent [2-torsion](2-torsion.md) point. These generate the stated group of order eight.
