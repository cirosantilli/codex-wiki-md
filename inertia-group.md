# Inertia group

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification group](ramification-group.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inertia_group)

The inertia group of a finite [Galois extension](finite-galois-extension.md) of local fields is the kernel of the action of its [Galois group](galois-group.md) on the residue field. In lower numbering it is the zeroth [ramification group](ramification-group.md) $G_0$.
