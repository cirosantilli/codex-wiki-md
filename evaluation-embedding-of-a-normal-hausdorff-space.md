# Evaluation embedding of a normal Hausdorff space

↑ **Parent:** [Topological embedding](topological-embedding.md)

[Continuous functions](continuous-function.md) $X\to[0,1]$ on a normal [Hausdorff space](hausdorff-space.md) separate points and [closed sets](closed-set.md) by [Urysohn's lemma](urysohn-s-lemma.md). The evaluation map into the product of their codomains is [injective](injective-function.md) and [continuous](continuous-function.md). For each point in an [open set](open-set.md), a coordinate equal to one there and zero outside the [open set](open-set.md) detects a smaller coordinate [neighborhood](neighbourhood-mathematics.md), proving the inverse [topology](topology-split.md).
