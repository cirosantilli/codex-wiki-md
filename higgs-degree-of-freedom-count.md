# Higgs degree-of-freedom count

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs mechanism](higgs-mechanism.md)

In four spacetime dimensions a massless vector has two physical polarizations, while a [Proca field](proca-field.md) has three. Before rearrangement, the electroweak sector has four vector fields and four real [Higgs doublet](higgs-field.md) components, hence twelve propagating [degrees of freedom](degree-of-freedom.md). In the broken description, [W bosons](w-boson.md) of both charges and the [Z boson](z-boson.md) contribute nine, the [photon](photon.md) contributes two, and the radial [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) contributes one. The three angular scalar components supply the three additional longitudinal polarizations. Gauge components and constraint variables are excluded from both counts. The [gluon](gluon.md) sector is unaffected.
