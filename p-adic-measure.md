# p-adic measure

↑ **Parent:** [Measure](measure.md)

For a [profinite group](profinite-group.md) $G$ and a complete [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md) $I$ with a continuous $\mathbb Z_p$-algebra structure, an $I$-valued measure is a bounded $I$-linear functional on continuous $I$-valued functions, with the [uniform norm](supremum-norm.md). Equivalently it assigns finitely additive values in $I$ to [clopen sets](clopen-set.md). [Locally constant functions](locally-constant-function.md) are uniformly dense, so [integration](integral.md) extends uniquely from finite sums. [Countable additivity](countable-additivity.md) in the usual real-measure sense is not imposed. The coefficient ring may have infinite [residue field](residue-field.md) and need not be compact.
