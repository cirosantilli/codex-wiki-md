# Bipartition of a graph

↑ **Parent:** [Bipartite graph](bipartite-graph-split.md)

A [bipartition](bipartition-of-a-graph.md) of a [graph](graph-split.md) divides its [vertex set](vertex-set.md) into two [independent sets](independent-set-graph-theory.md), so every [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) joins different parts. A [graph](graph-split.md) admitting such a [partition of a set](partition-of-a-set.md) is a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph-split.md). The two parts are not generally unique: separate [connected components of a graph](component-graph-theory.md) can have their sides exchanged independently, and [isolated vertices](isolated-vertex.md) can be assigned to either part.
