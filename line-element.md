# Line element

↑ **Parent:** [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric-split.md)

The line element of a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric-split.md) is its local expression for squared infinitesimal length. Along a parametrized [curve](curve.md) $x(t)$, the [arc length](arc-length.md) is $\int\sqrt{g_{ij}(x(t))\dot x^i\dot x^j}\,dt$. For a [circular cylinder](circular-cylinder.md) of radius $R$ parametrized by angle $\theta$ and axial position $z$, the [induced metric](induced-metric.md) gives $ds^2=R^2d\theta^2+dz^2$. Unrolling with $u=R\theta$ yields the Euclidean form $du^2+dz^2$, explaining the straight unrolled descriptions of [geodesics on a circular cylinder](geodesics-on-a-circular-cylinder.md).
