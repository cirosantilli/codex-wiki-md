# Binding energy per nucleon

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear binding energy](nuclear-binding-energy.md)

[Binding energy per nucleon](binding-energy-per-nucleon.md) divides conventional positive [nuclear binding energy](nuclear-binding-energy.md) by [mass number](mass-number.md). Typical well-bound nuclei have values of order $8\,\mathrm{MeV}$, and the broad maximum lies near iron-group nuclei. A mass correction added to free-nucleon masses instead has the opposite sign, $E_{\mathrm{corr}}/A=-B/A$.
