# Fixed-active-size obstruction for combinatorial lines

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorial line](combinatorial-line.md)

For any proposed positive active size $d$, the displayed two-colour [finite colouring](finite-coloring.md) of binary words gives opposite colours to the two points of every [combinatorial line](combinatorial-line.md) with exactly $d$ active coordinates. Their counts of the letter $2$ differ by $d$. Thus no dimension can guarantee a [monochromatic](monochromatic-set.md) line of one universally prescribed active size, even though the [Hales-Jewett theorem](hales-jewett-theorem.md) guarantees some active size. The colouring is allowed to depend on $d$ because $d$ is chosen before the colouring.
