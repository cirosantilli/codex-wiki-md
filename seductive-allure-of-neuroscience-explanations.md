# Seductive allure of neuroscience explanations

↑ **Parent:** [Cognitive bias](cognitive-bias.md)

An increase in the apparent quality of an explanation associated with [neuroscience](neuroscience-split.md) information that does not improve its logic. The original text-based experiment distinguishes this effect from a general claim that every brain image or biological explanation is misleading.[2008; explanation quality, irrelevant information and expertise](the-seductive-allure-of-neuroscience-explanations-by-deena-skolnick-weisberg-and-others.md)
