# One-sided kinetic solidification dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Morphological instability of a kinetically limited solidification front](morphological-instability-of-a-kinetically-limited-solidification-front.md)

Use undercooling $\Delta T=T_m-T_\infty$, [solidification attachment mobility](solidification-attachment-mobility.md) $\mathcal M$, velocity scale $U_0=\mathcal M\Delta T$, length $\ell=\kappa/U_0$ and time $\kappa/U_0^2$. Let $S=L/(c_p\Delta T)$ be the [latent-to-sensible heat ratio](latent-to-sensible-heat-ratio.md), $\theta=(T-T_\infty)/\Delta T$, and $\Gamma=\gamma_T/(\Delta T\ell)$. In the one-sided liquid-diffusion model, a travelling planar front has $\theta=S e^{-V\xi}$ and $V=1-S$. For a corrugation $\zeta e^{\sigma t+i\alpha y}$, the decaying thermal perturbation is $A_1e^{-p\xi}$, with $p^2-Vp=\sigma+\alpha^2$ and the branch $p=[V+\sqrt{V^2+4(\sigma+\alpha^2)}]/2$. The displaced kinetic condition gives $A_1=(SV-\sigma-\Gamma\alpha^2)\zeta$. The displaced [Stefan condition](stefan-condition.md) gives $S\sigma\zeta=pA_1-SV^2\zeta$. Eliminating $A_1$ proves the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md). Neglect of solid-side perturbation heat flux is part of this model.
