# Double circular cone

↑ **Parent:** [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry.md)

The union of the two infinite lateral conical sheets meeting at a common vertex is a double circular cone. The displayed example has axis $z$ and semi-angle $\pi/4$. It is smooth away from the vertex, where its defining [gradient](gradient.md) is nonzero. A [right circular cone](right-circular-cone.md) truncated on one side is a related bounded solid; this surface includes both axial directions and has no base disks.
