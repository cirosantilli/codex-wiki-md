# Two-term monotone-endpoint Laplace expansion

↑ **Parent:** [Laplace's method](laplace-s-method.md)

For a smooth real phase decreasing away from its endpoint maximum, put $t=\phi(a)-\phi(x)$ and write the transformed amplitude as $G(t)=-g(x(t))/\phi'(x(t))$. Its first two Taylor coefficients are $G(0)=-g(a)/\phi'(a)$ and $G'(0)=g'(a)/\phi'(a)^2-g(a)\phi''(a)/\phi'(a)^3$. Applying [Watson lemma](watson-s-lemma.md) gives the displayed two-term [Laplace integral](laplace-integral.md) expansion. Localization and a Taylor remainder estimate justify termwise integration.
