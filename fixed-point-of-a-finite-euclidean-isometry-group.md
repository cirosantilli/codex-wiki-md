# Fixed point of a finite Euclidean isometry group

↑ **Parent:** [Isometry group](isometry-group.md)

A finite [group](group-split.md) of [Euclidean isometries](euclidean-isometry.md) has a common [fixed point](fixed-point.md): average any orbit. Each [isometry](isometry.md) is an [affine map](affine-map.md) and therefore preserves this finite affine average; applying a [group](group-split.md) element merely permutes the orbit terms. To see that an isometry is affine, subtract its value at zero and use the [polarization identity](polarization-identity.md) to show that the resulting map preserves inner products. Images of a standard [orthonormal basis](orthonormal-basis.md) then determine a linear orthogonal map.
