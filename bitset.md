# Bitset

↑ **Parent:** [Data structure](data-structure-split.md)

A representation of a [finite set](finite-set.md) of bounded [nonnegative integers](natural-number.md) by a binary word: bit $i$ is 1 exactly when $i$ belongs to the set. Bitwise AND, OR, and XOR implement intersection, union, and symmetric difference. A large [integer](integer-split.md) can store the whole word when the programming language provides arbitrary-precision [integers](integer-split.md).
