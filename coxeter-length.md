# Coxeter length

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl group](weyl-group.md)

The Coxeter length $\ell(w)$ is the smallest number of simple reflections whose product is $w$. Its parity gives the sign of a Weyl-group element.

This is a particular [length function](length-function.md), namely word length for the simple reflections.
