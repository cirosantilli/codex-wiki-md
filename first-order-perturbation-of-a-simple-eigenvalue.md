# First-order perturbation of a simple eigenvalue

↑ **Parent:** [Eigenvalue sensitivity](eigenvalue-sensitivity.md)

If $A(t)=A+tE$ and $\lambda(t)$ is a [differentiable function](differentiable-function.md) defining a simple eigenvalue, then

$$
\lambda'(0)=\frac{u^TEv}{u^Tv}.
$$

The [Cauchy-Schwarz inequality](cauchy-schwarz-inequality.md) and the [operator norm](operator-norm.md) give

$$
|\lambda'(0)|\leq\|E\|_2s(\lambda).
$$
