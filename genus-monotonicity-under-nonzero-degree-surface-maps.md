# Genus monotonicity under nonzero-degree surface maps

↑ **Parent:** [Genus of a surface](genus-of-a-surface.md)

For a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) of nonzero [mapping degree](degree-of-a-continuous-mapping.md) between closed connected oriented surfaces, [rational cohomology injectivity of a nonzero-degree map](rational-cohomology-injectivity-of-a-nonzero-degree-map.md) gives an injection on $H^1$. Since the first [Betti number](betti-number.md) of an oriented surface of genus $g$ is $2g$, the source genus is at least the target genus.
