# Adsorption chemical potential

↑ **Parent:** [Langmuir adsorption model](langmuir-adsorption-model.md)

The [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) of an ideal single-occupancy [lattice gas](lattice-gas.md) with occupied-site internal [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) $z$ and [surface coverage](surface-coverage.md) $\theta$. The ratio $\theta/(1-\theta)$ accounts for the entropy of distributing particles among occupied and vacant [adsorption sites](adsorption-site.md).
