# Composition shift of a convective pre-main-sequence track

↑ **Parent:** [Convective contraction with power-law opacity](convective-contraction-with-power-law-opacity.md)

For [convective contraction with power-law opacity](convective-contraction-with-power-law-opacity.md) $\kappa\propto\rho T^4$ at fixed stellar mass and opacity normalization, $T_e\propto\mu^{1/2}R^{1/8}$ and $L\propto\mu^2R^{5/2}$. Eliminating $R$ gives $L\propto\mu^{-8}T_e^{20}$. Thus a larger [mean molecular weight](mean-molecular-weight.md) moves the track to larger [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md) at a given [luminosity](luminosity.md), with $T_e|_L\propto\mu^{2/5}$. Contraction decreases both temperature and luminosity for this particular opacity exponent.
