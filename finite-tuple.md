# Finite tuple

↑ **Parent:** [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md)

A finite [tuple](finite-tuple.md) is an ordered list of finitely many entries; coordinates can repeat, and their positions matter. A [tuple](finite-tuple.md) of length $n$ from a set $M$ is an element of the [Cartesian product](cartesian-product.md) $M^n$. The empty [tuple](finite-tuple.md) has length zero. Finite [tuples](finite-tuple.md) support [Scott formulas](scott-formula.md) in a [back-and-forth method](back-and-forth-method.md) construction, where matching [tuples](finite-tuple.md) determine a [partial isomorphism of structures](partial-embedding.md).
