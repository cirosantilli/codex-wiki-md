# Resolvent of the shifted harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Graph estimate for the harmonic oscillator](graph-estimate-for-the-harmonic-oscillator.md)

For $Au=u''-(1+x^2)u$ on $D(A)=\{u\in H^2(\mathbb R):x^2u\in L^2\}$, the form $a_\lambda(u,v)=\int u'\overline{v'}+(1+\lambda+x^2)u\bar v$ is coercive on the [one-dimensional harmonic oscillator form domain](one-dimensional-harmonic-oscillator-form-domain.md) when $\lambda>0$. The [Lax-Milgram theorem](lax-milgram-theorem.md) gives a unique weak solution of $(\lambda-A)u=g$. The [graph estimate for the harmonic oscillator](graph-estimate-for-the-harmonic-oscillator.md) puts it in $D(A)$, and $a_\lambda(u,u)\geq(1+\lambda)\|u\|_2^2$ gives the displayed [resolvent](resolvent-of-an-operator.md) bound. The domain is dense and the bounded resolvent proves closedness, so the [Hille-Yosida theorem](hille-yosida-theorem.md) gives a [contraction semigroup](contraction-semigroup.md). An [exponentially shifted semigroup](exponentially-shifted-semigroup.md) with generator $A+I$ solves the heat equation with oscillator potential.
