# Nonstandard prime in an elementary extension of the integers

↑ **Parent:** [Non-standard model of arithmetic](non-standard-model-of-arithmetic.md)

Name every [integer](integer-split.md) and add a new constant asserted to be prime and unequal to every named [integer](integer-split.md). Every finite part is satisfied by an ordinary prime outside a finite list, using [Euclid's theorem](euclid-s-theorem.md). [Compactness theorem](compactness-theorem.md) with the [elementary diagram](elementary-diagram-of-a-structure.md) gives an [elementary extension](elementary-extension.md) with the desired prime. A [countable](countable-set.md) [elementary substructure](elementary-substructure.md) retaining that prime cannot be [isomorphic](isomorphism.md) to the [integer](integer-split.md) [ring](ring.md), since any unital [ring isomorphism](ring-isomorphism.md) fixes every standard [integer](integer-split.md).
