# Electron-hole symmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Semiconductor](semiconductor-split.md)

Symmetry of the excitation spectrum under exchange of [conduction band](conduction-band.md) [electrons](electron.md) and [valence band](valence-band.md) [holes](electron-hole.md), after choosing a central zero of [energy](energy-split.md). In a two-band model with equal [effective masses](band-effective-mass.md) and equal [degeneracy factors](degeneracy-factor.md), [charge neutrality](charge-neutrality.md) in an [intrinsic semiconductor](intrinsic-semiconductor.md) fixes the [chemical potential](chemical-potential-split.md) at that central [energy](energy-split.md). Indeed the [Fermi-Dirac distribution](fermi-dirac-distribution.md) obeys $1-f(-E;\mu=0)=f(E;\mu=0)$, so excitation populations are equal at every positive [energy](energy-split.md).
