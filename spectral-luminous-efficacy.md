# Spectral luminous efficacy

↑ **Parent:** [Photometry (optics)](photometry-optics.md)

[Spectral luminous efficacy](spectral-luminous-efficacy.md) converts [radiant flux](radiant-flux.md) at one [wavelength](wavelength.md) to [luminous flux](luminous-flux.md). The [candela](candela.md) convention fixes $K_{\mathrm{cd}}$ exactly at $540\times10^{12}\,\mathrm{Hz}$, not at an exactly specified [wavelength](wavelength.md) of $555\,\mathrm{nm}$. [SI base unit: candela (cd)](si-base-unit-candela-cd.md)

In [vacuum](vacuum.md), that [frequency](frequency.md) corresponds to $c/(540\times10^{12}\,\mathrm{Hz})\simeq555.1712\,\mathrm{nm}$, using the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). In standard air it is approximately $555.017\,\mathrm{nm}$, close to the $555\,\mathrm{nm}$ maximum of the [photopic luminous efficiency function](photopic-luminous-efficiency-function.md). Thus $K_m$ is slightly larger than $683\,\mathrm{lm\,W^{-1}}$; using $K_m\simeq683\,\mathrm{lm\,W^{-1}}$ in ordinary calculations is a rounded convention. The exact anchor is $K(\lambda_a)=683\,\mathrm{lm\,W^{-1}}$.
