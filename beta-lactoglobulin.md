# Beta-lactoglobulin

↑ **Parent:** [Protein](protein-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beta-lactoglobulin)

A whey [protein](protein-split.md) used as a model solute in studies of [protein](protein-split.md) association and transport. Its effective [electric charge](electric-charge-split.md), association state and [hydrodynamic radius](hydrodynamic-radius.md) depend on solution conditions. In a specified spherical-particle model, its [electrophoretic mobility](electrophoretic-mobility.md) and [sedimentation](sedimentation.md) speed depend on its charge and buoyant mass through the same [Stokes drag law](stokes-s-law.md).
