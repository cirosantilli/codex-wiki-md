# Cross-disperser

↑ **Parent:** [Cross-dispersed echelle spectrograph](cross-dispersed-echelle-spectrograph.md)

A cross-disperser is a second dispersing element, often an [optical prism](prism-optics.md) or [diffraction grating](diffraction-grating.md), oriented to separate [optical spectra](optical-spectrum.md) perpendicular to their principal dispersion direction.
