# Second Bianchi identity

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann curvature tensor](riemann-curvature-tensor-split.md)

For the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md), the cyclic covariant derivative of curvature vanishes: $\nabla_{[e}R^a{}_{|b|cd]}=0$. Contracting this identity gives the [contracted Bianchi identity](contracted-bianchi-identity.md).
