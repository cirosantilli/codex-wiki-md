# Photon emission

↑ **Parent:** [Photon](photon.md)

[Photon](photon.md) emission creates a [photon](photon.md), for example in [bremsstrahlung](bremsstrahlung.md) or the decay of an excited material state. It produces a positive photon-number collision source when not balanced by inverse processes. The [photon number current](photon-number-current.md) is conserved only if its integrated creation and destruction rates cancel.
