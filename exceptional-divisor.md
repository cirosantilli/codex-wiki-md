# Exceptional divisor

↑ **Parent:** [Blowup of a complex manifold at a point](blowup-of-a-complex-manifold-at-a-point.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exceptional_divisor)

The exceptional divisor of the [blowup of a complex manifold at a point](blowup-of-a-complex-manifold-at-a-point.md) $p$ is the fiber over $p$. In complex dimension $n$ it is naturally the projective space $\mathbb P(T_pX)\cong\mathbb{CP}^{n-1}$ of tangent directions at the center.
