# Gamma-mixed Poisson aggregate with exponential claims

↑ **Parent:** [Random sum of independent claims](random-sum-of-independent-claims.md)

If $\Lambda$ has [gamma distribution](gamma-distribution.md) with shape $2$ and rate $p/q$, $N\mid\Lambda$ has [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) with intensity $\Lambda$, and the independent claim sizes have [exponential distribution](exponential-distribution.md) of mean $\mu$, where $p+q=1$, the aggregate law is $p^2\delta_0+2pq\,\operatorname{Exp}(p/\mu)+q^2\operatorname{Gamma}(2,\text{rate }p/\mu)$. This follows by expanding its [moment-generating function](moment-generating-function.md) as $[p+q\,p/(p-\mu t)]^2$.
