# Melting-front instability due to permeability contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Reactive infiltration instability](reactive-infiltration-instability.md)

A planar [advection-driven melting front in a porous matrix](advection-driven-melting-front-in-a-porous-matrix.md) with permeabilities $k$ ahead and $k+\Delta k$ behind has the ideal sharp-front [growth rate](growth-rate.md)

$$
\sigma=|\alpha|V\frac{\Delta k}{2k+\Delta k}.
$$

This follows by matching [harmonic](harmonic-function.md) pressure perturbations and normal [Darcy flux](darcy-velocity.md), then converting flux to front speed by the [enthalpy](enthalpy.md) balance. It predicts no finite fastest-growing wavelength when $\Delta k>0$.
