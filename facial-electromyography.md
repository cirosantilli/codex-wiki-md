# Facial electromyography

↑ **Parent:** [Electromyography](electromyography.md)

Recording electrical activity over facial [muscle](muscle.md) regions, such as the cheek and brow. It can detect small facial responses that are not obvious to an observer. A response compatible with a smile or frown does not by itself establish the corresponding subjective [emotion](emotion.md).
