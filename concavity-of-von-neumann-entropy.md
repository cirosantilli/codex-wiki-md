# Concavity of Von Neumann entropy

↑ **Parent:** [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md)

For [density operators](density-matrix.md) $\rho_1,\rho_2$ and $0\leq p\leq1$, [Von Neumann entropy](von-neumann-entropy-split.md) is [concave](concave-function.md):

$$
S(p\rho_1+(1-p)\rho_2)\geq pS(\rho_1)+(1-p)S(\rho_2).
$$
