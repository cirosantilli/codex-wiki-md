# Additivity of Seifert genus

↑ **Parent:** [Seifert genus](seifert-genus.md)

The [Seifert genus](seifert-genus.md) is additive under [connected sum of knots](connected-sum-of-knots.md):

$$
g_s(K\mathbin{\#}J)=g_s(K)+g_s(J).
$$

The upper bound comes from boundary-connected-summing minimal [Seifert surfaces](seifert-surface.md), while the reverse inequality follows by cutting any Seifert surface for the connected sum along a splitting sphere.
