# Automatic real bilinearity from a multiplicative norm

↑ **Parent:** [Normed algebra](normed-algebra.md)

Suppose a [ring](ring.md) has an underlying real [normed vector space](normed-vector-space.md) and its norm is multiplicative, without initially assuming real bilinearity of multiplication. For fixed $x$, distributivity makes $y\mapsto xy$ additive, while $\|xy-xz\|=\|x\|\|y-z\|$ makes it continuous. Additivity gives rational scalar compatibility, and continuity extends it to every real scalar. Apply the same argument in the other variable to obtain a [bilinear map](bilinear-map.md). The norm identity also excludes nonzero [zero divisors](zero-divisor.md).
