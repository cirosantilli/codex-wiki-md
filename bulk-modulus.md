# Bulk modulus

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)

The [bulk modulus](bulk-modulus.md) relates hydrostatic compressive pressure to fractional volume compression. In [isotropic](isotropy.md) three-dimensional [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md), the spherical part of the [stress tensor](cauchy-stress-tensor.md) is $\kappa\operatorname{tr}(\varepsilon)I$, so uniform dilation has [strain energy density](strain-energy-density.md) $\kappa(\operatorname{tr}\varepsilon)^2/2$. Positive [bulk modulus](bulk-modulus.md) and positive [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) make the isotropic [elastic stiffness tensor](elastic-stiffness-tensor.md) positive on nonzero symmetric [strains](strain.md).
