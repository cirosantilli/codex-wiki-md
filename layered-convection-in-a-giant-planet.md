# Layered convection in a giant planet

↑ **Parent:** [Delayed cooling of an inflated giant planet](delayed-cooling-of-an-inflated-giant-planet.md)

A stabilizing composition gradient can suppress large overturning motions while permitting [double-diffusive convection](double-diffusive-convection.md) in small layers separated by interfaces. If heat loss is less efficient than in a single well-mixed adiabat, the planet retains more entropy and contracts more slowly. This is a proposed delayed-cooling contribution to [hot-Jupiter radius inflation](hot-jupiter-radius-inflation.md), with an effectiveness that depends on the gradient and layer structure.
