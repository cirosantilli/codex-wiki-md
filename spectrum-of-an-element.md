# Spectrum of an element

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

The spectrum of $a$ in a unital complex algebra $A$ is

$$
\sigma_A(a)=\{\lambda\in\mathbb C:a-\lambda1\text{ is not invertible}\}.
$$

For a nonunital algebra it is defined in the [unitization of an algebra](unitization-of-an-algebra.md).
