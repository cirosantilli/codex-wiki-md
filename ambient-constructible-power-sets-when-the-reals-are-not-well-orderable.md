# Ambient constructible power sets when the reals are not well-orderable

↑ **Parent:** [Constructible power set](constructible-power-set.md)

If the ambient [power set](power-set.md) of $\omega$ is not well-orderable and $a\in L$, then the full ambient [power set](power-set.md) of $a$ belongs to $L$ exactly when $a$ is finite. [Finite set](finite-set.md) operations give the forward construction for finite $a$. If $a$ is infinite, its constructible well-order gives an [injection](injective-function.md) $\omega\to a$. The induced [injection](injective-function.md) of [power sets](power-set.md) would make the ambient [power set](power-set.md) of $\omega$ well-orderable if $\mathcal P(a)$ belonged to $L$.
