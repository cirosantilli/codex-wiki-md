# Cutting-plane method

↑ **Parent:** [Integer programming](integer-programming.md)

A cutting-plane method repeatedly adds valid inequalities that exclude a current relaxed solution while preserving all feasible integer solutions. [Gomory fractional cuts](gomory-fractional-cut.md) provide such inequalities from a fractional [simplex dictionary](simplex-dictionary.md).
