# Constant-speed entraining gravity current on a slope

↑ **Parent:** [Gravity current](gravity-current.md)

For steady flow with [entrainment coefficient](entrainment-coefficient.md) $E$, bottom-drag coefficient $c$ and slope angle $\theta$, a constant [buoyancy flux](buoyancy-flux.md) $B$ gives $h=h_0+Ex$, $g^{\prime}=B/(uh)$ and $u^3=B(\sin\theta-E\cos\theta/2)/(c+E)$. The half factor comes from hydrostatic pressure, while the denominator includes both bed drag and acceleration of entrained fluid.
