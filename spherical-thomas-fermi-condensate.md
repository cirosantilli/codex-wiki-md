<h1 id="spherical-thomas-fermi-condensate">Spherical Thomas–Fermi condensate</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Thomas–Fermi approximation for a condensate](thomas-fermi-approximation-for-a-condensate.md)

For repulsive [contact interactions](contact-interaction-quantum-mechanics.md) in a three-dimensional isotropic harmonic trap, neglecting the kinetic term in the [Gross–Pitaevskii equation](gross-pitaevskii-equation.md) gives $n(r)=(\mu-m\omega^2r^2/2)/U_0$ inside $R=\sqrt{2\mu/(m\omega^2)}$ and zero outside. Normalization yields $N=8\pi\mu R^3/(15U_0)$, hence $\mu=(\hbar\omega/2)(15Na/a_0)^{2/5}$ and $R=a_0(15Na/a_0)^{1/5}$, with [oscillator length](oscillator-length.md) $a_0=\sqrt{\hbar/(m\omega)}$. The [density](density.md) is parabolic but the [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) is its [square root](square-root.md). Neglected [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) rounds the edge and supplies a tail.
