# Spin glass

↑ **Parent:** [Spin model](spin-model-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_glass)

A [spin glass](spin-glass.md) has frozen, irregular local [spin magnetization](spin-magnetization.md) without the periodic magnetic order of a simple [ferromagnet](ferromagnet.md) or [antiferromagnet](antiferromagnetism.md). Competing interactions and [quenched disorder](quenched-disorder.md) make the distinction important: zero average [magnetization](magnetization.md) need not mean that every [spin](spin.md) is freely fluctuating.[pp. 1792–1794, introduction and interpretation of the frozen-moment order parameter](solvable-model-of-a-spin-glass-by-david-sherrington-and-scott-kirkpatrick.md)

The [frustrated Ising triangle](frustrated-ising-triangle.md) gives a small example of incompatible local preferences. The [Edwards-Anderson order parameter](edwards-anderson-order-parameter.md) and [spin overlap](spin-overlap.md) then ask a different question: how can one describe persistent local order when averaging the [spins](spin.md) themselves hides it? These concepts also connect interacting magnets to the [energy](energy-split.md) minima used by a [Hopfield network](hopfield-network.md) to retrieve a memory.
