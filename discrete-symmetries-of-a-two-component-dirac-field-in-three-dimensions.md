# Discrete symmetries of a two-component Dirac field in three dimensions

↑ **Parent:** [Dirac field](dirac-field.md)

For [gamma matrices](gamma-matrices.md) $\gamma^0=\sigma_3$, $\gamma^1=i\sigma_1$, $\gamma^2=i\sigma_2$ and [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1)$, a one-axis spatial reflection acts with $\gamma^1$ on the two-component [Dirac field](dirac-field.md). It preserves the [kinetic term](kinetic-term.md) and reverses the [Dirac mass term](dirac-mass-term.md). [Time reversal](t-symmetry.md) acts with $\gamma^2$ and includes conjugation of the operator coefficients; it has the same mass reversal. Their composition preserves a real mass. [Charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) can use $\psi_C=\gamma^1\psi^*$, because $\gamma^\mu\gamma^1=-\gamma^1\gamma^{\mu*}$. The mass bilinear is charge-conjugation even when [Grassmann field](grassmann-field.md) exchange signs are included, and the kinetic action is invariant up to a boundary term. These statements concern the classical free [action](action.md).
