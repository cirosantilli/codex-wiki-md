<h1 id="dilute-kohler-curve">Dilute Köhler curve</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Köhler theory](kohler-theory.md)

The first-order combination of the [Kelvin equation](kelvin-equation.md) and [Raoult's law](raoult-s-law.md) for a [chemical solution](solution-chemistry.md) droplet with $A/r\ll1$ and $B/r^3\ll1$. Its maximum is at $r_c=\sqrt{3B/A}$, with $S_c-1=2A/(3r_c)$. For ambient [saturation ratio](saturation-ratio.md) $1<S<S_c$, the smaller equilibrium radius is stable and the larger is unstable: the sign of condensational growth is the sign of $S-S_{\rm eq}(r)$. In particular, exceeding $r_c$ alone need not activate continued growth at subcritical [atmospheric supersaturation](atmospheric-supersaturation.md).
