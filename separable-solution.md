# Separable solution

↑ **Parent:** [Separation of variables](separation-of-variables.md)

A solution to a [partial differential equation](partial-differential-equation-split.md) that factors into [functions](function-split.md) of separate variables, for example $u(x,t)=X(x)T(t)$. For the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) $u_t=Du_{xx}$, substitution yields $T'/(DT)=X''/X$, so both sides are a common constant wherever division is valid. The resulting [ordinary differential equations](ordinary-differential-equation.md) must be solved together with the original [boundary conditions](boundary-condition.md). Individual separated solutions generate more general solutions by [superposition](superposition-principle.md) when the equation and boundary data are [linear](linearity.md).
