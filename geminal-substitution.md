# Geminal substitution

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleophilic substitution](nucleophilic-substitution.md)

In [geminal substitution](geminal-substitution.md), two incoming groups replace substituents on the same central atom. In cyclotriphosphazenes, $\mathrm{PCl_2\to PR_2}$ gives a geminal pair. Primary amino groups can enable a base-catalyzed proton-abstraction/chloride-elimination route that is unavailable after substitution by a secondary amine.
