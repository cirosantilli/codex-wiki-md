# Forward chaining

↑ **Parent:** [Logic programming](logic-programming.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_chaining)

Rule evaluation that repeatedly adds consequences of premises already known to hold. Positive [Datalog](datalog.md) evaluation computes its [least Herbrand model](least-herbrand-model.md) in this way.
