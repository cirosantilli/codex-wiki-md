# Abelian gauge theory

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge field](gauge-field.md)

An Abelian gauge theory has a commutative [gauge group](gauge-group.md). Its [gauge field strength](gauge-field-strength.md) has no commutator term, so the pure gauge Lagrangian has no cubic or quartic gauge-boson self-interactions.
