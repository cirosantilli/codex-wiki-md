# Adjacent-corner resistance of a resistor cube

↑ **Parent:** [Effective resistance](effective-resistance.md)

If every edge of a cube has [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) R, the [effective resistance](effective-resistance.md) between adjacent vertices is $7R/12$. Apply a unit [voltage](voltage.md) between the terminals. Reflection symmetries leave two interior potentials x,z and their complements. [Kirchhoff's current law](kirchhoff-node-law.md) gives $4x-z=2$, $4z-2x=1$, so $x=9/14$, $z=4/7$. The terminal current is $[1+2(1-x)]/R=12/(7R)$, proving the formula. No symmetry identifies vertices at different potentials across the source edge.
