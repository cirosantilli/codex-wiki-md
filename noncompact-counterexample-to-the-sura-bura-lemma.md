<h1 id="noncompact-counterexample-to-the-sura-bura-lemma">Noncompact counterexample to the Šura-Bura lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Šura-Bura lemma](sura-bura-lemma.md)

Set $p=(0,0)$, $q=(1,0)$, $\varepsilon_m=1/(m+2)$, and let $A_m$ be the polygonal arc joining $(\varepsilon_m,0)$, $(\varepsilon_m,m)$, $(1-\varepsilon_m,m)$, and $(1-\varepsilon_m,0)$. In the planar subspace $Z=\{p,q\}\cup\bigcup_m A_m$, each arc is [clopen](clopen-set.md). The component of $p$ is therefore just $\{p\}$. Any [clopen](clopen-set.md) neighborhood of $p$ contains the left endpoints of all sufficiently high arcs, hence those entire [connected](connected-space.md) arcs, and by closedness also the limit $q$ of their right endpoints. Thus its [quasi-component](quasi-component.md) is $\{p,q\}$. Higher arcs escape to infinity while their endpoints approach the two distinguished points.
