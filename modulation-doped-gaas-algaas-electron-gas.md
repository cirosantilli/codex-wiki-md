# Modulation-doped GaAs-AlGaAs electron gas

↑ **Parent:** [Modulation doping](modulation-doping.md)

A [two-dimensional electron gas](two-dimensional-electron-gas.md) supplied by remote donors in [aluminium gallium arsenide](aluminium-gallium-arsenide.md) and confined on the [gallium arsenide](gallium-arsenide.md) side of a [heterojunction](heterojunction.md). An undoped spacer separates ionized donors from mobile carriers, reducing impurity scattering. A conduction-band [band offset](band-offset.md) and self-consistent [band bending](band-bending.md) create the confinement well.
