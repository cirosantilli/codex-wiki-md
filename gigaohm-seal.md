# Gigaohm seal

↑ **Parent:** [Patch clamp](patch-clamp.md)

A [gigaohm seal](gigaohm-seal.md) is the high [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) contact between the glass rim and [cell membrane](cell-membrane-split.md), also called a [gigaseal](gigaohm-seal.md). It suppresses a bypass route around the membrane patch; it does not make the membrane patch itself impermeable. The 1981 methods used pulled, coated and heat-polished pipettes, a chlorided silver [electrode](electrode.md) and a pressure connection. Clean glass and suction supported tight seals; coating reduced pipette-to-bath [capacitance](capacitance.md) and recording [noise](noise-signal-processing.md), rather than being required for sealing.[pp. 85–86, Part I.1 and Figure 1](improved-patch-clamp-techniques-for-high-resolution-current-recording-from-cells-and-cell-free-membrane-patches-by-o-p-hamill-and-others.md)
