# Infinite propagation speed

↑ **Parent:** [Diffusion equation](diffusion-equation-split.md)

Infinite propagation speed means that a disturbance initially confined to a bounded region can affect arbitrarily distant points after any positive time. The strictly positive [heat kernel](heat-kernel.md) gives this behavior for a nontrivial nonnegative diffusion solution. It contrasts with the [finite propagation speed](finite-propagation-speed.md) of the [wave equation](wave-equation-split.md); a very small distant response is still mathematically nonzero.
