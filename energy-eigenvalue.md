# Energy eigenvalue

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum mechanics](quantum-mechanics-split.md)

An energy eigenvalue $E$ is an [eigenvalue](eigenvalue.md) of the Hamiltonian: $H|\psi\rangle=E|\psi\rangle$ for some nonzero state $|\psi\rangle$.
