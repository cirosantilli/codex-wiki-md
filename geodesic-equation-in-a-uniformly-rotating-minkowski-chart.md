# Geodesic equation in a uniformly rotating Minkowski chart

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating reference frame](rotating-reference-frame.md)

Set the inertial cylindrical angle to $\Phi=\phi+\omega t$. The [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md) becomes $ds^2=(c^2-\omega^2r^2)dt^2-2\omega r^2dt\,d\phi-dr^2-r^2d\phi^2-dz^2$. For a massive [geodesic](geodesic.md) parametrized by [proper time](proper-time.md), its [cyclic coordinates](cyclic-coordinate.md) give conserved covariant [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) components $p_t=m[(c^2-\omega^2r^2)\dot t-\omega r^2\dot\phi]$, $p_\phi=-mr^2(\dot\phi+\omega\dot t)$ and $p_z=-m\dot z$. Their combination gives $\dot t=(p_t-\omega p_\phi)/(mc^2)$. The radial [Euler-Lagrange equation](euler-lagrange-equation.md) gives the displayed coordinate [acceleration](acceleration.md), although the free worldline is straight in inertial coordinates. The conserved inertial [energy](energy-split.md) is $p_t-\omega p_\phi$, and the inertial axial [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) is $-p_\phi$.
