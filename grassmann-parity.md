# Grassmann parity

↑ **Parent:** [Grassmann algebra](grassmann-algebra.md)

The parity grading records whether a homogeneous element has even or odd Grassmann degree. Homogeneous elements obey $XY=(-1)^{|X||Y|}YX$. An odd differential uses the [graded Leibniz rule](graded-leibniz-rule.md); this is the sign bookkeeping behind [left-acting BRST differential](left-acting-brst-differential.md) calculations.
