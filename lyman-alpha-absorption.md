# Lyman-alpha absorption

↑ **Parent:** [Lyman-alpha line](lyman-alpha-line.md)

A neutral [hydrogen atom](hydrogen-atom.md) absorbs a photon near the Lyman-alpha resonance by excitation from $1s$ to $2p$. Absorption lines in a quasar spectrum can therefore locate [hydrogen](hydrogen.md) along the sightline by their redshift, subject to peculiar-velocity and line-profile effects.
