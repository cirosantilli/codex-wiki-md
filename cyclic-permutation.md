# Cyclic permutation

↑ **Parent:** [Permutation](permutation.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_permutation)

A cyclic permutation sends $a_1$ to $a_2$, then through $a_k$ back to $a_1$, fixing elements outside the cycle. A [transposition](transposition-permutation.md) is a two-cycle. Every finite [permutation](permutation.md) decomposes into disjoint cycles.
