# Position representation in quantum mechanics

↑ **Parent:** [Wave function](wave-function-split.md)

The [wavefunction](wave-function-split.md) is the [probability amplitude](probability-amplitude.md) of a [quantum state](quantum-state.md) in the generalized position [basis](basis.md). For a scalar particle on a line, the [position operator](position-operator.md) multiplies by $x$ and the [momentum operator](momentum-operator.md) acts as $-i\hbar\,d/dx$ on their appropriate domains. This makes the [canonical commutation relation](canonical-commutation-relation.md) $[x,p]=i\hbar$ explicit.
