# Antiperiodic boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Twisted boundary condition](twisted-boundary-condition.md)

An [antiperiodic boundary condition](antiperiodic-boundary-condition.md) is a phase twist of $\pi$ across the boundary. A Fourier mode then has $k=2\pi(n+1/2)/L$. It occurs in the even-fermion sector of a periodic [Jordan–Wigner transformation](jordan-wigner-transformation.md) and in imaginary-time fermionic thermal fields, for different underlying reasons.
