# Lattice sum

↑ **Parent:** [Series (mathematics)](series-mathematics.md)

A lattice sum is a [series](series-mathematics.md) indexed by the points of a [lattice](lattice.md). For example, comparison with annular lattice-point counts shows that $\sum_{(m,n)\ne(0,0)}(m^2+n^2)^{-s}$ converges when $s>1$.
