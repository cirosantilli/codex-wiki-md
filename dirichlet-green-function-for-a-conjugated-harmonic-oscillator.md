# Dirichlet Green function for a conjugated harmonic oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md)

For $D=d/dx$ and $L_a=(D-a)^2+b^2$ on $(0,L)$, with $b>0$ and $\sin(bL)\ne0$, the substitution $y=e^{ax}v$ gives $L_ay=e^{ax}(v''+b^2v)$. Here $x_<=\min(x,\xi)$ and $x_>=\max(x,\xi)$. The displayed [Green function](green-s-function-split.md) vanishes at the endpoints, is continuous at $x=\xi$, and its first [derivative](derivative.md) has jump one, so $L_{a,x}G=\delta(x-\xi)$. It is the usual sine [Dirichlet Green function](dirichlet-green-function.md) conjugated by the exponential factor. At $bL\in\pi\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}$ there is a homogeneous zero mode, so the inverse kernel does not exist without a [solvability condition](solvability-condition.md) and a normalization.
