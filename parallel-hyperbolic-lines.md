# Parallel hyperbolic lines

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic line](hyperbolic-line.md)

Two distinct [hyperbolic lines](hyperbolic-line.md) are parallel, in the limiting-parallel convention, when they do not intersect in the [hyperbolic plane](hyperbolic-plane.md) and have exactly one common [ideal endpoint](ideal-endpoint.md). In the [upper half-plane model](poincare-half-plane-model.md), distinct vertical lines are parallel, sharing infinity. Disjoint lines with no common [ideal endpoint](ideal-endpoint.md) are instead [ultraparallel hyperbolic lines](ultraparallel-hyperbolic-lines.md); only these have a [common perpendicular of ultraparallel hyperbolic lines](common-perpendicular-of-ultraparallel-hyperbolic-lines.md).
