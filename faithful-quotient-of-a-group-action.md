# Faithful quotient of a group action

↑ **Parent:** [Faithful group action](faithful-group-action.md)

For any [group action](group-action-split.md) with [group kernel](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) $K$, the [quotient group](quotient-group.md) $G/K$ has a [faithful group action](faithful-group-action.md) on the same [set](set-split.md), given by

$$
(gK)\cdot x=g\cdot x.
$$

To check independence of the [coset](coset.md) representative, replace $g$ by $gk$ with $k\in K$: then $(gk)\cdot x=g\cdot(k\cdot x)=g\cdot x$. If $gK$ fixes every point, then $g\in K$, so $gK=K$, the [identity element](identity-element.md) of the [quotient group](quotient-group.md). The [first isomorphism theorem](first-isomorphism-theorem.md) identifies this [quotient group](quotient-group.md) with the image of $\rho$ inside the [symmetric group](symmetric-group.md). The [group orbits](orbit-group-theory.md) stay the same because the possible point movements stay the same.

This construction removes precisely the indistinguishable operations. In [nonfaithful coset action of the symmetric group on three letters](nonfaithful-coset-action-of-the-symmetric-group-on-three-letters.md), it replaces six [group](group-split.md) elements by two [cosets](coset.md), each representing one visible operation. In [faithful coset action of the symmetric group on three letters](faithful-coset-action-of-the-symmetric-group-on-three-letters.md), the [group kernel](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) is already trivial, so all six distinct operations survive.
