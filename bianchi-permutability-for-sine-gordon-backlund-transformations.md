<h1 id="bianchi-permutability-for-sine-gordon-backlund-transformations">Bianchi permutability for sine-Gordon Bäcklund transformations</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon Bäcklund transformation](sine-gordon-backlund-transformation.md)

Two compatible Bäcklund steps commute after integration constants are matched. The displayed superposition relation constructs their common output algebraically from the seed and the two one-step outputs, in the reciprocal-parameter convention of the [Sine-Gordon Bäcklund transformation](sine-gordon-backlund-transformation.md). It uses the angular field $u=\beta\phi$. Smooth inverse-tangent branch continuation is required to retain the correct vacuum labels.
