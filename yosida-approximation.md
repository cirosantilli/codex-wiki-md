# Yosida approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Infinitesimal generator of a semigroup](infinitesimal-generator-of-a-semigroup.md)

For a [contraction semigroup](contraction-semigroup.md) with [semigroup generator](infinitesimal-generator-of-a-semigroup.md) $L$, the bounded operators $L_\lambda$ approximate $L$ on its [generator domain](generator-domain.md). The [Laplace-transform formula for a semigroup resolvent](laplace-transform-formula-for-a-semigroup-resolvent.md) and [strong continuity](strong-continuity.md) give $\lambda R_\lambda g\to g$ for every vector $g$: split $\int_0^\infty\lambda e^{-\lambda t}(P_tg-g)dt$ into a short interval and an exponentially small tail. For $f\in D(L)$, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives $R_\lambda Lf=\lambda R_\lambda f-f$, so $L_\lambda f=\lambda R_\lambda Lf\to Lf$. No [operator norm](operator-norm.md) convergence of $\lambda R_\lambda$ is implied.
