# Banach-Mazur distance

↑ **Parent:** [Banach space isomorphism](banach-space-isomorphism.md)

For isomorphic finite-dimensional [normed vector spaces](normed-vector-space.md), the Banach-Mazur distance is the infimum of $\|S\|\|S^{-1}\|$ over their linear [isomorphisms](isomorphism.md). If an $N$-dimensional subspace $E$ of a [Banach space](banach-space-split.md) of [cotype 2](cotype-2.md) satisfies the summing estimate $\pi_2(T)\le L\|T\|$ for operators from $\ell_\infty^N$, then $d(E,\ell_\infty^N)\ge\sqrt N/L$: compose an isomorphism with the inclusion and use $\pi_2(I_E)=\sqrt N$.
