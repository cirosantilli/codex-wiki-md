# Safronov number

↑ **Parent:** [Planetary scattering](planetary-scattering.md)

For a [planet](planet.md) on a circular stellar [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md), a common convention is $\Theta=\tfrac12(v_{\rm esc}/v_k)^2$. The escape-to-orbital-speed ratio measures the size of a strong [gravitational assist](gravitational-assist.md) relative to the stellar binding [energy](energy.md) of a nearby [comet](comet.md). Large values tend to favour ejection over collision; small values favour repeated weak encounters with more collision opportunities. This is a statistical comparison, dependent on the initial [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) and encounter geometry, rather than a universal outcome threshold.
