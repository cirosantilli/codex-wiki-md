# No-arbitrage

↑ **Parent:** [Arbitrage](arbitrage.md)

A market satisfies no-arbitrage when it admits no admissible [self-financing strategy](self-financing-portfolio.md) with zero initial cost, nonnegative terminal wealth almost surely, and a strictly positive gain with positive probability. The admissibility restriction excludes doubling strategies. Identical attainable cash flows then have the same price.
