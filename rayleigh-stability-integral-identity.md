# Rayleigh stability integral identity

↑ **Parent:** [Narrow-gap centrifugal stability equation](narrow-gap-centrifugal-stability-equation.md)

Multiply the [narrow-gap centrifugal stability equation](narrow-gap-centrifugal-stability-equation.md) by the complex conjugate mode and use [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) with zero endpoint radial velocities. This gives the displayed real [Rayleigh quotient](rayleigh-quotient.md). Its denominator is positive. Positive [Rayleigh discriminant](rayleigh-discriminant.md) throughout the gap gives positive $\omega^2$ for every nonzero mode, excluding exponential axisymmetric growth; nonnegativity permits marginal cases. In the alternative convention $\Phi=-\kappa_{\rm ep}^2$, the numerator is $-k^2\int\Phi|u|^2dr$, so the stable sign is $\Phi<0$. For rigid rotation, $\kappa_{\rm ep}^2=4\Omega^2$ gives $\omega_n^2=4\Omega^2k^2/[k^2+(n\pi/d)^2]$.
