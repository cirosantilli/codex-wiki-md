# Peak resonance cross section and branching fractions

↑ **Parent:** [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md)

At $E=E_R$, the [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md) loses its explicit dependence on the total [decay width](decay-width.md) after writing $\Gamma_{ab}=B_{ab}\Gamma$ and $\Gamma_X=B_X\Gamma$. For an unpolarized ultrarelativistic [electron](electron.md)-[Positron](positron.md) initial state and a spin-one resonance, $g=3/4$ and $p_i=E_R/2$, giving $\sigma_{\rm peak}=12\pi B_eB_X/E_R^2$ in [natural units](natural-units.md). An elastic resonant channel therefore measures the square $B_e^2$, rather than one power of the electronic [branching fraction](branching-fraction.md).
