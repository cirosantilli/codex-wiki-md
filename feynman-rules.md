# Feynman rules

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md)

[Feynman rules](feynman-rules.md) translate a [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md) into a contribution to a [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) or correlation function in [perturbation theory](perturbation-theory.md). They specify factors for external states, interaction vertices, internal [propagators](propagator.md), momentum-conservation constraints and loop integrals, with statistics-dependent signs and symmetry factors. For [quantum electrodynamics](quantum-electrodynamics.md), a photon-fermion vertex contains the coupling $e$, while a photon propagator supplies an inverse squared four-momentum. The complete amplitude also contains currents, so propagator powers alone do not determine the energy dependence of a [scattering cross-section](scattering-cross-section.md).
