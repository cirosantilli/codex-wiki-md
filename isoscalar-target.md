# Isoscalar target

↑ **Parent:** [Deep inelastic scattering](deep-inelastic-scattering.md)

A nuclear target with equal [proton](proton.md) and [neutron](neutron-split.md) contributions, or data corrected to that average. Its per-nucleon [PDFs](parton-distribution-function.md) are $q_N=(q_p+q_n)/2$. Charge symmetry gives $u_N=d_N=(u_p+d_p)/2$; using [proton](proton.md) down-quark density alone is not an isoscalar average.
