# Variance-minimizing quota share split

↑ **Parent:** [Quota share reinsurance](quota-share-reinsurance.md)

For any aggregate with positive finite [variance](variance-split.md) $v$, [quota share reinsurance](quota-share-reinsurance.md) with retained fraction $\alpha$ has total party [variance](variance-split.md) $v(\alpha^2+(1-\alpha)^2)=v/2+2v(\alpha-1/2)^2$. Equal sharing uniquely minimizes this objective. The two party totals remain dependent; adding their individual [variances](variance-split.md) is a different objective from the fixed [variance](variance-split.md) of their sum.
