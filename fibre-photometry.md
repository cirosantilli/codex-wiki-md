# Fibre photometry

↑ **Parent:** [Neuroimaging](neuroimaging.md)

Optical measurement of a fluorescent signal collected from a tissue volume. It can report population calcium or transmitter-sensor dynamics depending on the reporter; it is not automatically a single-neuron [spike train](spike-train.md).
