# Energy resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Spectroscopy](spectroscopy.md)

[Energy resolution](energy-resolution.md) specifies the characteristic [energy](energy-split.md) separation or broadening that an instrument can distinguish, with a stated width convention and measurement geometry. Resolving a [phonon](phonon.md) in [inelastic scattering](inelastic-scattering.md) requires measuring a small energy transfer as well as [momentum](momentum-split.md) transfer. It is distinct from the incident beam's central [energy](energy-split.md).
