# A subgroup of an amenable group is amenable

↑ **Parent:** [Amenable group](amenable-group.md)

Every [subgroup](subgroup.md) of an [amenable group](amenable-group.md) is amenable. One proof restricts an invariant mean after extending bounded functions from the subgroup to the whole group along a set of coset representatives. Consequently, a group containing a nonamenable subgroup is nonamenable.
