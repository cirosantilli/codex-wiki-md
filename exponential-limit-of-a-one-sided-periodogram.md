# Exponential limit of a one-sided periodogram

↑ **Parent:** [Periodogram](periodogram.md)

At an interior frequency, the real and imaginary Fourier coefficients of a short-memory [Gaussian](normal-distribution.md) time series converge jointly to [independent](independent-random-variables.md) centered normal variables, each with [variance](variance-split.md) $s(\omega)/2$, where $s$ is the [one-sided spectral density](one-sided-spectral-density-of-a-real-stationary-time-series.md). Their squared sum therefore converges to $s(\omega)\chi_2^2/2$. Its [variance](variance-split.md) tends to $s(\omega)^2$, so an unsmoothed [periodogram](periodogram.md) is not [consistent](consistency-statistics.md) when $s(\omega)>0$. Averaging nearby ordinates can reduce this persistent [variance](variance-split.md).
