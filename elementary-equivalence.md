# Elementary equivalence

↑ **Parent:** [Model theory](model-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_equivalence)

Two [first-order structures](first-order-structure.md) are elementarily equivalent when they satisfy the same [first-order sentences](first-order-sentence.md). They need not be isomorphic or embed into one another. Every [elementary embedding](elementary-embedding.md) implies [elementary equivalence](elementary-equivalence.md) of its source and target.
