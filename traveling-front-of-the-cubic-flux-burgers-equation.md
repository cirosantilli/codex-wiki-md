# Traveling front of the cubic-flux Burgers equation

↑ **Parent:** [Modified Burgers equation with cubic flux](modified-burgers-equation-with-cubic-flux.md)

For a front written in $s=Z+c\theta$ connecting $0$ at $s=-\infty$ to nonzero $\beta$ at $s=+\infty$, one integration gives $\epsilon c^2q'=q-cq^3/3$. The endpoint condition fixes $c=3/\beta^2$. Setting $w=q^2$ gives a logistic equation and the displayed profile, with the sign of $q$ selected by $\beta$. The propagation speed in the $\theta$ coordinate is $-\beta^2/3$, agreeing with the [Rankine-Hugoniot condition](rankine-hugoniot-conditions.md) for flux $-q^3/3$.
