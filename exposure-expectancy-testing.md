# Exposure expectancy testing

↑ **Parent:** [Exposure therapy](exposure-therapy.md)

Specifying a feared prediction, arranging an informative and appropriately safe approach task, and comparing the observed outcome with the prediction. The review should include uncertainty and alternative explanations rather than compel a reassuring conclusion. Necessary protections and accessibility adjustments are retained.
