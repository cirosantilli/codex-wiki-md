# Simple ramification point of a holomorphic map

↑ **Parent:** [Ramification point of a holomorphic map](ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md)

A [ramification point](ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) is simple when its [analytic ramification index](ramification-index-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) is two. Its contribution to [total ramification of a holomorphic map](total-ramification-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) is one. In suitable [local coordinates](local-coordinate.md), the [holomorphic map](holomorphic-map.md) is $t\mapsto t^2$; two nearby preimages merge there. A [ramification point](ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) with $e_p=3$ contributes two even though it is just one point.
