<h1 id="fagin-s-theorem">Fagin's theorem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Existential second-order logic](existential-second-order-logic.md)

A class of finite relational structures closed under isomorphism is definable by an [existential second-order logic](existential-second-order-logic.md) sentence exactly when its standard encodings form a language in [NP](np-complexity.md). No order on the structures is required; an auxiliary order can itself be included among the existentially guessed relations.
