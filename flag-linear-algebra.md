# Flag (linear algebra)

↑ **Parent:** [Vector subspace](vector-subspace.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_(linear_algebra))

A flag is a strictly nested chain of [vector subspaces](vector-subspace.md) of a fixed [vector space](vector-space-split.md). A [complete flag](complete-flag.md) contains a subspace of every possible dimension. For an action by [linear maps](linear-map.md), an invariant flag means that each of its [vector subspaces](vector-subspace.md) is an [invariant subspace](invariant-subspace.md). A [basis](basis.md) adapted to an invariant complete flag makes all these maps upper triangular, connecting [complete flags](complete-flag.md) to [simultaneous triangularization of a Lie algebra representation](simultaneous-triangularization-of-a-lie-algebra-representation.md).
