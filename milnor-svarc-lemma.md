<h1 id="milnor-svarc-lemma">Milnor–Švarc lemma</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Geometric group theory](geometric-group-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milnor–Švarc_lemma)

If a [group](group-split.md) has a [properly discontinuous group action](properly-discontinuous-group-action.md) by [isometries](isometry.md) that is also a [cocompact group action](cocompact-group-action.md) on a nonempty [proper metric space](proper-metric-space.md) that is a [geodesic metric space](geodesic-metric-space.md), then the [group](group-split.md) is finitely generated and every [orbit map](orbit-map.md) from the [word metric](word-metric.md) for any finite [generating set of a group](generating-set-of-a-group.md) is a [quasi-isometry](quasi-isometry.md). Finite stabilizers are permitted.
