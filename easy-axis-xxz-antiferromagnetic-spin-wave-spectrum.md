# Easy-axis XXZ antiferromagnetic spin-wave spectrum

↑ **Parent:** [Linear spin-wave approximation](linear-spin-wave-approximation.md)

After a [bipartite spin rotation](bipartite-spin-rotation.md), a leading [Holstein–Primakoff transformation](holstein-primakoff-transformation.md) expansion gives $H_2=-J\Delta NS^2+JS\sum_k[2\Delta n_k+\cos k(a_ka_{-k}+a_k^\dagger a_{-k}^\dagger)]$. The [bosonic Bogoliubov diagonalization](bosonic-bogoliubov-diagonalization.md) gives the displayed spectrum and [vacuum energy](vacuum-energy.md) $-J\Delta NS^2+JS\sum_k[\sqrt{\Delta^2-\cos^2k}-\Delta]$. The anisotropy gap is $2JS\sqrt{\Delta^2-1}$. At $\Delta=1$ the finite-angle transformation is singular at its [zero modes](zero-mode.md), and [quantum depletion of Néel order](quantum-depletion-of-neel-order.md) is infrared divergent in one dimension.
