# Sprague-Grundy theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Sprague-Grundy theory](sprague-grundy-theory.md)

Every finite acyclic [impartial game](impartial-game.md) under the [normal-play convention](normal-play-convention.md) has a [Grundy number](grundy-number.md). The [Grundy number](grundy-number.md) of a sum of independent games is the bitwise [exclusive or](exclusive-or.md) of their individual [Grundy numbers](grundy-number.md), so the sum is losing exactly when that value is zero.
