# Surface charge

↑ **Parent:** [Electric charge](electric-charge-split.md)

[Electric charge](electric-charge-split.md) concentrated on a [surface](topological-surface-split.md). Its local amount per unit [area](surface-area.md) is the [surface charge density](surface-charge-density.md) $\sigma$; in vacuum the jump of the outward normal [electric field](electric-field-split.md) is $\sigma/\epsilon_0$ by [Gauss's law](gauss-s-law.md).
