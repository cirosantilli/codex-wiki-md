# Trimethyl phosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

The phosphate triester $\mathrm{OP(OMe)_3}$, containing three P–O–C linkages and one terminal P–O group. Terminal and alkoxy P–O bonds must be distinguished.
