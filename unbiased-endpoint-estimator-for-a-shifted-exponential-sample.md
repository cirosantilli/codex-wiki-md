# Unbiased endpoint estimator for a shifted exponential sample

↑ **Parent:** [Unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md)

For independent $X_i=\theta+Y_i$ with $Y_i$ exponentially distributed at known rate $\lambda$, the joint [likelihood](likelihood-function.md) factors through the sample minimum $X_{(1)}$. Its excess over the endpoint is exponential with rate $n\lambda$, because $\mathbb P(\min Y_i>t)=e^{-n\lambda t}$. Thus the displayed estimator is an [unbiased estimator](unbiased-estimator.md), with [variance](variance-split.md) $(n\lambda)^{-2}$. Even if $\theta\ge0$, the estimator can be negative; truncation at zero introduces bias.
