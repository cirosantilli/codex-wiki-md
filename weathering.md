# Weathering

↑ **Parent:** [Geomorphology](geomorphology.md)

Physical breakdown or chemical alteration of [rock](rock-geology.md) at or near the surface. Distinguish in-place [weathering](weathering.md) from transport by [erosion](erosion.md). [Chemical weathering](chemical-weathering.md) changes mineral composition, whereas physical processes can fragment rock without requiring the same chemical transformations.
