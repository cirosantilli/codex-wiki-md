# Relative motion

↑ **Parent:** [Kinematics](kinematics.md)

The [position](position.md) and [velocity](velocity.md) of one body relative to another are the differences of their corresponding vectors in the same reference frame. Constant relative [velocity](velocity.md) gives an affine function of time, even when neither body is at rest.
