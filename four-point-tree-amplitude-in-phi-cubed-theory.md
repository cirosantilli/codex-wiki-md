# Four-point tree amplitude in phi cubed theory

↑ **Parent:** [Phi cubed theory](phi-cubed-theory.md)

The four-point [tree-level Feynman diagram](tree-level-feynman-diagram.md) has two cubic vertices joined by one propagator. The three ways to divide four labeled external legs into vertex pairs give the $s$, $t$, and $u$ channels. For interaction $+\lambda\phi^3/3!$, the amplitude is $\mathcal M=-\lambda^2[(s-m^2)^{-1}+(t-m^2)^{-1}+(u-m^2)^{-1}]$, with the [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md) understood. All channels interfere in its modulus squared.
