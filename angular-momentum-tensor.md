# Angular momentum tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Angular momentum](angular-momentum.md)

In $D$ spatial dimensions, rotations have an [antisymmetric tensor](antisymmetric-tensor.md) of conserved [Noether charges](noether-charge.md) when the dynamics is rotationally invariant. There are $D(D-1)/2$ independent rotation generators. In three dimensions the tensor is equivalent to the axial [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) vector through $L_i=\epsilon_{iab}L_{ab}/2$.
