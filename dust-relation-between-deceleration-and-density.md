# Dust relation between deceleration and density

↑ **Parent:** [Deceleration parameter](deceleration-parameter.md)

For [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) with zero [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), the [Friedmann acceleration equation](friedmann-acceleration-equation.md) gives $q=4\pi G\rho/(3H^2)=\Omega_m/2$. Spatial curvature need not vanish. Consequently $kc^2/a^2=(2q-1)H^2$; this relation does not hold for a general fluid mixture.
