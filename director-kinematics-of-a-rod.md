# Director kinematics of a rod

↑ **Parent:** [Material frame of a rod](material-frame-of-a-rod.md)

For a differentiable [material frame of a rod](material-frame-of-a-rod.md), there is a unique vector $\mathbf u$ such that $\mathbf d_i'=\mathbf u\times\mathbf d_i$. To see this, form the [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md) $R=(\mathbf d_1,\mathbf d_2,\mathbf d_3)$. Differentiation of $RR^T=I$ shows that $R'R^T$ is a [skew-symmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md); every three-dimensional [skew-symmetric matrix](skew-symmetric-matrix.md) has the unique form $\mathbf v\mapsto\mathbf u\times\mathbf v$. Primes may denote differentiation with respect to reference or current [arc length](arc-length.md), but that choice must be stated. In a planar bend about $\mathbf d_2$, reference differentiation gives $\mathbf d_3'=u\mathbf d_1$ and $\mathbf d_1'=-u\mathbf d_3$.
