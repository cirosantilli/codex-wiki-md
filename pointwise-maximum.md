# Pointwise maximum

↑ **Parent:** [Function](function-split.md)

The [function](function-split.md) taking the greater value at every common-domain point. For finitely many [affine functions](affine-function.md) it is a [convex function](convex-function.md), by bounding each affine branch by the [convex combination](convex-combination.md) of the endpoint maximum values. This is the finite-affine case of [pointwise maximum of convex functions](pointwise-maximum-of-convex-functions.md).
