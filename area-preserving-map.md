# Area-preserving map

↑ **Parent:** [Discrete dynamical system](discrete-dynamical-system.md)

A planar diffeomorphism is area-preserving if the [phase-space area](phase-space-area.md) of every measurable set is unchanged. The change-of-variables formula proves this property when $|\det DF|=1$ everywhere, and conversely gives that [determinant](determinant.md) condition for a smooth map preserving all sufficiently small areas. Such a map cannot have an asymptotically attracting [fixed point](fixed-point.md) with a positive-area local basin: iterates of a small invariant neighborhood would have to enter arbitrarily small disks while retaining its [phase-space area](phase-space-area.md). For a map with constant positive [determinant](determinant.md) $b$, invariance of a bounded region of positive [phase-space area](phase-space-area.md) instead forces $b=1$.
