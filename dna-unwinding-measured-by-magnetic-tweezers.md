# DNA unwinding measured by magnetic tweezers

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic tweezers](magnetic-tweezers.md)

At fixed [linking number](linking-number.md), local [DNA](deoxyribonucleic-acid.md) unwinding causes compensating [writhe](writhe.md). Its mechanical extension signal has opposite signs on positively and negatively supercoiled branches, distinguishing unwinding from simple shortening. The measured quantity is tether extension, converted through a torsional mechanical calibration, not direct optical separation of bases.[Linking-number argument and opposite-sign extension signals](promoter-unwinding-and-promoter-clearance-by-rna-polymerase-detection-by-single-molecule-dna-nanomanipulation-by-andrey-revyakin-and-others.md)
