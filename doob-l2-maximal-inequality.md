# Doob L2 maximal inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Martingale](martingale-split.md)

For a square-integrable martingale starting at zero,

$$
\mathbb E\sup_{s\leq t}|M_s|^2\leq4\mathbb E|M_t|^2.
$$

For a continuous local martingale stopped so that its quadratic variation is integrable, the [Itô isometry](ito-isometry.md) makes the right-hand side $4\mathbb E[M]_t$.

This is the $p=2$ specialization of [Doob's martingale inequality](doob-s-martingale-inequality.md).
