# Planck normalization of a cubic curvature interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Curvature bispectrum from a zeta zeta-prime-squared interaction](curvature-bispectrum-from-a-zeta-zeta-prime-squared-interaction.md)

The [comoving curvature perturbation](comoving-curvature-perturbation.md) is dimensionless. With spatial coordinates and time carrying inverse mass dimensions, the cubic interaction $\int dt\,d^3x\,a^3\epsilon^2\zeta\dot\zeta^2$ needs a mass-squared coefficient. Its standard gravitational normalization is $M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2$. Six [De Sitter curvature mode functions](de-sitter-curvature-mode-function.md) supply $M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^{-6}$; this vertex then supplies $M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^2$, leaving the physical $M_{\mathrm{Pl}}^{-4}$ curvature [primordial bispectrum](primordial-bispectrum.md). The [Wick-pairing multiplicity for a zeta zeta-prime-squared vertex](wick-pairing-multiplicity-for-a-zeta-zeta-prime-squared-vertex.md) is independently six: three choices of undifferentiated external leg times two differentiated-leg assignments.
