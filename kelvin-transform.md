# Kelvin transform

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic function](harmonic-function.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelvin_transform)

In three-dimensional [Euclidean space](euclidean-norm.md), spherical inversion together with the displayed weight maps [harmonic functions](harmonic-function.md) to [harmonic functions](harmonic-function.md) on the inverted domain. The [Laplacian](laplacian.md) satisfies $\Delta K_a u(\mathbf x)=(a/r)^5(\Delta u)(a^2\mathbf x/r^2)$. The formula is for $r>0$; a new singularity may occur at the origin.
