# Resolution by finitely generated free modules

↑ **Parent:** [Projective resolution](projective-resolution.md)

A [finitely generated module](finitely-generated-module.md) over a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md) admits a possibly infinite [free resolution](free-resolution.md) whose every term is finitely generated. Surject a finite free module onto it; the kernel is finitely generated because a finite free module is [Noetherian](noetherian-ring.md). Repeat this construction on the successive kernels. If the original module has finite [projective dimension](projective-dimension.md) $n$, its $n$th syzygy is projective by dimension shifting of the [Ext functor](ext-functor.md), giving a length-$n$ resolution with finite projective terms.
