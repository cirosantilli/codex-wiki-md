# Radial vector field

↑ **Parent:** [Vector field](vector-field.md)

A [vector field](vector-field.md) directed along the radius from a fixed origin, with magnitude depending only on radius. In three-dimensional Euclidean space and away from the origin, $\mathbf F=f(r)\mathbf r=rf(r)\hat{\mathbf r}$. The [product rule](product-rule.md) gives $\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=3f+rf'$, and the [curl](curl.md) vanishes because the derivative matrix is symmetric. These identities alone do not establish regularity at the origin.
