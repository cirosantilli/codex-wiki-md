# Flat closed immersion of finite presentation

↑ **Parent:** [Closed immersion](closed-immersion.md)

A nonempty flat [closed immersion](closed-immersion.md) of finite presentation into a connected [scheme](scheme.md) is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md). On a chart it is $A\to A/I$ with $I$ finitely generated. At a prime containing $I$, the quotient is a finite flat module over the [local ring](local-ring.md), hence free of rank one, so $I$ vanishes there. Finite generation extends this vanishing to a neighbourhood. Thus the image is open as well as closed; connectedness makes it all of the target, and the ideal is everywhere zero. Without finite presentation the conclusion can fail.
