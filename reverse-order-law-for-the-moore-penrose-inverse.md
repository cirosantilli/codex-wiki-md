<h1 id="reverse-order-law-for-the-moore-penrose-inverse">Reverse-order law for the Moore--Penrose inverse</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Moore-Penrose inverse](moore-penrose-inverse.md)

The identity $(AB)^\dagger=B^\dagger A^\dagger$ does not hold for arbitrary matrices. A sufficient condition is that $A$ have [full column rank](full-column-rank.md) and $B$ have [full row rank](full-row-rank.md).
