# Preheating

↑ **Parent:** [Reheating](reheating.md)

Nonperturbative particle production after [cosmic inflation](cosmic-inflation-split.md), for example by [parametric resonance](parametric-resonance.md) caused by an oscillating [inflaton](inflaton.md). Large occupation numbers can grow before a thermal bath exists. Backreaction, rescattering and subsequent thermalization affect the outcome, so an effective perturbative decay rate need not describe every stage.[Abstract and Introduction](towards-the-theory-of-reheating-after-inflation-by-lev-kofman-and-others.md)
