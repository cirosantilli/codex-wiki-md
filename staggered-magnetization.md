# Staggered magnetization

↑ **Parent:** [Néel order parameter](neel-order-parameter.md)

The staggered magnetization weights the local [magnetization](magnetization.md) with opposite signs on the two sublattices of a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md). It is nonzero in a [Néel state](neel-state.md) even when the uniform magnetization vanishes.
