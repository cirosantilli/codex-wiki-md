# Mittag-Leffler problem on a Riemann surface

↑ **Parent:** [Principal part of a meromorphic function](principal-part-of-a-meromorphic-function.md)

The Mittag-Leffler problem asks for a global [meromorphic function](meromorphic-function.md) with prescribed [principal parts](principal-part-of-a-meromorphic-function.md) at a discrete collection of points. For a finite collection, those principal parts define a [Čech cocycle](cech-cocycle-condition.md) for the sheaf $\mathcal O$; vanishing of its class in $\check H^1(X,\mathcal O)$ is exactly the condition that the problem has a solution.
