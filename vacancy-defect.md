# Vacancy defect

↑ **Parent:** [Crystallographic defect](crystallographic-defect.md)

A missing atom at a lattice site that would be occupied in the reference crystal. A [vacancy defect](vacancy-defect.md) is a point defect and can contribute [configurational entropy](configurational-entropy.md) because many choices of empty sites can have the same macroscopic energy. Formation energy and pressure-volume work oppose this entropy gain. Vacancies can be present in [thermal equilibrium](thermal-equilibrium-split.md); they need not all be preparation mistakes. Their allowed number and creation mechanism depend on whether the lattice size, atom number and surface reservoirs are fixed.
