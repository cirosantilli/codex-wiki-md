The [hypocentre](../../../../../hypocenter.md) is the point where rupture begins. The [epicentre](../../../../../epicenter.md) is its projection onto the [Earth](../../../../../earth.md)'s surface. An [earthquake centroid](../../../../../earthquake-centroid.md) is an effective spatial average of moment release over the rupture, with a corresponding mean release time. **Initiation and average release need not occur at the same place or time**, especially for an extended, asymmetric rupture.
