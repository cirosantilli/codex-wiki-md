<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Identify the event and pick calibrated [P wave](../../../../../p-wave.md) and, where available, [S wave](../../../../../s-wave.md) arrivals at stations of known position. Associate each pick with a [seismic phase](../../../../../seismic-phase.md) and uncertainty. An [earthquake location by arrival times](../../../../../earthquake-location-by-arrival-times.md) fits

$$
t_i^{\rm obs}=t_0+T_i(\mathbf x)+\epsilon_i
$$

for the three [hypocentre](../../../../../hypocenter.md) coordinates and [earthquake origin time](../../../../../earthquake-origin-time.md), using [travel times](../../../../../travel-time.md) from a specified [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) model. A grid search can initialize iterative [weighted least squares](../../../../../weighted-least-squares.md) or a probabilistic inversion. Station coverage must constrain depth as well as horizontal position; merely having four observations does not ensure a well-conditioned solution.

Errors include uncertain or misidentified onsets, clock and station-coordinate errors, wrong velocities or neglected three-dimensional structure, poor azimuthal coverage, inadequate station distances and depth–origin-time tradeoffs. Examine residual patterns and resolution, not only the minimum residual. A small formal [covariance](../../../../../covariance.md) within a wrong [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) model can accompany a systematically biased [hypocentre](../../../../../hypocenter.md).
