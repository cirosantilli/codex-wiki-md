↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Nearby events observed at the same station share most of their propagation path, so common model and station errors cancel in differential arrival times. Waveform [cross-correlation](../../../../../cross-correlation.md) can also determine their relative timing more precisely than separate onset picks. [Double-difference earthquake relocation](../../../../../double-difference-earthquake-relocation.md) fits

$$
(t_{ik}-t_{jk})_{\rm obs}-[T_k(\mathbf x_i)-T_k(\mathbf x_j)]
$$

along with the difference in origin times. **The relative cluster geometry can be precise while the entire cluster remains displaced.** Absolute anchoring still needs a well-calibrated [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) model or a source of known position.[2000, method and common-path rationale](../../../../../a-double-difference-earthquake-location-algorithm-method-and-application-to-the-northern-hayward-fault-california-by-f-waldhauser-and-w-ellsworth.md)
