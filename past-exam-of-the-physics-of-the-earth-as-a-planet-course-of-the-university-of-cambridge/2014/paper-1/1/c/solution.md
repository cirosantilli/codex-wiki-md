<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a [double-couple seismic source](../../../../../double-couple-seismic-source.md), measure [P-wave first-motion polarity](../../../../../p-wave-first-motion-polarity.md) signs and use source-to-station takeoff angles from the location and [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) model to plot them on a [focal sphere](../../../../../focal-sphere.md). Fit the alternating radiation-sign quadrants, optionally adding amplitude ratios and waveform information. The two nodal planes determine a [focal mechanism](../../../../../focal-mechanism.md), conventionally expressed by [fault strike](../../../../../fault-strike.md), [fault dip](../../../../../fault-dip.md) and [fault rake](../../../../../fault-rake.md).

A more general waveform inversion fits a [seismic moment tensor](../../../../../seismic-moment-tensor.md) to several components and stations. Its double-couple part supplies candidate slip and normal [vectors](../../../../../vector.md), but a general moment [tensor](../../../../../tensor.md) should not be forced to be double-couple without justification. Polarity calibration, near-nodal observations, path effects and takeoff-angle errors limit the result.

**Point-source radiation alone interchanges the [fault plane](../../../../../fault-plane.md) and auxiliary plane without changing the double couple.** Resolve this [fault-plane ambiguity](../../../../../fault-plane-ambiguity.md) using mapped rupture, aftershock geometry, [geodesy](../../../../../geodesy.md) or finite-rupture directivity. A dense set of polarity picks does not by itself remove that fundamental ambiguity.
