<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Using the same diameter convention and [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) estimate,

$$
\boxed{\frac{\rho^2g\lambda^3}{\eta^2}
\simeq\frac{(3000)^2(9.81)(6.0\times10^6)^3}
{(2.56\times10^{21})^2}
\simeq2.9\times10^{-15}.}
$$

Geometrical factors omitted in the scaling do not affect the conclusion: **[inertia](../../../../../inertia.md) is negligible**. Convective [inertia](../../../../../inertia.md) is smaller still by the small relief-to-wavelength ratio. [Mantle](../../../../../planetary-mantle.md) creep is extremely slow despite the large length scale.
