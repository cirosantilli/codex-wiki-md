<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Unsteady inertial [force density](../../../../../force-density.md) scales as $\rho v/\tau$, whereas viscous [force density](../../../../../force-density.md) scales as $\eta v/\lambda^2$. Using the preceding timescale gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{\text{unsteady inertia}}{\text{viscosity}}
\sim\frac{\rho\lambda^2}{\eta\tau}
\sim\frac{\rho^2g\lambda^3}{\eta^2}.}
$$

Convective [inertia](../../../../../inertia.md) has ratio $\rho v\lambda/\eta$. Since $v\sim h/\tau$, this is smaller by $h/\lambda$ for small relief. Thus the displayed estimate controls the larger inertial contribution in this model.
