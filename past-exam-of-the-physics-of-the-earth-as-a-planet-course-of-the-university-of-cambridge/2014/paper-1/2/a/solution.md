<h1 id="2/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Subtract the background [hydrostatic pressure](../../../../../hydrostatic-pressure.md). A relief amplitude $h$ produces a restoring [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) perturbation $p'\sim\rho gh$ and horizontal [gradient](../../../../../gradient.md) $\rho gh/\lambda$. [Incompressibility](../../../../../incompressible-flow.md) makes the disturbance penetrate a depth of order its horizontal scale. For [sinusoidal viscous rebound](../../../../../sinusoidal-viscous-rebound.md), with relaxation [speed](../../../../../speed.md) $v\sim h/\tau$, the viscous [force density](../../../../../force-density.md) is $\eta v/\lambda^2$. Balancing gives

$$
\eta\frac{h}{\tau\lambda^2}\sim\frac{\rho gh}{\lambda},
\qquad\boxed{\tau\sim\frac{\eta}{\rho g\lambda}.}
$$

This is a small-amplitude viscous half-space estimate; numerical factors from the sinusoidal geometry are absent from a scaling argument.
