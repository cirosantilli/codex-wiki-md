<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Write $T_s$ for the fixed surface [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and $\theta=\overline T-T_s$. The [heat capacity](../../../../../heat-capacity.md) per unit volume is $\rho_0c_p=k/\kappa$. Per unit horizontal area the layer stores [sensible heat](../../../../../sensible-heat.md) $(k/\kappa)d\theta$. Its increase equals the input less the output:

$$
\boxed{\frac{kd}{\kappa}\frac{d\overline T}{dt}=F-E(t).}
$$

The same equation holds for $\theta$ because $T_s$ is constant. It assumes the supplied constant-property, fixed-thickness layer, without internal [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) production, [latent heat](../../../../../latent-heat.md), or mechanical contributions to its [heat](../../../../../heat-split.md) budget. The infinite horizontal area is handled by taking the budget per unit area.
