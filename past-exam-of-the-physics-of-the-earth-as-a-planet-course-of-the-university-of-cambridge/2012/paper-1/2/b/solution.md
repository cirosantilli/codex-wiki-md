<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With the stated ratio $r=v_A/v_B=2$, the time difference equals the [travel time](../../../../../travel-time.md) of A: $\Delta t=R/v_A$. Plotting the squared differences against squared epicentral distances therefore gives a straight line of slope $M=1/v_A^2$ and intercept $B=(\Delta t_0)^2$.

Using the fitted vertex from (a), an ordinary least-squares line gives

$$
M=0.0156088\,\mathrm{s^2\,km^{-2}},\qquad B=221.904\,\mathrm{s^2}.
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{v_A=M^{-1/2}\simeq8.0\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}},\quad
v_B\simeq4.0\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}},\quad
z_0=\sqrt{B/M}\simeq119\,\mathrm{km}.}
$$

For graph-level uncertainties, taking $v_A=8.0\pm0.2\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$ and $\Delta t_0=15.0\pm0.5\,\mathrm{s}$ gives

$$
\delta z_0\simeq\sqrt{(v_A\delta\Delta t_0)^2+(\Delta t_0\delta v_A)^2}\simeq5\,\mathrm{km}.
$$

Thus **a defensible graphical depth estimate is $z_0\simeq120\pm5\,\mathrm{km}$**. It is not a calibrated [confidence interval](../../../../../confidence-interval.md). If each difference has independent uniform [rounding error](../../../../../round-off-error.md) within $\pm0.5\,\mathrm{s}$, a direct nonlinear fit to the unsquared differences gives $x_0=140.23\,\mathrm{km}$, $\Delta t_0=14.896\,\mathrm{s}$, and $v_A=8.005\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$; the fitted depth is again $119.24\,\mathrm{km}$. A [covariance](../../../../../covariance.md) calculation must include [correlations](../../../../../pearson-correlation-coefficient.md) between fitted slope, intercept, and vertex, rather than treating all three as independent precise measurements. Unmodelled velocity heterogeneity would add uncertainty beyond rounding.
