<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

New oceanic [lithosphere](../../../../../lithosphere.md) is hot and mechanically weak. As it cools, the depth over which rock can sustain elastic [stress](../../../../../stress.md) on the loading timescale generally increases, so **[effective elastic thickness](../../../../../effective-elastic-thickness.md) tends to increase with plate age at the time of loading**. It is this mechanical state when the load is emplaced, together with subsequent relaxation, that matters; the present age alone is insufficient for an ancient load.

The [half-space cooling model](../../../../../half-space-cooling-model.md) illustrates the thermal dependence. With age $\tau$, surface [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_s$, initial interior [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_m$ and constant [thermal diffusivity](../../../../../thermal-diffusivity.md) $\kappa$,

$$
T(z,\tau)=T_s+(T_m-T_s)
\operatorname{erf}\left(\frac{z}{2\sqrt{\kappa\tau}}\right).
$$

A chosen [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) $T_*$ lies at

$$
\boxed{z_*=2\sqrt{\kappa\tau}\,
\operatorname{erf}^{-1}\left(\frac{T_*-T_s}{T_m-T_s}\right)
\ \propto\sqrt\tau.}
$$

If the base of the mechanically strong layer approximately follows such an isotherm, $T_e$ initially follows a similar age trend. Cooling models constrained by ocean-floor depth and heat flow provide the thermal motivation; their fitted thermal thickness must not be mistaken for measured [elastic thickness](../../../../../effective-elastic-thickness.md).[1977, pages 803–806 and discussion of half-space versus finite-plate cooling](../../../../../an-analysis-of-the-variation-of-ocean-floor-bathymetry-and-heat-flow-with-age-by-barry-parsons-and-john-g-sclater.md)

In practice $T_e$ is an effective bending thickness rather than the depth of one universal [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). Stress-dependent yielding, [grain size](../../../../../grain-size.md), water content, composition, loading duration and reheating can alter it. Finite-plate cooling and basal heat input also limit indefinite square-root growth at old ages. Nor should $T_e$ be identified with either the thickness of the [crust](../../../../../planetary-crust.md) or the full seismically defined [lithosphere](../../../../../lithosphere.md).
