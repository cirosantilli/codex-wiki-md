**[Homologous temperature](../../../../../homologous-temperature.md) and deformation.** [Homologous temperature](../../../../../homologous-temperature.md) is $\Theta=T/T_m$, with both temperatures in [kelvin](../../../../../kelvin.md) and $T_m$ the appropriate pressure- and composition-dependent melting [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md). It measures proximity to melting more meaningfully than an absolute [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) alone. Pressure, composition and water can alter both $T_m$ and the flow law, so equal $\Theta$ does not imply identical rheology.

A useful thermally activated [material creep](../../../../../creep-deformation.md) law is

$$
\dot\epsilon=A\sigma^n d^{-p}
\exp\left[-\frac{E^*+PV^*}{RT}\right],
$$

with [stress](../../../../../stress.md) $\sigma$, [grain size](../../../../../grain-size.md) $d$, [activation energy](../../../../../activation-energy.md) $E^*$ and [activation volume](../../../../../activation-volume.md) $V^*$. Water activity can add another factor. Increasing [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) raises the rate at fixed [stress](../../../../../stress.md) when the activation enthalpy is positive, greatly lowering effective [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md). Solid material can therefore flow on geological timescales well below melting.

[Diffusion creep](../../../../../diffusion-creep.md) is approximately [linear](../../../../../linearity.md) in [stress](../../../../../stress.md): bulk-diffusion [Nabarro-Herring creep](../../../../../nabarro-herring-creep.md) has grain-size scaling $d^{-2}$, while grain-boundary [Coble creep](../../../../../coble-creep.md) has $d^{-3}$ in their ideal regimes. [Dislocation creep](../../../../../dislocation-creep.md) usually has a [stress](../../../../../stress.md) exponent greater than one and weaker grain-size dependence. Its effective [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) scales as $\eta_{\rm eff}\propto\sigma^{1-n}$ at fixed [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md), so stronger forcing changes the apparent [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md). At lower [homologous temperature](../../../../../homologous-temperature.md), brittle/frictional deformation or high-stress dislocation mechanisms can become important. The dominant process also depends on pressure and confinement; low [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) is not by itself a guarantee of brittle failure deep in a planet.

A [deformation mechanism map](../../../../../deformation-mechanism-map.md) compares the competing rates or strengths as functions of [stress](../../../../../stress.md) and [homologous temperature](../../../../../homologous-temperature.md) at specified [grain size](../../../../../grain-size.md), pressure and composition. Fine grains favour [diffusion creep](../../../../../diffusion-creep.md); larger [stress](../../../../../stress.md) favours nonlinear [dislocation creep](../../../../../dislocation-creep.md). There is no universal [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) dividing all these regimes. This sensitivity helps explain a strong outer [lithosphere](../../../../../lithosphere.md) above a slowly flowing solid [mantle](../../../../../planetary-mantle.md) and why modest thermal or water variations can reorganize deformation.

**Determining the actual planform of [mantle convection](../../../../../mantle-convection.md).** The task is to infer the geometry of upwellings, downwellings and connecting flow in the present [Earth](../../../../../earth.md), rather than to draw a preferred laboratory convection cell. Plate velocities and the distribution of subduction zones directly constrain surface motion and likely cold downwellings. Ridges, volcanism and hotspots provide further clues, but a ridge need not mark the centre of a deep plume, nor is every hotspot uniquely explained by one plume geometry.

[Seismic tomography](../../../../../seismic-tomography.md) supplies three-dimensional [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) structure from body-wave travel times, waveforms and surface-wave [dispersion](../../../../../wave-dispersion.md). Fast slab-like structures can often be traced beneath subduction zones into the lower [mantle](../../../../../planetary-mantle.md). The 1997 global P-wave study reported long narrow lower-mantle structures connected with convergence and interpreted some slab segments as reaching the deepest [mantle](../../../../../planetary-mantle.md); that is evidence for deep circulation, not proof that every slab follows one route.[Nature 386, pages 578–584; abstract on lower-mantle structures and mantle-wide flow](../../../../../evidence-for-deep-mantle-circulation-from-global-tomography-by-r-d-van-der-hilst-and-others.md) [Temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) affects seismic speed, but composition, phase changes, water and anisotropy also matter, so [velocity](../../../../../velocity.md) is not itself a flow map.

Gravity and the [geoid](../../../../../geoid.md) constrain the spatially integrated [density](../../../../../density.md) field and its induced boundary deformation. [Dynamic topography](../../../../../dynamic-topography.md) supplies information about vertical stresses, after separating crustal structure, isostatic loads and thermal subsidence. [Seismic anisotropy](../../../../../seismic-anisotropy.md) can constrain deformation and fabric orientations, subject to a mineral-physics relation between fabric and flow. Discontinuity topography and the [mantle](../../../../../planetary-mantle.md)'s heat budget provide further tests. Each observation has a different sensitivity to [density](../../../../../density.md), [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md), making their combination more useful than any one image.

Compare these observations with three-dimensional geodynamic models that solve creeping-flow force balance, mass conservation and thermal transport, including buoyancy from [temperature](../../../../../temperature-split.md) and composition, realistic plate motions, [viscosity](../../../../../dynamic-viscosity-split.md) structure and phase boundaries. Predict seismic observables, gravity, [geoid](../../../../../geoid.md) and [dynamic topography](../../../../../dynamic-topography.md) from the same model, and assess data coverage and resolution before interpreting a feature. Whole-mantle exchange, impeded or deflected slabs near transition zones, and broad deep anomalies can coexist; the evidence does not require identical stacked or single-layer cells everywhere. **The actual planform is constrained by joint seismic, gravitational, surface-kinematic and thermal evidence, with substantial nonuniqueness in its detailed flow and history.**
