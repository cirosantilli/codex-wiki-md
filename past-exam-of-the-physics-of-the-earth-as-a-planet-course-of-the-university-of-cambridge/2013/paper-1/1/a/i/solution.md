At the top, **$T(a,t)=T_0$**. Ocean water provides a cold [thermal reservoir](../../../../../../heat-bath.md) relative to [mantle](../../../../../../planetary-mantle.md) [temperatures](../../../../../../temperature-split.md), so a fixed surface [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) is a useful idealization. The model does not resolve near-axis hydrothermal circulation or spatially variable bottom-water [temperatures](../../../../../../temperature-split.md).
