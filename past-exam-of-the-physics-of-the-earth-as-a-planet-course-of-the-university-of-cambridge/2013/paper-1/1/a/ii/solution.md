<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

At the ridge axis, **$T(z,0)=T_1$ for $0<z<a$** in the intended cooling model. Upwelling and magmatic [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) supply continually bring hot material into the newly formed plate. The abrupt meeting of this hot interior with the cold upper boundary is an idealized corner discontinuity, resolved diffusively for $t>0$. The printed series fails this [initial condition](../../../../../../initial-condition.md), as shown in the parent Solution.
