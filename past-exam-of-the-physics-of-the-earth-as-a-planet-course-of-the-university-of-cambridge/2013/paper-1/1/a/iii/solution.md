At the bottom, **$T(0,t)=T_1$**. Convecting [mantle](../../../../../../planetary-mantle.md) is treated as maintaining the basal [temperature](../../../../../../temperature-split.md) and supplying [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) to the plate. This is a prescribed thermal boundary at fixed thickness, not a claim that the real mechanical base is an impermeable material interface.
