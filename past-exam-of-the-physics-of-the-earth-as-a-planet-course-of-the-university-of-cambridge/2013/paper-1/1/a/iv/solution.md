<h1 id="1/a/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

Far from the ridge, **$T(z,\infty)=T_0+\Delta T(1-z/a)$**. Transients have decayed and the [heat equation](../../../../../../heat-equation.md) reduces to $T_{zz}=0$. A finite basal [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) supply sustains this [gradient](../../../../../../gradient.md). Thermal thickness, seafloor depth and surface [heat](../../../../../../heat-split.md) flux therefore approach finite limits rather than continuing the laws of the [half-space cooling model](../../../../../../half-space-cooling-model.md) indefinitely.
