<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The depth-averaged added horizontal compressive [stress](../../../../../stress.md) is $\bar\sigma=F/a$. Resolving its [traction](../../../../../traction.md) on a plane dipping at angle $\delta$ gives the [fault shear stress from a plate-integrated force](../../../../../fault-shear-stress-from-a-plate-integrated-force.md),

$$
\bar\tau=\bar\sigma\sin\delta\cos\delta,
\qquad
\boxed{|\bar\tau|=\frac{F}{2a}\simeq1.30\times10^7\,\mathrm{Pa}=13\,\mathrm{MPa}
\quad(\delta=45^\circ).}
$$

Keeping the previous topographic correction gives about $13.8\,\mathrm{MPa}$. This is the mean additional shear from [ridge push](../../../../../ridge-push.md), assuming the [force](../../../../../force-split.md) is transmitted through thickness $a$ and resolving the nonisotropic [stress](../../../../../stress.md) increment. An isotropic lithostatic [pressure](../../../../../pressure-split.md) adds normal [traction](../../../../../traction.md) but no shear. The [force](../../../../../force-split.md) [integral](../../../../../integral-split.md) alone does not specify the local [stress](../../../../../stress.md) at each point on the [fault](../../../../../geological-fault.md) or establish failure.
