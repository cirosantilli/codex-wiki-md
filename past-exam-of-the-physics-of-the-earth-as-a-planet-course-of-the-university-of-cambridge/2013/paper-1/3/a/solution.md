<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For [group velocity from a period-dependent phase velocity](../../../../../group-velocity-from-a-period-dependent-phase-velocity.md), start with $k=\omega/c_n(\omega)$ rather than equating [group velocity](../../../../../group-velocity.md) to [phase velocity](../../../../../phase-velocity.md). Differentiating,

$$
\frac1{u_n}=\frac{dk}{d\omega}=\frac1{c_n}-\frac{\omega}{c_n^2}\frac{dc_n}{d\omega}.
$$

For the stated inverse-frequency term, $dc_n/d\omega=-a/\omega^2$, so

$$
\boxed{\frac{u_n}{c_n}=\frac1{1+a/(\omega c_n)}
=\frac{c_n}{c_n+a/\omega},\qquad
u_n=\frac{c_n^2}{c_n+a/\omega}<c_n.}
$$

The inequality assumes positive [frequency](../../../../../frequency.md) and physical positive [phase velocity](../../../../../phase-velocity.md), with $a>0$. The symbol $a$ here has units of acceleration and is unrelated to the plate thickness in Question 1.
