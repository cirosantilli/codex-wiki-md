<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Write the oscillation [period](../../../../../period-of-a-function.md) as $P=2\pi/\omega$, so locally $c_n=c_o+(a/2\pi)P$. The [first overtone](../../../../../first-overtone.md) is the lowest curve above the fundamental, not the [fundamental mode](../../../../../fundamental-mode.md) itself. Near $P=50\,\mathrm s$, Figure 1 gives approximately $c_1=5.13\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$. Representative nearby readings are about $4.96\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$ at $40\,\mathrm s$ and $5.30\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$ at $60\,\mathrm s$. Thus

$$
\left.\frac{dc_1}{dP}\right|_{50\,\mathrm s}\simeq0.017\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-2}},\qquad
\boxed{a\simeq2\pi(0.017)\simeq0.11\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-2}}
\simeq1.1\times10^2\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}.}
$$

The local group-velocity estimate is

$$
\boxed{u_1\simeq\frac{5.13}{1+50(0.017)/5.13}
\simeq4.4\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}.}
$$

These are graphical estimates, roughly $a=0.11\pm0.02\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-2}}$ and $u_1=4.4\pm0.1\,\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$, rather than high-precision fitted constants. The nearby straight-line approximation need not describe that mode over the whole plotted [period](../../../../../period-of-a-function.md) range. No source curve is reproduced here.
