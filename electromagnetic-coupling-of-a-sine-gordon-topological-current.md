# Electromagnetic coupling of a sine-Gordon topological current

↑ **Parent:** [Sine-Gordon kink](sine-gordon-kink.md)

The identically conserved [topological current](topological-current.md) $j$ couples to an external electromagnetic [gauge potential](gauge-field.md) through $-A_\mu j^\mu=-A_0\theta_x+A_1\theta_t$. A [gauge transformation](gauge-transformation.md) changes this coupling only by a divergence. The [Euler-Lagrange field equation](euler-lagrange-field-equation.md) becomes $\theta_{tt}-\theta_{xx}+\sin\theta+E=0$, with $E=\partial_tA_1-\partial_xA_0$. A unit [Sine-Gordon kink](sine-gordon-kink.md) has unnormalized charge $Q=2\pi$ and mass $8$. For spatially uniform forcing, its field momentum balance is $\dot P=QE$ when the boundary stress contributions cancel.
