# Centre-of-mass zero mode of a harmonic chain

↑ **Parent:** [Harmonic chain](harmonic-chain.md)

The uniform [displacement](displacement.md) $X=N^{-1}\sum_j u_j$ and total [momentum](momentum-split.md) $P=\sum_jp_j$ satisfy $[X,P]=i\hbar$. The nonzero-frequency [phonon](phonon.md) expansion alone yields the site [commutator](commutator.md) $i\hbar(\delta_{jl}-1/N)$; including $X,P$ restores the missing uniform part. The thermal free-coordinate factor requires a specified volume, pinning or a fixed [centre of mass](center-of-mass.md). Substituting zero frequency into an oscillator [partition function](canonical-partition-function.md) instead produces a spurious divergence without defining the physical [zero mode](zero-mode.md).
