# Nondecreasing function

↑ **Parent:** [Monotonic function](monotonic-function.md)

A [real-valued function](real-valued-function.md) $f:D\to\mathbb R$ is a [nondecreasing function](nondecreasing-function.md) if every pair $x<y$ in its [domain of a function](domain-of-a-function.md) satisfies $f(x)\leq f(y)$. Flat [intervals](interval-mathematics.md) are allowed, unlike for a [strictly increasing function](strictly-increasing-function.md). For example,

$$
p(x)=\min(1,\max(0,x))=\begin{cases}0,&x\leq0,\\x,&0<x<1,\\1,&x\geq1\end{cases}
$$

is a [continuous](continuous-function.md) [nondecreasing function](nondecreasing-function.md), but it repeats its output on each flat [interval](interval-mathematics.md).

A [nondecreasing function](nondecreasing-function.md) can also be [discontinuous](discontinuity-of-a-function.md). The [step function](step-function.md) $F(x)=0$ for $x<0$ and $F(x)=1$ for $x\geq0$ has a [jump discontinuity](jump-discontinuity.md) at zero without ever decreasing. It is the [cumulative distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md) of a [random variable](random-variable-split.md) that equals zero with [probability](probability.md) one; [quantile functions](quantile-function.md) handle such jumps through a generalized inverse. [Nondecreasing](nondecreasing-function.md) allocation [functions](function-split.md) permit [critical-value payments](critical-value-payment.md) in [single-parameter mechanisms](single-parameter-mechanism.md).
