# Quark number sum rule

↑ **Parent:** [Parton distribution function](parton-distribution-function.md)

The [parton distribution function](parton-distribution-function.md) is a number density. Integrating the quark distribution minus the antiquark distribution counts the net flavour charge of the [hadron](hadron.md), since every sea quark-antiquark pair contributes zero. The corresponding flavour-vector [conserved current](conserved-current.md) counts particles minus antiparticles. For a [proton](proton.md), the net up/down numbers are two and one; for a [neutron](neutron.md) they are one and two. The rule fixes the difference of distributions, not their sum, and does not imply absence of a sea.
