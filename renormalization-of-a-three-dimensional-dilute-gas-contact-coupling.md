# Renormalization of a three-dimensional dilute-gas contact coupling

↑ **Parent:** [Contact-interaction Bose-gas field integral](contact-interaction-bose-gas-field-integral.md)

The regulated bare coupling $g_0$ is matched to the physical two-body [scattering length](scattering-length-from-a-partial-wave-s-matrix.md). In the uniform one-loop [grand potential](grand-potential.md) this matching supplies the subtraction that makes $\tfrac12\int d^3k/(2\pi)^3[E_k-\varepsilon_k-\mu+\mu^2/(2\varepsilon_k)]$ ultraviolet finite. Merely using the physical coupling in an unregulated zero-point integral would retain a divergent answer. The contact description and its cutoff cannot be extended to arbitrary momenta independently of that matching.
