# Free commutative monoid

↑ **Parent:** [Commutative monoid](commutative-monoid.md)

The [free commutative monoid](free-commutative-monoid.md) on $d$ generators consists of the nonnegative integral sums of those generators, with unique coefficients. Its [semigroup algebra](semigroup-algebra.md) over a [commutative ring](commutative-ring.md) is the [polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md) in $d$ variables. In a smooth toric chart, the dual-cone lattice points form such a monoid, yielding affine-space coordinates.
