# Pure torsion of an incompressible cylinder

↑ **Parent:** [Torque and axial force of a twisted incompressible cylinder](torque-and-axial-force-of-a-twisted-incompressible-cylinder.md)

A [deformation map](deformation-map.md) twisting every cross-section of a [cylinder](cylinder-geometry.md) through an [angle](angle.md) proportional to its reference axial position, without radial or axial extension. In the local current and reference [cylindrical coordinate systems](cylindrical-coordinate-system.md), the [deformation gradient](deformation-gradient.md) is $F=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&\tau r\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}$. Its [determinant](determinant.md) is one. The twist per unit reference length is $\tau$; an end separation $L$ gives end rotation $\varphi=\tau L$. The physical [torque](torque.md) is $N=2\pi\int_0^A T_{\theta z}r^2\,dr$.
