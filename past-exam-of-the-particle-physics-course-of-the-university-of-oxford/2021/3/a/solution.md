<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A [mass eigenstate](../../../../mass-eigenstate.md) $\lvert\nu_i\rangle$ has a definite [neutrino mass](../../../../neutrino-mass.md) $m_i$ and acquires a definite propagation phase at fixed [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md). An [interaction eigenstate](../../../../interaction-eigenstate.md) is the [neutrino flavor](../../../../neutrino-flavor.md) selected by a [weak charged current](../../../../charged-current.md): $\nu_e$ is produced or detected together with an [electron](../../../../electron.md), and $\nu_\mu$ together with a [muon](../../../../muon.md). The two bases are related by a [unitary matrix](../../../../unitary-matrix.md), here the real [rotation matrix](../../../../rotation-matrix.md) $U$.

Inverting the supplied transformation gives $\lvert\nu_e\rangle=c\lvert\nu_1\rangle+s\lvert\nu_2\rangle$ and $\lvert\nu_\mu\rangle=-s\lvert\nu_1\rangle+c\lvert\nu_2\rangle$, where $c=\cos\theta$, $s=\sin\theta$. If $\theta=0$, these bases coincide: each produced [neutrino flavor](../../../../neutrino-flavor.md) propagates as one [mass eigenstate](../../../../mass-eigenstate.md), and there is no [neutrino oscillation](../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md), even if the two [masses](../../../../mass.md) differ. Conversely, equal [masses](../../../../mass.md) also prevent vacuum [neutrino oscillation](../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md), even when a nonzero basis-rotation angle is written down.
