<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

For a positive [electron neutrino](../../../../electron-neutrino.md) matter potential and the normal ordering implicit in positive $K$, choose $0\le\theta_m\le\pi/2$ by continuous diagonalization of the matter [Hamiltonian](../../../../hamiltonian.md). With $D=[(\cos2\theta-K\rho)^2+\sin^22\theta]^{1/2}$, the quadrant is fixed by

$$
\cos2\theta_m=\frac{\cos2\theta-K\rho}{D},\qquad\sin2\theta_m=\frac{\sin2\theta}{D}.
$$

At high density $\cos2\theta_m\to-1$, so

$$
\boxed{\theta_m\to\pi/2,\qquad \lvert\nu_e\rangle\simeq\lvert\nu_{2m}\rangle.}
$$

Taking a principal-value arctangent alone and concluding $\theta_m\to0$ would select the wrong branch. The production state is the second _matter propagation eigenstate_, rather than literally the vacuum [mass eigenstate](../../../../mass-eigenstate.md) while still inside the dense medium.

Assume the solar density decreases slowly enough for [adiabatic matter-enhanced neutrino conversion](../../../../adiabatic-matter-enhanced-neutrino-conversion.md), and neglect subsequent Earth matter effects. The state follows $\nu_{2m}$ continuously into the vacuum [mass eigenstate](../../../../mass-eigenstate.md) $\nu_2$. Since $\langle\nu_e\vert\nu_2\rangle=\sin\theta$,

$$
\boxed{P_{ee}^{\rm adiabatic}=\sin^2\theta=\sin^2(22.5^\circ)=\frac{2-\sqrt2}{4}\simeq0.1464.}
$$

This is substantially below the phase-averaged vacuum result $0.75$. There is no interference between two populated vacuum [mass eigenstates](../../../../mass-eigenstate.md) in this ideal adiabatic limit. More generally, after phase averaging, $P_{ee}=\tfrac12+\tfrac12\cos2\theta\cos2\theta_m$ at production if the evolution is adiabatic. In the high-density limit, a nonadiabatic level-jump [probability](../../../../probability.md) $P_c$ instead gives $P_{ee}=(1-P_c)\sin^2\theta+P_c\cos^2\theta$. Thus the supplied core-density condition alone does not guarantee the numerical Earth result: the usual solar adiabatic assumption is also needed. These are the stipulated two-flavor parameters, not a precision prediction for the measured three-flavor solar spectrum.
