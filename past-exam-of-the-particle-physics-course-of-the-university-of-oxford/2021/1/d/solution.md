<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At an [electron-positron collider](../../../../electron-positron-collider.md) tuned to the [charmonium](../../../../charmonium.md) [resonance](../../../../resonance.md), select the mixed leptonic [tau lepton](../../../../tau-particle.md) decays

$$
\tau^-\to e^-\bar\nu_e\nu_\tau,\qquad \tau^+\to\mu^+\nu_\mu\bar\nu_\tau,
$$

and their charge conjugates. Require exactly two well-reconstructed oppositely charged tracks, one identified [electron](../../../../electron.md) and one identified [muon](../../../../muon.md), little extra visible activity, visible [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) below the beam total, and significant [missing momentum](../../../../missing-momentum.md) pointing into the instrumented region. The multiple [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md) prevent full reconstruction of each [tau lepton](../../../../tau-particle.md), but the known initial [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md), lepton spectra and missing [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) support a statistical signal extraction. Off-resonance data constrain nonresonant [electron-positron annihilation](../../../../electron-positron-annihilation.md) into a [tau lepton](../../../../tau-particle.md) pair; that continuum is present at both beam [energies](../../../../energy-split.md).[Event selection using mixed electron-muon tau decays and continuum subtraction](../../../../first-measurement-of-the-branching-fraction-of-the-decay-psi-2s-to-tau-pairs-by-the-bes-collaboration.md)

A resonant [muon](../../../../muon.md) pair is one possible detector background: one [muon](../../../../muon.md) can be misidentified as an [electron](../../../../electron.md), while radiation escaping along the beam or a badly measured track produces apparent [missing energy](../../../../missing-energy.md). Particle-identification requirements and rejection of almost back-to-back tracks near the beam [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) suppress it. This background needs detector mismeasurement; an ideal measurement would distinguish it.

Above the open-charm threshold, $\psi(3770)\to D\bar D$ supplies an additional resonant background. Semileptonic decays of the two [D mesons](../../../../d-meson.md) can give an [electron](../../../../electron.md), a [muon](../../../../muon.md) and [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md); unreconstructed hadrons let some events pass the selection. This on-shell [D meson](../../../../d-meson.md)-pair background is kinematically forbidden at $\psi(3686)$.
