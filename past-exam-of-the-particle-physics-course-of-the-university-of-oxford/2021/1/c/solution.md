<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Assume the [charmonium](../../../../charmonium.md) state annihilates through a virtual [photon](../../../../photon.md), with [lepton universality](../../../../lepton-universality.md) and negligible higher-order corrections. For a [vector meson](../../../../vector-meson.md) of [mass](../../../../mass.md) $M$, the [partial decay width](../../../../partial-decay-width.md) into a [charged lepton](../../../../charged-lepton.md) pair contains both the [relativistic two-body decay](../../../../relativistic-two-body-decay.md) factor $\beta_\ell$ and a vector-current [spin](../../../../spin.md) factor:

$$
\Gamma_{\ell\ell}=C\left(1+\frac{2m_\ell^2}{M^2}\right)\sqrt{1-\frac{4m_\ell^2}{M^2}},\qquad
\beta_\ell=\sqrt{1-4m_\ell^2/M^2}.
$$

[PDF p. 3, Eqs. (1)–(2): vector-current lepton-mass correction](../../../../first-measurement-of-the-branching-fraction-of-the-decay-psi-2s-to-tau-pairs-by-the-bes-collaboration.md) For example, contracting the vector polarization sum with the [Dirac trace](../../../../gamma-matrix-trace.md) for the two final [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) gives a factor proportional to $M^2+2m_\ell^2$, explaining why retaining only $\beta_\ell$ is insufficient. Using $m_\tau=1.777\ \mathrm{GeV}$, $m_\mu=0.1057\ \mathrm{GeV}$ and $M=3.686\ \mathrm{GeV}$ gives

$$
\frac{\Gamma_{\tau\tau}}{\Gamma_{\mu\mu}}\simeq0.388,\qquad
\boxed{\mathcal B_{\tau\tau}\simeq0.008\times0.388=3.10\times10^{-3}=0.31\%.}
$$

The supplied total [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) also gives $\Gamma_{\mu\mu}=2.352\ \mathrm{keV}$ and $\Gamma_{\tau\tau}\simeq0.91\ \mathrm{keV}$. It cancels from the [branching fraction](../../../../branching-fraction.md) ratio.

The decisive change at $3.770\ \mathrm{GeV}$ is crossing the open-charm [D meson](../../../../d-meson.md)-pair threshold, approximately $3.73\ \mathrm{GeV}$. The [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md) can now create a light [quark](../../../../quark.md)-[antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) pair and rearrange $c\bar c$ into $D\bar D$, without annihilating the heavy pair. This increases the total [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) from hundreds of keV to tens of MeV. The [leptonic decay](../../../../leptonic-decay.md) [partial decay width](../../../../partial-decay-width.md) is additionally smaller because $\psi(3770)$ is predominantly a $1\,{}^3D_1$ state, whereas $\psi(3686)$ is predominantly $2\,{}^3S_1$; the short-distance annihilation overlap is suppressed for nonzero [orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md). A roughly hundredfold increase in total [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) together with an order-ten reduction in leptonic [partial decay width](../../../../partial-decay-width.md) explains the order-thousand reduction in [branching fraction](../../../../branching-fraction.md). Attributing all three orders of magnitude to the threshold alone would miss the second effect.[Charmonium listings: total and leptonic widths of psi(2S) and psi(3770)](../../../../review-of-particle-physics-by-particle-data-group-2022.md)
