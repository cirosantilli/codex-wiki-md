<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The three structures are the radiative cascades

$$
\psi(2S)\to\gamma_l\chi_{cJ},\qquad \chi_{cJ}\to\gamma_h J/\psi,\qquad J=0,1,2.
$$

In the [quark model](../../../../quark-model.md), the [J/psi meson](../../../../j-psi-meson.md) and $\psi(2S)$ are respectively $1\,{}^3S_1$ and $2\,{}^3S_1$ [charmonium](../../../../charmonium.md) states. Thus $L=0$, $S=1$, $J=1$, and [meson parity and charge conjugation](../../../../meson-parity-and-charge-conjugation.md) gives $J^{PC}=1^{--}$. The intermediate [chi c mesons](../../../../chi-c-meson.md) are $1\,{}^3P_J$: $L=1$, $S=1$, and [addition of angular momentum](../../../../addition-of-angular-momentum.md) permits $J=0,1,2$. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\chi_{c0}:0^{++},\qquad \chi_{c1}:1^{++},\qquad \chi_{c2}:2^{++}.}
$$

These are [electric dipole transitions](../../../../electric-dipole-transition.md), changing $L$ by one and preserving $S$ to leading order. A singlet $1\,{}^1P_1$ state has $J^{PC}=1^{+-}$ and is not another member of these spin-triplet cascades.

The primary [photon](../../../../photon.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is $(M_{2S}^2-M_{\chi_J}^2)/(2M_{2S})$, about $261$, $171$ and $128\ \mathrm{MeV}$ for $J=0,1,2$. The secondary [photon](../../../../photon.md) remains more energetic even after its boost into the parent [rest frame](../../../../rest-frame.md). Hence $m^2(J/\psi\gamma_h)=M_{\chi_J}^2$: with this quantity on the vertical axis, each [resonance](../../../../resonance.md) gives a horizontal band. A finite [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) and detector [measurement resolution](../../../../measurement-resolution.md) broaden each band; photon ordering removes the mirrored duplication of an unordered [Dalitz plot](../../../../dalitz-plot.md).

The faint band beside the negative-slope diagonal boundary is $\psi(2S)\to J/\psi\,\pi^0$, followed by $\pi^0\to\gamma\gamma$. The [neutral pion](../../../../neutral-pion.md) is predominantly $(u\bar u-d\bar d)/\sqrt2$, a ${}^1S_0$ [pseudoscalar meson](../../../../pseudoscalar-meson.md) with $J^{PC}=0^{-+}$. This production transition violates [isospin](../../../../isospin.md), accounting for its small rate. To locate the band, set $X=m^2(J/\psi\gamma_l)$ and $Y=m^2(J/\psi\gamma_h)$. [Four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) gives

$$
\boxed{X+Y=M_{2S}^2+M_{J/\psi}^2-m_{\gamma\gamma}^2.}
$$

For the [neutral pion](../../../../neutral-pion.md), $m_{\gamma\gamma}^2=m_{\pi^0}^2$ is small, putting the line just inside the $m_{\gamma\gamma}^2=0$ boundary. The allowed $J/\psi\,\eta$ channel, with the [eta meson](../../../../eta-meson.md) also $0^{-+}$, produces a parallel line further inside, at $m_{\gamma\gamma}^2=m_\eta^2$. The positive-slope edge $X=Y$ instead comes from ordering the two [photon energies](../../../../photon-energy.md); proximity to that edge alone would not identify the two-photon state.
