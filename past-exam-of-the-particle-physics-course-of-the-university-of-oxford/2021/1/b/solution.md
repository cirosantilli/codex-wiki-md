<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Useful examples with no net [quark flavor](../../../../quark-flavor.md) are the [eta c meson](../../../../eta-c-meson.md) $\eta_c=c\bar c$ and the [eta b meson](../../../../eta-b-meson.md) $\eta_b=b\bar b$. Examples with nonzero [quark flavor](../../../../quark-flavor.md) are the [kaon](../../../../kaon.md) $K^+=u\bar s$ and the [D meson](../../../../d-meson.md) $D^+=c\bar d$. These are [pseudoscalar mesons](../../../../pseudoscalar-meson.md): in the lowest [quark model](../../../../quark-model.md) configuration the constituent [spins](../../../../spin.md) combine to $S=0$, the relative [orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) is $L=0$, and the total [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) and [parity](../../../../parity.md) are $J^P=0^-$. For the self-conjugate examples, [meson parity and charge conjugation](../../../../meson-parity-and-charge-conjugation.md) also gives $C=(-1)^{L+S}=+1$. Electrical neutrality alone would not imply zero net [quark flavor](../../../../quark-flavor.md); for example $K^0=d\bar s$ has strangeness.

Restoring [reduced Planck constant](../../../../reduced-planck-constant.md), [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) and [mean lifetime](../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md) satisfy $\Gamma\tau=\hbar$. Thus the representative numbers correspond to

$$
\tau_{20\,\mathrm{MeV}}=\frac{6.582\times10^{-22}\ \mathrm{MeV\,s}}{20\ \mathrm{MeV}}=3.29\times10^{-23}\ \mathrm s,\qquad
\Gamma_{1\,\mathrm{ps}}=6.582\times10^{-10}\ \mathrm{MeV}.
$$

The [mean lifetimes](../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md) differ by at least $3.0\times10^{10}$. Hidden-heavy-flavor [quarkonium](../../../../quarkonium.md) can undergo [quark](../../../../quark.md)-[antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) [annihilation](../../../../annihilation.md) into [gluons](../../../../gluon.md), followed by [hadronization](../../../../hadronization.md). A $0^{-+}$ color-singlet state can annihilate into two [gluons](../../../../gluon.md). A lightest open-flavor [meson](../../../../meson.md) cannot simply annihilate through the [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md), which conserves net [quark flavor](../../../../quark-flavor.md). With no lighter accessible final state carrying that flavor, it must undergo a [weak interaction](../../../../weak-interaction.md) changing a constituent's flavor. The small [weak interaction](../../../../weak-interaction.md) amplitude, [CKM matrix](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) factors and available [Lorentz-invariant phase space](../../../../lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) produce a much smaller [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md).

**Qualification.** A [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) of order tens of MeV is not a universal property of flavor-neutral [pseudoscalar mesons](../../../../pseudoscalar-meson.md). The [neutral pion](../../../../neutral-pion.md) has $\Gamma\simeq7.8\ \mathrm{eV}$ and decays predominantly by the [electromagnetic interaction](../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md), a concrete counterexample. Its [mass](../../../../mass.md) lies below the lightest allowed hadronic final state. The examples above illustrate the intended annihilation-versus-open-flavor comparison; the numerical scale depends on thresholds and selection rules.[Meson summary tables: neutral-pion width and heavy pseudoscalar properties](../../../../review-of-particle-physics-by-particle-data-group-2022.md)
