<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

In a [relativistic two-body decay](../../../../relativistic-two-body-decay.md), [four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) in the parent's [rest frame](../../../../rest-frame.md) requires $\mathbf p_1^*=-\mathbf p_2^*$ and

$$
M=\sqrt{p^{*2}+m_1^2}+\sqrt{p^{*2}+m_2^2}.
$$

For fixed daughter [masses](../../../../mass.md), this fixes a unique $p^*$, independent of direction. In the [Lorentz-invariant phase space](../../../../lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) integral, the [Dirac delta distribution](../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) enforcing [conservation of energy](../../../../conservation-of-energy.md) therefore performs the radial [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) integration. Only the [solid angle](../../../../solid-angle.md) remains to be integrated; the resulting $p^*$ can be taken outside that integral, even if the [matrix element](../../../../matrix-element.md) depends strongly on direction.

For three or more final particles, [four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md) generally leaves continuously variable daughter [energies](../../../../energy-split.md) and [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md), so the [decay rate](../../../../decay-width.md) includes integrations over them, or equivalently over independent [invariant masses](../../../../invariant-mass.md) in a [Dalitz plot](../../../../dalitz-plot.md). Integrating over a variable daughter [invariant mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) is also necessary when an unstable daughter is treated with a finite [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) rather than a fixed [mass](../../../../mass.md).
