<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use a clean [Z boson](../../../../z-boson.md) sample with final-state radiation, $Z\to e^+e^-\gamma$, and independently calibrated [electron](../../../../electron.md) [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md). For measured $P=p_{e^+}+p_{e^-}$ and measured [photon](../../../../photon.md) direction $\hat n_\gamma$, write $p_\gamma=E_\gamma(1,\hat n_\gamma)$. The known [Z boson](../../../../z-boson.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) supplies

$$
M_Z^2=P^2+2E_\gamma(P^0-\mathbf P\cdot\hat n_\gamma),\qquad
\boxed{E_\gamma^{\rm pred}=\frac{M_Z^2-m_{ee}^2}{2(P^0-\mathbf P\cdot\hat n_\gamma)}.}
$$

Comparing the reconstructed [photon energy](../../../../photon-energy.md) with this prediction constrains its scale versus [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), detector position and conversion status. In practice fit the $e^+e^-\gamma$ [invariant mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) distribution, allowing for the physical [Z boson](../../../../z-boson.md) [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md), detector response and backgrounds, instead of assigning every event exactly $M_Z$. Select final-state-radiation topologies: an initial-state-radiation [photon](../../../../photon.md) is not part of the [Z boson](../../../../z-boson.md) decay and generally does not satisfy the same mass constraint. The calibration applies over the populated [photon energy](../../../../photon-energy.md) range; extrapolation to still higher [energies](../../../../energy-split.md) needs a validated response model.
