<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Assume unpolarized beams, negligible [electron](../../../../electron.md) and [muon](../../../../muon.md) [masses](../../../../mass.md), a single [scattering s-channel](../../../../scattering-s-channel.md) spin-one mediator with equal left and right vector couplings, and leading-order [QED](../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md). [Z boson](../../../../z-boson.md) exchange and radiative corrections are omitted. The angular factors are the [Wigner D-matrix](../../../../wigner-d-matrix.md) elements $d^1_{mm'}(\theta^\star)$, with $m,m'=\pm1$. Up to common phases and normalization they are $(1+\cos\theta^\star)$ for equal pair-spin projections and $(1-\cos\theta^\star)$ for opposite projections. The four orthogonal [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) channels add incoherently, giving

$$
2(1+\cos\theta^\star)^2+2(1-\cos\theta^\star)^2=4(1+\cos^2\theta^\star).
$$

With $s$ the squared [center-of-mass energy](../../../../center-of-mass-energy.md), the normalized [differential scattering cross-sections](../../../../differential-scattering-cross-section.md) are

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega}=\frac{\alpha^2}{4s}(1+\cos^2\theta^\star),\qquad
\frac{d\sigma}{d\theta^\star}=\frac{\pi\alpha^2}{2s}\sin\theta^\star(1+\cos^2\theta^\star).}
$$

The second expression includes the [solid angle](../../../../solid-angle.md) Jacobian: a distribution per angle is different from one per $\cos\theta^\star$. Integration gives $\sigma=4\pi\alpha^2/(3s)$.

[Parity](../../../../parity.md) reverses all [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) but leaves the [spin](../../../../spin.md) axial vector unchanged, so it interchanges $L\leftrightarrow R$. The $LR\to LR$ and $RL\to RL$ amplitudes have equal magnitudes, as do $LR\to RL$ and $RL\to LR$. Thus every configuration has the same rate as its parity transform, proving [parity conservation](../../../../parity-conservation.md). The summed distribution is also forward-backward symmetric. Strictly, [parity](../../../../parity.md) reverses both the beam and outgoing [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md), leaving their relative angle unchanged; forward-backward reflection by itself is not the definition of [parity](../../../../parity.md).
