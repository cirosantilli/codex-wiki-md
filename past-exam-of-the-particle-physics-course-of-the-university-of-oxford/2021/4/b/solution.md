<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For a massless [fermion](../../../../fermion.md), [chirality](../../../../chirality-physics.md) is fixed by [helicity](../../../../helicity.md). For a massless [antifermion](../../../../antifermion.md), the field-chirality label is opposite to its physical [helicity](../../../../helicity.md): a left-chiral field creates negative-helicity particles and positive-helicity antiparticles. At $E\gg m$, wrong-chirality amplitudes are suppressed by $m/E$, so chirality-preserving vector vertices conserve [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) along a physical fermion line up to these corrections.

For [electron-positron annihilation into a muon pair](../../../../electron-positron-annihilation-into-a-muon-pair.md) through a vector mediator, each annihilating or created pair therefore has opposite physical [helicities](../../../../helicity.md). Writing each pair in the order (negative particle, positive particle), the nonzero possibilities are

$$
\boxed{(L,R)_e\to(L,R)_\mu,\quad(L,R)_e\to(R,L)_\mu,\quad(R,L)_e\to(L,R)_\mu,\quad(R,L)_e\to(R,L)_\mu.}
$$

Here $L,R$ refer to physical [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) along each particle's own [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md), not to the sign of its spin projected on a single common axis. The incoming pair has total spin projection $m=\pm1$ along the [electron](../../../../electron.md) beam; the outgoing pair has $m'=\pm1$ along the [muon](../../../../muon.md) direction. Same-helicity pairs vanish in the strictly massless vector-current limit.
