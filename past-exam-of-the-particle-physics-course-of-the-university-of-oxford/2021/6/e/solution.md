<h1 id="6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Tag an energetic [initial-state radiation](../../../../initial-state-radiation.md) [photon](../../../../photon.md) in $e^+e^-\to\gamma Z^{(*)}\to\gamma+\text{invisible}$, observing a single-photon signature with [missing energy](../../../../missing-energy.md) and [missing momentum](../../../../missing-momentum.md). At known squared [center-of-mass energy](../../../../center-of-mass-energy.md) $s$, the leading single-[photon](../../../../photon.md) recoil [invariant mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) is

$$
\boxed{m_{\rm recoil}^2=(p_{e^+}+p_{e^-}-p_\gamma)^2=s-2\sqrt s\,E_\gamma.}
$$

Above the [Z boson](../../../../z-boson.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md), radiative-return events cluster at $m_{\rm recoil}\simeq M_Z$. At or near the pole the full off-shell recoil spectrum is fitted instead. Folding the [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](../../../../breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md) with the calculable [QED](../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md) radiation kernel gives a predicted tagged rate proportional to $\Gamma_e\Gamma_{\rm inv}$ for fixed total [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md). A fit with the known luminosity and visible line-shape parameters therefore determines $\Gamma_{\rm inv}$ directly from invisible events, rather than subtracting visible widths. Comparing identically selected radiative-return events with visible [Z boson](../../../../z-boson.md) decays can instead constrain $\Gamma_{\rm inv}/\Gamma_{\rm vis}$ while cancelling much of the luminosity normalization.

The detector needs efficient single-[photon](../../../../photon.md) triggering, good [photon energy](../../../../photon-energy.md) and angular resolution, reliable timing, and nearly hermetic charged-particle and [particle calorimeter](../../../../calorimeter-particle-physics.md) coverage. Forward vetoes reject radiative [Bhabha scattering](../../../../bhabha-scattering.md) with escaped [electrons](../../../../electron.md); additional-photon vetoes reject $\gamma\gamma$ events with a lost [photon](../../../../photon.md). Require sufficient transverse [photon momentum](../../../../photon-momentum.md) that a recoiling visible system could not all disappear down a beam hole. Timing rejects cosmic rays and beam backgrounds. Detector inefficiencies and beam holes must be measured, since visible final states with lost particles imitate the signal. The irreducible $e^+e^-\to\nu_e\bar\nu_e\gamma$ contribution from $t$-channel [W boson](../../../../w-boson.md) exchange and its interference must be included when isolating the [Z boson](../../../../z-boson.md) invisible [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md).
