<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For a selected [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) sample, subtract backgrounds and correct for [detector acceptance](../../../../detector-acceptance.md), reconstruction and particle-identification efficiency, trigger efficiency and live time. Schematically,

$$
\boxed{\sigma_{\rm fid}=\frac{N_{\rm obs}-N_{\rm bg}}{\mathcal L_{\rm int}\,\epsilon},\qquad
\sigma_{\rm total}=\frac{N_{\rm obs}-N_{\rm bg}}{\mathcal L_{\rm int}\,A\epsilon}.}
$$

The first expression applies when the published signal definition is a specified fiducial region; the second additionally extrapolates by acceptance $A$. Resolution-induced migration across selections must be unfolded or included in a detector-response fit. [Tau lepton](../../../../tau-particle.md) reconstruction also needs the relevant [branching fractions](../../../../branching-fraction.md) if only selected decay modes are counted. Initial- and final-state [QED](../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md) radiation and beam-[energy](../../../../energy-split.md) spread change the measured line shape; extracting a Born-level or pole [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) requires the corresponding radiative corrections. In the [electron](../../../../electron.md) channel, [Bhabha scattering](../../../../bhabha-scattering.md) also has a non-annihilation $t$-channel contribution that must be treated.

For [quarks](../../../../quark.md), [hadronization](../../../../hadronization.md) means the detector observes hadrons and [jets](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md), rather than free [quarks](../../../../quark.md). Correct the selection and any flavor tagging using fragmentation and decay modeling, including losses to heavy-hadron decays with [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md). In comparison with a pointlike [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md), also include the three [color charges](../../../../color-charge.md) per [quark flavor](../../../../quark-flavor.md) and [QCD](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) corrections, schematically $N_c[1+\alpha_s/\pi+\cdots]$ with $N_c=3$ at high [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). The color multiplicity is a physical production factor, not an extra factor by which to divide an already efficiency-corrected inclusive hadronic event count.
