<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Assume approximate [charge symmetry of nucleon parton distributions](../../../../charge-symmetry-of-nucleon-parton-distributions.md), the interchange of [up quarks](../../../../up-quark.md) and [down quarks](../../../../down-quark.md) under the [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) rotation carrying the [proton](../../../../proton.md) into the [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md):

$$
u^n=d^p,\quad d^n=u^p,\quad \bar u^n=\bar d^p,\quad \bar d^n=\bar u^p,\quad s^n=s^p,\quad\bar s^n=\bar s^p.
$$

Corrections from the [up quark](../../../../up-quark.md)-[down quark](../../../../down-quark.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) difference and the [electromagnetic interaction](../../../../electromagnetic-interaction.md) are neglected. With the proton-only shorthand in [d](../d.md),

$$
\boxed{F_2^p=\frac{x}{9}(4U+D+S),\qquad F_2^n=\frac{x}{9}(4D+U+S).}
$$

If charm is active, add the same $4x(c^p+\bar c^p)/9$ to each; heavier flavors are treated similarly. These formulae use the same $Q^2$ and factorization convention for all [parton distribution functions](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md).
