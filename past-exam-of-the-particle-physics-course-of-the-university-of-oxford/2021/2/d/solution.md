<h1 id="2/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

This labelled part supplies a definition rather than a separate task. Its notation is interpreted as an incoherent charge-squared sum: the [photon](../../../../photon.md) couples to a [quark](../../../../quark.md) with [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) $e e_q$, and the squared [scattering amplitude](../../../../scattering-amplitude.md) weights its contribution by $e_q^2$. To make the [antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) convention explicit, let

$$
U=u^p+\bar u^p,\qquad D=d^p+\bar d^p,\qquad S=s^p+\bar s^p.
$$

Then, restricting the displayed example to three active light [quark flavors](../../../../quark-flavor.md), the [deep-inelastic structure function](../../../../deep-inelastic-structure-function.md) is

$$
\boxed{F_2^p=x\left[\frac49U+\frac19(D+S)\right].}
$$

Thus the source's sum must either include [antiquarks](../../../../antiquark.md) as separate [partons](../../../../parton.md) or define each $q^p$ to include its antiquark distribution. It must not silently omit the [sea quarks](../../../../sea-quark.md), especially in the small-$x$ limit used below.
