<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With the [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md) in [a](../a.md), $q^2=-2EE'(1-\cos\theta)$, $p_2\cdot q=m_p(E-E')$, and $p_2\cdot p_1=m_pE$. Thus the measured beam [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $E$, scattered [electron](../../../../electron.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $E'$ and [scattering angle](../../../../scattering-angle.md) $\theta$ determine

$$
\boxed{Q^2=4EE'\sin^2\frac\theta2,\qquad y=1-\frac{E'}E,\qquad x=\frac{2EE'\sin^2(\theta/2)}{m_p(E-E')}.}
$$

Here $Q^2$ measures the spacelike [momentum transfer](../../../../momentum-transfer.md), $y$ is the fractional [energy transfer](../../../../energy-transfer.md), and the [Bjorken scaling variable](../../../../bjorken-scaling-variable.md) $x$ is the struck [parton](../../../../parton.md)'s longitudinal [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) fraction in the leading collinear description. The hadronic [invariant mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md) satisfies $W^2=(p_2+q)^2=m_p^2+Q^2(1/x-1)$; large $Q^2$ and $W^2$ select [deep inelastic scattering](../../../../deep-inelastic-scattering.md).

A beam spectrometer measures $E$ before a thin stationary hydrogen target. Tracking layers on both sides of a magnetic spectrometer measure the outgoing [electron](../../../../electron.md) direction, charge and curvature; an [electromagnetic calorimeter](../../../../electromagnetic-calorimeter.md) measures its [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). Matching the track to its [particle shower](../../../../particle-shower.md), with downstream [muon](../../../../muon.md) rejection where useful, separates the scattered [electron](../../../../electron.md) from hadronic tracks. A downstream hadronic detector measures the hadronic activity and supports event-quality checks. The sketch in [a](../a.md) shows these separate measurements.

The scattered [electron](../../../../electron.md) provides a clean, single-particle reconstruction. Reconstructing the entire hadronic system requires summing many charged and neutral fragments, correcting unequal [particle calorimeter](../../../../calorimeter-particle-physics.md) response and losses through beam holes, and sometimes accounting for escaping [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md). Thus the leptonic definition is experimentally convenient. It is not a fundamental requirement of [Lorentz invariance](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md): exact reconstruction of $p_X$ gives the same $q=p_X-p_2$, and hadronic or combined reconstructions can be preferable in some kinematic regions.
