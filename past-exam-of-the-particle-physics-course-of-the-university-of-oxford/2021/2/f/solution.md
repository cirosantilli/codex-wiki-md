<h1 id="2/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

Subtracting the [deep-inelastic structure functions](../../../../deep-inelastic-structure-function.md) in [e](../e.md) gives

$$
F_2^p-F_2^n=\frac{x}{3}(U-D).
$$

Thus at moderate and large $x$, the charge-weighted [up quark](../../../../up-quark.md) content of the [proton](../../../../proton.md) exceeds its [down quark](../../../../down-quark.md) content. In the [valence quark](../../../../valence-quark.md) dominated regime this is consistent with the $uud$ valence structure, compared with $udd$ for the [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md). The larger $2/3$ [up quark](../../../../up-quark.md) [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) makes the [proton](../../../../proton.md) scatter more strongly. This observation alone does not establish $u^p(x)=2d^p(x)$ pointwise: the valence-number sum rules constrain integrals, not every $x$ bin.

If the small-$x$ observation means equality to good _relative_ accuracy, it supports dominance by approximately charge-symmetric [sea quarks](../../../../sea-quark.md), with $U\simeq D$ and negligible relative valence excess. [Gluon splitting](../../../../gluon-splitting.md) creates [quark](../../../../quark.md)-[antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) pairs, and the leading [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md) treats light [quark flavors](../../../../quark-flavor.md) similarly. It does not follow that every [sea quark](../../../../sea-quark.md) flavor has exactly the same distribution at every $x$.

**Logical qualification.** Equality of the limiting values alone is weaker than equality of their ratio. For example $U=2$, $D=1$, $S=0$ near $x=0$ gives $F_2^p=x$ and $F_2^n=2x/3$: both tend to zero, although $U\ne D$ and $F_2^n/F_2^p=2/3$. The usual inference about a symmetric sea needs $F_2^n/F_2^p\to1$ or equivalent information about relative differences, rather than merely coincident zero limits.
