<h1 id="4/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

A charged [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) in a [semileptonic decay](../../../../semileptonic-decay.md) directly identifies the decaying charm [quark flavour](../../../../quark-flavor.md): ordinarily $D^0\to K^-\mu^+\nu_\mu$ and $\bar D^0\to K^+\mu^-\bar\nu_\mu$. With an independently tagged initial [quark flavour](../../../../quark-flavor.md), the wrong-sign charged [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) requires [neutral meson mixing](../../../../neutral-meson-mixing.md), rather than a competing doubly [Cabibbo suppressed](../../../../cabibbo-suppression.md) amplitude. Its leading time dependence is $e^{-\tau}(x^2+y^2)\tau^2/4$, which looks theoretically cleaner.

That method measures only the quadratic combination $x^2+y^2$ at leading order, with no direct access to their signs. The missing [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) also worsens kinematic and decay-time reconstruction. The fully reconstructed $K\pi$ channel has substantial yield, a clear invariant-[mass](../../../../mass.md) peak and a well-measured decay [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md). Most significantly, [quantum interference](../../../../quantum-interference.md) with the small direct amplitude produces a term linear in $x$ and $y$, enhancing sensitivity to small mixing. Its cost is the unknown strong phase $\delta$, so separating $x$ and $y$ needs further channels or an independent strong-phase measurement. Neither one unqualified rate measurement determines both parameters by itself.
