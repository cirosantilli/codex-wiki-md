<h1 id="4/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

In [neutral meson mixing](../../../../neutral-meson-mixing.md), the [mass eigenstates](../../../../mass-eigenstate.md) and [flavor eigenstates](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) differ. A state prepared as $D^0=c\bar u$ develops a $\bar D^0=u\bar c$ component because the effective [Hamiltonian operator](../../../../hamiltonian-quantum-mechanics.md) has off-diagonal [mass](../../../../mass.md) and [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) entries. In the [CP symmetry](../../../../cp-symmetry.md) limit implicit in the source, the propagating states are equal superpositions, with average [mass](../../../../mass.md) $M=(M_1+M_2)/2$ and average [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md) $\Gamma=(\Gamma_1+\Gamma_2)/2$.

To retain the source's **positive** $i\sin$ convention for $g_-$, take $M_2-M_1=\Delta M$ and $\Gamma_2-\Gamma_1=\Delta\Gamma$, with $g_\pm=(e_1\pm e_2)/2$, where $e_j=\exp(-iM_jt-\Gamma_jt/2)$. Then $x=\Delta M/\Gamma$ and $y=\Delta\Gamma/(2\Gamma)$ reproduce the given functions. Reversing an eigenstate label changes signs in intermediate formulae; it must also change the phase conventions consistently.

For equal widths, the mixed probability is proportional to $e^{-\Gamma t}\sin^2(\Delta M t/2)$. Thus the angular [angular frequency](../../../../angular-frequency.md) is $|\Delta M|$ in natural units, or $|\Delta M|/\hbar$ with units restored; the period is $2\pi\hbar/|\Delta M|$. A nonzero difference of [decay widths](../../../../decay-width.md) adds hyperbolic time dependence.

The ordinary neutral flavour-carrying [mesons](../../../../meson.md) that mix are $K^0$, $D^0$, $B_d^0$ and $B_s^0$ with their [antiparticles](../../../../antiparticle.md). A self-conjugate $\pi^0$ has no distinct antiparticle flavour to oscillate into. There are no long-lived top-flavoured [mesons](../../../../meson.md), since the [top quark](../../../../top-quark.md) decays before [hadronization](../../../../hadronization.md).
