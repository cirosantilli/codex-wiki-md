<h1 id="3/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

Represent a [quark](../../../../quark.md)-[antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) state by a matrix $F$ with $q_i\bar q_j\leftrightarrow E_{ij}$. Under [flavor symmetry](../../../../flavor-symmetry.md), $F\mapsto UFU^\dagger$, so an infinitesimal generator acts by $[T,F]$. The minus sign in this [commutator](../../../../commutator.md) is the [antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) transformation law; acting on the [quark](../../../../quark.md) alone would miss states.

Start from $\pi^+=E_{ud}$. Three useful first steps are

$$
[I_-,E_{ud}]=E_{dd}-E_{uu}=-\sqrt2\,\pi^0,\quad [U_+,E_{ud}]=-E_{us}=-K^+,\quad [V_-,E_{ud}]=E_{sd}=\bar K^0.
$$

Further actions give $[I_-,E_{us}]=E_{ds}=K^0$, $[I_-,E_{sd}]=-E_{su}=-K^-$ and $[I_-,\pi^0]=\sqrt2E_{du}=\sqrt2\pi^-$. Overall phases are conventional. Finally

$$
[V_-,E_{us}]=E_{ss}-E_{uu}=-\frac{\pi^0}{\sqrt2}-\sqrt{\frac32}\,\eta_8.
$$

Thus the second neutral octet state is obtained by removing the known $\pi^0$ component. Equivalently it is the normalized diagonal state orthogonal to both $\pi^0$ and the singlet. The full [meson octet](../../../../meson-octet.md) is

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\pi^+&=u\bar d,&\pi^-&=d\bar u,&\pi^0&=(u\bar u-d\bar d)/\sqrt2,\\
K^+&=u\bar s,&K^0&=d\bar s,&K^-&=s\bar u,&\bar K^0&=s\bar d,\\
\eta_8&=(u\bar u+d\bar d-2s\bar s)/\sqrt6.
\end{aligned}}
$$

Their flavour matrices are traceless; the independent singlet is $\eta_1=(u\bar u+d\bar d+s\bar s)/\sqrt3$. The source uses $\eta'$ for this ideal singlet. That notation is a basis convention here: the physical [eta and eta prime mesons](../../../../eta-and-eta-prime-mesons.md) is not an exactly pure singlet because physical neutral [pseudoscalar mesons](../../../../pseudoscalar-meson.md) mix.[Section 15.3, singlet-octet wave functions and physical pseudoscalar mixing](../../../../quark-model-in-the-2024-review-of-particle-physics.md)
