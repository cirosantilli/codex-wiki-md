<h1 id="3/5/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [5](../5.md)

The neutral [vector mesons](../../../../vector-meson.md) with [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) zero have the same conserved quantum numbers, so an off-diagonal [mass matrix](../../../../mass-matrix.md) can mix the singlet $V_1=(u\bar u+d\bar d+s\bar s)/\sqrt3$ and octet $V_8=(u\bar u+d\bar d-2s\bar s)/\sqrt6$. Choose phases such that

$$
\omega=\cos\theta\,V_1+\sin\theta\,V_8,\qquad \phi=\sin\theta\,V_1-\cos\theta\,V_8.
$$

At the ideal [mixing angle](../../../../mixing-angle.md) $\tan\theta=1/\sqrt2$, or $\theta\simeq35.3^\circ$, these become

$$
\boxed{\omega\simeq(u\bar u+d\bar d)/\sqrt2,\qquad\phi\simeq s\bar s}.
$$

The overall sign of $\phi$ is unobservable. The [omega meson](../../../../omega-meson.md) near $782\ \mathrm{MeV}$ decays predominantly to three [pions](../../../../pion.md), whereas the [phi meson](../../../../phi-meson.md) near $1020\ \mathrm{MeV}$ strongly favours [kaon](../../../../kaon.md)-[antikaon](../../../../antikaon.md) channels when they are open. Suppression of its nonstrange channels agrees with the [OZI rule](../../../../ozi-rule.md) and a predominantly strange state. The [omega meson](../../../../omega-meson.md) is below the two-[kaon](../../../../kaon.md) threshold, so its absence of that channel alone would not establish the [mixing angle](../../../../mixing-angle.md); masses and the broader decay pattern provide the evidence together.[Section 15.3, vector mixing near the ideal angle and identification of omega and phi](../../../../quark-model-in-the-2024-review-of-particle-physics.md)
