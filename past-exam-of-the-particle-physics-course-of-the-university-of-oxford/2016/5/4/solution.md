<h1 id="5/4/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [4](../4.md)

The [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) coupling $1\otimes1\to1$, with third component $-1$, has the normalized state

$$
|M^-\rangle=\frac{|\rho^0\pi^-\rangle-|\rho^-\pi^0\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

The two [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](../../../../clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md) have equal squared magnitude. Neglecting electromagnetic effects and charged-neutral [mass](../../../../mass.md) differences, the two [rho meson](../../../../rho-meson.md) charge channels have equal rates. Their subsequent strong decays are $\rho^0\to\pi^+\pi^-$ and $\rho^-\to\pi^-\pi^0$, with nearly unit probabilities. Consequently

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Gamma(M^-\to\pi^-\pi^+\pi^-)}{\Gamma(M^-\to\pi^-\pi^0\pi^0)}=1}
$$

in the ideal [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) model.

Each final state contains one pair of identical [pions](../../../../pion.md). Summing the alternative resonant-pair assignments coherently, with the corresponding identical-particle [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md) factors, does not generate an extra factor of two for just one channel. In the narrow-[rho meson](../../../../rho-meson.md) approximation the equality is already evident from equal $\rho\pi$ rates; the consistent [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) amplitudes extend it to the integrated three-body distributions.

The tabulated ratio $9.02/9.30=0.970$ is close to this expectation but is not exactly one. Treating the printed errors as independent gives an uncertainty about $0.012$, so a percent-level comparison requires [isospin breaking](../../../../isospin-breaking.md), radiative corrections, realistic resonant dynamics and experimental correlations. The idealized argument predicts the leading pattern rather than an exact identity for the rounded measurements.
