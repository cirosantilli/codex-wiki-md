<h1 id="5/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The local [Fermi interaction](../../../../fermi-interaction.md) gives a three-body [leptonic decay](../../../../leptonic-decay.md) width of the form

$$
\Gamma(l\to a\bar\nu_a\nu_l)=\frac{G_F^2m_l^5}{192\pi^3}f(z),\qquad z=\frac{m_a^2}{m_l^2},\qquad f(z)=1-8z+8z^3-z^4-12z^2\log z,
$$

at tree level with negligible [neutrino masses](../../../../neutrino-mass.md) and universal weak [coupling physics](../../../../coupling-constant-physics.md) values. The fifth power follows from the dimension of $G_F$ and the three-body [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md); $f$ results from integrating the [V-A interaction](../../../../v-a-interaction.md) [matrix element](../../../../matrix-element.md) over the reduced [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md) when the charged daughter has nonzero [mass](../../../../mass.md). It is more than a purely geometric volume correction because the spin-dependent [matrix element](../../../../matrix-element.md) is included.

As $z\to0$, $f(z)\to1$; increasing daughter [mass](../../../../mass.md) reduces the available [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and suppresses the rate. The largest ratio is $m_\mu/m_\tau$, so the $\tau\to\mu$ correction is visibly below one. The [electron](../../../../electron.md) is extremely light relative to either parent. Using the [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) [masses](../../../../mass.md) gives

$$
\boxed{f_{\tau\mu}\simeq0.9725601,\quad f_{\mu e}\simeq0.99981295,\quad f_{\tau e}\simeq0.99999934}.
$$

These reproduce the supplied factors to their stated accuracy. Radiative and finite-[W boson](../../../../w-boson.md) corrections are separate small corrections when a precision [lepton universality](../../../../lepton-universality.md) test is performed.
