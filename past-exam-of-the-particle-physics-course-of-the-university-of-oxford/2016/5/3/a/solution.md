<h1 id="5/3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Lepton universality](../../../../../lepton-universality.md) means that the weak [charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md) couples equally to the three charged [lepton](../../../../../lepton.md) families after accounting for [masses](../../../../../mass.md) and known radiative corrections. In the ratio of two [tau lepton](../../../../../tau-particle.md) [leptonic decay](../../../../../leptonic-decay.md) widths, the parent [coupling physics](../../../../../coupling-constant-physics.md), [mean lifetime](../../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md) and $m_\tau^5$ cancel:

$$
\frac{B_\mu}{B_e}=\frac{|g_\mu|^2}{|g_e|^2}\frac{f_{\tau\mu}}{f_{\tau e}}.
$$

The universal prediction is $B_\mu/B_e=0.97256/0.9999993=0.9725607$. The measured ratio is $17.41/17.83=0.97644$, giving

$$
\boxed{\frac{|g_\mu|}{|g_e|}=1.00199\pm0.00161}
$$

if the quoted errors are treated as independent. This is consistent with unity at about $1.2$ standard deviations. An actual precision fit must retain the measured covariance and the small radiative corrections; the rounded table alone does not supply those correlations.
