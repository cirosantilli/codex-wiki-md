<h1 id="5/3/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Assuming that the two listed [leptonic decay](../../../../../leptonic-decay.md) categories exhaust the leptonic modes, the inclusive hadronic [branching fraction](../../../../../branching-fraction.md) is $100-17.83-17.41=64.76\%$. Thus

$$
R_\tau=\frac{\Gamma(\tau\to\text{hadrons}\,\nu)}{\Gamma(\tau\to e\bar\nu_e\nu_\tau)}=\frac{64.76}{17.83}=3.632.
$$

At the simplest massless [quark](../../../../../quark.md) level, the [W boson](../../../../../w-boson.md) couples to $u\bar d$ and $u\bar s$ in three [color charge](../../../../../color-charge.md) states, so equal quark and [lepton](../../../../../lepton.md) weak [coupling physics](../../../../../coupling-constant-physics.md) values predict

$$
\boxed{R_\tau^{(0)}=N_c(|V_{ud}|^2+|V_{us}|^2)\simeq3.0024\simeq3}.
$$

This is a rough check of [color multiplicity in a decay width](../../../../../color-multiplicity-in-a-decay-width.md) and universal weak-current normalization. It is much less precise than the two-[lepton](../../../../../lepton.md) ratio because hadronic [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) and [electroweak interaction](../../../../../electroweak-interaction.md) corrections are sizeable: the data require a net correction factor near $3.632/3.0024\simeq1.21$. They must be included before extracting a quark-to-[lepton](../../../../../lepton.md) [coupling physics](../../../../../coupling-constant-physics.md) ratio. It would be incorrect to interpret the excess over three directly as a failure of weak universality. The displayed exclusive hadronic modes alone would underestimate the inclusive sum.
