<h1 id="5/3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) is the unitary change of basis between down-type [quark](../../../../../quark.md) [mass eigenstates](../../../../../mass-eigenstate.md) and the combinations appearing in the weak [charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md). In particular, $u\bar d$ and $u\bar s$ production carry $V_{ud}$ and $V_{us}$. Exclusive [tau lepton](../../../../../tau-particle.md) decays into a charged [pseudoscalar meson](../../../../../pseudoscalar-meson.md) give

$$
\frac{B(\tau\to K\nu)}{B(\tau\to\pi\nu)}=\frac{f_K^2|V_{us}|^2}{f_\pi^2|V_{ud}|^2}\frac{(1-m_K^2/m_\tau^2)^2}{(1-m_\pi^2/m_\tau^2)^2},
$$

up to small radiative corrections. The [pseudoscalar decay constants](../../../../../pseudoscalar-decay-constant.md) specify the vacuum-to-[meson](../../../../../meson.md) axial-current [matrix elements](../../../../../matrix-element.md), $\langle0|\bar q\gamma^\mu\gamma^5u|P(p)\rangle=if_Pp^\mu$.

The observed [branching fraction](../../../../../branching-fraction.md) ratio is $0.70/10.83=0.06464$, and the kinematic factor is approximately $0.8622$. Hence the directly determined combination is

$$
\boxed{\frac{f_K|V_{us}|}{f_\pi|V_{ud}|}\simeq0.2738}.
$$

With an **independent**, representative lattice input $f_K/f_\pi\simeq1.193$, this becomes $|V_{us}/V_{ud}|\simeq0.2295$, close to the source's rounded expectation $0.224/0.975=0.2297$. Conversely that expectation and the hadronic input predict a [branching fraction](../../../../../branching-fraction.md) ratio near $0.0648$. Without the decay-constant input the table does not isolate a pure [Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix](../../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) ratio. Setting $f_K=f_\pi$ would give approximately $0.0455$, demonstrating the importance of [flavor symmetry](../../../../../flavor-symmetry.md) breaking.[Sections 72.1–72.2, axial-current definition, radiative corrections and lattice decay-constant ratios](../../../../../leptonic-decays-of-charged-pseudoscalar-mesons-in-the-2024-review-of-particle-physics.md)
