<h1 id="6/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The [Dirac adjoint](../../../../dirac-adjoint.md) is $\bar u=u^\dagger\gamma^0$. Because $\gamma^5$ is [Hermitian](../../../../hermitian-operator.md) and anticommutes with $\gamma^0$,

$$
\overline{(1-\gamma^5)u_A}=u_A^\dagger(1-\gamma^5)\gamma^0=\bar u_A(1+\gamma^5).
$$

Anticommutation with $\gamma^\mu$ then gives

$$
\boxed{\overline{(1-\gamma^5)u_A}\gamma^\mu u_B=\bar u_A(1+\gamma^5)\gamma^\mu u_B=\bar u_A\gamma^\mu(1-\gamma^5)u_B}.
$$

Thus the left-chiral [charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) can be written with its [chiral projector](../../../../chiral-projector.md) acting on either external [Dirac spinor](../../../../dirac-spinor.md). More precisely, $\bar u_A\gamma^\mu P_Lu_B=\overline{P_Lu_A}\gamma^\mu P_Lu_B$, since the opposite projection vanishes. The [Dirac adjoint](../../../../dirac-adjoint.md) reverses the matrix projector, $\overline{P_Lu}=\bar uP_R$; overlooking this reversal would give a wrong zero. The source uses $1-\gamma^5=2P_L$, so no additional factor of two is introduced when moving that entire factor.
