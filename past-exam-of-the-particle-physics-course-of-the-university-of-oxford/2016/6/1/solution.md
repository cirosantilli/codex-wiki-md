<h1 id="6/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

A [projection operator](../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) satisfies $P^2=P$; an orthogonal [projection operator](../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) also has $P^\dagger=P$. It extracts a subspace while leaving already projected states unchanged. Since $(\gamma^5)^2=1$ and $(\gamma^5)^\dagger=\gamma^5$, the [chiral projectors](../../../../chiral-projector.md) are

$$
\boxed{P_L=\frac{1-\gamma^5}{2},\qquad P_R=\frac{1+\gamma^5}{2}},\qquad P_LP_R=0,\quad P_L+P_R=1.
$$

They separate the [Dirac spinor](../../../../dirac-spinor.md) into opposite [fermion chirality](../../../../chirality-physics.md) components. The [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) weak [charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) contains $\bar\psi\gamma^\mu P_L\psi$, selecting left-chiral particle fields and the corresponding right-helicity massless [antiparticles](../../../../antiparticle.md); right-chiral charged [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) are weak-isospin singlets. The [weak neutral current](../../../../neutral-current.md) can couple to both [fermion chirality](../../../../chirality-physics.md) components with different strengths. [Chirality](../../../../chirality-physics.md) and [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) should not be equated for a massive [fermion](../../../../fermion.md): a Lorentz boost can reverse its [helicity](../../../../helicity.md), while the field representation defining [chirality](../../../../chirality-physics.md) is a different notion.
