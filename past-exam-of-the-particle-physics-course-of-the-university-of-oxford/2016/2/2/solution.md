<h1 id="2/2/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [2](../2.md)

The relevant sequence is [electron capture](../../../../electron-capture.md) followed by nuclear [gamma decay](../../../../gamma-decay.md):

$$
{}^{152m}_{63}\mathrm{Eu}+e^-_K\longrightarrow{}^{152}_{62}\mathrm{Sm}^{*}+\nu_e,\qquad{}^{152}\mathrm{Sm}^{*}\longrightarrow{}^{152}\mathrm{Sm}+\gamma.
$$

The nuclear [spin angular momenta](../../../../spin.md) are $0\to1\to0$. The captured [K-shell electron](../../../../k-shell-electron.md) has total [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) $1/2$, so the initial atom-plus-[electron](../../../../electron.md) system cannot be treated as having zero [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md). The atomic vacancy also produces subsequent [X-rays](../../../../x-ray.md) or [Auger electrons](../../../../auger-electron.md); these are not the selected nuclear transition.

Choose the [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) along $+z$ and the recoil [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) along $-z$. For a negative-[helicity](../../../../helicity.md) [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md), $s_{\nu z}=-1/2$. With the two initial [electron spin](../../../../electron-spin.md) projections, [angular momentum conservation](../../../../conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) allows the daughter to have $m_z=0$ or $+1$. A [photon](../../../../photon.md) emitted exactly along the recoil direction is transverse, and the $1\to0$ transition selects the $m_z=+1$ component. Its [spin angular momentum](../../../../spin.md) points along $+z$, opposite to its [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md).

Thus in the selected collinear geometry the daughter and [photon](../../../../photon.md) have the same negative [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) as the [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md), while their [spin angular momenta](../../../../spin.md) point opposite to the [neutrino spin](../../../../neutrino-spin.md). Reversing all these [spin](../../../../spin.md) projections gives the positive-[helicity](../../../../helicity.md) alternative. In normalized notation,

$$
\boxed{h_\gamma=h_{\mathrm{Sm}^{*}}=h_\nu},\qquad h=\frac{\mathbf S\cdot\widehat{\mathbf p}}{s}.
$$

The actual eigenvalues of $\mathbf S\cdot\widehat{\mathbf p}$ are $\pm1/2$ for the [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) and $\pm1$ for the [photon](../../../../photon.md); the equality concerns their normalized signs.
