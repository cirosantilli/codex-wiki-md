<h1 id="2/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The nuclear [gamma ray](../../../../gamma-ray.md) can be absorbed by ground-state [samarium-152](../../../../samarium-152.md), excite the same level and be re-emitted: nuclear [nuclear resonance fluorescence](../../../../nuclear-resonance-fluorescence.md). Its [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) in the absorber rest frame must overlap the nuclear absorption line within the relevant natural and thermal broadening. Atomic radiation from refilling the [K shell](../../../../k-shell.md) does not have the required transition [energy](../../../../energy-split.md).

Let the excitation [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) be $E_0$ and the nuclear [mass](../../../../mass.md) be $M$. A free stationary emitter loses recoil [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) while a free stationary absorber requires recoil [energy](../../../../energy-split.md):

$$
E_{\mathrm{em}}\simeq E_0-\frac{E_0^2}{2Mc^2},\quad E_{\mathrm{abs}}\simeq E_0+\frac{E_0^2}{2Mc^2},\quad E_{\mathrm{abs}}-E_{\mathrm{em}}\simeq\frac{E_0^2}{Mc^2}.
$$

For $E_0\simeq0.96\ \mathrm{MeV}$ the gap is about $6.5\ \mathrm{eV}$. A [photon](../../../../photon.md) emitted along the daughter recoil receives a positive [Doppler shift](../../../../doppler-effect.md) of approximately $E_0p_\nu/(Mc)$, whereas one emitted opposite the recoil is redshifted. Emission before the daughter slows down therefore selects [photons](../../../../photon.md) roughly opposite to the [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md). Comparable [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) and [photon energies](../../../../photon-energy.md) nearly compensate the recoil mismatch; finite line widths and thermal motion permit overlap. Exact compensation need not hold for every atom, and this is not an assumption of recoil-free emission.

Magnetized iron supplies polarized [electrons](../../../../electron.md). Their [Compton scattering](../../../../compton-scattering.md) [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) depends on the relative [electron spin](../../../../electron-spin.md) and [photon helicity](../../../../photon-helicity.md), so the iron attenuates the two circular polarizations differently. Reverse the magnetization and measure the resonance-selected count asymmetry $A=(N_+-N_-)/(N_++N_-)$. With a calibrated analysing power $a$ and background correction, $A=aP_\gamma$ determines the sign and magnitude of the circular polarization. A [scintillation counter](../../../../scintillation-counter.md) such as NaI(Tl), coupled to a [photomultiplier](../../../../photomultiplier.md), determines the [gamma ray](../../../../gamma-ray.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) from calibrated pulse height; a [semiconductor detector](../../../../semiconductor-detector.md) could perform the same spectroscopic function.
