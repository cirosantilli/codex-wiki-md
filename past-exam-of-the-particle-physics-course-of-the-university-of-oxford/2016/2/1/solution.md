↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

An observable that reverses sign under [parity](../../../../parity.md) but has a nonzero expectation in a suitably prepared experiment tests [parity violation](../../../../parity-violation.md). A polar [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) reverses, whereas an axial [spin angular momentum](../../../../spin.md) does not. Thus $\mathbf J\cdot\mathbf p$ and [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) are parity-odd; an apparatus asymmetry must be separated from a physical asymmetry by reversing the preparation or analyser.

In the [Wu experiment](../../../../wu-experiment.md), polarized [cobalt-60](../../../../cobalt-60.md) nuclei preferentially emit [beta decay](../../../../beta-decay.md) [electrons](../../../../electron.md) in one direction relative to the nuclear [spin angular momentum](../../../../spin.md). The distribution contains $A\mathbf P\cdot\mathbf p_e/E_e$; its sign changes under spatial inversion. Reversing the nuclear polarization reverses the observed asymmetry.[Nuclear polarization and the beta angular asymmetry](../../../../experimental-test-of-parity-conservation-in-beta-decay-by-chien-shiung-wu-and-others.md)

In the [Goldhaber experiment](../../../../goldhaber-experiment.md), the sign of the circular polarization of selected [gamma rays](../../../../gamma-ray.md) determines the sign of the [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) [helicity](../../../../helicity.md). A preference for negative [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) is parity-odd because spatial inversion would produce the positive-[helicity](../../../../helicity.md) state. This provides a different experimental observable from the [electron](../../../../electron.md) angular distribution.
