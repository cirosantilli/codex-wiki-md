<h1 id="1/3/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [3](../3.md)

The [photon propagator](../../../../photon-propagator.md) gives approximately $d\sigma_{\mathrm{NC}}/dQ^2\propto1/(Q^2)^2$, whereas a massive [W boson](../../../../w-boson.md) gives $d\sigma_{\mathrm{CC}}/dQ^2\propto1/(Q^2+M_W^2)^2$. Up to different [coupling physics](../../../../coupling-constant-physics.md) values, [parton distribution functions](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md) and angular factors,

$$
\frac{\sigma_{\mathrm{CC}}}{\sigma_{\mathrm{NC}}}\simeq C(Q^2)\left(\frac{Q^2}{Q^2+M_W^2}\right)^2.
$$

Consequently the low-$Q^2$ [charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) suppression disappears as $Q^2$ approaches $M_W^2$. The source graph shows comparable [cross sections](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) at $Q^2$ of order $10^4\ \mathrm{GeV}^2$, so the relevant [mass](../../../../mass.md) scale is of order $10^2\ \mathrm{GeV}$.

A more quantitative, still rough reading uses the $e^-p$ ratio $R_1\simeq0.006$ near $q_1=300\ \mathrm{GeV}^2$ and $R_2\simeq0.17$ near $q_2=2000\ \mathrm{GeV}^2$, where $q_i$ denotes $Q^2$, not $Q$. If $C$ varies slowly over this interval, set $\kappa=\sqrt{R_2/R_1}\,q_1/q_2$. Then

$$
\kappa=\frac{q_1+M_W^2}{q_2+M_W^2},\qquad \boxed{M_W=\sqrt{\frac{\kappa q_2-q_1}{1-\kappa}}\simeq80\ \mathrm{GeV}}.
$$

The reading is compatible with roughly $70$–$100\ \mathrm{GeV}$; it is not a precision [mass](../../../../mass.md) determination. In particular, [Z boson](../../../../z-boson.md) exchange contributes to the [neutral current](../../../../neutral-current.md) at high $Q^2$, and the [parton distribution functions](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md) do not cancel exactly. Equality of two inclusive [cross sections](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) alone does not define $M_W$.
