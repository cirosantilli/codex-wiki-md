<h1 id="1/1/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

The [parton model](../../../../parton-model.md) treats a fast [proton](../../../../proton.md) as an ensemble of approximately collinear, nearly free [quarks](../../../../quark.md) and [antiquarks](../../../../antiquark.md). A hard [deep inelastic scattering](../../../../deep-inelastic-scattering.md) collision resolves one [parton](../../../../parton.md) on a time scale short compared with its binding motion. The probabilities are [parton distribution functions](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md); the simplest approximation neglects intrinsic transverse [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md), [quark masses](../../../../quark-mass.md) and the [proton](../../../../proton.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) in the hard collision. [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) introduces logarithmic violations of [Bjorken scaling](../../../../bjorken-scaling.md), so the [parton distribution functions](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md) depend on a resolution scale as well as a [parton momentum fraction](../../../../parton-momentum-fraction.md).

Write the incident [electron](../../../../electron.md) and [proton](../../../../proton.md) [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md) as $k,P$, the scattered [electron](../../../../electron.md) [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) as $k'$, and set $q=k-k'$. With the $(+---)$ [Minkowski metric](../../../../minkowski-metric.md), the [Bjorken scaling variable](../../../../bjorken-scaling-variable.md) and the inelasticity are

$$
Q^2=-q^2>0,\qquad y=\frac{P\cdot q}{P\cdot k},\qquad x_B=\frac{Q^2}{2P\cdot q}=\frac{Q^2}{sy},\qquad s\simeq4E_eE_p.
$$

An incident [parton](../../../../parton.md) with [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) $\xi P$ emerges on shell, giving $(\xi P+q)^2\simeq2\xi P\cdot q-Q^2=0$. Thus $\xi=x_B$: the observable [Bjorken scaling variable](../../../../bjorken-scaling-variable.md) measures the struck [parton momentum fraction](../../../../parton-momentum-fraction.md) in this approximation.

For clarity, measure the [scattering angle](../../../../scattering-angle.md) $\vartheta$ from the **incoming electron direction**, and denote the outgoing [electron](../../../../electron.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) by $E'_e$. Direct [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md) products give

$$
Q^2=4E_eE'_e\sin^2\frac{\vartheta}{2},\qquad y=1-\frac{E'_e}{E_e}\cos^2\frac{\vartheta}{2},\qquad \boxed{x_B=\frac{E_eE'_e\sin^2(\vartheta/2)}{E_eE_p\left[1-(E'_e/E_e)\cos^2(\vartheta/2)\right]}}.
$$

The expression uses the known beam [energies](../../../../energy-split.md) and two measured quantities. An [electromagnetic calorimeter](../../../../electromagnetic-calorimeter.md) measures $E'_e$; a [tracking detector](../../../../tracking-detector.md), the collision [interaction vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) and the position of the [electromagnetic shower](../../../../electromagnetic-shower.md) determine $\vartheta$. Coverage close to the incoming [electron](../../../../electron.md) direction matters at small $Q^2$. A [hadronic calorimeter](../../../../hadronic-calorimeter.md) supplies an independent consistency check and rejects backgrounds. If the angle is instead measured from the incoming [proton](../../../../proton.md), its sine and cosine half-angle factors interchange.
