<h1 id="6/d/v/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [V](../v.md)

A [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) signal is correct for the reactor's **antineutrinos**, through [inverse beta decay](../../../../../inverse-beta-decay.md),

$$
\bar\nu_e+p\to e^++n,\qquad E_{\bar\nu,\rm th}\simeq1.806\ \mathrm{MeV}.
$$

A true [electron neutrino](../../../../../electron-neutrino.md) [charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md) interaction instead produces an [electron](../../../../../electron.md), for example $\nu_e+n\to e^-+p$. This corrects the source's loose use of “neutrino” for the reactor beam.

After slowing, the [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) annihilates with an [electron](../../../../../electron.md), generally producing two $511$ keV [photons](../../../../../photon.md). The prompt visible [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) includes both the [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) kinetic [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and annihilation [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md); neglecting the small [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) recoil,

$$
E_{\rm prompt}\simeq T_{e^+}+2m_e\simeq E_{\bar\nu}-0.782\ \mathrm{MeV}.
$$

A delayed [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) capture supplies a powerful coincidence tag. Detecting only two annihilation [photons](../../../../../photon.md) discards useful prompt [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) and does not uniquely identify the [charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md) event.
