<h1 id="6/d/vi/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vi](../vi.md)

The relevant resolution condition is on the [oscillation phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md), not solely on which [mixing angle](../../../../../mixing-angle.md) is being measured. Since $\Delta\propto L/E$,

$$
\sigma_\Delta\simeq|\Delta|\frac{\sigma_E}{E}.
$$

At the same medium baseline, the fast $\theta_{13}$-dependent ripples have phases about $37.5$ times larger than the slow $\theta_{12}$ component. Resolving those ripples therefore requires a much better fractional [energy resolution](../../../../../energy-resolution.md). At 47 km, $|\Delta_{23}|\simeq59.5$; avoiding substantial phase smearing requires a resolution appreciably better than $1/59.5\simeq1.7\%$.

However, a short-baseline $\theta_{13}$ experiment near 1.24 km and a $\theta_{12}$ experiment near 46.5 km each operate near their relevant first maximum, $\Delta\simeq\pi/2$. Their intrinsic fractional phase-resolution requirements are then comparable. The smaller $\theta_{13}$ disappearance amplitude demands careful relative calibration and background control, but it does not by itself impose a universally finer [energy resolution](../../../../../energy-resolution.md). The proposal is conditional on baseline and analysis, rather than a general requirement.
