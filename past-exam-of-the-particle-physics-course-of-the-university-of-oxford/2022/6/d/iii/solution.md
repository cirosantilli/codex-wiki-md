<h1 id="6/d/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

A fission reactor supplies predominantly [electron antineutrinos](../../../../../electron-antineutrino.md), with typical [energies](../../../../../energy-split.md) of a few MeV. Even if they oscillate to [muon antineutrinos](../../../../../muon-antineutrino.md), producing a real [muon](../../../../../muon.md) through a [charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md) requires roughly the [muon](../../../../../muon.md) rest [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), over $100$ MeV for a free nucleon target. For example,

$$
E_{\rm th}(\bar\nu_\mu p\to\mu^+n)
=\frac{(m_n+m_\mu)^2-m_p^2}{2m_p}\simeq113\ \mathrm{MeV}.
$$

Thus a [muon](../../../../../muon.md) spectrometer cannot observe this appearance channel from a reactor. Measure [electron antineutrino](../../../../../electron-antineutrino.md) disappearance instead, most commonly through [inverse beta decay](../../../../../inverse-beta-decay.md), $\bar\nu_ep\to e^+n$, using the prompt [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) and delayed [neutron](../../../../../neutron-split.md) signal.
