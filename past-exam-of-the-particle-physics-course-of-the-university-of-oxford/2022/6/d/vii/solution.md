<h1 id="6/d/vii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Vii](../vii.md)

In vacuum the electron-flavour survival [probability](../../../../../probability.md) depends only on $|U_{ei}|^2$ and the [mass-squared differences](../../../../../mass-squared-difference.md). Complex conjugation of the [lepton mixing matrix](../../../../../lepton-mixing-matrix.md) therefore leaves it unchanged:

$$
\boxed{P(\nu_e\to\nu_e)=P(\bar\nu_e\to\bar\nu_e),\qquad
\frac{\partial P_{ee}}{\partial\delta}=0.}
$$

Thus a reactor disappearance measurement cannot search for the intrinsic [CP violation](../../../../../cp-violation.md) phase in the standard three-[neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) model. Sensitivity to that phase calls for suitable appearance channels and, usually, comparison of [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) and [antineutrino](../../../../../antineutrino.md) beams. Matter can produce differences between propagation of [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md) and [antineutrinos](../../../../../antineutrino.md); these are not by themselves intrinsic [CP violation](../../../../../cp-violation.md), and are small for the proposed short reactor baseline.
