<h1 id="6/d/iv/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iv](../iv.md)

The [radiation length](../../../../../radiation-length.md) $X_0$ is the characteristic length for high-[energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) loss by [bremsstrahlung](../../../../../bremsstrahlung.md); smaller $X_0$ means **more** radiative loss for the same traversed thickness. To limit that loss, minimize $x/X_0$, choosing less material or a larger $X_0$, rather than minimizing $X_0$ itself. At reactor [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) [energies](../../../../../energy-split.md) of a few MeV, ionization and subsequent annihilation matter more than high-[energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) [bremsstrahlung](../../../../../bremsstrahlung.md). A sufficiently large [scintillation counter](../../../../../scintillation-counter.md) measures the deposited [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md), so containment of the [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) and annihilation [photons](../../../../../photon.md), light yield and calibration are the relevant design issues.[Sections 34.4.1–34.4.4: collision losses, radiation length, bremsstrahlung and critical energy](../../../../../review-of-particle-physics-by-particle-data-group-2022.md)
