<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For [quarks](../../../../quark.md) in an $N_c$-dimensional internal space $V$, complete antisymmetrization of three labels corresponds to a [Young diagram](../../../../young-diagram.md) with one column of three boxes, the partition $(1,1,1)$. Its [representation](../../../../group-representation.md) is the [exterior power](../../../../exterior-power.md) $\bigwedge^3V$ and has [dimension](../../../../dimension-vector-space.md)

$$
\dim\bigwedge^3V=\binom{N_c}{3}.
$$

For $N_c=2$, the column is too tall: $\bigwedge^3\mathbb C^2=0$. With only two labels at least two of the three coincide, and the [Young tableau](../../../../young-tableau.md) column antisymmetrizer annihilates the state. For $N_c=3$, the filled column $r,g,b$ spans a one-dimensional determinant [representation](../../../../group-representation.md). It is a [colour singlet](../../../../colour-singlet.md) of [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) because every group element has determinant one.

Equivalently, the [tensor product of group representations](../../../../tensor-product-of-group-representations.md) decomposes as

$$
\boxed{3\otimes3\otimes3=10_{[3]}\oplus8_{[2,1]}\oplus8_{[2,1]}\oplus1_{[1,1,1]}.}
$$

The two mixed-symmetry terms account for $16$ dimensions, so the total is $10+8+8+1=27$. Antisymmetry alone establishes **at least** three internal states. Exactly three follows in the usual [special unitary group](../../../../special-unitary-group.md) model when the observed three-[quark](../../../../quark.md) [baryon](../../../../baryon.md) must be a [colour singlet](../../../../colour-singlet.md): for $N_c>3$ the antisymmetric third [exterior power](../../../../exterior-power.md) has dimension greater than one and is not a singlet. This extra assumption should be made explicit.
