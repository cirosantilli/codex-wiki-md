<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

In [gluon-fusion Higgs production](../../../../gluon-fusion-higgs-production.md), two [gluons](../../../../gluon.md) couple to a closed [top quark](../../../../top-quark.md) loop, and the [Higgs boson](../../../../higgs-boson.md) couples to that massive [quark](../../../../quark.md) through its [Yukawa interaction](../../../../yukawa-interaction.md). The right panel of the diagram in [c](../c.md) shows one explicit colour-flow component: internal $t_r\to t_b$ at one [gluon](../../../../gluon.md) insertion, then $t_b\to t_r$ at the other; the colour-neutral [Higgs boson](../../../../higgs-boson.md) insertion preserves the internal [color charge](../../../../color-charge.md). Include both loop orderings. The [color charge](../../../../color-charge.md) factor is

$$
\operatorname{tr}(T^aT^b)=\frac12\delta^{ab},
$$

so the two incoming [gluons](../../../../gluon.md) are projected onto a [colour singlet](../../../../colour-singlet.md). The [Yukawa coupling](../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) is $y_t=\sqrt2m_t/v$, and the $h\bar tt$ vertex has strength $m_t/v$. No direct tree-level $hgg$ [mass](../../../../mass.md) coupling is needed. Integrating out a sufficiently heavy [top quark](../../../../top-quark.md) generates an effective interaction proportional to $(h/v)G^a_{\mu\nu}G^{a\mu\nu}$, which is allowed by [gauge invariance](../../../../gauge-invariance.md) even though the [gluon](../../../../gluon.md) is massless.

There is also no kinematic obstacle: two massless particles with nonparallel [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) can have nonzero total [invariant mass](../../../../invariant-mass.md). In particular, head-on [gluons](../../../../gluon.md) have $\hat s=4E_1E_2$, providing the [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) needed for the [Higgs boson](../../../../higgs-boson.md) at the [LHC](../../../../large-hadron-collider.md).
