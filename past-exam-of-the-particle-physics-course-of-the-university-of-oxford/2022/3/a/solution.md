<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The $\Delta^{++}$ [Delta baryon](../../../../delta-baryon.md) has [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) $+2$ and [valence quark](../../../../valence-quark.md) content $uuu$. In its ground state the spatial wavefunction has orbital [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) $L=0$ and is symmetric. Its [spin](../../../../spin.md) is $3/2$; the maximal projection state $u_\uparrow u_\uparrow u_\uparrow$ is symmetric in [spin](../../../../spin.md), and its [quark flavour](../../../../quark-flavor.md) wavefunction is symmetric as well. Three identical [fermions](../../../../fermion.md) require an antisymmetric total wavefunction by the [Pauli exclusion principle](../../../../pauli-exclusion-principle.md). A further internal degree of freedom must therefore supply the antisymmetry.

With three [color charges](../../../../color-charge.md), that factor is the normalized [colour singlet](../../../../colour-singlet.md)

$$
\boxed{|C\rangle=\frac{|rgb\rangle+|gbr\rangle+|brg\rangle-|rbg\rangle-|grb\rangle-|bgr\rangle}{\sqrt6}.}
$$

It changes sign under exchange of any two [quarks](../../../../quark.md). The remaining symmetric factors then give the required antisymmetric total state. This argument motivates [color charge](../../../../color-charge.md) independently of the already present [quark flavour](../../../../quark-flavor.md) and [spin](../../../../spin.md) labels.
