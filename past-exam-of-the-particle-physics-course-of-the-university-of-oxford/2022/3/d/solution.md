<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Represent the [color charges](../../../../color-charge.md) by $r=(1,0,0)^T$, $g=(0,1,0)^T$ and $b=(0,0,1)^T$. The [Gell-Mann matrices](../../../../gell-mann-matrices.md) satisfy

$$
\lambda^4r=b,\qquad\lambda^5r=ib,\qquad
\frac{\lambda^4-i\lambda^5}{2}=|b\rangle\langle r|.
$$

The physical [quark](../../../../quark.md)-[gluon](../../../../gluon.md) [Feynman vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) contains $g_sT^a$ with $T^a=\lambda^a/2$; the off-diagonal combination therefore allows $r\to b$. [Color charge](../../../../color-charge.md) conservation assigns the emitted [gluon](../../../../gluon.md) the colour-flow label $r\bar b$.

**The claimed impossibility of $r\to r$ is false.** A direct counterexample from the printed matrices is

$$
\boxed{\lambda^3r=r,\qquad\lambda^8r=\frac{r}{\sqrt3}.}
$$

Thus both diagonal [gluons](../../../../gluon.md) have nonzero $r\to r$ vertex elements, $(T^3)_{rr}=1/2$ and $(T^8)_{rr}=1/(2\sqrt3)$. They correspond to the traceless combinations

$$
\frac{r\bar r-g\bar g}{\sqrt2},\qquad
\frac{r\bar r+g\bar g-2b\bar b}{\sqrt6}.
$$

A [Gell-Mann matrix](../../../../gell-mann-matrices.md) is a generator rather than a finite rotation, but exponentiating a diagonal generator also leaves the red basis direction fixed up to a phase. The actual excluded object is the identity generator, proportional to the [colour singlet](../../../../colour-singlet.md) $(r\bar r+g\bar g+b\bar b)/\sqrt3$. Adding it would extend [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) to [unitary group](../../../../unitary-group.md) and introduce a ninth singlet vector boson. A colour-preserving vertex among the existing eight [gluons](../../../../gluon.md) does **not** require that extra boson and does not make the ordinary diagonal [gluons](../../../../gluon.md) colour singlets.
