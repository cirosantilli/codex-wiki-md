<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Choose the [exchange-positive CP convention for neutral kaon mixing](../../../../exchange-positive-cp-convention-for-neutral-kaon-mixing.md), $CP|K^0\rangle=|\bar K^0\rangle$. Neglecting [CP violation](../../../../cp-violation.md), the [kaon CP eigenstates](../../../../kaon-cp-eigenstates.md) can then be written

$$
|K_S\rangle=\frac{|K^0\rangle+|\bar K^0\rangle}{\sqrt2},\qquad
|K_L\rangle=\frac{|K^0\rangle-|\bar K^0\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
$$

Writing $A=A(\bar D^0\to K^0\pi^0)$ and $B=A(\bar D^0\to\bar K^0\pi^0)$, projection on these coherent final states gives

$$
\boxed{A_S=\frac{A+B}{\sqrt2}=\frac{A}{\sqrt2}(1+B/A),\qquad
A_L=\frac{A-B}{\sqrt2}=\frac{A}{\sqrt2}(1-B/A).}
$$

This is the convention implicit in the printed sum. Changing the sign convention for the [neutral antikaon](../../../../neutral-antikaon.md) changes both its coefficient in the [kaon CP eigenstates](../../../../kaon-cp-eigenstates.md) and $B$; it cannot change either physical [decay rate](../../../../decay-width.md). The extremely small $K_S$–$K_L$ [mass](../../../../mass.md) difference can be ignored in the two-body [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md).
