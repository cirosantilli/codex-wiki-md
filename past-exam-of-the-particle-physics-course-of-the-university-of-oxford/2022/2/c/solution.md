<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Put $r=B/A$. Equal two-body [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md) gives $\Gamma_{S,L}\propto|A|^2|1\pm r|^2/2$, so the [decay-rate asymmetry](../../../../decay-rate-asymmetry.md) is exactly

$$
\mathcal R=\frac{|1+r|^2-|1-r|^2}{|1+r|^2+|1-r|^2}
=\frac{2\operatorname{Re}r}{1+|r|^2}.
$$

The stated magnitude assumption fixes $|r|=\tan^2\theta_C$. Retaining the relative [strong phase](../../../../strong-phase.md) $\delta$ in the convention of [b](../b.md) therefore gives

$$
\boxed{\mathcal R=\frac{2\tan^2\theta_C\cos\delta}{1+\tan^4\theta_C}
=2\tan^2\theta_C\cos\delta+O(\tan^6\theta_C).}
$$

**A magnitude assumption alone does not determine $\mathcal R$.** For example, $r=i\tan^2\theta_C$ has the stipulated magnitude but gives $\mathcal R=0$, whereas a real positive $r$ gives a positive asymmetry. With the additional aligned-amplitude assumption $\delta=0$, conventionally used for this idealised extraction, the intended leading result is $\boxed{\mathcal R\simeq2\tan^2\theta_C}$. The sign of $r$ here refers to the complete amplitude after choosing the [kaon](../../../../kaon.md) phase convention, not to a bare [CKM matrix](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) product. A flavour-symmetry relation can supply further information, but is stronger than the printed magnitude assumption.
