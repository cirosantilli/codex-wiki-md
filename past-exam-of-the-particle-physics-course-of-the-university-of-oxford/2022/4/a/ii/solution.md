<h1 id="4/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

The [sagitta](../../../../../sagitta.md) is the maximum distance between the circular track and its chord. The perpendicular bisector of a chord of length $L$ gives a right triangle with hypotenuse $R$ and sides $L/2$ and $R-s$. Hence

$$
s=R-\sqrt{R^2-L^2/4}=\frac{L^2}{8R}+O(L^4/R^3)
\simeq\boxed{\frac{|q|BL^2}{8p}}.
$$

At fixed [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md), [magnetic field](../../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) and lever arm, $p=|q|BL^2/(8s)$, so differentiation and [propagation of uncertainty](../../../../../propagation-of-uncertainty.md) give

$$
\boxed{\frac{\sigma(p)}p=\frac{\sigma(s)}s=\frac{8p\,\sigma(s)}{|q|BL^2}.}
$$

Thus a fixed spatial [measurement uncertainty](../../../../../measurement-uncertainty.md) produces a relative [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) uncertainty growing linearly with $p$. If [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) is expressed in $\mathrm{GeV}/c$, $B$ in tesla, $L,s$ in metres and $z=q/e$, restore the usual conversion $p=0.2998|z|BR$.
