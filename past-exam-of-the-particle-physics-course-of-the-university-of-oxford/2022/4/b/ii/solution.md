<h1 id="4/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Let $\ell=\ln(\theta_{\max}/\theta_{\min})$. The supplied second moment gives the single-scatter [root mean square](../../../../../root-mean-square.md) angle

$$
\theta_{\rm rms,1}=\sqrt{\langle\theta^2\rangle}\simeq\sqrt2\,\theta_{\min}\sqrt\ell.
$$

For independent scatters with uniform azimuth, the small angular-deflection vectors have zero mean. Their cross terms average to zero, so their [variances](../../../../../variance-split.md) add. After $N$ scatters,

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{RMS}(\theta)=\sqrt{N\langle\theta^2\rangle}
\simeq\theta_{\min}\sqrt{2N\ell}.}
$$

This is the [root mean square](../../../../../root-mean-square.md) magnitude of the two-dimensional angular deflection. Its projection into one specified plane has a [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) smaller by a factor of two, hence a [root mean square](../../../../../root-mean-square.md) smaller by $\sqrt2$.
