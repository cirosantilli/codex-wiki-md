<h1 id="4/b/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For a thickness $x$ with [number density](../../../../../number-density.md) $\rho_n$ of scattering centres, the mean count is $N=\rho_nx\sigma$. Using the total [cross section](../../../../../cross-section-physics.md) in [i](../i.md) and the [variance](../../../../../variance-split.md) addition in [ii](../ii.md),

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{RMS}(\theta)^2
&\simeq\rho_nx\frac{16\pi K}{p^2\theta_{\min}^2}\,2\theta_{\min}^2\ell\\
&=\frac{32\pi K\rho_nx\ell}{p^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{RMS}(\theta)\simeq\sqrt{32\pi K\rho_n\ln(\theta_{\max}/\theta_{\min})}\,\frac{\sqrt x}{p}.}
$$

The stated assumptions make the prefactor independent of $x$ and approximately independent of $p$. The lower-cutoff factors cancel except inside the slowly varying logarithm. Treating $N$ as a [Poisson random variable](../../../../../poisson-distribution.md) rather than a fixed count gives the same result after replacing $N$ by its mean.
