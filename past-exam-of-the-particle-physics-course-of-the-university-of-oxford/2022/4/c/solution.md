<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At high [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md), the fixed spatial [measurement uncertainty](../../../../measurement-uncertainty.md) dominates because [ii](../a/ii.md) grows as $p$. At lower, still relativistic [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md), [Multiple Coulomb scattering](../../../../multiple-coulomb-scattering.md) dominates the relative uncertainty, which approaches the approximately constant term in [iv](../b/iv.md). Below the relativistic regime the additional velocity dependence of scattering must also be included.

Three complementary improvements are: increase $B$ and the tracking lever arm $L$ to enlarge the magnetic bend; improve hit-position resolution and alignment to reduce $\sigma(s)$; and reduce passive material and detector-layer thickness to reduce $x$ and hence [Multiple Coulomb scattering](../../../../multiple-coulomb-scattering.md). For independent contributions one commonly writes

$$
\frac{\sigma(p)}p\simeq\sqrt{(Ap)^2+C^2},
\qquad A=\frac{8\sigma(s)}{|q|BL^2},
$$

where $C$ is the scattering term for the specified material distribution and track estimator.
