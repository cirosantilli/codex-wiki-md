<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [covariant derivative](../../../../covariant-derivative.md) $D_\mu$ differentiates a field while preserving its transformation law under the local [electroweak gauge symmetry](../../../../electroweak-gauge-symmetry.md) $SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y$. Here $\partial_\mu$ is the ordinary spacetime derivative and $\mu$ a [Lorentz index](../../../../lorentz-index.md). The [gauge fields](../../../../gauge-field.md) $W^j_\mu$, $j=1,2,3$, are the three [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md) fields; $B_\mu$ is the [U(1)](../../../../circle-group.md) field. The corresponding dimensionless [coupling constant](../../../../coupling-constant-physics.md) is $g$ for [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md) and $g'$ for [U(1)](../../../../circle-group.md).

The $\tau_j$ are [weak isospin](../../../../weak-isospin.md) generators in the representation of the differentiated field, with $\tau_j=\sigma_j/2$ on a doublet and $\tau_j=0$ on an [SU(2)](../../../../su-2-group.md) singlet. The repeated index $j$ is summed. The generator $Y/2$ supplies the field's [weak hypercharge](../../../../hypercharge.md) in the convention $Q=\tau_3+Y/2$, where $Q$ is its fractional [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md). The factor $-i$ follows the convention of Hermitian generators. The two connection terms compensate the derivatives of spacetime-dependent [gauge transformations](../../../../gauge-transformation.md).
