<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Write $f=(v+h)/\sqrt2$. The [Pauli matrices](../../../../pauli-matrices.md) give the [weak isospin](../../../../weak-isospin.md) operators

$$
\tau^+=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\tau^-=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\tau_3=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

For the [Higgs doublet](../../../../higgs-field.md) $\Phi=(0,f)^T$ with [weak hypercharge](../../../../hypercharge.md) $Y=1$,

$$
\boxed{\tau^+\Phi=\begin{pmatrix}f\\0\end{pmatrix},\quad
\tau^-\Phi=0,\quad
\tau_3\Phi=\begin{pmatrix}0\\-f/2\end{pmatrix},\quad
Q\Phi=(\tau_3+\tfrac12 I)\Phi=0.}
$$

The [Higgs field](../../../../higgs-field.md) direction, including its [vacuum expectation value](../../../../vacuum-expectation-value.md), is electrically neutral. Consequently the electromagnetic [U(1)](../../../../circle-group.md) leaves the vacuum invariant: [electroweak symmetry breaking](../../../../electroweak-symmetry-breaking.md) preserves electromagnetism and the [photon](../../../../photon.md) remains massless. The result concerns this neutral vacuum direction; it does not say that both components of the full [Higgs doublet](../../../../higgs-field.md) have zero [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md).
