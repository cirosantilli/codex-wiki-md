<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The intermediate [photon](../../../../photon.md) carries [spin](../../../../spin.md) $J=1$. In the massless limit, a back-to-back [fermion](../../../../fermion.md)-[antifermion](../../../../antifermion.md) pair coupled to its vector current has opposite [helicities](../../../../helicity.md), so the initial and final projections along their respective axes are $m,m'=\pm1$. A rotation between these axes contributes the [Wigner D-matrix](../../../../wigner-d-matrix.md) elements $d^1_{m'm}(\theta)$. The relevant elements have magnitudes $(1+\cos\theta)/2$ and $(1-\cos\theta)/2$. Summing the four squared [helicity amplitudes](../../../../helicity-amplitude.md) gives the angular factor

$$
2\left(\frac{1+\cos\theta}{2}\right)^2+
2\left(\frac{1-\cos\theta}{2}\right)^2=1+\cos^2\theta.
$$

The common coupling and normalization factors recover the result in [d](../d.md). The $m=0$ terms do not contribute: the massless external vector currents eliminate the corresponding [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) configurations.
