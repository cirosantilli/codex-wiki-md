<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Define the [Mandelstam variables](../../../../mandelstam-variables.md) for the scattering labels by $s=(p_1+p_2)^2$, $t=(p_1-p_3)^2=q^2$ and $u=(p_1-p_4)^2$. Dropping the [electron](../../../../electron.md) and [muon](../../../../muon.md) [masses](../../../../mass.md) gives

$$
p_1\cdot p_2=p_3\cdot p_4=\frac{s}{2},\qquad
p_1\cdot p_4=p_3\cdot p_2=-\frac{u}{2}.
$$

These identities follow by expanding the corresponding squared [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md), using $p_i^2=0$ and [four-momentum conservation](../../../../four-momentum-conservation.md). Substitution into the supplied spin-averaged [matrix element](../../../../matrix-element.md) yields

$$
\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}
=\frac{8e^4}{t^2}\left(\frac{s^2}{4}+\frac{u^2}{4}\right)
=\boxed{2e^4\frac{s^2+u^2}{t^2}}.
$$

The $1/4$ initial [spin](../../../../spin.md) average is already included in the given expression; no further averaging factor belongs here.
