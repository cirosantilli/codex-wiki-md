<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

To implement [crossing symmetry](../../../../crossing-symmetry.md) with the annihilation labels, replace the old scattering [four-momenta](../../../../four-momentum.md) by $(p_1,-p_3,-p_2,p_4)$. The scattering [Mandelstam variables](../../../../mandelstam-variables.md) then become $(s_{\rm old},t_{\rm old},u_{\rm old})=(t,s,u)$, where now $s=(p_1+p_2)^2$, $t=(p_1-p_3)^2$ and $u=(p_1-p_4)^2$. Consequently

$$
\overline{|\mathcal M_{\rm ann}|^2}=2e^4\frac{t^2+u^2}{s^2}.
$$

In the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md), take $\theta$ between the incoming [electron](../../../../electron.md) and outgoing positive [muon](../../../../muon.md), $p_3$. For negligible [masses](../../../../mass.md), $t=-s(1-\cos\theta)/2$ and $u=-s(1+\cos\theta)/2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{\overline{|\mathcal M_{\rm ann}|^2}=e^4(1+\cos^2\theta).}
$$

Defining $\theta$ using the outgoing negative [muon](../../../../muon.md) interchanges $t$ and $u$ and leaves the result unchanged.
