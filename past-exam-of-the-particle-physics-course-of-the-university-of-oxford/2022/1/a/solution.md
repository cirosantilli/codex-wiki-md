<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

An incoming [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) contributes a [Dirac spinor](../../../../dirac-spinor.md) $u_s(p)$ and an outgoing [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) contributes its [Dirac adjoint](../../../../dirac-adjoint.md) $\bar u_s(p)=u_s^\dagger(p)\gamma^0$. The label $s$ specifies a [spin](../../../../spin.md) state; $p$ is the on-shell [four-momentum](../../../../four-momentum.md). Incoming and outgoing [antifermions](../../../../antifermion.md) instead contribute $\bar v_s(p)$ and $v_s(p)$.

The exchanged [boson](../../../../boson.md) is a virtual [photon](../../../../photon.md), represented in the [Feynman rules](../../../../feynman-rules.md) by the [photon propagator](../../../../photon-propagator.md) $-ig_{\mu\nu}/(q^2+i\epsilon)$ in [Feynman gauge](../../../../feynman-gauge.md). Its [momentum transfer](../../../../momentum-transfer.md) is $q=p_1-p_3=p_4-p_2$; unlike an external real [photon](../../../../photon.md), it need not satisfy $q^2=0$. Each [Feynman vertex](../../../../interaction-vertex.md) contracts a [gamma matrix](../../../../gamma-matrices.md) with this [propagator](../../../../propagator.md) and contributes $-ie\gamma^\mu$ in a conventional [QED](../../../../quantum-electrodynamics.md) sign convention. The [coupling constant](../../../../coupling-constant-physics.md) $e$ sets the interaction strength, with [fine-structure constant](../../../../fine-structure-constant.md) $\alpha=e^2/(4\pi)$. One may reverse the sign of the [photon](../../../../photon.md) field and both vertex signs without changing the result. The [gamma matrices](../../../../gamma-matrices.md) obey $\{\gamma^\mu,\gamma^\nu\}=2g^{\mu\nu}I$, with [Minkowski metric](../../../../minkowski-metric.md) $g=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$.
