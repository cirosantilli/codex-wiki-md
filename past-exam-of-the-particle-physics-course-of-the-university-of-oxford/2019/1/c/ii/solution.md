<h1 id="1/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Choose $W^+\to\mu^+\nu_\mu$. Important backgrounds include [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) production of heavy-flavour [hadrons](../../../../../hadron.md) with semileptonic decays, in-flight [pion](../../../../../pion.md) or [kaon](../../../../../kaon.md) decays, and [hadrons](../../../../../hadron.md) penetrating the calorimeters. These generally have accompanying jet activity; semileptonic decays can contain real [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md), while jet mismeasurement can fake [missing transverse momentum](../../../../../missing-transverse-momentum.md). Heavy-flavour tracks can originate at a [displaced decay vertex](../../../../../displaced-decay-vertex.md). Require a high-$p_T$ isolated [muon](../../../../../muon.md), agreement between inner tracking and the external [muon](../../../../../muon.md) system, and substantial [missing transverse momentum](../../../../../missing-transverse-momentum.md) inconsistent with [detector](../../../../../particle-detector.md) noise or a mismeasured jet.

The genuine electroweak backgrounds include $Z\to\mu^+\mu^-$ with one [muon](../../../../../muon.md) lost and $W\to\tau\nu\to\mu\nu\nu\nu$. Veto a second reconstructed opposite-sign [muon](../../../../../muon.md) and use the dimuon [invariant mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md) when both are present to reject the [Z boson](../../../../../z-boson.md) channel. The tau-mediated spectrum is softer and contains extra missing [particles](../../../../../particle-physics.md); template fits and control samples estimate its residual contribution. [Detector acceptance](../../../../../detector-acceptance.md) determines how much of the lost-muon background survives.
