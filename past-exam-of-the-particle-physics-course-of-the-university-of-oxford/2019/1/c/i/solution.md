<h1 id="1/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

The useful modes are $\boxed{W^+\to e^+\nu_e\ \text{and}\ W^+\to\mu^+\nu_\mu}$ and their charge conjugates. An [electron](../../../../../electron.md) gives an isolated track and localized [electromagnetic shower](../../../../../electromagnetic-shower.md); a [muon](../../../../../muon.md) gives an isolated penetrating track. Both allow a well-calibrated [charged-lepton](../../../../../charged-lepton.md) [transverse momentum](../../../../../transverse-momentum.md) and a [missing transverse momentum](../../../../../missing-transverse-momentum.md) measurement for the [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md).

The [tau lepton](../../../../../tau-particle.md) decays before detection, usually adding further [neutrinos](../../../../../neutrino.md). Its visible [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) is only part of the original tau [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md), so the simple two-body endpoint is lost. For the direct [electron](../../../../../electron.md) and [muon](../../../../../muon.md) modes, the [transverse mass](../../../../../transverse-mass.md)

$$
M_T^2=2p_T^\ell p_T^\nu[1-\cos(\Delta\varphi)]
$$

also has an ideal endpoint at $M_W$ and avoids needing the unmeasured [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) longitudinal [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md). Fits to this distribution or the [charged-lepton](../../../../../charged-lepton.md) $p_T$ spectrum determine $M_W$.
