<h1 id="1/c/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

For the selected [muon](../../../../../muon.md) channel, finite curvature resolution in the magnetic tracking system broadens the reconstructed [transverse momentum](../../../../../transverse-momentum.md) spectrum. A high-$p_T$ track has small [sagitta](../../../../../sagitta.md), so spatial measurement errors, alignment errors and magnetic-field calibration errors become important; at high [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) an approximately fixed uncertainty in $1/p_T$ gives $\delta p_T\propto p_T^2$. [Multiple Coulomb scattering](../../../../../multiple-coulomb-scattering.md) contributes at lower [momenta](../../../../../momentum-split.md). If the [transverse mass](../../../../../transverse-mass.md) is fitted, finite hadronic-recoil resolution additionally smears the inferred [missing transverse momentum](../../../../../missing-transverse-momentum.md). These are experimental effects, distinct from the physical recoil and width in [ii](../../b/ii.md).
