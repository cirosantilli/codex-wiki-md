<h1 id="1/b/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[Initial-state radiation](../../../../../initial-state-radiation.md) of [gluons](../../../../../gluon.md) gives the [W boson](../../../../../w-boson.md) nonzero [transverse momentum](../../../../../transverse-momentum.md). Its transverse [Lorentz boost](../../../../../lorentz-boost.md) then changes the [charged-lepton](../../../../../charged-lepton.md) $p_T$, washing out the sharp $M_W/2$ edge and permitting events above it. The recoil must be modelled with [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) and constrained by measured [hadronic](../../../../../hadron.md) activity. Independently, the finite [W boson](../../../../../w-boson.md) [decay width](../../../../../decay-width.md) spreads its actual [invariant mass](../../../../../invariant-mass.md), also smearing the fixed-mass endpoint. Either is a theoretical broadening mechanism; neither is a [detector-resolution](../../../../../particle-detector.md) effect.
