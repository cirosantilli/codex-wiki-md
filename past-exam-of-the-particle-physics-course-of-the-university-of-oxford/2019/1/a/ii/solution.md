<h1 id="1/a/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Neglect the participating [quark](../../../../../quark.md) and [lepton](../../../../../lepton.md) [masses](../../../../../mass.md), so that the [weak charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md) selects a left-[helicity](../../../../../helicity.md) $u$ and a right-[helicity](../../../../../helicity.md) $\bar d$. Choose $+z$ along the [proton](../../../../../proton.md) beam in the [partonic](../../../../../parton-model.md) [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md). Then the [up quark](../../../../../up-quark.md) moves along $+z$ with [spin projection](../../../../../spin.md) $-1/2$, while the [down antiquark](../../../../../down-antiquark.md) moves along $-z$ with [helicity](../../../../../helicity.md) $+1/2$ and also has [spin projection](../../../../../spin.md) $-1/2$ along $+z$. Thus

$$
\boxed{J_z(W^+)=-1.}
$$

The collinear two-body initial state has no orbital projection about its collision axis. In this approximation the produced [W boson](../../../../../w-boson.md) therefore has the indicated transverse [polarization](../../../../../polarization-waves.md); its longitudinal spin state is absent. The unpolarized [hadron](../../../../../hadron.md) beams contain other constituent [spin states](../../../../../spin.md), but these do not participate in this [massless](../../../../../massless-particle.md) [charged-current](../../../../../charged-current.md) subprocess.

In the final state the [Positron](../../../../../positron.md) has [helicity](../../../../../helicity.md) $+1/2$ and the [electron neutrino](../../../../../electron-neutrino.md) has [helicity](../../../../../helicity.md) $-1/2$. Along the outgoing [charged-lepton](../../../../../charged-lepton.md) direction their combined angular-momentum projection is $+1$, because the oppositely moving [neutrino](../../../../../neutrino.md) has [spin projection](../../../../../spin.md) $+1/2$ on that axis. Finite [masses](../../../../../mass.md), noncollinear production and sea contributions modify the ideal configuration.
