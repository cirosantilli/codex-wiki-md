<h1 id="1/a/iii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Iii](../iii.md)

Let $\theta^\star$ be the angle from the incoming [up quark](../../../../../up-quark.md), along the [proton](../../../../../proton.md) direction, to the outgoing charged [antilepton](../../../../../antilepton.md) in the [W boson](../../../../../w-boson.md) rest frame. The initial and final [spin projections](../../../../../spin.md) in [ii](../ii.md) are $-1$ and $+1$. The [Wigner small-d matrix](../../../../../wigner-small-d-matrix.md) therefore gives

$$
\mathcal M(\theta^\star)\propto d^1_{-1,+1}(\theta^\star)
=\frac{1-\cos\theta^\star}{2}.
$$

After squaring and normalizing over $c=\cos\theta^\star$, the [charged-lepton](../../../../../charged-lepton.md) [angular distribution](../../../../../angular-distribution.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac1\Gamma\frac{d\Gamma}{dc}=\frac38(1-c)^2,\qquad -1<c<1.}
$$

Its integral is one since $\int_{-1}^{1}(1-c)^2dc=8/3$. The charged [antilepton](../../../../../antilepton.md) is preferentially emitted toward the [antiproton](../../../../../antiproton.md). Measuring the angle from the incoming [down antiquark](../../../../../down-antiquark.md) instead replaces $c$ by $-c$ and produces $(1+c)^2$; the axis convention is part of the answer. Per unit solid angle the normalized density is $3(1-\cos\theta^\star)^2/(16\pi)$.
