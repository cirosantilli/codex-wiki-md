<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Use $B^0=d\bar b$ and $\bar B^0=\bar d b$. A [box Feynman diagram](../../../../box-feynman-diagram.md) with two [W bosons](../../../../w-boson.md) and internal up-type [quarks](../../../../quark.md) changes one neutral [flavour](../../../../quark-flavor.md) into the other. The [top quark](../../../../top-quark.md) contribution dominates the dispersive [mixing amplitude](../../../../neutral-b-meson-mixing.md) and carries the squared CKM combination involving $V_{td},V_{tb}$. With a consistent meson phase convention,

$$
\frac qp\simeq\frac{V_{td}V_{tb}^*}{V_{td}^*V_{tb}}.
$$

The illustration in [b](../b.md) shows this box and the dominant decay topology. A suitable common final state is $f=J/\psi\,K_S$, reconstructed for example through $J/\psi\to\mu^+\mu^-$ and $K_S\to\pi^+\pi^-$. The leading $\bar b\to\bar c c\bar s$ tree transition gives $B^0\to J/\psi K^0$, while the conjugate gives $\bar B^0\to J/\psi\bar K^0$. [Neutral kaon mixing](../../../../neutral-kaon-mixing.md) projects both kaon flavours onto the observed $K_S$.

The final state is approximately a [CP eigenstate](../../../../cp-eigenstate.md) with $\eta_f=-1$: $J/\psi$ and $K_S$ each have CP approximately $+1$, and their relative [orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) is $L=1$ to couple the vector charmonium to the spin-zero parent. Neglecting the small kaon CP impurity and subleading [decay amplitudes](../../../../decay-amplitude.md),

$$
\boxed{\lambda_f=\frac qp\frac{\bar A_f}{A_f}\simeq-e^{-2i\beta},\qquad
\operatorname{Im}\lambda_f=\sin2\beta.}
$$

There are two indistinguishable paths, direct decay and mixing followed by decay, so their amplitudes interfere. For $\Delta\Gamma_d\simeq0$ and negligible direct [CP violation](../../../../cp-violation.md), the [time-dependent neutral-B CP asymmetry](../../../../time-dependent-neutral-b-cp-asymmetry.md) is

$$
\boxed{\frac{\Gamma(\bar B^0(t)\to f)-\Gamma(B^0(t)\to f)}
{\Gamma(\bar B^0(t)\to f)+\Gamma(B^0(t)\to f)}
=\sin2\beta\,\sin(\Delta m_d t).}
$$

Measure the production [flavour](../../../../quark-flavor.md) and proper decay time, fit the oscillation coefficient, and extract $\sin2\beta$. This determines $\beta$ up to the usual trigonometric ambiguities unless further information is used. The cancellation of the dominant decay phase is why this channel isolates the angle involving $V_{td}$ without needing $V_{ub}$.
