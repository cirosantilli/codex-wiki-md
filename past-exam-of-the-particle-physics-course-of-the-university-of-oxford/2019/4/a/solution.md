<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [CKM matrix](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) converts down-type [quark mass](../../../../quark-mass.md) eigenstates into the combinations entering the [weak charged current](../../../../charged-current.md). Its entry $V_{ij}$ controls a [charged-current](../../../../charged-current.md) transition between up-type [flavour](../../../../quark-flavor.md) $i$ and down-type [flavour](../../../../quark-flavor.md) $j$. In the three-generation [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md), $V=U_{uL}^\dagger U_{dL}$ is a product of [unitary matrices](../../../../unitary-matrix.md) from diagonalizing the [quark mass](../../../../quark-mass.md) matrices, so $V^\dagger V=1$.

The [Wolfenstein parametrization](../../../../wolfenstein-parametrization.md) displays the hierarchy

$$
|V|\sim\begin{pmatrix}1&\lambda&\lambda^3\\\lambda&1&\lambda^2\\\lambda^3&\lambda^2&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus diagonal transitions dominate; first-second generation mixing is larger than second-third generation mixing, and direct first-third generation mixing is smallest. The parameters $A,\rho,\eta$ refine this order estimate. With nondegenerate [quark masses](../../../../quark-mass.md), a nonzero irreducible weak phase permits [CP violation](../../../../cp-violation.md); at leading order the [Jarlskog invariant](../../../../jarlskog-invariant.md) is $J\simeq A^2\lambda^6\eta$. Individual complex entries are not enough: the phase must survive allowed quark-field rephasings.

The exact physical [CKM matrix](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) is unitary, whereas its displayed cubic truncation is only approximately so. For example its first-row norm is $1+\lambda^4/4+A^2\lambda^6(\rho^2+\eta^2)$, which is not exactly one for nonzero $\lambda$. The omitted higher-order terms repair this; [unitarity](../../../../unitary-operator.md) must be tested to the retained order.
