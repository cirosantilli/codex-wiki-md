<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the stationary target, $p=(m_N,\mathbf0)$. The invariant definition immediately gives

$$
\boxed{y=\frac{p\cdot(k-k')}{p\cdot k}
=\frac{m_N(E_\nu-E_\mu)}{m_NE_\nu}
=1-\frac{E_\mu}{E_\nu}.}
$$

No [massless](../../../../massless-particle.md) approximation is needed for this laboratory identity when binding and target recoil conventions are neglected.

For the angular relation, specify the neutrino-parton [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) and neglect [masses](../../../../mass.md) there. The incoming and outgoing [leptons](../../../../lepton.md) then each have [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) $\sqrt{\widehat s}/2$. Their [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) transfer satisfies

$$
Q^2=2\left(\frac{\sqrt{\widehat s}}2\right)^2(1-\cos\theta^\star)
=\frac{\widehat s}{2}(1-\cos\theta^\star).
$$

Since $Q^2=xys$ and $\widehat s=xs$, cancellation gives

$$
\boxed{y=\frac{1-\cos\theta^\star}{2},\qquad
1-y=\frac{1+\cos\theta^\star}{2}.}
$$

This identifies the [antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) weight $(1-y)^2$ with the corresponding squared angular factor. The relevant [massless](../../../../massless-particle.md) condition is sufficient [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) in the lepton-parton subprocess; $E_\nu\gg m_\mu$ alone does not control every endpoint configuration.

The frame qualifier matters: the complete neutrino-nucleon [centre-of-mass](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) final state contains a variable-mass [hadronic](../../../../hadron.md) system. In that frame, still neglecting [masses](../../../../mass.md) but retaining $x$, direct two-body kinematics for $\mu+X$ gives instead

$$
y=\frac{1-\cos\theta_{\nu N}}{1-\cos\theta_{\nu N}+x(1+\cos\theta_{\nu N})}.
$$

For example $x=1/2$ and $\theta_{\nu N}=\pi/2$ give $y=2/3$, not $1/2$. Thus the simple angle-only relation implicitly uses the [partonic](../../../../parton-model.md) frame.
