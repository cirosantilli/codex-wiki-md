<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the leading-order [parton model](../../../../parton-model.md): scatter incoherently from an approximately on-shell collinear [quark](../../../../quark.md) carrying nucleon [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) fraction $x$, neglect [quark](../../../../quark.md) and [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) [masses](../../../../mass.md), use an unpolarized target, and neglect nuclear binding and higher-twist corrections. The condition $Q^2\gg m_p^2$ suppresses target-mass effects, while $Q^2\ll M_W^2$ permits the local [Fermi interaction](../../../../fermi-interaction.md) instead of the full W propagator. CKM-weighted accessible final flavours are approximated by their leading light-quark sums. The struck-quark [centre-of-mass](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is $\widehat s\simeq xs$, with $s=(k+p)^2\simeq2m_NE_\nu$.

A [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) scatters through $\nu d\to\mu^-u$ or $\nu s\to\mu^-c$, and through the corresponding crossed [antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) channels such as $\nu\bar u\to\mu^-\bar d$. The chiral [charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) makes the [massless](../../../../massless-particle.md) [quark](../../../../quark.md) contribution independent of $y$ and the [antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) contribution proportional to $(1-y)^2$. The scattering probability is weighted by the [parton distribution functions](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md); the explicit factor $x$ comes from $\widehat s=xs$. In the usual convention $q(x)dx$ is a number probability density, while $xq(x)$ is a [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) density. Approximate [Bjorken scaling](../../../../bjorken-scaling.md) leaves mainly $x$ dependence; [QCD](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) evolution adds a logarithmic $Q^2$ dependence.

**The printed proton-density formula is not an isoscalar formula in the standard PDF convention.** For a [proton](../../../../proton.md), neglecting incoming charm and taking accessible CKM sums as unity, the conventional leading-order result is

$$
\frac{d^2\sigma^{\nu p}}{dxdy}=\frac{G_F^2s}{\pi}\,x\left[d_p+s_p+(1-y)^2\bar u_p\right].
$$

For an [isoscalar target](../../../../isoscalar-target.md) per nucleon, average [proton](../../../../proton.md) and [neutron](../../../../neutron-split.md) and use [charge symmetry of nucleon parton distributions](../../../../charge-symmetry-of-nucleon-parton-distributions.md): $d_n=u_p$, $\bar u_n=\bar d_p$, $s_n=s_p$. This gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{d^2\sigma^{\nu N}}{dxdy}=\frac{G_F^2s}{2\pi}\,x
\left[u_p+d_p+2s_p+(1-y)^2(\bar u_p+\bar d_p)\right].}
$$

The standard [normalization](../../../../normalization.md) follows equivalently by inserting $F_2^{\nu p}=2x(d+s+\bar u)$ and $xF_3^{\nu p}=2x(d+s-\bar u)$ into the [charged-current](../../../../charged-current.md) structure-function formula.[Section 18.1, Eqs. (18.8) and (18.19): [charged-current](../../../../charged-current.md) [normalization](../../../../normalization.md) and [proton](../../../../proton.md) [quark](../../../../quark.md) combinations](../../sources/structure-functions-by-particle-data-group-2024.md)

A concrete discrepancy remains even with a simple valence model: take $u_p=2h(x)$, $d_p=h(x)$ and all sea densities zero. The correctly averaged isoscalar bracket is $3h(x)$, while the printed bracket is only $h(x)$ with the same prefactor. Thus the intended [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) and $x$ dependence can be explained, but the literal printed isoscalar claim cannot be derived. If the symbols were instead defined as [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) densities, the extra explicit $x$ would also need removal.
