<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a valence-dominated [isoscalar target](../../../../isoscalar-target.md), $u_v^N(x)=d_v^N(x)=q_v(x)$. A left-chiral incident [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) has unsuppressed scattering on a left-chiral [quark](../../../../quark.md) and the $(1-y)^2$ weight for a right-chiral [quark](../../../../quark.md). Summing the up and down contributions yields

$$
\boxed{\frac{d^2\sigma_{\rm NC}^{\nu N}}{dxdy}
=\frac{G_F^2s}{\pi}\,xq_v(x)
\left[G_L^2+G_R^2(1-y)^2\right],}
$$

where $G_L^2=(g_L^u)^2+(g_L^d)^2$ and $G_R^2=(g_R^u)^2+(g_R^d)^2$. The common [normalization](../../../../normalization.md) here uses the conventional Fermi coupling and [PDFs](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md) in [a](../a.md); the requested $y$ shape is independent of an overall [normalization](../../../../normalization.md) convention.

Put $w=\sin^2\theta_W$. With $I_3^u=+1/2$, $I_3^d=-1/2$, $Q_u=2/3$ and $Q_d=-1/3$,

$$
g_L^u=\frac12-\frac23w,\quad g_L^d=-\frac12+\frac13w,
\qquad g_R^u=-\frac23w,\quad g_R^d=\frac13w,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{G_L^2=\frac12-w+\frac59w^2,\qquad
G_R^2=\frac59w^2,\qquad
\frac{d\sigma_{\rm NC}}{dy}\propto
\left(\frac12-w+\frac59w^2\right)+\frac59w^2(1-y)^2.}
$$

Fit the relative constant and $(1-y)^2$ components to determine their coupling ratio, or use the [NC-to-CC cross-section ratio](../../../../neutrino-neutral-to-charged-current-ratio.md) to constrain the absolute combination. In this valence limit, integrating over $0<y<1$ gives

$$
\boxed{R_\nu=\frac{\sigma_{\rm NC}^{\nu N}}{\sigma_{\rm CC}^{\nu N}}
=G_L^2+\frac13G_R^2
=\frac12-w+\frac{20}{27}w^2.}
$$

The same valence [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) integral cancels between numerator and denominator. Experimentally, NC events have [hadronic](../../../../hadron.md) activity without the [charged-current](../../../../charged-current.md) outgoing [muon](../../../../muon.md); known fluxes or CC [normalization](../../../../normalization.md), [muon](../../../../muon.md) identification and acceptance corrections are needed. Sea-quark contributions, charm thresholds, [QCD](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) corrections and departures from isoscalarity must be included for a precision [weak-mixing-angle](../../../../weinberg-angle.md) measurement.
