<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Only the [singlet](../../../../singlet-representation.md) $|1\rangle$ and isoscalar [octet](../../../../octet-representation.md) member $|8_0\rangle$ can mix without violating [isospin](../../../../isospin.md): they have the same $I=I_3=S=0$ and, for the vector multiplet, the same $J^{PC}=1^{--}$. The neutral [triplet](../../../../triplet-representation.md) member has $I=1$ and is excluded in the [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) limit.

Set $|\psi_a\rangle=|1\rangle$ and $|\psi_b\rangle=|8_0\rangle$, with precisely the phases in [a](../a.md). Define $|n\rangle=(|u\bar u\rangle+|d\bar d\rangle)/\sqrt2$. Taking overlaps gives

$$
|n\rangle=\sqrt{\frac23}|1\rangle-\frac1{\sqrt3}|8_0\rangle,
\qquad
|s\bar s\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt3}|1\rangle+\sqrt{\frac23}|8_0\rangle.
$$

Therefore the source's rotation convention is satisfied by

$$
\boxed{\cos\theta=\sqrt{\frac23},\qquad
\sin\theta=-\frac1{\sqrt3},\qquad
\theta=-\arctan\frac1{\sqrt2}\simeq-35.26^\circ.}
$$

This yields $|\omega\rangle=|n\rangle$ and $|\phi\rangle=|s\bar s\rangle$ exactly. Reversing the [octet](../../../../octet-representation.md) phase changes the quoted sign, with an irrelevant compensating phase on the physical phi state; exchanging the basis order gives the complementary angle. The physical statement of [ideal vector-meson mixing](../../../../ideal-vector-meson-mixing.md) is the pure strange and pure nonstrange content, rather than a convention-independent sign for the angle.
