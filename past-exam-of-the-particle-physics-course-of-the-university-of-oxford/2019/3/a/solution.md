<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use the [orthonormal](../../../../orthonormal-set.md) basis $|u\bar u\rangle,|d\bar d\rangle,|s\bar s\rangle$. The normalized [flavour](../../../../quark-flavor.md) [singlet](../../../../singlet-representation.md) is

$$
\boxed{|1\rangle=\frac{|u\bar u\rangle+|d\bar d\rangle+|s\bar s\rangle}{\sqrt3},
\qquad I=I_3=S=0.}
$$

The [SU(3)](../../../../su-3-group.md) invariant contraction pairs each fundamental [flavour](../../../../quark-flavor.md) with its conjugate. Applying any supplied raising operator to the [singlet](../../../../singlet-representation.md) gives zero because its [quark](../../../../quark.md) and [antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) contributions cancel.

To fix the neutral [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) [triplet](../../../../triplet-representation.md) phase consistently with the source's charged states, apply $I_+$ to $-|d\bar u\rangle$:

$$
I_+[-|d\bar u\rangle]=|d\bar d\rangle-|u\bar u\rangle.
$$

Thus one missing member of the [meson octet](../../../../meson-octet.md) is

$$
\boxed{|T_0\rangle=\frac{|d\bar d\rangle-|u\bar u\rangle}{\sqrt2},\qquad I=1,\ I_3=S=0.}
$$

Indeed $I_+|T_0\rangle=\sqrt2|u\bar d\rangle$, the correct [ladder operator](../../../../ladder-operator.md) strength for the $I=1$ [triplet](../../../../triplet-representation.md).

The other missing state is orthogonal to both $|1\rangle$ and $|T_0\rangle$. For coefficients $(a,b,c)$, [orthogonality](../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md) requires $a=b$ and $a+b+c=0$. Choose the convenient overall phase $c>0$ and normalize:

$$
\boxed{|8_0\rangle=\frac{2|s\bar s\rangle-|u\bar u\rangle-|d\bar d\rangle}{\sqrt6},
\qquad I=I_3=S=0.}
$$

It can also be obtained by adding the supplied ladder operations $V_+[-|s\bar u\rangle]=|s\bar s\rangle-|u\bar u\rangle$ and $U_+[-|s\bar d\rangle]=|s\bar s\rangle-|d\bar d\rangle$. The three displayed states are mutually [orthonormal](../../../../orthonormal-set.md). The [meson octet](../../../../meson-octet.md) here is the traceless adjoint component of $\bar3\otimes3$; the source's word “antisymmetric” does not impose antisymmetry on all its quark-antiquark [flavour](../../../../quark-flavor.md) wavefunctions.
