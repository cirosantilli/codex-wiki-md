<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The ordering of [Weyl components](../../../../weyl-spinor.md) depends on the supplied basis transformation. In this paper use $u_W=Uu_D=(\xi,\eta)^{\mathsf T}$. Applying the printed $U$ to the printed Dirac matrices gives

$$
\gamma_W^0=\begin{pmatrix}0&I\\I&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\gamma_W^i=\begin{pmatrix}0&-\sigma_i\\\sigma_i&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\gamma_W^5=\begin{pmatrix}I&0\\0&-I\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Thus $\xi$ is right chiral and $\eta$ left chiral in this ordering. The free [massless](../../../../massless-particle.md) [Weyl equations](../../../../weyl-equation.md) are

$$
\boxed{i(\partial_t+\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\boldsymbol\nabla)\xi=0,
\qquad i(\partial_t-\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\boldsymbol\nabla)\eta=0.}
$$

They describe [spin-one-half](../../../../spin.md) [massless](../../../../massless-particle.md) fields in the two inequivalent chiral [spinor representations of the Lorentz group](../../../../spinor-representation-of-the-lorentz-group.md). Their rotation generators agree, but their [boost generators](../../../../opposite-weyl-boost-generators.md) have opposite signs; spatial parity interchanges the two. A positive-energy [particle](../../../../particle-physics.md) in the $\xi$ field has [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) $+1/2$, and one in the $\eta$ field has [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) $-1/2$. Their antiparticles have the opposite [helicities](../../../../helicity.md), so a [Weyl field](../../../../weyl-field.md) is not a field containing only one [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) for both [particles](../../../../particle-physics.md) and antiparticles.

[Neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md) provide the observed ultrarelativistic approximation to a left-chiral [particle](../../../../particle-physics.md) and right-helicity antiparticle. However, [neutrino oscillation](../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md) establishes nonzero [neutrino mass](../../../../neutrino-mass.md) differences, so physical [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md) are not all exactly [massless](../../../../massless-particle.md) Weyl [particles](../../../../particle-physics.md). Chiral [Weyl fields](../../../../weyl-field.md) occur in the [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md), but an isolated exactly [massless](../../../../massless-particle.md) elementary Weyl [particle](../../../../particle-physics.md) is not established by [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) observations. Whether [neutrino mass](../../../../neutrino-mass.md) eigenstates are Dirac or Majorana remains a separate question.[Sections 14.2, 14.4 and 14.9.2: massive-neutrino extensions, mass-induced oscillations, and the Dirac–Majorana question](../../sources/neutrino-masses-mixing-and-oscillations-by-particle-data-group-2024.md)
