<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Take $p_z>0$ and $E\gg m$. Then $\sqrt{E+p_z}\simeq\sqrt{2E}$ while $\sqrt{E-p_z}=m/\sqrt{E+p_z}\simeq m/\sqrt{2E}$. Hence

$$
\boxed{u_+(p)\longrightarrow\sqrt{2E}\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\0\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
u_-(p)\longrightarrow\sqrt{2E}\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) operator is $\widehat h=\tfrac12\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_3,\sigma_3)$ for [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) along $+z$. It gives

$$
\boxed{h[u_+]=+\tfrac12,\qquad h[u_-]=-\tfrac12.}
$$

The positive-helicity state becomes entirely right chiral, while the negative-helicity state becomes entirely left chiral. The suppressed opposite-chirality amplitude is of relative order $m/(2E)$; at finite [mass](../../../../mass.md) [chirality](../../../../chirality-physics.md) is not the same as [helicity](../../../../helicity.md). If $p_z<0$, [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) is measured along the reversed [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and the labels of the two spin-axis states interchange.
