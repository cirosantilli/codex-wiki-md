<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With the [gamma matrices](../../../../gamma-matrices.md) derived in [a](../a.md), the momentum-space [Dirac equation](../../../../dirac-equation.md) reads

$$
(E-\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf p)\xi=m\eta,
\qquad (E+\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf p)\eta=m\xi.
$$

For a positive-energy [particle](../../../../particle-physics.md) at rest, $E=m>0$ and $\mathbf p=0$, so $\xi=\eta$. The [Dirac adjoint](../../../../dirac-adjoint.md) in this basis is $\bar u=(\eta^\dagger,\xi^\dagger)$, and the specified [normalization](../../../../normalization.md) gives

$$
\bar u u=\eta^\dagger\xi+\xi^\dagger\eta=2\xi^\dagger\xi=2m.
$$

Therefore, for any normalized two-component [spinor](../../../../spinor.md) $\chi$,

$$
\boxed{\xi(0)=\eta(0)=\sqrt m\,\chi,\qquad
u_W(0)=\sqrt m\begin{pmatrix}\chi\\\chi\end{pmatrix},\qquad \chi^\dagger\chi=1.}
$$

Equivalently, choosing $\chi_+=(1,0)^{\mathsf T}$ and $\chi_-=(0,1)^{\mathsf T}$ gives the two independent rest-frame [spin states](../../../../spin.md). A strictly [massless](../../../../massless-particle.md) [particle](../../../../particle-physics.md) has no rest frame; this step requires $m>0$.
