↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

[Quadrupole magnets](../../../../quadrupole-magnet.md) provide transverse restoring forces proportional to displacement from the reference orbit. Their [magnetic field](../../../../magnetic-field-split.md) increases linearly away from the axis, so an off-axis [particle](../../../../particle-physics.md) receives a kick that can bring its trajectory back toward the design orbit. A magnetic [quadrupole](../../../../quadrupole-magnet.md) focuses in one transverse plane and defocuses in the other; a single [quadrupole](../../../../quadrupole-magnet.md) cannot confine both planes.

An [alternating-gradient focusing](../../../../strong-focusing.md) lattice alternates [quadrupole](../../../../quadrupole-magnet.md) orientations with drift spaces. With suitable strengths and separations, a focusing-defocusing sequence gives stable [betatron oscillations](../../../../betatron-oscillation.md) in both planes. The focusing acts on transverse position, while the phase advance through the lattice converts an initial angular deviation into position and then a corrective kick. The beam is thus transported within a bounded envelope rather than allowed to drift into the pipe. Dipoles provide the main bending; the [quadrupole](../../../../quadrupole-magnet.md) lattice controls the small deviations.
