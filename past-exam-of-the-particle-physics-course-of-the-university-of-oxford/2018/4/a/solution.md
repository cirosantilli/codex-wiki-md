<h1 id="4/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use dimensionless physical [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) $h=\mathbf S\cdot\hat{\mathbf p}$, with [spin](../../../../spin.md) [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) $\pm1/2$ in [natural units](../../../../natural-units.md). In the [Dirac representation](../../../../dirac-representation-of-the-gamma-matrices.md),

$$
\boxed{h_D=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\hat{\mathbf p}&0\\0&\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\hat{\mathbf p}\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The entries $1$ in the supplied transformation denote two-by-two [identities](../../../../identity-matrix.md). Since its [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) acts only on the upper/lower [spinor](../../../../spinor.md) blocks, it [commutes](../../../../commuting-operators.md) with the repeated [Pauli block](../../../../pauli-matrices.md). Therefore $u^{-1}=u$ and

$$
\boxed{h_W=u h_Du^{-1}=h_D.}
$$

The [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) remain the physical [spin projection](../../../../spin.md) along the direction of motion, $+1/2$ or $-1/2$. A convention omitting the factor one half instead labels them $\pm1$; this changes no measurement [probabilities](../../../../probability.md). A change of [gamma-matrix representation](../../../../gamma-matrices.md) cannot change the [spin](../../../../spin.md) observable.
