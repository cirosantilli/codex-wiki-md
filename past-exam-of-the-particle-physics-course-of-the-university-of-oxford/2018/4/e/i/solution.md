<h1 id="4/e/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

For an incoming $\mu^-$, a putative $e^-\mu^-\to\nu_e\nu_\mu$ process would change total [electric charge](../../../../../electric-charge-split.md) from $-2$ to zero. It is forbidden: both negative [charged leptons](../../../../../charged-lepton.md) would have to emit a $W^-$, and a single exchanged charged boson cannot connect those two emission vertices. Ordinary [elastic](../../../../../elastic-scattering.md) $e^-\mu^-\to e^-\mu^-$ scattering remains possible through $t$-channel [photon](../../../../../photon.md) or $Z$ exchange, shown in the figure, but this is not the [charged-current](../../../../../charged-current.md) reaction of part d. The [neutral-current](../../../../../neutral-current.md) weak contribution is distinct from the electromagnetic one.
