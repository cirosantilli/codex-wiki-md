↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

A [proton](../../../../../proton.md) contains [up quarks](../../../../../up-quark.md) on which the [charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md) can act:

$$
\boxed{e^-u\longrightarrow\nu_e d}
$$

through $W^-$ exchange, weighted by $V_{ud}$. Spectator [quarks](../../../../../quark.md) complete the hadronic final state. At the exclusive nucleon level the charge assignment is $e^-p\to\nu_e n$; at the high [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) transfers specified, the generally dominant description is inclusive $e^-p\to\nu_e X$ with [proton](../../../../../proton.md) structure and hadronic breakup, not an unsuppressed pointlike proton-to-neutron transition. The [quark-level](../../../../../quark.md) diagram in the figure displays this allowed charge flow. The [electron](../../../../../electron.md)'s [left-chiral](../../../../../chirality-physics.md) selection is the same as in part d.
