<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Put $r=p/(E+m)$ and assume $p>0$. The common [plane wave](../../../../plane-wave.md) has unit [modulus](../../../../complex-modulus.md). The component [norm](../../../../norm.md) is

$$
\psi^\dagger\psi=|N|^2[(1+r)^2+4(1-r)^2+(1-r)^2+4(1+r)^2]=10|N|^2(1+r^2).
$$

For unit [spinor](../../../../spinor.md) [probability density](../../../../probability-density.md) choose

$$
\boxed{N=\frac1{\sqrt{10(1+r^2)}}=\sqrt{\frac{E+m}{20E}},}
$$

where $E^2=p^2+m^2$ was used. This is [spinor](../../../../spinor.md) [normalization](../../../../normalization.md), not square [normalization](../../../../normalization.md) of a [plane wave](../../../../plane-wave.md) over infinite space; a box or [wavepacket](../../../../wave-packet.md) gives the corresponding spatial [normalization](../../../../normalization.md). If the convention is instead $\psi^\dagger\psi=2E$, use $N=\sqrt{(E+m)/10}$.

For [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) along $+z$, the [positive-helicity](../../../../helicity.md) components are the first and third, while the second and fourth have negative [helicity](../../../../helicity.md). Hence

$$
\boxed{P(h=+1/2)=2|N|^2(1+r^2)=\frac15,\qquad P(h=-1/2)=8|N|^2(1+r^2)=\frac45.}
$$

Both are independent of [speed](../../../../speed.md). In the source's chiral block order the exact [left-chiral](../../../../chirality-physics.md) weight is $1/2+3p/(10E)$, tending to $4/5$ at high [energy](../../../../energy-split.md); [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) and [chirality](../../../../chirality-physics.md) [probabilities](../../../../probability.md) agree only in that limit.
