<h1 id="5/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

A fast charged particle loses [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) by [ionizing](../../../../ionization.md) and exciting [silicon](../../../../silicon.md), creating [electron–hole pairs](../../../../electron-hole-pair.md) along its path. In a reverse-biased [microstrip diode](../../../../silicon-microstrip-detector.md), the [electric field](../../../../electric-field-split.md) separates and drifts the [electrons](../../../../electron.md) and [holes](../../../../electron-hole.md) toward opposite [electrodes](../../../../electrode.md). Their motion induces a [current](../../../../electric-current.md) on the strips, which is integrated by the readout electronics to locate the hit.

The bias depletes the bulk of mobile equilibrium carriers, reducing conduction and [capacitance](../../../../capacitance.md), and supplies rapid directed charge collection before [recombination](../../../../recombination-cosmology.md) or trapping. Without a collecting field, [diffusion](../../../../diffusion.md) is slow and charge spreads or recombines, degrading signal and position measurement. The signal is generated by the traversing particle's [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) deposition, not by the bias creating the [ionization](../../../../ionization.md) charge.
