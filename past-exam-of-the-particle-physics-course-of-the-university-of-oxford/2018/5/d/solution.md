<h1 id="5/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The source labels $L$ as the full chord from $P_1$ to $P_3$, with $L/2$ on each side of its midpoint. The [Right triangle](../../../../right-triangle.md) between the [Circle](../../../../circle.md) centre, chord midpoint and endpoint gives

$$
(R-s)^2+(L/2)^2=R^2,\qquad
\boxed{R=\frac{L^2}{8s}+\frac s2\simeq\frac{L^2}{8s}.}
$$

For fixed geometry and field, the inverse [transverse momentum](../../../../transverse-momentum.md) is

$$
\frac1{p_T}\simeq\frac{8s}{0.299792458\,|q/e|BL^2}.
$$

If the [uncertainty](../../../../measurement-uncertainty.md) is dominated by the central point, $\sigma_s\simeq\sigma_x$. Linear [propagation of uncertainty](../../../../propagation-of-uncertainty.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\sigma(1/p_T)\simeq\frac{8\sigma_x}{0.299792458\,|q/e|BL^2}.}
$$

Using $|q/e|=1$, $B=2\,\mathrm T$, $L=0.6\,\mathrm m$ and $\sigma_x=28.87\,\mu\mathrm m$,

$$
\boxed{\sigma(1/p_T)=1.070\times10^{-3}\,(\mathrm{GeV}/c)^{-1}.}
$$

The inverse-momentum resolution is approximately [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) independent in this spatial-error-only model; the relative [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) resolution grows as $p_T\sigma(1/p_T)$. Endpoint errors and [multiple scattering](../../../../multiple-scattering.md) are excluded by the stated assumptions.
