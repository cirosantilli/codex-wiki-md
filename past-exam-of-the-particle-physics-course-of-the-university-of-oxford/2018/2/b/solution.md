<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At leading order, neglect the [transverse momentum](../../../../transverse-momentum.md) of the incoming [partons](../../../../parton.md) and of the produced [on-shell](../../../../on-shell.md) boson. In the hadron [centre-of-mass frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md),

$$
p_+=\frac{\sqrt s}{2}(x_+,0,0,x_+),\qquad
p_-=\frac{\sqrt s}{2}(x_-,0,0,-x_-).
$$

Then $m_W^2=(p_++p_-)^2=x_+x_-s$, and

$$
y=\frac12\ln\frac{x_+}{x_-}.
$$

Solving these product and ratio equations gives

$$
\boxed{x_+=\sqrt\tau e^y,\qquad x_-=\sqrt\tau e^{-y},\qquad \sqrt\tau=\frac{80.4}{2000}=0.0402.}
$$

Over $-2<y<2$, each [momentum fraction](../../../../parton-momentum-fraction.md) ranges between

$$
\boxed{0.00544<x_\pm<0.2970.}
$$

They are anticorrelated: increasing $y$ increases $x_+$ while decreasing $x_-$. Higher-order recoil changes the simple two-to-one [kinematics](../../../../kinematics.md).
