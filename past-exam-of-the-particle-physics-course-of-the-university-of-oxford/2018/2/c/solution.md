<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The leading subprocess is $u\bar d\to W^+$. There are two beam assignments, and [charge conjugation](../../../../charge-conjugation.md) relates [antiproton](../../../../antiproton.md) [PDFs](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md) to [proton](../../../../proton.md) [PDFs](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md). Therefore

$$
\frac{d\sigma}{dy}\propto u_p(x_+)\bar d_{\bar p}(x_-)+\bar d_p(x_+)u_{\bar p}(x_-)
=u(x_+)d(x_-)+\bar d(x_+)\bar u(x_-).
$$

In a narrow-width leading-order [normalization](../../../../normalization.md) the prefactor is $\pi g^2/(12s)$ when $|V_{ud}|=1$; only the [PDF](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md) dependence is needed for the fractions below.

Write the common sea [PDF](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md) as $S=\bar u=\bar d=\bar s=\bar c=\Sigma/8$. The [Valence and sea decomposition of proton-antiproton W production](../../../../valence-and-sea-decomposition-of-proton-antiproton-w-production.md) gives the three [parton](../../../../parton.md) luminosities

$$
\boxed{\mathcal L_{VV}=u_v(x_+)d_v(x_-),\quad
\mathcal L_{VS}=u_v(x_+)S(x_-)+S(x_+)d_v(x_-),\quad
\mathcal L_{SS}=2S(x_+)S(x_-).}
$$

The factor two in the sea-sea term includes both beam assignments. The [antiproton](../../../../antiproton.md)'s [valence antiquarks](../../../../valence-quark.md) make valence-valence [Annihilation](../../../../annihilation.md) possible.
