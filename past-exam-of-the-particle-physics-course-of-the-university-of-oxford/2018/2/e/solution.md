<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Use leptonic decays: an isolated high [transverse momentum](../../../../transverse-momentum.md) positive [electron](../../../../electron.md) or [muon](../../../../muon.md), together with substantial [missing transverse momentum](../../../../missing-transverse-momentum.md) from the [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md), provides a clean $W^+$ signature. The [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) [transverse momentum](../../../../transverse-momentum.md) has a [Jacobian](../../../../w-decay-jacobian-peak.md) scale near $m_W/2$, and the [lepton](../../../../lepton.md)–missing-momentum [transverse mass](../../../../transverse-mass.md) has an edge near $m_W$, broadened by [width](../../../../decay-width.md), recoil and resolution. [Lepton](../../../../lepton.md) identification, isolation and a prompt primary-vertex origin suppress [Jets](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md) faking [leptons](../../../../lepton.md) and heavy-flavour decays. Missing-momentum quality requirements suppress mismeasured multijet events; a veto on a second oppositely [charged lepton](../../../../charged-lepton.md) suppresses $Z\to\ell^+\ell^-$ when both are reconstructed. Hadronic $W$ decays have a much larger ordinary-QCD background.
