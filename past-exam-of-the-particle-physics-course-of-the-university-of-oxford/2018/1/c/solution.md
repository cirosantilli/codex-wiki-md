<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the longitudinal state,

$$
\int |\mathcal M_0|^2d\Omega=m_W^2g^2\,2\pi\int_{-1}^{1}\frac{1-z^2}{2}\,dz=\frac{4\pi}{3}m_W^2g^2.
$$

Insert this and $p^*=m_W/2$ into the [phase-space](../../../../lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) formula:

$$
\boxed{\Gamma_\ell=\frac{g^2m_W}{48\pi}.}
$$

Rotational invariance makes the integrated [decay probability](../../../../decay-width.md) independent of the orientation of the initial [spin](../../../../spin.md). Both transverse [polarizations](../../../../particle-polarization.md) therefore have the same rate. An unpolarized sample averages these three equal rates; it does not add three copies. Hence its [width](../../../../decay-width.md) into one specified charged-lepton [flavour](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) is also $g^2m_W/(48\pi)$.
