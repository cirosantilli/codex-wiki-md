<h1 id="1/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

At a hadron collider, select $W\to\ell\nu$ events and reconstruct the [transverse mass](../../../../transverse-mass.md)

$$
m_T=\sqrt{2p_T^\ell p_T^\nu(1-\cos\Delta\phi)},
$$

using [missing transverse momentum](../../../../missing-transverse-momentum.md) for the [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md). Fit the shape, especially the region above the nominal [Jacobian](../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) edge, with finite-width resonance templates convolved with detector resolution, recoil modelling and backgrounds. The [Breit-Wigner](../../../../breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md) [width](../../../../decay-width.md) controls this off-shell tail. Calibrated [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and missing-momentum resolutions must be separated from the physical [width](../../../../decay-width.md). This is a lineshape measurement; the [lifetime](../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md) is far too short for a direct flight-distance measurement.
