<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Assume only [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) decay channels, negligible final-state [masses](../../../../mass.md), three [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) families, three [quark](../../../../quark.md) [colours](../../../../color-charge.md) and a [unitary](../../../../unitary-operator.md) [CKM matrix](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md). The [top-quark](../../../../top-quark.md) channels are kinematically closed. Summing the allowed [quark](../../../../quark.md) channels gives $3\sum_{i=u,c}\sum_{j=d,s,b}|V_{ij}|^2\simeq6$ times one leptonic [width](../../../../decay-width.md), while the [leptons](../../../../lepton.md) contribute three. Neglecting [QCD](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) and [electroweak](../../../../electroweak-interaction.md) [radiative corrections](../../../../radiative-correction.md),

$$
\Gamma_W\simeq9\Gamma_\ell=\frac{9g^2m_W}{48\pi}.
$$

The source's rounded CKM entries give almost the same factor; their tiny nonunitarity is not physically significant. Consequently

$$
\boxed{g^2=\frac{48\pi\Gamma_W}{9m_W}=0.4356\pm0.0083,\qquad g=0.6600\pm0.0063.}
$$

The propagated [uncertainty](../../../../measurement-uncertainty.md) uses $\delta g/g=\tfrac12\delta\Gamma_W/\Gamma_W$, taking $m_W$ as fixed. It is the measurement [uncertainty](../../../../measurement-uncertainty.md) within the stated tree-level approximation; omitted [radiative corrections](../../../../radiative-correction.md) are an additional modelling limitation. In particular [QCD](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) increases the hadronic [width](../../../../decay-width.md) and lowers the inferred [coupling](../../../../gauge-coupling.md) when included.

The printed electromagnetic comparison $4\pi/137$ is $e^2$, not the [probability amplitude](../../../../probability-amplitude.md) [coupling](../../../../gauge-coupling.md) $e$. Compare like quantities:

$$
\boxed{\frac{g^2}{e^2}\simeq4.75,\qquad e=\sqrt{4\pi/137}\simeq0.303,\qquad g/e\simeq2.18.}
$$

Equivalently $\alpha_W=g^2/(4\pi)\simeq0.0347$ exceeds $\alpha_{\mathrm{EM}}\simeq1/137$. The [weak interaction](../../../../weak-interaction.md) is weak at low [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) primarily because of the massive [propagator](../../../../propagator.md), rather than an exceptionally small fundamental [coupling](../../../../gauge-coupling.md).
