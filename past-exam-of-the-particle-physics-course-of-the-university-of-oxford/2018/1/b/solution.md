<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

With relativistically normalized one-particle states, [Fermi's golden rule](../../../../fermi-s-golden-rule.md) becomes

$$
\Gamma=\frac1{2M}\int |\mathcal M|^2(2\pi)^4\delta^{(4)}(P-p_1-p_2)
\frac{d^3p_1}{(2\pi)^3 2E_1}\frac{d^3p_2}{(2\pi)^3 2E_2}.
$$

In the parent [rest frame](../../../../rest-frame.md), the three-momentum [delta function](../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) fixes $\mathbf p_2=-\mathbf p_1$. The [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) [delta function](../../../../dirac-delta-function.md) fixes the common magnitude $p^*$; its radial [Jacobian](../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) converts the remaining [phase-space](../../../../lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) factor into

$$
\boxed{\Gamma=\frac{p^*}{32\pi^2M^2}\int|\mathcal M|^2\,d\Omega.}
$$

Only one [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) magnitude remains because the two final [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) are constrained, not independent. Explicitly,

$$
p^*=\frac{\sqrt{[M^2-(m_1+m_2)^2][M^2-(m_1-m_2)^2]}}{2M}.
$$

For leptonic [W boson](../../../../w-boson.md) decay both final [masses](../../../../mass.md) are small compared with $m_W$, including the [tau](../../../../tau-particle.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) at the precision requested, so $p^*\simeq m_W/2$.
