<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For the [Pole forward-backward asymmetry for charged leptons](../../../../pole-forward-backward-asymmetry-for-charged-leptons.md), [integrating](../../../../integral-split.md) the normalized distribution over the two hemispheres cancels the even term in the numerator. Since $\int_{-1}^{1}(1+z^2)dz=8/3$ and $\int_0^1z\,dz-\int_{-1}^0z\,dz=1$,

$$
\boxed{A_{FB}=\frac34\left(\frac{L^2-R^2}{L^2+R^2}\right)^2
=\frac34\left[\frac{1-4\sin^2\theta_W}{1-4\sin^2\theta_W+8\sin^4\theta_W}\right]^2.}
$$

Within this single-$Z$, tree-level model, a nonzero asymmetry requires unequal left/right [coupling](../../../../gauge-coupling.md) magnitudes, demonstrating [parity violation](../../../../parity-violation.md) in the vertices. For $g_L=g_R$ the odd angular term vanishes and $A_{FB}=0$; equal magnitudes with opposite signs also give zero. This inference is process-specific: a forward-backward asymmetry is not universally a parity-odd observable, since reversing both beam and outgoing [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) leaves their relative [Angle](../../../../angle.md) unchanged. Purely electromagnetic [Bhabha scattering](../../../../bhabha-scattering.md) is a concrete parity-conserving process with a forward-peaked, asymmetric distribution.

For the supplied $\sin^2\theta_W=0.23153$,

$$
\boxed{A_{FB}^{\mathrm{pred}}=0.01620.}
$$

The measurement differs by $0.0188-0.01620=0.00260$, or $0.79$ times its quoted [uncertainty](../../../../measurement-uncertainty.md) of $0.0033$, so it is consistent with the prediction. Statistical fluctuations alone preclude exact equality; [photon](../../../../photon.md) exchange, [initial-state radiation](../../../../initial-state-radiation.md) and [electroweak](../../../../electroweak-interaction.md) [radiative corrections](../../../../radiative-correction.md) also modify the idealized pure-resonance prediction and provide physical reasons for small differences.
