<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $z=\cos\theta^\star$. Rotating the spin-one intermediate state between initial and final axes gives [probability amplitudes](../../../../probability-amplitude.md) proportional to $d^1_{\pm1,\pm1}$ for equal [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) [helicities](../../../../helicity.md) and $d^1_{\mp1,\pm1}$ for opposite ones. Thus, apart from a common positive [normalization](../../../../normalization.md),

| Channel | Angular weight \\ | $LL$ | $|g_L^eg_L^f|^2(1+z)^2$ \\ | $RR$ | $|g_R^eg_R^f|^2(1+z)^2$ \\ | $LR$ | $|g_L^eg_R^f|^2(1-z)^2$ \\ | $RL$ | $|g_R^eg_L^f|^2(1-z)^2$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

The common factor $1/4$ from the [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) elements is absorbed into the [normalization](../../../../normalization.md). Distinct external [helicities](../../../../helicity.md) are [orthogonal](../../../../orthogonal-vectors.md), so an unpolarized rate adds their squared [probability amplitudes](../../../../probability-amplitude.md) incoherently.

**The source interchanges the conventional left and right [electron](../../../../electron.md) [coupling](../../../../gauge-coupling.md) labels.** With $g_L=T_3-Q\sin^2\theta_W$ and $g_R=-Q\sin^2\theta_W$, the physical [electron](../../../../electron.md) values are

$$
g_L=-\frac12+\sin^2\theta_W,\qquad g_R=\sin^2\theta_W.
$$

At $\sin^2\theta_W=0$, for example, the left [electron](../../../../electron.md) still carries [weak isospin](../../../../weak-isospin.md) $-1/2$, while the right [electron](../../../../electron.md) is a [weak singlet](../../../../singlet-representation.md); the printed assignment gives the opposite result. This fixes how the helicity-channel table should be interpreted.[§10.1: [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) [neutral-current](../../../../neutral-current.md) interaction and weak-isospin assignments](../../../../electroweak-model-and-constraints-on-new-physics-by-j-erler-and-a-freitas-2024.md)

For a [muon](../../../../muon.md) final state, [lepton universality](../../../../lepton-universality.md) gives the same pair of [couplings](../../../../gauge-coupling.md). Crucially, swapping both labels does not change the summed distribution. Put $L=g_L$, $R=g_R$; then

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\sigma}{dz}=\mathcal N\left[(L^4+R^4)(1+z)^2+2L^2R^2(1-z)^2\right]
=\mathcal N\left[(L^2+R^2)^2(1+z^2)+2(L^2-R^2)^2z\right].}
$$

Here $\mathcal N$ includes the resonance [propagator](../../../../propagator.md), initial [spin](../../../../spin.md) average and common [coupling](../../../../gauge-coupling.md) [normalization](../../../../normalization.md). Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{\frac1\sigma\frac{d\sigma}{dz}=\frac38(1+z^2)+\frac34\mathcal A_\ell^2z,\qquad
\mathcal A_\ell=\frac{L^2-R^2}{L^2+R^2}.}
$$

The source's simultaneous label interchange reverses $\mathcal A_\ell$ but leaves its square unchanged.
