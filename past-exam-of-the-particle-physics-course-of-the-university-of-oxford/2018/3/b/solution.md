<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The two indistinguishable paths to the same final state must be added as [probability amplitudes](../../../../probability-amplitude.md):

$$
\mathcal A_f(t)=A_f g_+(t)+\frac qp\bar A_f g_-(t)=A_f[g_+(t)+\lambda g_-(t)].
$$

Taking the [squared modulus](../../../../complex-modulus.md) gives the time-dependent [decay rate](../../../../decay-width.md), with the common final-state [phase-space](../../../../lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md) [normalization](../../../../normalization.md) absorbed into the [probability amplitudes](../../../../probability-amplitude.md):

$$
\boxed{\Gamma(K(t)\to f)=|A_f|^2\left[|g_+|^2+|\lambda|^2|g_-|^2+2\operatorname{Re}(\lambda g_-g_+^*)\right].}
$$

The cross term is the [interference](../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) between direct decay and decay after [flavour](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) conversion. For reference, diagonal propagation gives $g_\pm=(e_\ell\pm e_h)/2$, where $e_j=e^{-im_jt-\Gamma_jt/2}$ and $t$ is [proper time](../../../../proper-time.md). The unfactored expression remains usable if $A_f=0$, when the ratio $\lambda$ itself is undefined.
