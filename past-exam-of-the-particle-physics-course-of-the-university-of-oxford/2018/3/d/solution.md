<h1 id="3/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

Introduce all four physical [probability amplitudes](../../../../probability-amplitude.md): $A_f,\bar A_f,A_{\bar f},\bar A_{\bar f}$. Under exchange of the [flavour](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) and final-state labels, define

$$
\boxed{\bar\lambda=\frac pq\frac{A_{\bar f}}{\bar A_{\bar f}}.}
$$

This [CP-conjugate decay-amplitude ratio](../../../../cp-conjugate-decay-amplitude-ratio.md) gives the conjugate-channel rates

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\Gamma(\bar K(t)\to\bar f)&=|\bar A_{\bar f}|^2\left[|g_+|^2+|\bar\lambda|^2|g_-|^2+2\operatorname{Re}(\bar\lambda g_-g_+^*)\right],\\
\Gamma(K(t)\to\bar f)&=|A_{\bar f}|^2\left[|g_+|^2+|\bar\lambda^{-1}|^2|g_-|^2+2\operatorname{Re}(\bar\lambda^{-1}g_-g_+^*)\right].
\end{aligned}}
$$

[CP](../../../../cp-symmetry.md) is a [unitary](../../../../unitary-operator.md) discrete transformation, so exchanging state labels does not mean complex-conjugating every strong [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) by fiat. Nor should one assume that CP-conjugate [probability amplitudes](../../../../probability-amplitude.md) are equal while studying [CP violation](../../../../cp-violation.md).

The equality $\bar\lambda=\lambda^{-1}$ holds when

$$
\boxed{\frac{A_{\bar f}}{\bar A_{\bar f}}=\frac{A_f}{\bar A_f},}
$$

in particular when $\bar f$ and $f$ are the same [CP eigenstate](../../../../cp-eigenstate.md) ray, with a consistent state [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md). It is not automatic for two distinct CP-conjugate final states. For example, $p=q$, $A_f=\bar A_{\bar f}=2$ and $\bar A_f=A_{\bar f}=1$ conserve the paired decay [probability amplitudes](../../../../probability-amplitude.md) but give $\lambda=\bar\lambda=1/2$, not inverse values.
