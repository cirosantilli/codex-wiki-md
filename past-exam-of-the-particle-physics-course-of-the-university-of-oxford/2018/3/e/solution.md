<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Assume the usual [semileptonic](../../../../semileptonic-decay.md) [flavour](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) selection rule $\Delta S=\Delta Q$, negligible wrong-sign decay [probability amplitudes](../../../../probability-amplitude.md), and equal magnitudes for CP-conjugate allowed [probability amplitudes](../../../../probability-amplitude.md). Then $f_+=\pi^-\ell^+\nu$ is reached directly only from $K^0$, and $f_-=\pi^+\ell^-\bar\nu$ only from $\bar K^0$. Put $|A_{f_+}|=|\bar A_{f_-}|=|A|$. The four rates are

$$
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\Gamma(K(t)\to f_+)&=|A|^2|g_+|^2,&\Gamma(\bar K(t)\to f_-)&=|A|^2|g_+|^2,\\
\Gamma(K(t)\to f_-)&=|A|^2|q/p|^2|g_-|^2,&\Gamma(\bar K(t)\to f_+)&=|A|^2|p/q|^2|g_-|^2.
\end{aligned}}
$$

There is only one path to each tagged [semileptonic](../../../../semileptonic-decay.md) channel, so no direct/mixed decay [interference](../../../../interference-wave-propagation.md) survives. The [semileptonic wrong-sign mixing asymmetry](../../../../semileptonic-wrong-sign-mixing-asymmetry.md) is

$$
\boxed{a_{\mathrm{SL}}=\frac{\Gamma(\bar K(t)\to f_+)-\Gamma(K(t)\to f_-)}{\Gamma(\bar K(t)\to f_+)+\Gamma(K(t)\to f_-)}
=\frac{1-|q/p|^4}{1+|q/p|^4}.}
$$

It isolates [CP violation in mixing](../../../../cp-violation-in-neutral-meson-mixing.md) under the stated assumptions. Allowing direct [semileptonic](../../../../semileptonic-decay.md) [CP violation](../../../../cp-violation.md) or wrong-sign [probability amplitudes](../../../../probability-amplitude.md) would spoil that isolation.[[CP violation](../../../../cp-violation.md) in the [Quark](../../../../quark.md) Sector, §13.1.3, equations (13.22)–(13.24): mixing and [semileptonic](../../../../semileptonic-decay.md) wrong-sign asymmetry](../../../../review-of-particle-physics-by-particle-data-group-2022.md)
