↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Starting with an [antikaon](../../../../neutral-antikaon.md) instead gives

$$
\bar{\mathcal A}_f(t)=\bar A_f g_++\frac pq A_f g_-=\bar A_f[g_++\lambda^{-1}g_-].
$$

Thus

$$
\boxed{\Gamma(\bar K(t)\to f)=|\bar A_f|^2\left[|g_+|^2+|\lambda^{-1}|^2|g_-|^2+2\operatorname{Re}(\lambda^{-1}g_-g_+^*)\right].}
$$

This factored form assumes $A_f,\bar A_f\ne0$; otherwise use the preceding [probability amplitude](../../../../probability-amplitude.md). The three mechanisms are separated below. Comparisons for distinct final states must pair $f$ with its [CP](../../../../cp-symmetry.md) conjugate $\bar f$.
