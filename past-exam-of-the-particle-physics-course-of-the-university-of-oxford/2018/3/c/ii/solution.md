↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

[CP violation in mixing](../../../../../cp-violation-in-neutral-meson-mixing.md) occurs when

$$
\boxed{|q/p|\ne1.}
$$

The flavour-transition [probabilities](../../../../../probability.md) are proportional to $|q/p|^2|g_-|^2$ for $K^0\to\bar K^0$, and $|p/q|^2|g_-|^2$ for the reverse. Their inequality remains when decay [probability amplitudes](../../../../../probability-amplitude.md) themselves conserve [CP](../../../../../cp-symmetry.md). The coefficients multiplying the mixed-decay terms in the rate equations encode this asymmetry.
