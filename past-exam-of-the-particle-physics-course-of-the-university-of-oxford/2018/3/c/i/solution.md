<h1 id="3/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

Direct [CP violation](../../../../../cp-violation.md) means unequal magnitudes of CP-conjugate decay [probability amplitudes](../../../../../probability-amplitude.md):

$$
\boxed{|A_f|\ne|\bar A_{\bar f}|.}
$$

It changes [decay probabilities](../../../../../decay-width.md) even at $t=0$, before any mixing. For a common [CP eigenstate](../../../../../cp-eigenstate.md) final state, this reduces to $|A_f|\ne|\bar A_f|$. Usually it requires interfering decay contributions with different [weak phases](../../../../../weak-phase-cp-violation.md) and different [strong phases](../../../../../absorptive-phase-of-a-decay-amplitude.md); it is not equivalent to a nonzero weak [phase](../../../../../phase-waves.md) in one isolated [probability amplitude](../../../../../probability-amplitude.md).
