<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [flavour states](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) $K^0=d\bar s$ and $\bar K^0=s\bar d$ have definite [Strangeness](../../../../strangeness.md) and are distinguished by flavour-sensitive production or decay. The [propagation states](../../../../mass-eigenstate.md) diagonalize the [effective Hamiltonian](../../../../effective-hamiltonian-for-neutral-meson-mixing.md) $H_{\mathrm{eff}}=M-i\Gamma/2$; their complex [eigenvalues](../../../../eigenvalue.md) give a pole [mass](../../../../mass.md) and a [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md). The common [flavour](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) combinations specified in the source assume the usual [CPT-symmetric](../../../../cpt-symmetry.md) two-state description and are normalized with $|p|^2+|q|^2=1$.

Choose the [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) convention $CP|K^0\rangle=|\bar K^0\rangle$. If mixing conserves [CP](../../../../cp-symmetry.md), $p=q$ can be chosen, and the symmetric and antisymmetric combinations are [CP](../../../../cp-symmetry.md) even and odd. More generally the equality must be understood with the corresponding flavour-state [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) convention; $|p|=|q|$ alone is not a statement fixing an arbitrary written [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md).

The almost [CP-even](../../../../cp-eigenstate.md) state decays rapidly to [two pions](../../../../pion.md), whereas the almost [CP-odd](../../../../cp-eigenstate.md) state is largely restricted to slower channels such as [three pions](../../../../pion.md). Their very different [lifetimes](../../../../mean-lifetime-physics.md) are easier to distinguish than their tiny [mass](../../../../mass.md) splitting, motivating the names $K_S^0$ and $K_L^0$. In the source's first notation the subscript $L$ means _light_, not _long-lived_: the light state is identified with the short-lived [kaon](../../../../kaon.md), and the heavy state with the long-lived [kaon](../../../../kaon.md). Below, $\ell,h$ label light/heavy to avoid that collision.
