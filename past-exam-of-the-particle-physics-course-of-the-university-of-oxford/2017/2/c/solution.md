<h1 id="2/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The three [on-shell](../../../../on-shell.md) spatial [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) have nine components. Four [conservation](../../../../conservation-law.md) constraints leave five, and an unobserved overall [spatial rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) removes three for the [scalar](../../../../scalar-field.md) decay, leaving two internal kinematic variables. The pair invariants obey

$$
\boxed{s_{12}+s_{13}+s_{23}=M_0^2+m_1^2+m_2^2+m_3^2.}
$$

Thus two determine the third. They also determine each rest-frame daughter [energy](../../../../energy-split.md),

$$
E_i=\frac{M_0^2+m_i^2-s_{jk}}{2M_0},
$$

and hence all [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) magnitudes and pairwise angles. The [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) triangle is fixed up to [overall orientation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md). The [Dalitz plot](../../../../dalitz-plot.md) describes this internal [kinematics](../../../../kinematics.md), rather than claiming to record three independently oriented [Four-vectors](../../../../four-vector.md). Polarized parents or additional measured [spin](../../../../spin.md) information can require orientation variables beyond the two invariant axes.
