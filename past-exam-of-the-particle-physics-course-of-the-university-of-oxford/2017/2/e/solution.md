<h1 id="2/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The charged two-pion and three-pion channels can be assigned to the same spin-zero [kaon](../../../../kaon.md) by their parent [mass](../../../../mass.md) and lifetime. For two spinless [pions](../../../../pion.md), total [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) zero forces their relative [orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) to zero, so the product of their [intrinsic parities](../../../../intrinsic-parity.md) gives

$$
\boxed{P_{2\pi}=(-1)^2(-1)^0=+1.}
$$

For three spinless [pions](../../../../pion.md), use Jacobi orbital angular [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) $l,L$. Coupling them to total zero requires $l=L$, and hence

$$
\boxed{P_{3\pi}=(-1)^3(-1)^{l+L}=-1.}
$$

Thus both channels cannot conserve [parity](../../../../parity.md) in decays of one parent with a definite [parity](../../../../parity.md). The [weak decay](../../../../weak-interaction.md) interaction permits the observed coexistence.

[Dalitz plots](../../../../dalitz-plot.md) identify the three-body events, determine their internal [kinematics](../../../../kinematics.md), and allow [amplitude](../../../../probability-amplitude.md) and [angular-momentum analyses](../../../../angular-momentum.md) through their populations and [resonance](../../../../resonance.md) structures. Together with the [spin](../../../../spin.md) and parent-identity evidence, they support the [parity](../../../../parity.md) assignment. They are not a standalone parity-odd observable: reversing all three [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) leaves every $s_{ij}$, and therefore every point on the plot, unchanged. A flat distribution by itself cannot establish [parity violation](../../../../parity-violation.md) without the channel and [spin](../../../../spin.md) information.
