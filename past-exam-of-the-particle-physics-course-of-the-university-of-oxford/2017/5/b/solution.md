<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Treat a [nucleon](../../../../nucleon.md) as incoherent collinear spin-half [quarks](../../../../quark.md) and [antiquarks](../../../../antiquark.md) carrying fractions $x$ of its [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md). Neglect their [masses](../../../../mass.md) and transverse motion, use a [charged-current](../../../../charged-current.md) four-fermion interaction, and take $Q^2\ll m_W^2$. The subprocess [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is $\hat s\simeq xs$. [Antineutrino](../../../../antineutrino.md) scattering on a [quark](../../../../quark.md) has the same selected-current [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) factor $(1-y)^2$ as in part a; crossing to an [antiquark](../../../../antiquark.md) changes the allowed [spin](../../../../spin.md) pairing and gives a flat distribution. Multiplying the elementary strength by the respective [parton densities](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\frac{d^2\sigma(\bar\nu N)}{dx\,dy}=\frac{G_F^2sx}{\pi}\left[(1-y)^2q(x)+\bar q(x)\right].}
$$

Here $q,\bar q$ are the effective [weak flavour combinations](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md) with the [isoscalar](../../../../isoscalar-target.md) proton-neutron average and [CKM weights](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) absorbed into their definitions. With only light [flavours](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) and [charge symmetry](../../../../charge-symmetry-of-nucleon-parton-distributions.md), the relevant up and down combinations coincide on an [isoscalar target](../../../../isoscalar-target.md). Rescaling those density definitions requires rescaling the prefactor consistently.

The equation is a leading-order scaling result: it neglects logarithmic [QCD](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) evolution, heavy-quark thresholds, [higher-twist](../../../../higher-twist.md) and [target-mass](../../../../deep-inelastic-scattering.md) corrections, [nuclear modifications](../../../../nuclear-parton-distribution-function.md), and [weak-propagator](../../../../w-boson-propagator.md) suppression. The current produces $\mu^+$ for an incident [muon](../../../../muon.md) [antineutrino](../../../../antineutrino.md). At higher [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) transfer the weak propagator supplies a factor approximately $[m_W^2/(m_W^2+Q^2)]^2$, and at low [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) [resonance](../../../../resonance.md) and quasielastic scattering invalidate a pure [DIS](../../../../deep-inelastic-scattering.md) description.
