<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For a [nucleon](../../../../nucleon.md) at rest and beam [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) well above its [mass](../../../../mass.md) scales, $s\simeq2m_NE$. If the leading [parton distributions](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md) are approximately independent of beam [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), integration over $x,y$ therefore makes both total [cross sections](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) approximately proportional to $E$. The plotted plateaus are evidence for scattering on approximately pointlike constituents in this range, with the departures expected from [QCD](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) evolution and threshold effects.

Define the momentum-weighted contents $Q=\int_0^1xq(x)dx$ and $\bar Q=\int_0^1x\bar q(x)dx$. Since $\int_0^1(1-y)^2dy=1/3$, the two [cross sections](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) obey

$$
\frac{\sigma_\nu}{E}\propto Q+\frac{\bar Q}{3},\qquad
\frac{\sigma_{\bar\nu}}{E}\propto\frac Q3+\bar Q.
$$

Read the original high-energy plateaus as approximately $0.67$ and $0.33$ in the plotted $10^{-38}\,\mathrm{cm^2/GeV}$ units. With $R=\sigma_{\bar\nu}/\sigma_\nu\simeq1/2$ and $r=\bar Q/Q$,

$$
R=\frac{1/3+r}{1+r/3},\qquad
\boxed{r=\frac{3R-1}{3-R}\simeq0.20.}
$$

Using the literal readings $0.33/0.67$ gives $r=0.1905$, so twenty percent is an appropriate graphical estimate, not a precision fit. A purely valence target would have $R=1/3$, while a fully symmetric sea would give $R=1$.

The [neutrino-antineutrino DIS sea momentum ratio](../../../../neutrino-antineutrino-dis-sea-momentum-ratio.md) is a ratio of [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) fractions in the effective [flavour](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) combinations, not an unweighted count of [antiquarks](../../../../antiquark.md). The cross-section integral contains $xq(x)$: it cannot determine $\int\bar q/\int q$ without additional assumptions about their shapes. A distribution growing at small $x$ can have a large number integral while its [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) integral remains finite.
