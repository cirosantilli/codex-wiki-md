↑ **Parent:** [1](../1.md)

Assume first an ultrarelativistic [charged-current](../../../../../charged-current.md) interaction with negligible [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [mass](../../../../../mass.md) in the centre-of-mass [spin](../../../../../spin.md) analysis. Choose the incident [antineutrino](../../../../../antineutrino.md) along $+z$. Its right [helicity](../../../../../helicity.md) gives [spin](../../../../../spin.md) $+z$; the interacting [left-helicity](../../../../../helicity.md) [electron](../../../../../electron.md) moves along $-z$ and also has [spin](../../../../../spin.md) $+z$. The total initial projection is $+1$. The final [antineutrino](../../../../../antineutrino.md) and selected [electron](../../../../../electron.md) likewise have total [spin](../../../../../spin.md) $+1$ along the outgoing [antineutrino](../../../../../antineutrino.md) direction. The spin-one [rotation](../../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) therefore gives

$$
\mathcal M\propto d^1_{11}(\theta^\star)=\frac{1+\cos\theta^\star}{2},\qquad
\boxed{\frac{d\sigma_{\mathrm{CC}}}{d\cos\theta^\star}\propto(1+\cos\theta^\star)^2.}
$$

An unpolarized [electron](../../../../../electron.md) target changes the [normalization](../../../../../normalization.md) through the [spin](../../../../../spin.md) average, not this selected-current shape. For [electron](../../../../../electron.md) [antineutrinos](../../../../../antineutrino.md) the [charged-current](../../../../../charged-current.md) diagram is the $s$-channel $W^-$ graph. A [contact approximation](../../../../../fermi-interaction.md) further requires $m_e^2\ll s\ll m_W^2,m_Z^2$.

**The full Standard Model also has [neutral-current](../../../../../neutral-current.md) $Z$ exchange.** In the contact limit it supplies a right-electron contribution as well as interference in the left-electron channel. With $g_L=1/2+\sin^2\theta_W$, $g_R=\sin^2\theta_W$, the full high-energy shape is proportional to $g_L^2(1+\cos\theta^\star)^2/4+g_R^2$. Thus the pure squared angular form is an explicitly stated [charged-current](../../../../../charged-current.md) approximation, not an exact prediction for all Standard Model contributions. At [momentum](../../../../../momentum-split.md) transfers comparable to the [weak-boson](../../../../../electroweak-gauge-boson.md) [masses](../../../../../mass.md), propagators must also be retained.[§10.3.1, equation (10.30) and following electron-neutrino charged-current shift: both left- and right-electron weights](../../../../../electroweak-model-and-constraints-on-new-physics-by-j-erler-and-a-freitas-2024.md)
