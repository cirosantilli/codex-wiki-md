<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

For nonzero [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md), physical [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) is the [spin projection](../../../../spin.md) along the direction of motion,

$$
\boxed{\hat h=\frac{\mathbf S\cdot\mathbf p}{|\mathbf p|}=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\hat{\mathbf p}&0\\0&\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\hat{\mathbf p}\end{pmatrix},\qquad h=\pm\frac12.}
$$

The convention without $1/2$ labels the same two [eigenspaces](../../../../eigenspace.md) by $\pm1$. Write $\hat{\mathbf p}=(\sin\theta\cos\varphi,\sin\theta\sin\varphi,\cos\theta)$. [Normalized](../../../../normalization.md) [two-component eigenvectors](../../../../spinor.md) are

$$
\chi_+=\begin{pmatrix}\cos(\theta/2)\\e^{i\varphi}\sin(\theta/2)\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\chi_-=\begin{pmatrix}-e^{-i\varphi}\sin(\theta/2)\\\cos(\theta/2)\end{pmatrix},\qquad
(\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\hat{\mathbf p})\chi_\pm=\pm\chi_\pm.
$$

Each can carry an arbitrary [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md). Before imposing an [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) equation, the general [four-component](../../../../dirac-spinor.md) [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) [eigenvector](../../../../eigenvector.md) is $(a\chi_\pm,b\chi_\pm)^{\mathsf T}$, with arbitrary complex $a,b$; unit [Norm](../../../../norm.md) imposes $|a|^2+|b|^2=1$. For a [positive-energy](../../../../energy-eigenvalue-split.md) free [electron](../../../../electron.md), the [Dirac equation](../../../../dirac-equation.md) fixes their ratio and gives

$$
\boxed{u_\pm(\mathbf p)=\sqrt{\frac{E+m}{2E}}\begin{pmatrix}\chi_\pm\\\displaystyle\pm\frac{|\mathbf p|}{E+m}\chi_\pm\end{pmatrix},\qquad u_\pm^\dagger u_\pm=1.}
$$

Multiplying by an arbitrary overall [amplitude](../../../../probability-amplitude.md) and the common [plane wave](../../../../plane-wave.md) supplies the corresponding free [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) eigenstate. [Helicity](../../../../helicity.md) has no preferred direction at $\mathbf p=0$. For a massive particle a [boost](../../../../lorentz-boost.md) can overtake its [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and change its [helicity](../../../../helicity.md); it should not be identified with [Lorentz-invariant](../../../../lorentz-invariance.md) [chirality](../../../../chirality-physics.md).
