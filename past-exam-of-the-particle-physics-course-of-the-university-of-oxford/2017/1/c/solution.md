<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

In the supplied [Dirac representation](../../../../dirac-representation-of-the-gamma-matrices.md), $\gamma^5$ exchanges the upper and lower blocks. Therefore

$$
P_L=\frac{1-\gamma^5}{2}=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}I&-I\\-I&I\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Define $\eta=[I-\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf p/(E+m)]\chi$. Acting with this [projector](../../../../projection-linear-algebra.md) gives

$$
\boxed{\psi_L=P_L\psi=\frac N2\begin{pmatrix}\eta\\-\eta\end{pmatrix}e^{i(\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf r-Et)}.}
$$

For completeness, its two independent components are

$$
\eta_1=\frac{\sqrt5}{3}\left(1-\frac{p_z}{E+m}\right)-\frac{2(p_x-ip_y)}{3(E+m)},\qquad
\eta_2=-\frac{\sqrt5(p_x+ip_y)}{3(E+m)}+\frac23\left(1+\frac{p_z}{E+m}\right).
$$

The equal-and-opposite block structure verifies $\gamma^5\psi_L=-\psi_L$. Projection isolates a [chiral component](../../../../chirality-physics.md) of the massive [Dirac](../../../../dirac-field.md) solution; that component alone generally does not satisfy an independent massive [Dirac equation](../../../../dirac-equation.md), since the [mass](../../../../mass.md) couples both [chiralities](../../../../chirality-physics.md).
