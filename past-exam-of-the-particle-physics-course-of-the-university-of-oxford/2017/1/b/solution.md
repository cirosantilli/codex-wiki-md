<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Let $\chi=(\sqrt5/3,2/3)^{\mathsf T}$, so $\chi^\dagger\chi=1$. The [Pauli identity](../../../../pauli-matrices.md) $(\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf p)^2=|\mathbf p|^2I$ gives

$$
\psi^\dagger\psi=|N|^2\left(1+\frac{|\mathbf p|^2}{(E+m)^2}\right)=|N|^2\frac{2E}{E+m},
$$

using $E^2=|\mathbf p|^2+m^2$. Thus unit [spinor](../../../../spinor.md) [probability density](../../../../probability-density.md) is obtained with

$$
\boxed{N=\sqrt{\frac{E+m}{2E}}.}
$$

An arbitrary constant [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) is immaterial. For the alternative [Lorentz normalization](../../../../relativistic-normalization-of-a-one-particle-state.md) $u^\dagger u=2E$, choose $N=\sqrt{E+m}$. Neither convention makes an infinite [plane wave](../../../../plane-wave.md) [square-integrable](../../../../square-integrable-function.md); a box-normalized state includes an additional $V^{-1/2}$, or one forms a normalizable [wave packet](../../../../wave-packet.md).
