<h1 id="1/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

The [charged weak current](../../../../charged-current.md) contains $\bar\psi\gamma^\mu P_L\psi$. For an ultrarelativistic external [fermion](../../../../fermion.md), it selects [negative helicity](../../../../helicity.md); its [antifermion](../../../../antifermion.md) partner has [positive helicity](../../../../helicity.md). Wrong-helicity [charged-current](../../../../charged-current.md) [amplitudes](../../../../probability-amplitude.md) vanish in the massless limit and are suppressed by powers of $m/E$ at finite [mass](../../../../mass.md). The active [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) is consequently [left-helicity](../../../../helicity.md) and its [antineutrino](../../../../antineutrino.md) [right-helicity](../../../../helicity.md) in this limit.

This is a [charged-current](../../../../charged-current.md) statement, not a prohibition on every [right-helicity](../../../../helicity.md) Standard Model [fermion](../../../../fermion.md). Electromagnetic and strong vector interactions act on both [chiralities](../../../../chirality-physics.md) and conserve [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) along a massless [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) line. A [neutral-current](../../../../neutral-current.md) $Z$ vertex also couples to both charged-fermion [chiralities](../../../../chirality-physics.md) with unequal strengths. For example, a [right-helicity](../../../../helicity.md) [electron](../../../../electron.md) can scatter electromagnetically: it is not excluded from the Standard Model. In [vector-current](../../../../vector-current.md) pair [annihilation](../../../../annihilation.md) the massless [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) and [antifermion](../../../../antifermion.md) have opposite [helicities](../../../../helicity.md); a purely [charged-current](../../../../charged-current.md) vertex keeps only the left-fermion/right-antifermion choice. [Yukawa interactions](../../../../yukawa-interaction.md) connect left and right fields and must be distinguished from these [vector-current](../../../../vector-current.md) selection rules.
