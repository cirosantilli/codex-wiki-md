<h1 id="6/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Top decay occurs before a [colour-bound](../../../../hadronization.md) hadron can form. Using representative scales $\Gamma_t\sim1$–$1.5\,\mathrm{GeV}$ and $\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}\sim0.2\,\mathrm{GeV}$,

$$
\boxed{\tau_t\sim\frac\hbar{\Gamma_t}\sim5\times10^{-25}\,\mathrm s
<\tau_{\mathrm{had}}\sim\frac\hbar{\Lambda_{\mathrm{QCD}}}\sim3\times10^{-24}\,\mathrm s.}
$$

Thus [top quarks](../../../../top-quark.md) do not live long enough to form ordinary top-containing mesons. The decay products still participate in [QCD](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) radiation and [hadronization](../../../../hadronization.md), so “free decay” here means decay before binding, not a stable observable free [coloured](../../../../color-charge.md) asymptotic particle.
