<h1 id="6/d/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The literal $t\to qZ\gamma$ final state has a three-vertex [loop](../../../../loop-feynman-diagram.md) using the [Standard Model quartic vertex](../../../../quartic-w-photon-z-gauge-coupling.md) $W^+W^-Z\gamma$. The two other vertices are $t\to d_iW^+$ and $d_iW^+\to q$, with $q=u,c$. The internal [down-type quark](../../../../down-quark.md) joins those two vertices, while two charged-vector propagators join them through the quartic vertex emitting both neutral bosons. The bottom panel of the figure shows this topology, satisfying the three-vertex hint without dropping the [photon](../../../../photon.md). Its additional electromagnetic coupling is contained in the four-leg vertex; counting vertices alone does not determine coupling order.

The companion single-$Z$ [penguin](../../../../penguin-diagram.md) diagram illustrates $t\to qZ$: it instead attaches the neutral boson to the internal [quark](../../../../quark.md) line and has three cubic vertices. These are different final states. A complete [gauge-invariant](../../../../gauge-invariance.md) [amplitude](../../../../probability-amplitude.md) requires all companion attachments; for $qZ\gamma$ these include [photon](../../../../photon.md) radiation from charged lines as well as the quartic graph.

Both [flavour](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) sums have the form

$$
\mathcal A(t\to qZ[\gamma])\propto\sum_{i=d,s,b}V_{ti}V_{qi}^*F_i,
$$

where the [loop](../../../../loop-feynman-diagram.md) function also depends on the final-state [kinematics](../../../../kinematics.md). After cancellation of a mass-independent part by [GIM mechanism](../../../../gim-mechanism.md), the [bottom-quark](../../../../bottom-quark.md) [mass](../../../../mass.md) gives the largest surviving contribution: $m_b^2\gg m_s^2,m_d^2$, while $|V_{tb}V_{cb}|\simeq0.042$ is not unusually small compared with the strange combination. The larger $|V_{cb}|$ than $|V_{ub}|$ then favours final [charm](../../../../charm-quark.md). Schematically,

$$
\boxed{t\to cZ\gamma\text{ with virtual }b\text{ is the favoured flavour combination},\qquad
\frac{|\mathcal A_c|^2}{|\mathcal A_u|^2}\sim\frac{|V_{cb}|^2}{|V_{ub}|^2}\simeq110.}
$$

The same [flavour](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) preference applies to the separate two-body modes $t\to cZ$ and $t\to c\gamma$. This estimates a relative [flavour](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) preference, not an absolute [branching ratio](../../../../branching-fraction.md). [Loop](../../../../loop-feynman-diagram.md) factors and mass-difference suppression remain, so these channels are fully compatible with [GIM](../../../../gim-mechanism.md). A resolved extra [photon](../../../../photon.md) also introduces another electromagnetic coupling and three-body [phase space](../../../../lorentz-invariant-phase-space.md); an experimental $Z\gamma$ definition needs photon-energy and [isolation](../../../../particle-isolation.md) cuts to distinguish hard radiation from the soft limit.
