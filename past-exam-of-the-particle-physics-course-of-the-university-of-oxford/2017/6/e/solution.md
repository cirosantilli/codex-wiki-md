<h1 id="6/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

For a $t\to cZ$ search, the cleanest neutral-boson reconstruction is $Z\to e^+e^-$ or $\mu^+\mu^-$: require an [isolated](../../../../lepton-isolation.md) opposite-sign same-flavour pair near the $Z$ [mass](../../../../mass.md) and a [jet](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md) forming the top [mass](../../../../mass.md) with that pair. In top-pair events, tagging the other top through its ordinary $bW$ decay provides a [bottom jet](../../../../bottom-quark-jet-tagging.md) and, if its $W$ is leptonic, another [isolated](../../../../lepton-isolation.md) lepton and [missing transverse momentum](../../../../missing-transverse-momentum.md). Kinematic reconstruction and heavy-flavour information help reject vector-boson-plus-jets and other backgrounds. Hadronic or invisible $Z$ decays have higher rate but less distinctive backgrounds or incomplete [mass](../../../../mass.md) reconstruction.

For the literal $t\to cZ\gamma$ channel, additionally require a prompt [isolated](../../../../particle-isolation.md) [photon](../../../../photon.md) and reconstruct the top with $Z+\gamma+$[jet](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md). For the alternative $t\to c\gamma$, use an [isolated](../../../../particle-isolation.md) energetic [photon](../../../../photon.md) and [jet](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md) near the top [mass](../../../../mass.md), again exploiting the other top as a tag. These are search signatures; no present-day observation claim is needed to interpret the historical question.
