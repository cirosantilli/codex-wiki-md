<h1 id="6/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The weak [charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) couples an [up-type quark](../../../../up-quark.md) to the rotated down field $d'_{iL}=\sum_jV_{ij}d_{jL}$. A [neutral current](../../../../neutral-current.md) is [flavour](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) universal within equal-charge [quarks](../../../../quark.md). Rotating it into the [mass](../../../../mass.md) basis therefore gives

$$
\sum_i\bar d'_{iL}\gamma^\mu d'_{iL}
=\sum_{j,k}\bar d_{jL}\gamma^\mu(V^\dagger V)_{jk}d_{kL}
=\sum_j\bar d_{jL}\gamma^\mu d_{jL}.
$$

The off-diagonal terms vanish by [CKM unitarity](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md), so no tree-level [flavour-changing neutral current](../../../../flavor-changing-neutral-current.md) is generated. In the two-generation description, the orthogonal strange-charm partner completes the [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) whose single down-strange combination alone would have produced an off-diagonal neutral coupling. The complete multiplet supplies the cancellation.

At [loop](../../../../loop-feynman-diagram.md) level, a flavour-changing [amplitude](../../../../probability-amplitude.md) has the structure

$$
\mathcal A_{a\to b}\propto\sum_iV_{ai}V_{bi}^*F(m_i^2/m_W^2),\qquad a\ne b.
$$

If all internal [masses](../../../../mass.md) were equal, $F$ would be common and $\sum_iV_{ai}V_{bi}^*=0$. Subtracting an arbitrary common value makes the suppression explicit:

$$
\boxed{\mathcal A_{a\to b}\propto\sum_iV_{ai}V_{bi}^*\left[F(m_i^2/m_W^2)-F(m_{\mathrm{ref}}^2/m_W^2)\right].}
$$

This is the [Glashow-Iliopoulos-Maiani mechanism](../../../../glashow-iliopoulos-maiani-mechanism.md). Unequal [masses](../../../../mass.md) leave small loop-level [flavour-changing neutral currents](../../../../flavor-changing-neutral-current.md), so the statement that they are never observed would be too strong: the mechanism suppresses them and forbids them at tree level. The cancellation requires complex [CKM](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) products; the rounded table of absolute values alone must not be summed as if every [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) were positive.
