<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [lepton mixing matrix](../../../../lepton-mixing-matrix.md) relates [charged-current](../../../../charged-current.md) [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) [flavour fields](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) to [mass eigenfields](../../../../mass-eigenstate.md): $\nu_{\alpha L}=\sum_iU_{\alpha i}\nu_{iL}$. Nondegenerate [masses](../../../../mass.md) then give relative propagation [phases](../../../../phase-waves.md) and [flavour](../../../../flavor-eigenstate.md) [oscillations](../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md). This is the three-massive-neutrino extension of the minimal massless-neutrino Standard Model.

A [unitary](../../../../unitary-matrix.md) three-by-three [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) has nine real parameters: three [rotation](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) angles and six [phases](../../../../phase-waves.md). For [Dirac](../../../../dirac-field.md) leptons, the independent field rephasings remove five [phases](../../../../phase-waves.md), because one common [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) acts trivially. Three angles and one physical [Dirac phase](../../../../cp-violation.md) remain. For [Majorana](../../../../majorana-neutrino.md) [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md) two additional [phases](../../../../phase-waves.md) cannot be removed, but they cancel from ordinary [oscillation](../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md) [probabilities](../../../../probability.md).

**The [phases](../../../../phase-waves.md) printed in the lower two rows do not give a [unitary matrix](../../../../unitary-matrix.md) for general $\delta$.** A consistent convention is $U=R_{23}U_{13}(\delta)R_{12}$, where the real [rotations](../../../../rotation-mathematics.md) use the indicated [mixing angles](../../../../mixing-angle.md) and

$$
\boxed{U_{13}(\delta)=\begin{pmatrix}c_{13}&0&s_{13}e^{-i\delta}\\0&1&0\\-s_{13}e^{i\delta}&0&c_{13}\end{pmatrix}.}
$$

The lower-row [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) factors in the resulting full [matrix](../../../../matrix.md) are $e^{+i\delta}$, opposite to the $e^{-i\delta}$ in $U_{e3}$. For the source's all-minus assignment, the [scalar](../../../../scalar-field.md) product of the [electron](../../../../electron.md) row with the conjugated [muon](../../../../muon.md) row is $-2i\,s_{23}s_{13}c_{13}\sin\delta$, which is nonzero at $\delta=\pi/2$ and nonzero [mixing](../../../../flavour-mixing.md). This supplies an explicit counterexample. The electron-row moduli used below are unaffected by this correction.[Neutrino Masses, Mixing, and Oscillations, §14.3 and equation (14.34): opposite signs of the physical phase in the standard matrix](../../../../review-of-particle-physics-by-particle-data-group-2022.md)
