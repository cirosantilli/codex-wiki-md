<h1 id="3/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

Place a far detector near the first atmospheric [disappearance](../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md) maximum, approximately $1.2\,\mathrm{km}$ for the source's monochromatic [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and splitting, and a nearly identical near detector a few hundred metres from the [reactor](../../../../nuclear-reactor.md) complex. Their flux-corrected comparison largely cancels [reactor](../../../../nuclear-reactor.md) power, emitted-spectrum and absolute-efficiency uncertainties. For a broad [reactor](../../../../nuclear-reactor.md) spectrum one fits energy-dependent [disappearance](../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md) instead of treating that single baseline as a universal exact optimum.

Detect $\bar\nu_ep\to e^+n$ in a [hydrogen-rich target](../../../../inverse-beta-decay.md), for example [liquid scintillator](../../../../liquid-scintillator.md) with neutron-capture enhancement. The prompt [Positron](../../../../positron.md) signal estimates [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md); the delayed neutron-capture signal supplies a time-and-position coincidence that strongly reduces random backgrounds. Use sufficient target [mass](../../../../mass.md) and exposure, low radioactivity, overburden and a [muon veto](../../../../active-detector-veto.md) to reduce cosmogenic backgrounds, [calibrated](../../../../calorimeter-calibration.md) relative [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) response and efficiency, and monitored [reactor](../../../../nuclear-reactor.md) powers and baselines. Account for multiple [reactor](../../../../nuclear-reactor.md) cores, spent-fuel backgrounds and accidental coincidences. At kilometre baselines the slow solar [phase](../../../../phase-waves.md) is small, so the [disappearance](../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md) depth directly probes $\sin^2(2\theta_{13})$ with little sensitivity to the [CP phase](../../../../cp-violation.md) or [mass ordering](../../../../neutrino-mass-ordering.md).
