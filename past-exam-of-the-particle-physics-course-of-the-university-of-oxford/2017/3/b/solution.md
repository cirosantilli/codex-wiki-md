<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use $\operatorname{Re}(e^{ia}-1)=\cos a-1=-2\sin^2(a/2)$. With $k=1/4$, the source's $\Delta_{ij}=2k(\phi_i-\phi_j)$ is precisely $(\phi_i-\phi_j)/2=(m_i^2-m_j^2)L/(4E)$. The electron-row [probabilities](../../../../probability.md) are $c_{12}^2c_{13}^2$, $s_{12}^2c_{13}^2$, and $s_{13}^2$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{P_{ee}=1-c_{13}^4\sin^2(2\theta_{12})\sin^2\Delta_{12}
-\sin^2(2\theta_{13})\left[c_{12}^2\sin^2\Delta_{13}+s_{12}^2\sin^2\Delta_{23}\right].}
$$

Neither $\theta_{23}$ nor the [Dirac phase](../../../../cp-violation.md) appears in vacuum [electron-flavour survival](../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md). The same vacuum [survival expression](../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md) applies to [reactor](../../../../nuclear-reactor.md) [electron](../../../../electron.md) [antineutrinos](../../../../antineutrino.md). A sufficiently large [matter effect](../../../../neutrino-matter-potential.md) would require a different propagation calculation, but is negligible at the short [reactor](../../../../nuclear-reactor.md) baselines used for measuring $\theta_{13}$.
