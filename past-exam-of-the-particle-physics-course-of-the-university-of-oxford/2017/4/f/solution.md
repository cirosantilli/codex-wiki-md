<h1 id="4/f/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [F](../f.md)

For independent contributions the parametrization means

$$
\boxed{\sigma^2(E)=A^2E+B^2+C^2E^2.}
$$

The [stochastic](../../../../statistical-fluctuation.md) coefficient $A$ includes fluctuations in the sampled [shower](../../../../electromagnetic-shower.md) fraction, the finite number of signal quanta, and intrinsic [shower](../../../../electromagnetic-shower.md) fluctuations. A number of detected quanta proportional to $E$ has fluctuations proportional to $\sqrt E$, giving relative resolution proportional to $E^{-1/2}$. The [noise](../../../../noise-signal-processing.md) coefficient $B$ describes an approximately fixed energy-equivalent readout uncertainty, including [electronic noise](../../../../electronic-noise.md) and, for specified running conditions, [Pile-up](../../../../collision-pileup.md) contributions. The constant term $C$ represents residual fractional nonuniformity, [calibration](../../../../calorimeter-calibration.md) and response variations, imperfect correction for inactive material, and leakage effects over the fitted range. The simple three-term model is an approximation; leakage and [Pile-up](../../../../collision-pileup.md) need not follow these powers exactly.

A [homogeneous calorimeter](../../../../homogeneous-calorimeter.md) uses the showering material itself as the active medium, for example a [scintillating crystal](../../../../scintillator.md). It removes the absorber-versus-active sampling fluctuation and can have a much smaller [stochastic](../../../../statistical-fluctuation.md) term, but it does not eliminate [signal statistics](../../../../poisson-distribution.md) or intrinsic [shower](../../../../electromagnetic-shower.md) fluctuations. Its [noise](../../../../noise-signal-processing.md) and constant terms are not automatically smaller: they depend on light collection, [calibration](../../../../calorimeter-calibration.md), temperature, radiation damage, electronics and geometry. [Homogeneous detectors](../../../../homogeneous-calorimeter.md) can offer excellent [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) resolution and compact construction, but large crystal volumes can be costly and fine longitudinal segmentation difficult. [Sampling detectors](../../../../sampling-calorimeter.md) often provide economical large coverage and flexible segmentation with robust [absorbers](../../../../sampling-calorimeter.md), at the cost of sampling fluctuations and inactive material. If [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) is expressed in GeV, $A$ has units $\mathrm{GeV}^{1/2}$, $B$ has units GeV and $C$ is dimensionless.
