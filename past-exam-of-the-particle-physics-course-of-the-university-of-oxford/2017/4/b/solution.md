↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For [bremsstrahlung](../../../../bremsstrahlung.md), the [electron](../../../../electron.md) scatters from the [nucleus](../../../../atomic-nucleus-split.md) by virtual-photon exchange and emits a real [photon](../../../../photon.md): $e^-Z\to e^-\gamma Z$. The two lowest-order graphs have emission before or after the Coulomb scattering. One is drawn in the figure; interchanging the emission and exchange vertices gives the other. Their sum is needed for the full gauge-consistent [amplitude](../../../../probability-amplitude.md). Reversing the charged-lepton arrows gives [Positron](../../../../positron.md) [bremsstrahlung](../../../../bremsstrahlung.md), with the same underlying electromagnetic mechanism.
