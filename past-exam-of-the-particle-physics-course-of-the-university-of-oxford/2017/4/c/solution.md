<h1 id="4/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

At low charged-lepton [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), collisional [ionization](../../../../ionization.md) and [excitation](../../../../elementary-excitation.md) dominate over radiation. A slowing [Positron](../../../../positron.md) also eventually annihilates, usually yielding [photons](../../../../photon.md). Low-energy [photons](../../../../photon.md) lose [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) through [Compton scattering](../../../../compton-scattering.md) and, at sufficiently low [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), [photoelectric absorption](../../../../photoelectric-effect.md); [pair production](../../../../pair-production.md) is kinematically unavailable below its threshold.

The [electron](../../../../electron.md) [critical energy](../../../../critical-energy-of-an-electromagnetic-shower.md) $E_c$ is the [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) where mean [bremsstrahlung](../../../../bremsstrahlung.md) and collisional losses are equal. With $-(dE/dx)_{\mathrm{rad}}\simeq E/X_0$ and a slowly varying collisional loss,

$$
\boxed{\left|\frac{dE}{dx}\right|_{\mathrm{rad},E_c}=\left|\frac{dE}{dx}\right|_{\mathrm{coll},E_c}.}
$$

Above this [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), radiation and [pair creation](../../../../pair-production.md) multiply [shower](../../../../electromagnetic-shower.md) particles efficiently. Below it, charged particles mainly deposit their [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) by collisions and the [shower](../../../../electromagnetic-shower.md) dies out. [Critical energy](../../../../critical-energy-of-an-electromagnetic-shower.md) is material dependent; it is not a new photon-production threshold.
