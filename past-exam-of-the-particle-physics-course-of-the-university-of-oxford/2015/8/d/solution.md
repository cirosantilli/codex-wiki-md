↑ **Parent:** [D](../d.md)

The [CKM matrix](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) appears in the [charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) of [b](../../5/b.md). For a tree semileptonic transition, a schematic extraction is $\Gamma=G_F^2|V_{ij}|^2\times\text{phase space}\times\text{hadronic factor}\times(1+\text{corrections})$. Measure an absolute rate or differential spectrum, calculate or independently determine the hadronic factor, and solve for the modulus. The process is not a direct count of [quark](../../../../quark.md) flavours in a detector: form factors, decay constants, nuclear structure and radiation can limit the result.

- $|V_{ud}|$: superallowed nuclear $0^+\to0^+$ [beta decay](../../../../beta-decay.md), with neutron and pion [beta decay](../../../../beta-decay.md) cross-checks; correct for nuclear and radiative effects.
- $|V_{us}|$: $K\to\pi\ell\nu$ spectra with the $K\pi$ form factor; compare $K\to\mu\nu$ and $\pi\to\mu\nu$ using decay constants.
- $|V_{ub}|$ and $|V_{cb}|$: exclusive $B\to\pi\ell\nu$ and $B\to D^{(*)}\ell\nu$, or inclusive charmless/charmed semileptonic spectra; separate the rare charmless component and control the hadronic calculation.
- $|V_{cd}|$ and $|V_{cs}|$: charm semileptonic or leptonic decays with form factors or decay constants, and charm production by [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md); charm-tagged [W boson](../../../../w-boson.md) decays give a complementary constraint.
- $|V_{tb}|$: single-[top quark](../../../../top-quark.md) production and the ratio of $t\to Wb$ to all $t\to Wq$ decays, under stated assumptions about allowed interactions.
- $|V_{td}|$ and $|V_{ts}|$: neutral B-[meson](../../../../meson.md) mixing frequencies and rare loop-induced decays, with loop dynamics and hadronic inputs. Mixing-frequency ratios cancel some common uncertainties.

Rate moduli alone do not determine the complex phase. CP-asymmetric decay distributions and interference in neutral-[meson](../../../../meson.md) systems, or tree-level $B\to DK$ interference, constrain the phase and the [CKM unitarity triangle](../../../../ckm-unitarity-triangle.md). [Unitarity](../../../../unitary-operator.md) supplies consistency checks and indirect determinations, but a fit that imposes it must be distinguished from a direct measurement of an element.
