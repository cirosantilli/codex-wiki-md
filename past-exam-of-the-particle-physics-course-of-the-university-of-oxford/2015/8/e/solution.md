<h1 id="8/e/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [E](../e.md)

A general-purpose collider detector surrounds the interaction region with complementary layers. The layout makes the measured signals useful together: tracks establish charged trajectories, calorimeters sum deposited [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), and outer detectors identify penetrating [muons](../../../../muon.md).

- An inner vertex detector, usually fine silicon pixels or strips, measures precise hits and displaced [decay vertices](../../../../decay-vertex.md). A larger tracking volume follows charged trajectories through a magnetic field. Curvature supplies transverse [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and charge sign; hit geometry and known field determine the track. Neutral particles leave no direct ionization track.
- An electromagnetic [calorimeter](../../../../calorimeter.md), using dense crystals or absorber/sampling layers, measures [electrons](../../../../electron.md) and [photons](../../../../photon.md) through electromagnetic [particle showers](../../../../particle-shower.md). Electrons have matching tracks; unconverted [photons](../../../../photon.md) ordinarily do not. Segmentation provides position and shower-shape discrimination.
- A deeper hadronic [calorimeter](../../../../calorimeter.md) samples [hadronic showers](../../../../hadronic-shower.md) from [hadrons](../../../../hadron.md) and measures [jet](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md) [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). Its larger depth contains nuclear cascades; invisible binding losses, leakage and differing electromagnetic/hadronic response require calibration.
- An external [muon detector](../../../../muon-detector.md), behind most of the absorber, records long penetrating tracks. Matching them to inner tracks identifies [muons](../../../../muon.md); magnetic instrumentation can give an additional [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) measurement. Hadron punch-through and decays in flight are backgrounds.
- Dedicated [particle identification](../../../../particle-identification.md) uses specific ionization, time of flight, [Cherenkov radiation](../../../../cherenkov-radiation.md) or transition radiation to distinguish species over appropriate [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) ranges. The choice depends on the collider and physics goals.
- Forward detectors and luminosity monitors cover small polar angles and constrain normalization. Fast trigger and data-acquisition systems select useful collisions within bandwidth limits while recording efficiencies and timing.

Near-hermetic coverage permits a missing transverse [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) estimate from the calibrated vector sum, signaling [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md) or other invisible particles but also sensitive to detector failures and escaping [jets](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md). At a hadron collider the initial parton longitudinal [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) are unknown, so missing longitudinal [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) cannot be inferred in the same way; an $e^+e^-$ collider supplies stronger initial-state constraints. Every subsystem needs alignment, calibration and independent control samples for reliable combined reconstruction.
