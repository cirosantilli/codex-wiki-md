<h1 id="8/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

The [bottom quark](../../../../bottom-quark.md) has charge $-1/3$ and left-handed weak isospin $-1/2$, requiring a charge-$+2/3$ partner in a complete [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md) weak doublet. Completing the third [quark generation](../../../../quark-generation.md) also maintains the quark/lepton cancellation of [gauge anomalies](../../../../gauge-anomaly.md). A three-generation [CKM matrix](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md) permits an irreducible [CP violation](../../../../cp-violation.md) phase. Flavour constraints and loop corrections, including their strong top-[mass](../../../../mass.md) dependence in electroweak precision observables, provided indirect evidence before direct production. These arguments identify the required partner and its interactions; they do not by themselves fix its [mass](../../../../mass.md).

The Tevatron collided [protons](../../../../proton.md) and [antiprotons](../../../../antiproton.md) at $\sqrt s=1.8\,\mathrm{TeV}$. [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) produces $t\bar t$, mainly through $q\bar q$ annihilation there. Each [top quark](../../../../top-quark.md) decays predominantly to $Wb$ before [hadronization](../../../../hadronization.md), giving $W^+W^-b\bar b$. The dilepton channel has two isolated oppositely charged [leptons](../../../../lepton.md), missing transverse [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) from [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md), and bottom [jets](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md). The single-lepton channel has an isolated [electron](../../../../electron.md) or [muon](../../../../muon.md), missing transverse [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md), and four leading [jets](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md), two consistent with bottom decay. The all-hadronic channel has more background. Secondary-vertex and soft-lepton tags identify bottom [hadrons](../../../../hadron.md).

CDF and D0 reported independent excesses in 1995, with event kinematics and reconstructed [masses](../../../../mass.md) consistent with a common very heavy [quark](../../../../quark.md). Fits assign [jets](../../../../jet-particle-physics.md) to the two decay chains and compare with expected $W+$jets, multijet and instrumental backgrounds. Agreement of counts, bottom content and reconstruction across channels is stronger evidence than an excess in one event class alone.
