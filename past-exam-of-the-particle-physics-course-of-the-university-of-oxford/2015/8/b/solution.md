<h1 id="8/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Nuclear fusion in the [Sun](../../../../sun.md) produces predominantly [electron neutrinos](../../../../electron-neutrino.md), through the pp chain and subsidiary reactions. The historical solar-neutrino deficit could have reflected either a source-model error or flavour transformation. In a two-flavour approximation, mixing gives the vacuum survival law derived in [a](../../4/a.md). In matter the additional [electron neutrino](../../../../electron-neutrino.md) [charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) potential is $V_e=\sqrt2G_FN_e$. Dropping a common diagonal term, the flavour Hamiltonian is

$$
H_f=\frac{\Delta m^2}{4E}\begin{pmatrix}-\cos2\theta&\sin2\theta\\\sin2\theta&\cos2\theta\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}V_e&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The [MSW effect](../../../../msw-effect.md) enhances mixing near $2EV_e=\Delta m^2\cos2\theta$. An initially electron-like high-density state follows its instantaneous [mass eigenstate](../../../../mass-eigenstate.md) approximately adiabatically as solar density falls. After phase averaging, high-energy solar survival approaches $\sin^2\theta_{12}$, while low-energy vacuum-averaged survival approaches $1-\tfrac12\sin^22\theta_{12}$, neglecting the small third-angle correction.

The [Sudbury Neutrino Observatory](../../../../sudbury-neutrino-observatory.md) used about a kilotonne of heavy water viewed by photomultipliers. Its three channels separate flavour from total active flux:

$$
\nu_e+d\to p+p+e^-\quad(\mathrm{CC}),\qquad
\nu_x+d\to p+n+\nu_x\quad(\mathrm{NC}),\qquad
\nu_x+e^-\to\nu_x+e^-\quad(\mathrm{ES}).
$$

The [charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) channel measures electron flavour, the [neutral current](../../../../neutral-current.md) channel measures all active flavours nearly equally, and elastic [scattering](../../../../scattering.md) is more sensitive to electron flavour but also detects the others. [Cherenkov radiation](../../../../cherenkov-radiation.md) gives the charged-particle [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and direction. Neutrons were detected through capture [gamma rays](../../../../gamma-ray.md), with later salt loading and dedicated counters improving the neutral-current separation. The different angular patterns, light isotropy and [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) spectra permit a combined channel fit, with radioactive backgrounds and calibrations controlled.

The measured electron flux was lower than the total active flux, while the latter agreed with solar fusion expectations. This directly established a non-electron component and removed a simple overall source-flux error as the explanation. [SNO neutral-current flavour-transformation report, 2002](../../../../direct-evidence-for-neutrino-flavor-transformation-from-neutral-current-interactions-by-sno-collaboration.md) [Super-Kamiokande](../../../../super-kamiokande.md) supplied high-statistics directional elastic-scattering data; radiochemical chlorine/gallium experiments supplied complementary thresholds, and reactor antineutrino disappearance independently confirmed the solar [mass-squared difference](../../../../mass-squared-difference.md). Together these support large-angle mixing at $\Delta m_{21}^2\sim7.5\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{eV}^2$ rather than the atmospheric splitting in [c](../../4/c.md).
