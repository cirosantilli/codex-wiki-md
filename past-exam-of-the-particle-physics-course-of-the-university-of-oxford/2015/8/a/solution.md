<h1 id="8/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The [Goldhaber experiment](../../../../goldhaber-experiment.md) inferred [neutrino helicity](../../../../neutrino-helicity.md) from the [circular polarization](../../../../circular-polarization.md) of a nuclear [gamma ray](../../../../gamma-ray.md). A spin-zero metastable europium-152 nucleus captures an inner-shell [electron](../../../../electron.md), producing a spin-one excited samarium-152 daughter and an [electron neutrino](../../../../electron-neutrino.md); the daughter promptly emits a roughly $960\,\mathrm{keV}$ [gamma ray](../../../../gamma-ray.md) to its spin-zero ground state. For a nearly stationary initial atom, the daughter recoils opposite the [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md). Selecting [photons](../../../../photon.md) emitted along that recoil relates the daughter [spin](../../../../spin.md) projection, the [photon helicity](../../../../photon-helicity.md) and the [neutrino helicity](../../../../neutrino-helicity.md).

The selection used resonant absorption and scattering in samarium. Nuclear recoil normally shifts an emitted [gamma ray](../../../../gamma-ray.md) below the absorption resonance; the daughter recoil from [electron capture](../../../../electron-capture.md) supplies a compensating forward Doppler shift. Resonant scattering therefore preferentially selects forward photons, fixing their direction relative to the otherwise invisible [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md). A samarium scatterer and scintillation counting identify this resonant component. Transmission through magnetized iron, whose [Compton scattering](../../../../compton-scattering.md) depends on the relative electron and photon [spins](../../../../spin.md), then analyzes the [circular polarization](../../../../circular-polarization.md). Reversing the magnetization reverses the analyzing asymmetry and helps control instrumental offsets.

The selected [photon](../../../../photon.md) and [neutrino](../../../../neutrino.md) normalized helicities have the same sign: their [momenta](../../../../momentum-split.md) are opposite, while the daughter angular-momentum accounting gives opposite spin projections along the recoil axis. The observed negative photon helicity therefore establishes predominantly left-helicity [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md), consistent with the [V-A interaction](../../../../v-a-interaction.md). It measures a helicity correlation, not a tiny [neutrino mass](../../../../neutrino-mass.md) or a literal observation of the neutral particle's trajectory.[1958, pp. 1015–1017: capture cascade, resonant selection and polarization measurement](../../../../helicity-of-neutrinos-by-maurice-goldhaber-and-others.md)
