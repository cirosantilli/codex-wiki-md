↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

At the [Z boson](../../../../z-boson.md) pole, the accessible primary [fermion](../../../../fermion.md) pairs are $e^+e^-$, $\mu^+\mu^-$, $\tau^+\tau^-$, the three $\nu_i\bar\nu_i$ channels, and $u\bar u,d\bar d,s\bar s,c\bar c,b\bar b$. The [top quark](../../../../top-quark.md) is too heavy. The charged channels also have [photon](../../../../photon.md) exchange, and the electronic channel has a substantial nonresonant [Bhabha scattering](../../../../bhabha-scattering.md) contribution. On-shell [W boson](../../../../w-boson.md) pairs are above threshold; they are not primary $Z$ decays.

A [tau lepton](../../../../tau-particle.md) pair gives two approximately back-to-back narrow low-multiplicity cones, usually one or three charged tracks per cone, with overall charge zero and missing [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) from [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md). Opposite hemispheres can show electron, muon or hadronic tags. A [decay vertex](../../../../decay-vertex.md) or track impact-parameter information helps establish the short [tau lepton](../../../../tau-particle.md) flight. Reject [Bhabha scattering](../../../../bhabha-scattering.md) and dimuon events by their nearly full visible beam [energy](../../../../energy-split.md), particle identification and little missing [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md); reject ordinary [quark](../../../../quark.md) multijets by their broader high-multiplicity activity and larger visible [invariant masses](../../../../invariant-mass.md). Control two-[photon](../../../../photon.md) backgrounds with visible transverse [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md), forward activity and event geometry. Selection efficiencies depend on decay mode and must be calibrated.
