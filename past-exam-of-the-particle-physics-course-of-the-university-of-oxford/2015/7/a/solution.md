<h1 id="7/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

The basic [Feynman diagrams](../../../../feynman-diagram.md) have $\tau^-\to\nu_\tau W^{-*}$ followed by $W^{-*}\to e^-\bar\nu_e$, $\mu^-\bar\nu_\mu$, or $d\bar u,s\bar u$, with the latter pairs undergoing [hadronization](../../../../hadronization.md). See the charged-current diagrams in [a](../../2/a.md). Neglect all daughter [masses](../../../../mass.md), [QCD](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) corrections and inaccessible charm final states, and assume [lepton universality](../../../../lepton-universality.md). Each leptonic mode then has one unit of [decay width](../../../../decay-width.md); the two accessible [quark](../../../../quark.md) combinations have three [color charge](../../../../color-charge.md) states and total CKM weight $|V_{ud}|^2+|V_{us}|^2\simeq1$. Therefore

$$
\Gamma_e:\Gamma_\mu:\Gamma_{\rm had}=1:1:3,\qquad B_{\rm had}=\frac35=0.60.
$$

The result is an inclusive [parton model](../../../../parton-model.md) estimate. Perturbative [QCD](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) corrections increase the hadronic contribution, while [muon mass](../../../../muon-mass.md), nonperturbative dynamics and radiative corrections also matter; the physical fraction is roughly $0.65$.

Every ordinary hadronic mode already contains a [tau neutrino](../../../../tau-neutrino.md), so it contains a [lepton](../../../../lepton.md) as well as [hadrons](../../../../hadron.md) if invisible particles are counted. For an additional visible charged [lepton](../../../../lepton.md), a virtual [photon](../../../../photon.md) can internally convert in a hadronic mode, for example $\tau^-\to\nu_\tau\pi^-\gamma^*\to\nu_\tau\pi^-e^+e^-$. The extra electromagnetic vertices suppress such modes; they are not a large fraction of the inclusive width. Alternatively a daughter [pion](../../../../pion.md) or [kaon](../../../../kaon.md) can decay semileptonically or leptonically in flight, for example $\tau^-\to\nu_\tau\pi^-K^0$ followed by a semileptonic neutral-[kaon](../../../../kaon.md) decay. Those are secondary [decay chains](../../../../decay-chain.md), not independent primary [tau lepton](../../../../tau-particle.md) branching fractions. Lepton-flavour-violating neutrinoless modes such as $\tau^-\to\mu^-\pi^+\pi^-$ do not contribute appreciably in the [Standard Model](../../../../standard-model-split.md).
