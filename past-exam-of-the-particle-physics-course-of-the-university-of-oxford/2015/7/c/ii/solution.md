<h1 id="7/c/ii/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Ii](../ii.md)

Write the leptonic partial [decay width](../../../../../decay-width.md) as

$$
\Gamma(\tau\to\ell\bar\nu_\ell\nu_\tau)=\frac{G_F^2m_\tau^5}{192\pi^3}\left|\frac{g_\tau g_\ell}{g_\mu g_e}\right|^2 f(r_\ell)(1+\delta_\ell),\quad
f(r)=1-8r+8r^3-r^4-12r^2\ln r,\quad r_\ell=\frac{m_\ell^2}{m_\tau^2}.
$$

Here $G_F$ is defined from [muon decay](../../../../../muon-decay.md), so the coupling denominator fixes the convention. Measure $\Gamma_\ell=B_\ell/\tau_\tau$. The ratio $B_\mu/B_e$ tests $|g_\mu/g_e|^2$ after the known [phase space](../../../../../phase-space.md) and radiative corrections. Comparing $B_e/\tau_\tau$ with the [muon](../../../../../muon.md) lifetime and the fifth power of the [mass](../../../../../mass.md) ratio tests $|g_\tau/g_\mu|^2$. Hadronic comparisons such as tau-to-pion versus pion-to-muon decay supply additional universality tests with their own hadronic and electromagnetic corrections.
