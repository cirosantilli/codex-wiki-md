<h1 id="7/c/i/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [I](../i.md)

In $\tau^-\to\ell^-\bar\nu_\ell\nu_\tau$, charge and missing [four-momentum](../../../../../four-momentum.md) show an invisible neutral component beyond the visible charged [lepton](../../../../../lepton.md). The distributions are consistent with three-body [V-A interaction](../../../../../v-a-interaction.md) decay; hadronic modes such as $\tau^-\to\pi^-\nu_\tau$ instead have two-body missing-mass kinematics. Production and decay accounting assigns $L_\tau(\tau^-)=L_\tau(\nu_\tau)=1$ and their antiparticles minus one. Searches for wrong-flavour and neutrinoless channels constrain violations. These observations establish a useful approximately conserved tau-family number in ordinary interactions, rather than proving an exact law from missing [energy](../../../../../energy-split.md) alone.

To establish that the invisible particle is distinct from $\nu_e$ and $\nu_\mu$, combine decay evidence with the absence of unsuppressed tau production by conventional electron- and muon-flavour beams, and direct [tau neutrino](../../../../../tau-neutrino.md) [charged current](../../../../../charged-current.md) interactions. [DONUT](../../../../../donut.md) observed the short displaced [tau lepton](../../../../../tau-particle.md) decay in nuclear emulsion after a neutrino interaction. [DONUT first direct tau-neutrino interaction report](../../../../../observation-of-tau-neutrino-interactions-by-donut-collaboration.md) [Neutrino oscillation](../../../../../neutrino-oscillation.md) means individual family numbers are not exact conserved quantities during propagation, even though they are an excellent approximation for these local decay assignments.
