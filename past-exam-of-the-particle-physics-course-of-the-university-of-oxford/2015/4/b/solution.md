<h1 id="4/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

The Fermilab primary [proton](../../../../proton.md) beam strikes a target, producing secondary [pions](../../../../pion.md) and [kaons](../../../../kaon.md). Horn focusing selects positive parents; their decays $\pi^+\to\mu^+\nu_\mu$ and $K^+\to\mu^+\nu_\mu$ produce a predominantly [muon neutrino](../../../../muon-neutrino.md) beam. A [decay tunnel](../../../../decay-tunnel.md) permits decay before the remaining charged secondaries encounter an absorber. A near detector constrains the flux and interaction spectrum, while the distant [MINOS](../../../../minos.md) detector measures its change.

The alternating [scintillation counters](../../../../scintillation-counter.md) and magnetized iron give a sampling [calorimeter](../../../../calorimeter.md) and a magnetic tracking volume. A [charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) $\nu_\mu N\to\mu^-X$ event has a long negative [muon](../../../../muon.md) track and a localized [hadronic shower](../../../../hadronic-shower.md). Estimate $E_\mu$ by range if contained or magnetic curvature if exiting, and $E_X$ by the calibrated scintillator pulse-height sum. Reconstruct $E_\nu\simeq E_\mu+E_X$, with corrections for nuclear binding, invisible [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) and detector response. The [muon](../../../../muon.md) charge separates most wrong-sign [muon antineutrino](../../../../muon-antineutrino.md) events; short showers without the track are primarily [neutral current](../../../../neutral-current.md) candidates. The source's $730\,\mathrm{km}$ baseline is used in the calculations below.
