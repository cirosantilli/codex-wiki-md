In particular, no measured minimum [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) survives. For maximal [mixing angle](../../../../mixing-angle.md), the first minimum of [muon neutrino](../../../../muon-neutrino.md) survival obeys

$$
1.27|\Delta m^2|\frac{730}{E_{\min}}=\frac\pi2,\qquad
|\Delta m^2|[\mathrm{eV}^2]=1.6943\times10^{-3}E_{\min}[\mathrm{GeV}].
$$

A restored spectrum or fitted data are needed to choose $E_{\min}$. This is a relation for the survival minimum; a minimum in the raw event spectrum also depends on flux, [cross section](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) and smearing. For context only, accelerator and atmospheric results put the splitting at a few $10^{-3}\,\mathrm{eV}^2$, versus the solar splitting about $7.5\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{eV}^2$, roughly thirty times smaller. This known scale is not a reconstruction of the absent graph. [MINOS combined three-flavour analysis, 2014](../../../../combined-analysis-of-muon-neutrino-disappearance-and-electron-neutrino-appearance-by-minos-collaboration.md)

Finite event statistics, [hadronic shower](../../../../hadronic-shower.md) [energy resolution](../../../../energy-resolution.md), the [muon](../../../../muon.md) scale, near-to-far flux extrapolation, interaction and nuclear modeling, backgrounds and detector calibration limit the precision; the baseline itself is comparatively well known. In a two-flavour vacuum fit the sign of $\Delta m^2$ is inaccessible. Evidence for dominant $\nu_\mu\to\nu_\tau$ includes the zenith-angle-dependent atmospheric deficit in [Super-Kamiokande](../../../../super-kamiokande.md), approximately preserved active [neutral current](../../../../neutral-current.md) rates, small electron-flavour appearance at this scale, atmospheric tau appearance and direct tau appearance in [OPERA](../../../../opera-experiment.md). The latter had already reported four candidates and $4.2\sigma$ evidence in 2014; a later five-candidate discovery result is not needed for this June 2015 answer. [OPERA tau-appearance result, 2014](../../../../observation-of-tau-neutrino-appearance-in-the-cngs-beam-by-opera-collaboration.md) These tests constrain a dominant sterile conversion as well as conversion into [electron neutrinos](../../../../electron-neutrino.md).
