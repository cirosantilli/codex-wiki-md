<h1 id="3/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Direct formation in $e^+e^-$ through one virtual [photon](../../../../photon.md) selects the [vector meson](../../../../vector-meson.md) quantum numbers $J^{PC}=1^{--}$. The nonzero $K_S^0K_L^0$ mode and absence of $K_S^0K_S^0$ are consistent with this assignment: the two spin-zero [kaons](../../../../kaon.md) require odd [orbital angular momentum](../../../../orbital-angular-momentum.md) for negative [parity](../../../../parity.md), whereas identical $K_S^0$ [bosons](../../../../boson.md) cannot have an odd relative wave. This argument neglects the tiny [CP violation](../../../../cp-violation.md) in neutral [kaons](../../../../kaon.md).

Assuming the $\rho\pi$ decays conserve [isospin](../../../../isospin.md), their equal rates indicate $I=0$. The [Clebsch-Gordan coefficients](../../../../clebsch-gordan-coefficients.md) give

$$
|0,0\rangle=\frac{|\rho^+\pi^-\rangle-|\rho^0\pi^0\rangle+|\rho^-\pi^+\rangle}{\sqrt3}.
$$

An $I=1,I_3=0$ state lacks $\rho^0\pi^0$, while $I=2,I_3=0$ has squared coefficients $1:4:1$ rather than $1:1:1$. The branching fractions alone determine neither $J$ nor these relative phases; the single-[photon](../../../../photon.md) formation and approximate [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md) [isospin](../../../../isospin.md) conservation are essential additional inputs.
