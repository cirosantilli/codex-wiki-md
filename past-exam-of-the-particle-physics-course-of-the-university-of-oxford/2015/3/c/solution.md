The recoverable numerical conversion is nevertheless definite. With $M\simeq3.1\,\mathrm{GeV}$, $B_{ee}=0.060$, $\epsilon=0.50$, and $1\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-2}=389400\,\mathrm{nb}$, [b](../b.md) gives

$$
\Gamma[\mathrm{keV}]=0.2315\,\mathcal I_{ee}^{\rm obs}[\mathrm{nb\,MeV}].
$$

This coefficient assumes the plotted exclusive electronic sample has not already been corrected for acceptance. A restored area, with continuum subtraction and acceptance convention checked, would complete the estimate. A tabulated modern [J/psi meson](../../../../j-psi-meson.md) width would be external information rather than a reading of these data.

The narrow [scattering resonance](../../../../scattering-resonance.md) indicates suppressed [strong interaction](../../../../strong-interaction.md) decay. In the [quark model](../../../../quark-model.md), $c\bar c$ is below the open-[charm quark](../../../../charm-quark.md) $D\bar D$ threshold, so its constituents cannot simply emerge in separate charmed [mesons](../../../../meson.md). The [OZI rule](../../../../ozi-rule.md) suppresses annihilation into light [hadrons](../../../../hadron.md); for a $1^{--}$ color-singlet state the leading purely gluonic channel needs three [gluons](../../../../gluon.md). The observed leptonic channels remain competitive. Its mass and $I=0$ fit [charmonium](../../../../charmonium.md); the small width alone would not uniquely identify the new [quark flavour](../../../../quark-flavor.md) without the wider spectrum and threshold evidence.
