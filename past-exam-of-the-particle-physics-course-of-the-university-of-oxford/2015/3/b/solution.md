<h1 id="3/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

For the resonant contribution only, subtract nonresonant [Bhabha scattering](../../../../bhabha-scattering.md) and its interference before interpreting a fitted area. The [integrated narrow vector resonance area](../../../../integrated-narrow-vector-resonance-area.md) follows from the [Breit-Wigner resonance cross section](../../../../breit-wigner-resonance-cross-section.md), with $J=1$, $s_{e^-}=s_{e^+}=1/2$, and $p\simeq M/2$, becomes

$$
\sigma_{ee}(E)=\frac{3\pi}{M^2}\frac{\Gamma_{ee}^2}{(E-M)^2+\Gamma^2/4}.
$$

Across a narrow [scattering resonance](../../../../scattering-resonance.md), treat the prefactor and partial [decay widths](../../../../decay-width.md) as constant and extend the integral over $E-M$ to the real line. Since $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}du/(u^2+a^2)=\pi/a$,

$$
\mathcal I_{ee}\equiv\int\sigma_{ee}(E)\,dE=\frac{6\pi^2}{M^2}\frac{\Gamma_{ee}^2}{\Gamma}=\frac{6\pi^2}{M^2}B_{ee}^2\Gamma,\qquad
\Gamma=\frac{M^2\mathcal I_{ee}}{6\pi^2B_{ee}^2}.
$$

A normalized beam-energy response convolves the line shape without changing this full area. It can completely change the observed peak height and apparent width. If a uniform acceptance $\epsilon$ is not already unfolded, use $\mathcal I_{ee}=\mathcal I_{ee}^{\rm obs}/\epsilon$. The distinction between $B_{ee}^2$ for this exclusive final state and $B_{ee}$ for an inclusive final state is essential.
