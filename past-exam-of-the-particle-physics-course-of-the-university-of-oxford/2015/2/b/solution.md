<h1 id="2/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Assume unpolarized beams, $s\gg m_\mu^2$, a single exchanged [photon](../../../../photon.md), full azimuthal coverage and no radiative corrections. Take $c_*=\cos\theta^*$ between incoming $e^-$ and outgoing $\mu^-$. The four nonzero [helicity amplitudes](../../../../helicity-amplitude.md) have magnitudes $e^2(1+c_*)$ for equal incoming/outgoing negative-particle [helicities](../../../../helicity.md), and $e^2(1-c_*)$ for opposite ones. Averaging four initial spin states and summing the final ones gives

$$
\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}=\frac{e^4}{4}\{2(1+c_*)^2+2(1-c_*)^2\}=e^4(1+c_*^2).
$$

The massless two-body [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md) gives

$$
\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega_*}=\frac{\alpha^2}{4s}(1+c_*^2),\qquad
\frac{d\sigma}{dc_*}=\frac{\pi\alpha^2}{2s}(1+c_*^2),\qquad
\sigma=\frac{4\pi\alpha^2}{3s}.
$$

The even distribution reflects the equal left/right [photon](../../../../photon.md) couplings and is symmetric under interchange of forward and backward directions.
