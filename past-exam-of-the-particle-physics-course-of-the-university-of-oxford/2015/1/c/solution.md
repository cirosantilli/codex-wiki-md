<h1 id="1/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

Strike a target with high-energy [protons](../../../../proton.md), collect negative [pions](../../../../pion.md) and [kaons](../../../../kaon.md) with magnetic horns, and let them decay in a long evacuated [decay tunnel](../../../../decay-tunnel.md): $\pi^-\to\mu^-\bar\nu_\mu$ and $K^-\to\mu^-\bar\nu_\mu$. Absorb residual [hadrons](../../../../hadron.md) and range out [muons](../../../../muon.md) in shielding. Focusing selects the negative parent sign and therefore enriches [muon antineutrinos](../../../../muon-antineutrino.md); other parent decays leave a finite contamination. Monitor the parent flux and use a near detector to constrain the unoscillated spectrum.

A massive segmented [calorimeter](../../../../calorimeter.md) supplies interaction probability and measures the [hadronic shower](../../../../hadronic-shower.md). Alternate dense absorber plates with [scintillation counters](../../../../scintillation-counter.md) or tracking planes, and magnetize the iron or add a downstream magnetic [muon detector](../../../../muon-detector.md). The signal is a positive [muon](../../../../muon.md) plus a [hadronic shower](../../../../hadronic-shower.md); the sign rejects much of the [muon neutrino](../../../../muon-neutrino.md) contamination. For a contained [muon](../../../../muon.md), its range gives [energy](../../../../energy-split.md); for a penetrating track, curvature gives $p_\perp[\mathrm{GeV}]=0.2998 B[\mathrm T]R[\mathrm m]$. Multiple measurements and a field map determine the fitted [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) and sign. Summed calibrated shower response estimates the struck [quark](../../../../quark.md)'s visible [hadronization](../../../../hadronization.md) energy, not the energy of a free observable [quark](../../../../quark.md). Correct for invisible nuclear losses, noncompensating response, leakage and acceptance. Neglecting target recoil corrections, $E_\nu\simeq E_\mu+E_{\rm had}$ and $y\simeq E_{\rm had}/E_\nu$.
