<h1 id="1/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

Use the [parton model](../../../../parton-model.md), neglect [antiquarks](../../../../antiquark.md), binding and target [mass](../../../../mass.md) corrections, [quark masses](../../../../quark-mass.md), [muon mass](../../../../muon-mass.md), [QCD scaling violation](../../../../qcd-scaling-violation.md) and the [W boson](../../../../w-boson.md) propagator correction. The struck [quark](../../../../quark.md) is effectively free during the short interaction and carries [parton momentum fraction](../../../../parton-momentum-fraction.md) $x$, giving $\hat s\simeq xs$. The [charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) reaction is $\bar\nu_\mu u_i\to\mu^+d_j$. Its [helicity amplitude](../../../../helicity-amplitude.md) has the same $(1-y)$ factor as in [a](../a.md). Define the effective [parton distribution function](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md) by $q(x)=\sum_{i,j}|V_{ij}|^2u_i(x)$, summing only kinematically accessible final [quarks](../../../../quark.md); for valence-only [protons](../../../../proton.md) and accessible $d,s$ this is approximately $u(x)$. It is not the unweighted sum $u+d$.

$$
\frac{d^2\sigma}{dx\,dy}=\sum_{i,j}u_i(x)|V_{ij}|^2\frac{G_F^2xs}{\pi}(1-y)^2=\frac{G_F^2sx}{\pi}(1-y)^2q(x).
$$

Here $y=p\cdot(k-k')/(p\cdot k)$ is the inclusive [deep inelastic scattering](../../../../deep-inelastic-scattering.md) variable, and $x=Q^2/(2p\cdot(k-k'))$ in the scaling limit. Integrating $0<y<1$ gives $1/3$, hence

$$
\sigma(\bar\nu_\mu p)=\frac{G_F^2s}{3\pi}\int_0^1xq(x)\,dx\simeq\frac{2G_F^2m_pE}{3\pi}\int_0^1xq(x)\,dx\propto E.
$$

The moment measures the [momentum](../../../../momentum-split.md) fraction in the active [quarks](../../../../quark.md). Approximate linearity is evidence for scattering from pointlike spin-one-half constituents with approximately scale-independent [parton distribution functions](../../../../parton-distribution-function.md), rather than coherent scattering from an extended [proton](../../../../proton.md). It is an intermediate-energy prediction: thresholds, experimental [deep inelastic scattering](../../../../deep-inelastic-scattering.md) cuts, [QCD scaling violation](../../../../qcd-scaling-violation.md) and eventually the finite [W boson](../../../../w-boson.md) propagator modify it.
