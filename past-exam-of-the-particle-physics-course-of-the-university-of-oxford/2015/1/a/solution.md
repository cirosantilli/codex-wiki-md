<h1 id="1/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Treat the [electron](../../../../electron.md) and [electron antineutrino](../../../../electron-antineutrino.md) as effectively [massless particles](../../../../massless-particle.md) in the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md), retain only the [charged current](../../../../charged-current.md), and use the [Fermi interaction](../../../../fermi-interaction.md): $m_e^2\ll s\ll M_W^2$. Take $\theta^*$ between incoming and outgoing [electron antineutrino](../../../../electron-antineutrino.md) directions. The [V-A interaction](../../../../v-a-interaction.md) selects a right-helicity [electron antineutrino](../../../../electron-antineutrino.md) and a left-helicity [electron](../../../../electron.md). Their opposite incoming directions give total [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) projection one. The outgoing pair has the same projection along its own axis. The [Wigner D-matrix](../../../../wigner-d-matrix.md) element is $d^1_{11}(\theta^*)=(1+\cos\theta^*)/2$, so the squared [helicity amplitude](../../../../helicity-amplitude.md) is proportional to $(1+\cos\theta^*)^2$. The inactive initial [electron](../../../../electron.md) [helicity](../../../../helicity.md) is included in the unpolarized normalization supplied by the paper.

The boost from the [centre-of-momentum frame](../../../../center-of-momentum-frame.md) to the [laboratory frame](../../../../laboratory-frame.md) has $\beta=E/(E+m_e)$, while the incoming [electron antineutrino](../../../../electron-antineutrino.md) has $E_*=(s-m_e^2)/(2\sqrt s)=m_eE/\sqrt s$ and $s=m_e^2+2m_eE$. Thus

$$
\frac{E'}E=\frac{E+m_e+E\cos\theta^*}{2E+m_e},\qquad y=\frac{E(1-\cos\theta^*)}{2E+m_e}\simeq\frac{1-\cos\theta^*}{2}.
$$

The requested identity therefore uses $E\gg m_e$; it is not exact at finite target [mass](../../../../mass.md). With $c_*=\cos\theta^*$, the absolute [Jacobian determinant](../../../../jacobian-determinant.md) is $|dy/dc_*|=1/2$, giving

$$
\frac{d\sigma}{dc_*}=\frac{G_F^2s}{8\pi}(1+c_*)^2,\qquad
\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega_*}=\frac{G_F^2s}{16\pi^2}(1+c_*)^2,\qquad
\sigma=\frac{G_F^2s}{3\pi}.
$$

The suppression at backward [scattering](../../../../scattering.md) follows from [angular momentum](../../../../angular-momentum.md) conservation: rotating projection $+1$ through $\pi$ cannot leave projection $+1$ along the original axis. Replacing $1+c_*$ by $2(1-y)$ gives the supplied $(1-y)^2$ dependence.
