<h1 id="6/a/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [A](../a.md)

Use $g^{\mu\nu}=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,-1,-1)$ and restore $\hbar$ temporarily. The free [Dirac equation](../../../../dirac-equation.md) is $(i\hbar\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu-m)\psi=0$, with $c=1$. Its Hermitian conjugate is $-i\hbar(\partial_\mu\psi^\dagger)(\gamma^\mu)^\dagger-m\psi^\dagger=0$. Multiplying on the right by $\gamma^0$ and using $(\gamma^\mu)^\dagger\gamma^0=\gamma^0\gamma^\mu$ gives the adjoint equation

$$
i\hbar(\partial_\mu\bar\psi)\gamma^\mu+m\bar\psi=0,\qquad \bar\psi=\psi^\dagger\gamma^0.
$$

The [Dirac adjoint](../../../../dirac-adjoint.md) and original equations give $(\partial_\mu\bar\psi)\gamma^\mu\psi=im\bar\psi\psi/\hbar$ and $\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu\psi=-im\bar\psi\psi/\hbar$. Adding gives the [continuity equation](../../../../continuity-equation.md)

$$
\partial_\mu j^\mu=0,\qquad j^\mu=\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi,\qquad j^0=\psi^\dagger\psi\geq0,\qquad \mathbf j=\psi^\dagger\boldsymbol\alpha\psi.
$$

The [Dirac current](../../../../dirac-current.md) transforms as a [four-vector](../../../../four-vector.md); the positive density is the time component, not a [Lorentz scalar](../../../../lorentz-scalar.md). With ordinary units the number current is $c\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi$ and the [electron](../../../../electron.md) charge current is $-e$ times it.
