<h1 id="5/b/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [B](../b.md)

In a dense beam dump, long-lived [pions](../../../../pion.md) and [kaons](../../../../kaon.md) usually interact and lose [energy](../../../../energy-split.md) before decaying. Their residual stopped decays yield low-energy [neutrinos](../../../../neutrino.md), below the useful high-energy tau-interaction sample. Prompt charmed [mesons](../../../../meson.md) have much shorter lifetimes and can decay before reinteraction. This suppresses the conventional [muon neutrino](../../../../muon-neutrino.md) background relative to the prompt component. The production sequence is [Quantum chromodynamics](../../../../quantum-chromodynamics.md) creation of $c\bar c$, principally $gg\to c\bar c$ with $q\bar q\to g^*\to c\bar c$ also allowed, followed by [hadronization](../../../../hadronization.md) $c\bar s\to D_s^+$ and its charge conjugate. The $gg$ process includes both quark-exchange diagrams and the three-[gluon](../../../../gluon.md) diagram. Fragmentation also produces other [hadrons](../../../../hadron.md); the [proton](../../../../proton.md) does not turn directly into one isolated [Ds meson](../../../../ds-meson.md).

$$
D_s^+(c\bar s)\to W^{+*}\to\tau^+\nu_\tau,\qquad
\tau^+\to\bar\nu_\tau W^{+*}\to\bar\nu_\tau(\ell^+\nu_\ell\ \text{or}\ u\bar d,u\bar s).
$$

The charge-conjugate chain gives a prompt [tau antineutrino](../../../../tau-antineutrino.md) and a secondary [tau neutrino](../../../../tau-neutrino.md). Thus each leptonic [Ds meson](../../../../ds-meson.md) chain produces both tau-flavour signs. The corresponding [Feynman diagrams](../../../../feynman-diagram.md) are shown in [a](../../2/a.md).

For the [CKM matrix](../../../../cabibbo-kobayashi-maskawa-matrix.md), diagonalizing the up/down [quark mass](../../../../quark-mass.md) matrices by $U_{uL},U_{dL}$ leaves $V=U_{uL}^\dagger U_{dL}$ in the [charged current](../../../../charged-current.md):

$$
\mathcal L_{\rm cc}=-\frac g{\sqrt2}\bar u_{Li}\gamma^\mu V_{ij}d_{Lj}W^+_\mu+\mathrm{h.c.},\qquad V^\dagger V=I.
$$

The three-generation [unitary matrix](../../../../unitary-matrix.md) has three physical [mixing angles](../../../../mixing-angle.md) and one [CP violation](../../../../cp-violation.md) phase after field rephasings. At fixed kinematics and [hadronic matrix element](../../../../hadronic-matrix-element.md), rates are proportional to $|V_{ij}|^2$; [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md), parent lifetimes, form factors and [helicity suppression](../../../../helicity-suppression.md) must also be included when comparing [branching fractions](../../../../branching-fraction.md).

For a charged [pseudoscalar meson](../../../../pseudoscalar-meson.md) $P^+=(c\bar q)$, [leptonic pseudoscalar decay width](../../../../leptonic-pseudoscalar-decay-width.md) gives

$$
\Gamma(P^+\to\tau^+\nu_\tau)=\frac{G_F^2f_P^2|V_{cq}|^2m_Pm_\tau^2}{8\pi}\left(1-\frac{m_\tau^2}{m_P^2}\right)^2.
$$

The [Ds meson](../../../../ds-meson.md) channel is Cabibbo-favoured, $|V_{cs}|\simeq0.974$, whereas the charged [D meson](../../../../d-meson.md) channel uses $|V_{cd}|\simeq0.224$. Taking $m_{D_s}\simeq1.968$, $m_D\simeq1.870$ and $m_\tau\simeq1.777$ in GeV gives

$$
\frac{\Gamma(D_s^+\to\tau^+\nu)}{\Gamma(D^+\to\tau^+\nu)}\simeq75\left(\frac{f_{D_s}}{f_D}\right)^2.
$$

Including the approximate lifetime ratio $\tau_{D_s}/\tau_D\simeq0.48$ and the given production ratio $5.2/11$ gives a tau yield ratio about $17(f_{D_s}/f_D)^2$, already much greater than one even with equal decay constants. This is an illustrative estimate using approximate additional mass/lifetime inputs, not a determination from the production [cross sections](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) alone. A neutral [D meson](../../../../d-meson.md) cannot annihilate to the charged $\tau\nu$ pair by [electric charge](../../../../electric-charge-split.md) conservation; its semileptonic tau modes have insufficient [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md) once the required strange or nonstrange recoil [hadron](../../../../hadron.md) is included. Light [mesons](../../../../meson.md) are below threshold; bottom production is much smaller at this fixed-target [energy](../../../../energy-split.md). Consequently neither the larger $D^0$ production rate nor ordinary light-hadron production outweighs the [Ds meson](../../../../ds-meson.md) tau source.
