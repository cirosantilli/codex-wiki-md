<h1 id="5/c/solution">Solution</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [C](../c.md)

For this dump scheme, there is no practical parent-sign or lifetime selection that isolates only the relatively rare [Ds meson](../../../../ds-meson.md) tau channel: other charmed [hadrons](../../../../hadron.md) promptly produce [electron neutrinos](../../../../electron-neutrino.md) and [muon neutrinos](../../../../muon-neutrino.md) by semileptonic decay. Both electron and muon modes have the same [charged current](../../../../charged-current.md) couplings and similar available [phase space](../../../../phase-space.md), so comparable $\nu_e$ and $\nu_\mu$ fluxes are plausible. The [tau lepton](../../../../tau-particle.md) also decays into electron and muon channels. A rare tau-producing channel cannot remove the abundant prompt backgrounds, and its two-step chain emits both tau-flavour signs.

The quoted fractions are beam-composition inputs, not calculable from the three supplied charm production [cross sections](../../../../cross-section-physics.md) alone: spectra, [branching fractions](../../../../branching-fraction.md), geometric acceptance and interaction weighting matter. Impossibility of a pure tau source is a practical statement about this production method, not a fundamental prohibition on selecting tau flavour at an oscillation maximum.

Nearly pure [electron neutrino](../../../../electron-neutrino.md) sources can use selected radioactive [beta-plus decay](../../../../beta-plus-decay.md) or [electron capture](../../../../electron-capture.md) parents; [beta-minus decay](../../../../beta-minus-decay.md) and reactors instead produce [electron antineutrinos](../../../../electron-antineutrino.md). A boosted radioactive-ion beam gives directional electron-flavour flux. Nearly pure [muon neutrino](../../../../muon-neutrino.md) sources use sign-selected [pion](../../../../pion.md) two-body decays, a tuned decay-pipe length and removal of undecayed parents and [muons](../../../../muon.md), reducing contamination from [muon decay](../../../../muon-decay.md) and [kaon](../../../../kaon.md) modes. Stored [muons](../../../../muon.md) themselves give a known two-flavour beam, not a single-flavour source.
