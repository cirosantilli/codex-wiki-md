# Good-solvent force-extension scaling

↑ **Parent:** [Tensile blob](tensile-blob.md)

For a long flexible [polymer](polymer-split.md) in a [good solvent](good-solvent.md), a [tensile blob](tensile-blob.md) of size $\xi_t\sim k_BT/f$ contains $g\sim(\xi_t/a)^{1/\nu}$ statistical segments. Aligning $N/g$ blobs gives $R\sim(N/g)\xi_t=Na(fa/k_BT)^{1/\nu-1}$. The three-dimensional [Flory approximation](flory-approximation.md) $\nu=3/5$ gives the displayed power. The regime requires $a\ll\xi_t\ll aN^\nu$: below its [force](force-split.md) range the coil has ordinary [linear response](linear-response-theory.md), and above it finite link extension matters. Numerical amplitudes depend on [solvent](solvent.md) quality and segment conventions.[abstract and section I, p. 386](excluded-volume-effects-and-stretched-polymer-chains-by-philip-alan-pincus.md)
