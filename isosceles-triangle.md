# Isosceles triangle

↑ **Parent:** [Triangle](triangle.md)

An isosceles [triangle](triangle.md) has two equal sides. Its [angles](angle.md) opposite those sides are equal: reflecting the triangle across the bisector of the angle between the equal sides exchanges them and fixes the opposite side as a set. A [triangle](triangle.md) formed by two [radii](radius.md) of one [circle](circle.md) is therefore isosceles.
