# Light-by-light scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Two-photon physics](two-photon-physics.md)

The elastic scattering of two [photons](photon.md) into two [photons](photon.md). In vacuum [QED](quantum-electrodynamics.md) there is no photon self-coupling at tree level; its leading [scattering amplitude](scattering-amplitude.md) is the sum of six oriented charged-fermion [box diagrams](box-diagram.md). The [superficial degree of divergence](superficial-degree-of-divergence.md) is zero, but the photon [Ward identities](ward-identity.md) forbid the required local dimension-four $A^4$ [counterterm](counterterm.md). The complete [one-loop Feynman diagram](one-loop-feynman-diagram.md) sum is consequently [ultraviolet finite](ultraviolet-finiteness.md). This does not remove its finite [four-momentum](four-momentum.md) dependence or its physical thresholds.
