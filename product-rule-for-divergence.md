# Product rule for divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence](divergence.md)

For a scalar field $\phi$ and vector field $F$,

$$
\nabla\mathbin\cdot(\phi F)=\nabla\phi\mathbin\cdot F+\phi\nabla\mathbin\cdot F.
$$
