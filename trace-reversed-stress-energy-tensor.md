# Trace-reversed stress-energy tensor

↑ **Parent:** [Stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor-split.md)

In four spacetime dimensions, the trace reversal of a [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor-split.md) has the displayed form, where $T=g^{\mu\nu}T_{\mu\nu}$. Its [trace](matrix-trace.md) is $-T$, so applying trace reversal twice returns the original [tensor](tensor.md). Taking the [trace](matrix-trace.md) of the [Einstein field equations](einstein-field-equations-split.md) without a cosmological constant gives $R=-\kappa T$; substitution yields $R_{\mu\nu}=\kappa\overline T_{\mu\nu}$. In a general dimension $d>2$, solving for the [Ricci tensor](ricci-tensor.md) instead uses $T_{\mu\nu}-g_{\mu\nu}T/(d-2)$.
