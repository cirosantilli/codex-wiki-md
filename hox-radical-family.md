# HOx radical family

↑ **Parent:** [Atmospheric chemistry](atmospheric-chemistry.md)

In the usual atmospheric convention, [hydroxyl radical](hydroxyl-radical.md) and HO2 form a rapidly interconverting hydrogen-oxide [radical](radical-chemistry.md) family. Some budgets also explicitly include H; state the counting convention. [Radical](radical-chemistry.md) production, recycling and termination differ from the abundance of the [water](water.md) reservoir.
