# Heat flow from a triangular initial temperature

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

On $0<x<L$ with zero-temperature endpoints, let the initial [temperature](temperature-split.md) rise linearly from zero to height $H$ at $L/2$ and then fall linearly to zero. [Integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) on the two halves gives [Fourier sine series](fourier-sine-series.md) coefficients $8H\sin(n\pi/2)/(\pi^2n^2)$. Under the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) $T_t=DT_{xx}$ with $D>0$, each coefficient decays by $e^{-D(n\pi/L)^2t}$, giving the displayed solution. Only odd spatial modes occur; absolute coefficient summability proves [uniform convergence](uniform-convergence.md) for $t\geq0$ via the [Weierstrass M-test](weierstrass-m-test.md). This spatial profile also occurs in a [midpoint-plucked fixed-end string](midpoint-plucked-fixed-end-string.md), where the time factors oscillate instead of decaying.
