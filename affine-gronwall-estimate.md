# Affine Gronwall estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Gronwall inequality](gronwall-inequality.md)

If a nonnegative [continuous function](continuous-function.md) on $[0,h]$ satisfies $v(t)\leq ct+A\int_0^t v(s)\,ds$ with $c,A>0$, put $W(t)=ct+A\int_0^t v(s)\,ds$. Then $v\leq W$, $W(0)=0$ and $W'\leq c+AW$. Multiplication by the [integrating factor](integrating-factor.md) $e^{-At}$ and [integration](integral-split.md) prove the displayed estimate. For $A=0$ its limiting interpretation is $v(t)\leq ct$.
