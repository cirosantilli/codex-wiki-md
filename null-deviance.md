# Null deviance

↑ **Parent:** [Residual deviance](residual-deviance.md)

The [null deviance](null-deviance.md) is the unscaled [deviance](exponential-family-deviance.md) of the intercept-only mean model relative to the saturated model at a common [dispersion parameter](dispersion-parameter.md) $\phi$, retaining any known [generalized linear model offset](generalized-linear-model-offset.md). It supplies a baseline against which predictor terms improve the fit. Its difference from a fitted model's [residual deviance](residual-deviance.md), divided by $\phi$, is the statistic for a [likelihood-ratio test](likelihood-ratio-test.md) of those added terms, with nuisance parameters handled consistently in both fits. In Poisson and binomial families, $\phi=1$.
