# Psychoactive drug

↑ **Parent:** [Medicine](medicine.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychoactive_drug)

A psychoactive drug is a substance that changes mental activity, mood or perception. In population [causal inference](causal-inference-split.md), its per-user adverse-event risk and its total population harm are distinct: the latter also depends on how many people use it, for how long, and on substitution between substances.
