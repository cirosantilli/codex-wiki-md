# Dirichlet heat-reaction threshold on the unit square

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

For zero [Dirichlet boundary conditions](dirichlet-boundary-condition.md) on the unit square, the modes $2\sin(j\pi x)\sin(k\pi y)$ have heat-reaction growth rates $\kappa-\pi^2(j^2+k^2)$. Their largest rate is $\kappa-2\pi^2$. Hence the solution is an [L2 norm](l2-norm.md) contraction for $\kappa\leq2\pi^2$ and decays uniformly at positive times as $t\to\infty$ when the inequality is strict. At equality the first sine mode is stationary. For larger fixed $\kappa$ the equation remains well posed on every finite time interval, but has a growing mode; long-time contractivity is a different property from finite-time well-posedness.
