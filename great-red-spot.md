# Great Red Spot

↑ **Parent:** [Jupiter](jupiter.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Red_Spot)

The [Great Red Spot](great-red-spot.md) is a large atmospheric vortex in Jupiter's southern hemisphere. Its counterclockwise circulation corresponds to a high-pressure system in the rotating-fluid interpretation.[NASA description: counterclockwise circulation in the southern hemisphere indicates high pressure](the-great-red-spot-an-ancient-storm-in-jupiter-s-atmosphere-by-national-aeronautics-and-space-administration.md)
