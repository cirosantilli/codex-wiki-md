# Reciprocal-coordinate reduction of a beam-type equation

↑ **Parent:** [Second-order linear differential equation](second-order-linear-differential-equation.md)

For $t>0$, the [change of variables](change-of-variables-formula.md) $\tau=1/t$ and $u(t)=v(\tau)/\tau$ converts $t^4u''+\lambda^2u=0$ into $v''+\lambda^2v=0$. The [chain rule](chain-rule.md) gives $u'=v-\tau v'$ and $u''=\tau^3v''$. Thus $u=t[A\cos(\lambda/t)+B\sin(\lambda/t)]$. The prefactor cancels the derivative term introduced by inverting the independent variable.
