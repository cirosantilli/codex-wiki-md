# Gamow peak

↑ **Parent:** [Thermonuclear reaction rate](thermonuclear-reaction-rate.md)

For a nonresonant charged-particle [thermonuclear reaction rate](thermonuclear-reaction-rate.md), the thermal high-energy tail favors low $E$ while the [Coulomb barrier](coulomb-barrier.md) penetration factor favors high $E$. Their product peaks in an intermediate energy window. Resonant reactions such as the [Triple-alpha process](triple-alpha-process.md) require an additional resonance treatment.
