# Nonvanishing discriminant of a complex lattice

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass elliptic differential equation](weierstrass-elliptic-differential-equation.md)

The three nonzero half-period classes make the odd derivative of the [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md) vanish. Their values are distinct: two equal values would force at least four zeros for a function with only one double pole. Thus they are three distinct roots of $4X^3-g_2X-g_3$, whose monic discriminant gives the displayed factor. This lattice discriminant differs by $(2\pi)^{12}$ from the normalized [modular discriminant](modular-discriminant.md) for the lattice $\mathbb Z+\mathbb Z\tau$.
