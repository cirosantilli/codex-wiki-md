# Dienophile

↑ **Parent:** [Diels-Alder reaction](diels-alder-reaction.md)

The unsaturated partner that combines with a conjugated [diene](diene.md) in a Diels–Alder reaction. Electron-withdrawing substituents often enhance reactivity in a normal-electron-demand process.
