# Identity arithmetic function

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet convolution](dirichlet-convolution.md)

The [identity arithmetic function](identity-arithmetic-function.md) is $\operatorname{id}(n)=n$ on the [positive integers](positive-integer.md), for example $\operatorname{id}(1)=1$ and $\operatorname{id}(12)=12$. It is a [completely multiplicative function](completely-multiplicative-function.md) and gives the [sum-of-divisors function](sum-of-divisors-function.md) through $\operatorname{id}*\mathbf1=\sigma_1$ under [Dirichlet convolution](dirichlet-convolution.md). It is distinct from the [Identity for Dirichlet convolution](identity-for-dirichlet-convolution.md) $\varepsilon$. The identity $\operatorname{id}=\mathbf1*\varphi$, where $\varphi$ is [Euler's totient function](euler-totient-function.md), is equivalent to $\sum_{d\mid n}\varphi(d)=n$.
