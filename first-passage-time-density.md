# First-passage-time density

↑ **Parent:** [First-passage time](first-passage-time.md)

When a [first-passage time](first-passage-time.md) $\tau$ has an absolutely continuous distribution, its density is $f_\tau(t)=-S'(t)$, where $S(t)=\mathbb P(\tau>t)$ is its [survival function](survival-function.md). In a [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md), this density is the total probability flux from surviving states into the absorbing set.
