# Quasinilpotent element

↑ **Parent:** [Spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md)

An element of a complex unital [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md) is quasinilpotent if its [spectral radius](spectral-radius.md) is zero, equivalently its [spectrum of an element](spectrum-of-an-element.md) is $\{0\}$. A [nilpotent element](nilpotent.md) is quasinilpotent, but the converse need not hold. The [Volterra convolution algebra on a finite interval](volterra-convolution-algebra-on-a-finite-interval.md) gives powers which never vanish, yet have factorially decreasing norms, making the inverse series for $\lambda e-x$ converge for every $\lambda\ne0$.
