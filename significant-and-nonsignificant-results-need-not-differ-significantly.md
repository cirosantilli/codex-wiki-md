# Significant and nonsignificant results need not differ significantly

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical hypothesis test](statistical-hypothesis-test-split.md)

For independent effect estimates $1.01$ with [standard error](standard-error.md) $0.50$, and $0.99$ with standard error $0.51$, one two-sided [Wald test](wald-test.md) is significant at five percent and the other is not. But their difference is $0.02$ with standard error $\sqrt{0.50^2+0.51^2}\approx0.714$, giving a negligible test statistic. Hence different significance labels do not establish an [interaction](interaction-statistics.md) or [between-study heterogeneity](between-study-heterogeneity.md); compare the effects directly.[Printed p. 328, Introduction; pp. 328–329, theoretical example](the-difference-between-significant-and-not-significant-is-not-itself-statistically-significant-by-andrew-gelman-and-hal-stern.md)
