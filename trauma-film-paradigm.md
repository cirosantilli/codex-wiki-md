# Trauma-film paradigm

↑ **Parent:** [Intrusive memory](intrusive-memory.md)

An experimental analogue using distressing film material to study later involuntary [memories](memory-in-animals.md). It permits controlled manipulations and prospective diaries but is not equivalent to clinical [trauma](psychological-trauma.md) [exposure](exposure-therapy.md) or a [diagnosis](clinical-diagnosis.md) of [PTSD](post-traumatic-stress-disorder.md).[PLOS ONE 4, e4153; trauma-film analogue, 30-minute delay, ten-minute visuospatial task, one-week intrusions and recognition](can-playing-the-computer-game-tetris-reduce-the-build-up-of-flashbacks-for-trauma-a-proposal-from-cognitive-science-by-emily-a-holmes-and-others.md)
