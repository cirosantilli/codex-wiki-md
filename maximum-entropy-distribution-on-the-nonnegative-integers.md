# Maximum entropy distribution on the nonnegative integers

↑ **Parent:** [Information entropy](information-entropy-split.md)

Among distributions on $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ with fixed [expected value](expected-value-split.md) $\mu$, the [geometric distribution](geometric-distribution.md) with success probability $1/(1+\mu)$ uniquely maximizes [information entropy](information-entropy-split.md). Its entropy is

$$
(1+\mu)h_2\!\left(\frac1{1+\mu}\right).
$$
