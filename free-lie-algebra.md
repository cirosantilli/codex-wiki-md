# Free Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md)

The [Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) over a commutative ring $R$ generated by a set $X$ subject only to bilinearity, alternation and the [Jacobi identity](jacobi-identity.md). Every map from $X$ to the underlying set of an $R$-[Lie algebra](lie-algebra-split.md) extends uniquely to a [Lie algebra homomorphism](lie-algebra-homomorphism.md). It can be constructed from bracketed words by quotienting by those identities; elements are represented by [Lie polynomials](lie-polynomial.md).
