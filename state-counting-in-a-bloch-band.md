# State counting in a Bloch band

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch's theorem](bloch-s-theorem-split.md)

A crystal with $N$ [primitive unit cells](primitive-unit-cell.md) has $N$ distinct [wavevectors](wavevector.md) in one [Brillouin zone](brillouin-zone.md). For a rectangular periodic sample, $k_i=2\pi l_i/L_i$, and the product of the numbers of allowed values along the three directions equals the number of cells. Each orbital-derived [energy band](energy-band.md) therefore holds $N$ states per [spin](spin.md) orientation, or $2N$ with twofold spin degeneracy. This means two states per primitive cell per spin-degenerate band, independently of how many atoms the cell contains.
