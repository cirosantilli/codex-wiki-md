# Two-dimensional anti-de Sitter spacetime

↑ **Parent:** [Anti-de Sitter spacetime](anti-de-sitter-spacetime.md)

The unit hyperboloid $(X^0)^2+(X^1)^2-(X^2)^2=1$ in ambient signature $(++-)$ has $X^0=\cosh r\cos t$, $X^1=\cosh r\sin t$, $X^2=\sinh r$. It gives the displayed metric with $r\in\mathbb R$ and periodic $t$ modulo $2\pi$. Its time circles are timelike, so the [universal cover](universal-cover.md), with $t\in\mathbb R$, removes these [closed timelike curves](closed-timelike-curve.md). The metric alone does not prescribe the global identification. Both versions have constant negative curvature and two spatial ends.
