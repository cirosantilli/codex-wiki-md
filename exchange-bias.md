# Exchange bias

↑ **Parent:** [Magnetic anisotropy](magnetic-anisotropy.md)

A displacement of a ferromagnetic [hysteresis](hysteresis.md) loop's field centre caused by coupling to a prepared, sufficiently pinned magnetic environment, commonly an [antiferromagnet](antiferromagnetism.md). Field cooling through the ordering/blocking range can establish a unidirectional interfacial spin preference. With switching fields $H_-,H_+$, the shift is $H_E=(H_++H_-)/2$, distinct from coercive half-width $(H_+-H_-)/2$. Reversing the cooling field, testing a full major loop and comparing uncoupled/zero-field-cooled controls help identify genuine bias.
