# Field trace

↑ **Parent:** [Algebraic number theory](algebraic-number-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_trace)

The field trace $\operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(x)$ is the trace of multiplication by $x$, equivalently the sum of its conjugates in a separable extension.
