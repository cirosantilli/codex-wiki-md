# Heat equation maximum principle

↑ **Parent:** [Heat equation](heat-equation.md)

On a [closed manifold](closed-manifold.md) with a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md), the minimum of a smooth heat solution cannot fall below its initial minimum. At a spatial minimum, $\Delta u\le0$ for the [positive Laplace-Beltrami operator](positive-laplace-beltrami-operator.md). Adding a small increasing function of time and considering the first crossing gives the assertion. Applying the result to nonnegative initial functions shows that the [Riemannian heat kernel](riemannian-heat-kernel.md) is nonnegative.
