# Unruh effect

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum field theory in curved spacetime](quantum-field-theory-in-curved-spacetime.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unruh_effect)

An observer at constant proper acceleration in the Minkowski vacuum detects a thermal response at this temperature. The associated [Rindler coordinates](rindler-coordinates.md) separate the observer's accessible wedge from a horizon. This is an observer-dependent particle/detector response, not an assertion that inertial observers measure a thermal bath.
