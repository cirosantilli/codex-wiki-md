# Weak interaction

↑ **Parent:** [Standard Model](standard-model-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_interaction)

The [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md) changes particle species through the [charged current](charged-current.md) and also acts through the [neutral current](neutral-current.md). Familiar examples include [beta-minus decay](beta-minus-decay.md) and [neutrino](neutrino.md) scattering. It is short-range and chiral: its [W boson](w-boson.md) and [Z boson](z-boson.md) couplings distinguish [chirality](chirality-physics.md). Above the electroweak scale it belongs to the unbroken $SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y$ [electroweak interaction](electroweak-interaction.md). At much lower [energies](energy-split.md), [W boson](w-boson.md) exchange is approximated by the [Fermi interaction](fermi-interaction.md).

A particularly direct window into the [weak interaction](weak-interaction.md) is the [Wu experiment](wu-experiment.md): a sample with oriented nuclear [spins](spin.md) emits [electrons](electron.md) preferentially toward one end of its [spin](spin.md) axis. The comparison tests [parity conservation](parity-conservation.md), because [spin](spin.md) is an [axial vector](pseudovector.md) and [momentum](momentum-split.md) is a [polar vector](polar-vector.md). The [polarized beta-decay angular distribution](polarized-beta-decay-angular-distribution.md) translates that geometric distinction into a measurable counting asymmetry.
