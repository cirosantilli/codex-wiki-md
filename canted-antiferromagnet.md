# Canted antiferromagnet

↑ **Parent:** [Antiferromagnetism](antiferromagnetism.md)

An ordered magnet whose mainly opposed sublattice moments have a small uncompensated transverse component, producing weak [ferromagnetism](ferromagnetism.md).
