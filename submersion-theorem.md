# Submersion theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Submersion](submersion.md)

If $F:X^n\to Y^m$ is a [submersion](submersion.md) at $p$, there are local coordinates in which

$$
F(x^1,\ldots,x^n)=(x^1,\ldots,x^m).
$$

Every fiber of a submersion is consequently an [embedded submanifold](embedded-submanifold.md) of codimension $m$.
