# Second moment of area

↑ **Parent:** [Linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_moment_of_area)

The second moment of area of a cross-section about an axis is $I=\int y^2\,dA$, where $y$ is the perpendicular distance from that axis. Its dimensions are length to the fourth power. For a circular section of radius $a$, polar integration gives $I=\int_0^a r^3dr\int_0^{2\pi}\sin^2\theta d\theta=\pi a^4/4$. A slender rod with [Young's modulus](young-s-modulus.md) $E$ has [filament bending modulus](filament-bending-modulus.md) $B=EI$: the axial [strain](strain.md) caused by [curvature](curvature.md) $\kappa$ is $-y\kappa$, and integrating the [linear elasticity](linear-elasticity.md) energy density $E y^2\kappa^2/2$ over the section gives $B\kappa^2/2$ per unit length.
