# Background gauge-field renormalization identity

↑ **Parent:** [Charge renormalization](charge-renormalization.md)

For a background [gauge field](gauge-field.md) with $A_0=Z_A^{1/2}A$ and $g_0=\mu^{\epsilon/2}Z_gg$, background gauge covariance preserves the combination $gA$ in the [gauge covariant derivative](gauge-covariant-derivative.md). Its [Ward identity](ward-identity.md) therefore gives the displayed relation. This refers to background-field [renormalization](renormalization.md), not an unrestricted identity between the coupling and the ordinary quantum-gluon wave-function factor. Gauge-invariant one-loop matter determinants can be renormalized with this identity to extract their coupling running from vacuum polarization.
