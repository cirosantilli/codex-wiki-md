# Cardinality of strictly increasing natural-number sequences

↑ **Parent:** [Cardinal number](cardinal-number-split.md)

Strictly increasing natural-number [sequences](sequence.md) form a [subset](subset.md) of $\mathbb N^{\mathbb N}$, giving the continuum [upper bound](upper-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md). Map each binary [sequence](sequence.md) $\epsilon$ to $a_n=2n+\epsilon_n$ for the matching [lower bound](lower-bound-in-a-partially-ordered-set.md): adjacent values differ by at least one and the binary [sequence](sequence.md) can be recovered.
