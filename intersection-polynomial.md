# Intersection polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial method in combinatorics](polynomial-method-in-combinatorics.md)

For a finite [set](set-split.md) $A\subseteq[n]$ and a finite list $L$ of allowed intersection sizes, the displayed [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) satisfies $p_A(\chi_B)=\prod_{\ell\in L}(|A\cap B|-\ell)$ at [characteristic vectors of sets](characteristic-vector-of-a-set.md). For an $r$-[uniform set family](uniform-set-family.md) whose pairwise intersection sizes lie in $L\subseteq\{0,\ldots,r-1\}$, these [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) vanish at all other family members and have nonzero values at their own members. Their restrictions are therefore [linearly independent](linear-independence.md). [Multilinear reduction on the Boolean cube](multilinear-reduction-on-the-boolean-cube.md) and [homogenisation on a uniform layer](homogenisation-on-a-uniform-layer.md) then prove the [Ray-Chaudhuri–Wilson theorem](ray-chaudhuri-wilson-theorem.md) bound.
