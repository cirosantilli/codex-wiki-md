# Cauchy-kernel dressing for the KdV equation

↑ **Parent:** [Inverse scattering transform](inverse-scattering-transform.md)

Put $E_k=e^{i(kx+k^3t)}$, $I=\int_L\varphi(l)d\lambda(l)$ and $q=-I_x$. For the dressing operator $\mathcal Fh=h+iE_k\int_Lh(l)/(k+l)d\lambda(l)$, differentiating gives $\mathcal F(M\varphi)=0$ and $\mathcal F(N\varphi)=3ikM\varphi$, where $M=\partial_x^2-ik\partial_x+q$ and $N=\partial_t+\partial_x^3+3q\partial_x$. Uniqueness of the [linear integral equation](linear-integral-equation.md) therefore supplies a [Lax pair](lax-pair.md). Its [commutator](commutator.md) identity is $[N,M]=-3q_xM+(q_t+q_{xxx}+6qq_x)$, which gives the [KdV equation](korteweg-de-vries-equation.md) when the eigenfunction is not identically zero. Differentiation under the integral and the uniqueness assumption are essential hypotheses.
