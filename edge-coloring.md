# Edge coloring

↑ **Parent:** [Graph coloring](graph-coloring.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_coloring)

An edge colouring assigns a colour to every [edge of a graph](edge-of-a-graph.md). In a red-blue edge colouring of a [complete graph](complete-graph.md), a triangle is monochromatic when its three edges have the same colour.
