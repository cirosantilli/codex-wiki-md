# Geometric delay

↑ **Parent:** [Interferometric baseline](interferometric-baseline.md)

The difference in plane-wave propagation time between two [antennas](antenna-radio.md) is the projected baseline along the source direction divided by the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). Its sign depends on the ordering of the receivers. A monochromatic signal acquires relative phase $2\pi\nu\tau_g$; its change with Earth rotation is compensated by [fringe stopping](fringe-stopping.md) for the chosen reference direction.
