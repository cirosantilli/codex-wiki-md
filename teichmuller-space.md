<h1 id="teichmuller-space">Teichmüller space</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Teichmüller theory](teichmuller-theory.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teichmüller_space)

For a fixed closed oriented surface $S_g$, a point is a marked [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md) $(X,f:S_g\to X)$, modulo [biholomorphisms](biholomorphism.md) intertwining the markings up to [homotopy](homotopy.md). For $g\geq2$, [Fenchel–Nielsen coordinates](fenchel-nielsen-coordinates.md) give real dimension $6g-6$.
