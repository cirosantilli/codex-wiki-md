# Shifted quartic Higgs potential normalization

↑ **Parent:** [Higgs field potential](higgs-field-potential.md)

In [unitary gauge](unitary-gauge.md), $\phi^\dagger\phi-v^2/2=vH+H^2/2$, so this [Higgs potential](higgs-field-potential.md) gives $M_H^2=2\kappa v^2$, a cubic coefficient $\kappa v$ and a quartic coefficient $\kappa/4$. The corresponding [Feynman rules](feynman-rule.md) are $-3iM_H^2/v$ and $-3iM_H^2/v^2$. Writing the potential with an additional factor $1/2$ changes the relation between its named coupling and the [Higgs boson](higgs-boson.md) mass; it does not change these mass-based rules.
