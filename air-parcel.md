# Air parcel

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysics](geophysics-split.md)

An [air parcel](air-parcel.md) is a small material volume followed through an atmospheric flow. The elementary parcel model assumes rapid pressure adjustment to its environment but negligible heat and material exchange during the displacement. Its [potential temperature](potential-temperature.md) is conserved during reversible dry [adiabatic process](adiabatic-process.md) at fixed composition.
