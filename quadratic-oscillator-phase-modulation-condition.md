# Quadratic oscillator phase-modulation condition

↑ **Parent:** [Second-order resonance from quadratic oscillator coupling](second-order-resonance-from-quadratic-oscillator-coupling.md)

For $u''+u=\epsilon u^2$, a leading oscillation $U_0=A(T)e^{it}+\overline A(T)e^{-it}$ has slow time $T=\epsilon^2t$. The first-order forcing contains only zero and double frequencies. At second order the fundamental forcing is $(10/3)|A|^2A-2iA_T$; eliminating [resonance](resonance.md) gives the displayed equation. Its modulus is constant and its phase drifts. A leading real amplitude $R=2|A|$ therefore has frequency $1-5R^2\epsilon^2/12+O(\epsilon^3)$. This replaces a secular phase term by a uniformly modulated oscillation.
