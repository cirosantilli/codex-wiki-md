# Conductor-discriminant formula

↑ **Parent:** [Conductor of an abelian extension](conductor-of-an-abelian-extension.md)

For a finite [abelian extension](abelian-extension.md) of [number fields](number-field.md), its [relative discriminant](relative-discriminant.md) [ideal](ideal.md) is the product of the finite conductor [ideals](ideal.md) of the characters of its [Galois group](galois-group.md), with the [trivial character](trivial-character.md) contributing one. In a multiquadratic extension of $\mathbb Q$, the nontrivial characters correspond to its quadratic subfields, and their conductors are the [absolute values](absolute-value.md) of the corresponding [fundamental discriminants](fundamental-discriminant.md). This gives an efficient discriminant computation and can prove that a compositum is unramified over a quadratic subfield.
