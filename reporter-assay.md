# Reporter assay

↑ **Parent:** [Biology laboratory technique](biology-laboratory-technique.md)

An assay in which a measurable output is linked to a chosen regulatory sequence or activity. Reporter [RNA](ribonucleic-acid.md) abundance, [biological translation](translation-biology.md) and [protein](protein-split.md) stability can affect the same endpoint differently.
