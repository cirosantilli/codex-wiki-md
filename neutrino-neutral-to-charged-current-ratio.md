# Neutrino neutral-to-charged current ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Deep inelastic scattering](deep-inelastic-scattering.md)

For an isoscalar valence target and [massless](massless-particle.md) leading-order scattering, $R_\nu=G_L^2+G_R^2/3$, where $G_{L/R}^2=(g_{L/R}^u)^2+(g_{L/R}^d)^2$. The factor $1/3$ is $\int_0^1(1-y)^2dy$. With Standard Model [quark](quark.md) couplings and $w=\sin^2\theta_W$, this becomes $1/2-w+20w^2/27$. Sea [quarks](quark.md) and threshold, nuclear and [QCD](quantum-chromodynamics.md) corrections matter beyond this limit.
