# Stack frame

↑ **Parent:** [Call stack](call-stack.md)

The record for one active invocation in a [call stack](call-stack.md), containing information such as local [variables](variable-mathematics.md) and the place to resume its caller. Returning removes that invocation's [stack frame](stack-frame.md), allowing the caller's computation to continue.
