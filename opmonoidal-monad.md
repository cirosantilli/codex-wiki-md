# Opmonoidal monad

↑ **Parent:** [Monad](monad.md)

A [monad](monad.md) whose endofunctor is an [opmonoidal functor](opmonoidal-functor.md) and whose unit and multiplication are [opmonoidal natural transformations](opmonoidal-natural-transformation.md). Its [Eilenberg-Moore category](eilenberg-moore-category.md) has a tensor of algebras $(X,a)\otimes(Y,b)=(X\otimes Y,(a\otimes b)T_2)$ and unit $(I,T_0)$.
