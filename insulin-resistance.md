# Insulin resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Diabetes](diabetes.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insulin_resistance)

A reduced biological response to [insulin](insulin.md); it is a property of response, not simply a low [insulin](insulin.md) [concentration](concentration.md). The [glucose clamp technique](glucose-clamp-technique.md) helps separate these quantities.
