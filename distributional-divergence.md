# Distributional divergence

↑ **Parent:** [Divergence](divergence.md)

For a locally integrable [vector field](vector-field.md), this formula defines its divergence as a [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md). For a piecewise [smooth](smooth-function.md) field, a jump of its normal component across an interface adds a surface measure to the [smooth](smooth-function.md) divergences. A [continuous](continuous-function.md) normal component eliminates that measure, as follows by [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) on the two sides.
