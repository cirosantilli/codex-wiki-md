# Nullity of a composition

↑ **Parent:** [Rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md)

For finite-dimensional [linear maps](linear-map.md) $T:U\to V$ and $S:V\to W$, the restriction of $T$ to $\ker(ST)$ maps onto $\operatorname{im}T\cap\ker S$ and has kernel $\ker T$. The [rank-nullity theorem](rank-nullity-theorem.md) on this restriction proves the title identity. Since the intersection has dimension at most $\dim\ker S$, it implies $n(ST)\leq n(T)+n(S)$. Equivalently, $r(ST)\geq r(T)+r(S)-\dim V$, the [rank inequality for a composition](rank-inequality-for-a-composition.md). Also $\operatorname{im}(ST)=S(\operatorname{im}T)$ directly gives the upper bounds $r(ST)\leq\min(r(S),r(T))$.
