# Torque-free rigid-body invariants

↑ **Parent:** [Euler equations for a torque-free rigid body](euler-equations-for-a-torque-free-rigid-body.md)

The [Euler equations for a torque-free rigid body](euler-equations-for-a-torque-free-rigid-body.md) conserve rotational kinetic energy and squared angular momentum,

$$
E=\frac12\sum_{i=1}^3I_i\omega_i^2,
\qquad
L^2=\sum_{i=1}^3I_i^2\omega_i^2.
$$

Their common level sets are the intersection of two ellipsoids in angular-velocity space.
