# Two-qubit entropy from the reduced determinant

↑ **Parent:** [Entanglement entropy](entanglement-entropy.md)

For a unit-trace two-dimensional reduced state of a bipartite [pure state](pure-state.md), [determinant](determinant.md) $D$ gives [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) $(1\pm\sqrt{1-4D})/2$. Their binary entropy quantifies [entanglement](entangled-state.md). Natural logarithms give a maximum $\ln2$; base-two logarithms give one bit.
