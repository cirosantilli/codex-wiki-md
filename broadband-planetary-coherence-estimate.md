# Broadband planetary coherence estimate

↑ **Parent:** [Optical coherence](optical-coherence.md)

For an unresolved incoherent luminous disc of [angular diameter](angular-diameter.md) $\alpha$, transverse optical coherence has scale $\lambda/\alpha$; a uniform circular brightness profile puts its first zero at $1.21967\lambda/\alpha$. A receiver transmitting a continuous [frequency](frequency.md) band of width $\Delta\nu$ instead has [temporal coherence](temporal-optical-coherence.md) scale $1/\Delta\nu$ and longitudinal length $c/\Delta\nu$. Angular source size and spectral filtering control different correlations. Atmospheric [phase](phase-waves.md) fluctuations and instrument averaging can further reduce measured fringe visibility.
