# Strengthened Weierstrass condition

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass excess function](weierstrass-excess-function.md)

For a $C^1$ curve $u$, some $\delta>0$ must make the [Weierstrass excess function](weierstrass-excess-function.md) nonnegative for every $x\in[a,b]$, every trial slope $q\in\mathbb R$, and all $y,p$ satisfying $|y-u(x)|<\delta$, $|p-u'(x)|<\delta$. Thus the supporting-line property persists in a tube of nearby heights and reference slopes. The term “strengthened” here refers to this neighborhood requirement; strict excess for $q\ne p$ is a further condition.
