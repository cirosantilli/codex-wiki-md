# Spectral accuracy

↑ **Parent:** [Spectral method](spectral-method.md)

A [spectral method](spectral-method.md) has [spectral accuracy](spectral-accuracy.md) for a class of smooth data when its error decreases faster than any fixed inverse power of the truncation size. For periodic [Fourier series](fourier-series-split.md), this follows from arbitrarily many integrations by parts when every derivative matches across the periodic boundary. An analytic periodic extension to a complex strip gives exponential coefficient decay. Analyticity inside one period and matching only endpoint values do not suffice: a jump in a derivative of the periodic extension yields algebraic decay instead.
