# Ramification of the derivative of the Weierstrass elliptic function

↑ **Parent:** [Weierstrass elliptic function](weierstrass-elliptic-function.md)

On its period [torus](torus.md), the [derivative](derivative.md) has one triple [pole](pole.md), so degree three. The [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](riemann-hurwitz-formula.md) gives total ramification six, counting each [ramification point](ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) with weight $e_p-1$. The number of distinct [ramification points](ramification-point-of-a-holomorphic-map.md) is five when $g_2\ne0$ and three when $g_2=0$. Indeed $\wp''=6\wp^2-g_2/2$ by the [Weierstrass elliptic differential equation](weierstrass-elliptic-differential-equation.md). If $g_2\ne0$, each of its two possible $\wp$-values has two distinct unramified preimages for $\wp$, because coincidence with a cubic branch root would force a repeated root. These four simple zeros of $\wp''$ give local degree two for $\wp'$, and its triple [pole](pole.md) contributes local degree three. If $g_2=0$, nonsingularity forces $g_3\ne0$; the two simple zeros of $\wp$ are double zeros of $\wp''$ and each gives local degree three for $\wp'$. Along with its [pole](pole.md) these are three distinct points, each of weight two. A hexagonal [period lattice](period-lattice.md) realizes this second case by its sixth-root rotational symmetry.
