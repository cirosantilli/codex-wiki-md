# Caplet

↑ **Parent:** [Interest-rate cap](interest-rate-cap.md)

One payment period of an [interest-rate cap](interest-rate-cap.md). For $1+\delta K>0$, its value at the reset date is $(1-(1+\delta K)P(T,U))^+$. Thus the caplet is equivalent before reset to $1+\delta K$ units of a put expiring at $T$ on the maturity-$U$ [zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md), with strike $(1+\delta K)^{-1}$.
