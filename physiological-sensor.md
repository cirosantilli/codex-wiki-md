# Physiological sensor

↑ **Parent:** [Homeostasis](homeostasis.md)

A [physiological sensor](physiological-sensor.md) detects a relevant condition and changes a signal. In [thermoregulation](thermoregulation.md), temperature-sensitive [neurons](neuron.md) supply information about internal and [skin](skin.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md). Sensing a disturbance and sensing its internal consequence need not be the same operation.
