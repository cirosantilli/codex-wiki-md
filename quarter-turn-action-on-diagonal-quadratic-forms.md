# Quarter-turn action on diagonal quadratic forms

↑ **Parent:** [Group representation](group-representation.md)

Let $J$ be the plane [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md) through $\pi/2$, generating a [cyclic group](cyclic-group.md) of order four. A [quadratic form](quadratic-form-split.md) $ax^2+by^2$ has its two coefficients exchanged by a quarter-turn and unchanged by a half-turn. Thus the pullback action on this two-dimensional space gives the displayed [group representation](group-representation.md): $D(J^k)=D(J)^k$, with $D(J)^2=I$. It is not a [faithful representation](faithful-representation.md), because its [kernel of a group homomorphism](kernel-of-a-group-homomorphism.md) is $\{I,J^2\}$. Its two invariant coefficient lines correspond to $x^2+y^2$ and $x^2-y^2$, on which a quarter-turn acts by $+1$ and $-1$, respectively. Restricting to this diagonal space is valid for these signed coordinate permutations; a general [linear transformation](linear-map.md) can introduce an $xy$ term.
