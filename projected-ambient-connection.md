# Projected ambient connection

↑ **Parent:** [Affine connection](affine-connection.md)

For a [Riemannian metric](riemannian-metric.md) on an ambient manifold and an [embedded submanifold](embedded-submanifold.md), let $\pi:TN|_M\to TM$ be [orthogonal projection](orthogonal-projection.md). Project the [restriction of a connection to an embedded submanifold](restriction-of-a-connection-to-an-embedded-submanifold.md) to obtain $D_XY=\pi\nabla_XY$. Since $\pi Y=Y$, the [Leibniz rule](leibniz-rule.md) and linearity over [smooth functions](smooth-function.md) hold, making $D$ an [affine connection](affine-connection.md). If the ambient connection is the [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md), pairing with tangent vectors shows that $D$ is a [metric connection](metric-connection.md); projecting the zero-torsion identity shows it is a [torsion-free connection](torsion-free-connection.md). By uniqueness, $D$ is the induced [Levi-Civita connection](levi-civita-connection.md).
