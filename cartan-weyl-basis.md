# Cartan-Weyl basis

↑ **Parent:** [Highest-weight representation](highest-weight-representation.md)

A Cartan-Weyl basis consists of a basis of a [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) together with root vectors $E_\alpha$. It makes weight raising and lowering explicit through $[H,E_\alpha]=\alpha(H)E_\alpha$.

A Chevalley basis imposes an additional integral normalization on these root vectors; that stricter basis is not interchangeable with an arbitrary Cartan-Weyl basis.
