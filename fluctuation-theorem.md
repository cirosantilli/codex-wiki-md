# Fluctuation theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy production](entropy-production.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluctuation_theorem)

A fluctuation theorem compares the [probability](probability.md) of a fluctuating [sample trajectory](sample-path.md) with that of its reverse. Positive mean [entropy production](entropy-production.md) permits negative individual realizations: what is constrained is their relative [probability](probability.md), not a prohibition on every reversal. The finite statement below compares specified forward and reverse [probability laws](probability-distribution.md); replacing the reverse law by the forward law requires an additional symmetry. The [biased-step entropy production model](biased-step-entropy-production-model.md) exhibits that symmetry exactly, while the [Crooks fluctuation theorem](crooks-fluctuation-theorem.md) compares two different driving protocols.
