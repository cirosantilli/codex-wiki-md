# Two-factor normal linear model

↑ **Parent:** [Normal linear model](normal-linear-model.md)

A two-factor [normal linear model](normal-linear-model.md) describes independent responses with factor-specific means and common normal error variance. The additive mean $\mu+\alpha_a+\gamma_j$ assumes no [interaction term](interaction-term.md); the full mean $\mu+\alpha_a+\gamma_j+\delta_{aj}$ permits the first factor effect to vary with the second. Constraints or [treatment coding](treatment-coding.md) identify the parameters. In a balanced two-by-five design the full model has ten free cell means.
