# Isothermal-isobaric ensemble

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical physics](statistical-physics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isothermal–isobaric_ensemble)

An equilibrium [statistical ensemble](statistical-ensemble.md) with fixed particle number, [pressure](pressure-split.md) and [temperature](temperature-split.md), allowing [volume](volume.md) and [energy](energy-split.md) to fluctuate. With a temperature-independent volume measure, its joint weight is proportional to $e^{-\beta(E+PV)}$. Its normalization and any temperature-dependent prefactor must be specified when taking thermodynamic derivatives.
