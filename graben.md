# Graben

↑ **Parent:** [Normal faulting](normal-faulting.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graben)

A relatively down-dropped block bounded by opposing [normal faults](normal-fault.md). A small secondary [graben](graben.md) can form within the distributed surface rupture zone of one [earthquake](earthquake.md).
