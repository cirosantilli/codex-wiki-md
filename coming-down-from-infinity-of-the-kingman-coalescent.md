# Coming down from infinity of the Kingman coalescent

↑ **Parent:** [Kingman's infinite coalescent](kingman-s-infinite-coalescent.md)

For $t>0$, the number $B_t$ of blocks is finite almost surely. On a finite restriction, the expected time to reach $m$ blocks is at most $\sum_{j>m}2/[j(j-1)]=2/m$. [Markov inequality](markov-inequality.md), followed by increasing the restriction, gives $\mathbb P(B_t>m)\leq2/(mt)$. Let $m\to\infty$, then use rational times and monotonicity to obtain the assertion simultaneously for every $t>0$.
