# Yoneda embedding

↑ **Parent:** [Yoneda lemma](yoneda-lemma.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoneda_embedding)

The Yoneda embedding sends $A$ to $\mathcal C(-,A)$. The [Yoneda lemma](yoneda-lemma.md) identifies natural transformations $yA\to yB$ with morphisms $A\to B$, so the embedding is [full and faithful](full-and-faithful-functor.md). It preserves every limit that exists in $\mathcal C$.
