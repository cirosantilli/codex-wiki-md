# Common symbiosis signaling pathway

↑ **Parent:** [Signal transduction](signal-transduction-split.md)

A partly shared plant signaling route used in arbuscular mycorrhizal and root-nodule symbioses. Nuclear calcium oscillations and [CCaMK](calcium-and-calmodulin-dependent-protein-kinase.md) are central elements; partner-specific reception and downstream programs remain distinct.
