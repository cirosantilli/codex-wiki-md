# Polyol process

↑ **Parent:** [Nanowire](nanowire.md)

A solution route in which a polyol serves as [solvent](solvent.md) and reducing medium for metal [nucleation](nucleation.md) and growth. For [silver](silver.md) wires, controlled seed structure and capping chemistry can favour axial growth. [Temperature](temperature-split.md), [reduction](reduction-chemistry.md) rate, seed population, halides, oxygen and [ligand](ligand-biochemistry.md) coverage act together; the method is not defined by a single universal recipe.
