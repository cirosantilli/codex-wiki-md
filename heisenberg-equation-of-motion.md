# Heisenberg equation of motion

↑ **Parent:** [Heisenberg picture](heisenberg-picture.md)

For an operator $O_H=U^\dagger O_SU$ in the [Heisenberg picture](heisenberg-picture.md), differentiating the evolution operators gives $dO_H/dt=i[H_H,O_H]+U^\dagger(\partial O_S/\partial t)U$ in units $\hbar=1$. With no explicit operator time dependence, the second term vanishes. Applying this [commutator](commutator.md) identity to the [canonical momentum](canonical-momentum.md) and [real scalar field](real-scalar-field.md) gives their operator field equations.
