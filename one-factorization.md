# One-factorization

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect matching](perfect-matching.md)

A one-factorization partitions every edge of a graph into [perfect matchings](perfect-matching.md). Each matching uses every vertex exactly once. A [total of synthemes](total-of-synthemes.md) is precisely a one-factorization of the complete graph on six points.
