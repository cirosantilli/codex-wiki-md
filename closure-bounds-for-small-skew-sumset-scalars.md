# Closure bounds for small skew-sumset scalars

↑ **Parent:** [Sum-product phenomenon](sum-product-phenomenon.md)

Write $D(t)=|A+tA|/|A|$ and suppose some nonzero $b$ has $D(b)\leq K$. The mixed-set [Plünnecke-Ruzsa inequality](plunnecke-ruzsa-inequality.md) gives $|3A-2A|\leq K^5|A|$ and $|A+A|\leq K^2|A|$. The [Ruzsa covering lemma](ruzsa-covering-lemma.md) covers $tA$ by at most $D(t)$ translates of $A-A$. Covering two dilates proves the addition bound. First covering $sA$, then both copies of $rA$ in $r(A-A)$, proves the multiplication bound. The [Ruzsa triangle inequality](ruzsa-triangle-inequality.md) also gives $D(-r)\leq K^2D(r)$. These bounds transfer controlled additive expansion through a bounded-complexity expression in [scalars](scalar.md).
