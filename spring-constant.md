# Spring constant

↑ **Parent:** [Spring](spring.md)

The spring constant is the positive proportionality coefficient in the ideal [spring](spring.md) [restoring force](restoring-force.md) $F=-kx$. Its SI unit is newtons per metre. With a constant attached [mass](mass.md) $m$, it sets the [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) $\omega=\sqrt{k/m}$ of [simple harmonic motion](simple-harmonic-motion.md). Doubling $k$ doubles the restoring [force](force.md) at a fixed [displacement](displacement.md), but increases that [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) only by a factor $\sqrt2$.
