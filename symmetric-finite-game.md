# Symmetric finite game

↑ **Parent:** [Finite game](finite-game.md)

A [finite game](finite-game.md) is symmetric when all players have the same [pure strategies](pure-strategy.md) and permuting the players permutes their payoffs. In the anonymous special case, a player's payoff depends on their own action and the multiset of opposing actions. A [symmetric equilibrium](symmetric-equilibrium.md) exists even when there are more than two players, by the [gain-map proof of symmetric equilibrium](gain-map-proof-of-symmetric-equilibrium.md).
