# Density operator controllability

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum controllability](quantum-controllability.md)

A closed system has density operator controllability when every pair of [density operators](density-matrix.md) with the same spectrum can be connected by its controls. Under the standard finite-dimensional Hamiltonian assumptions, this holds exactly for [Dynamical Lie algebra](dynamical-lie-algebra.md) $\mathfrak u(N)$ or $\mathfrak{su}(N)$. This definition ranges over every spectral orbit; a particular degenerate spectrum can be controllable under a smaller group.
