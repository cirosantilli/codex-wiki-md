# Skew polynomial ring of an automorphism

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial ring](polynomial-ring.md)

For an [automorphism](automorphism.md) $\sigma$ of a unital [ring](ring.md) $A$, this [ring](ring.md) consists of finite sums $\sum_{j\geq0}a_jt^j$, with multiplication determined by $ta=\sigma(a)t$. Both left and right coefficient normal forms are available because $\sigma$ is invertible. The [noncommutative Hilbert basis theorem](noncommutative-hilbert-basis-theorem.md) implies that it is a [right Noetherian ring](right-noetherian-ring.md) when $A$ is, and a [left Noetherian ring](left-noetherian-ring.md) when $A$ is. Invertibility of $\sigma$ matters in this two-sided formulation.
