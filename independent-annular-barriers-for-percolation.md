# Independent annular barriers for percolation

↑ **Parent:** [One-arm probability](one-arm-probability.md)

Closed dual [graph cycles](cycle-in-a-graph.md) in disjoint square rings depend on disjoint primal [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md) sets. In independent [bond percolation](bond-percolation-split.md) their barrier events are independent. If each event has [probability](probability.md) at least $\delta>0$, an open [graph path](path-in-a-graph.md) from the origin across $k$ rings has [probability](probability.md) at most $(1-\delta)^k$. A geometric sequence of radii turns this estimate into the [polynomial critical one-arm upper bound](polynomial-critical-one-arm-upper-bound.md).
