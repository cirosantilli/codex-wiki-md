# Collisional phase relaxation and Lorentzian linewidth

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure broadening](pressure-broadening.md)

If phase-randomizing collisions form a Poisson process of mean interval $\tau_c$, field correlation survives only when no such collision occurs during the delay. Thus its magnitude decays as $e^{-|\tau|/\tau_c}$. The [Wiener-Khinchin theorem](wiener-khinchin-theorem.md) gives the normalized [Lorentzian line shape](lorentzian-line-shape.md)

$$
L(\omega)=\frac1\pi\frac{1/\tau_c}{(\omega-\omega_0)^2+(1/\tau_c)^2}.
$$

Its [full width at half maximum](full-width-at-half-maximum.md) is $2/\tau_c$ in [angular frequency](angular-frequency.md) or $1/(\pi\tau_c)$ in ordinary [frequency](frequency.md). A population lifetime is a different time constant: lifetime-limited amplitude decay at half the population rate introduces the familiar factor of two. The definition of the supplied lifetime must therefore accompany any numerical linewidth.
