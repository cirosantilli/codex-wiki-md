# Maximum-entropy regularization functional

↑ **Parent:** [Variational regularization](variational-regularization.md)

This [convex function](convex-function.md) penalizes positive densities using negative entropy, with $0\log0=0$ and infinite value for negative inputs. Its scalar derivative is $\log x$ on $x>0$. On a bounded domain, restricting to uniformly positive bounded functions gives a simple setting for a [Fréchet derivative](frechet-derivative.md). Its [Bregman distance](bregman-divergence.md) is the [generalized Kullback–Leibler divergence](generalized-kullback-leibler-divergence.md). With fixed total mass, minimizing this negative-entropy expression maximizes the corresponding entropy.
