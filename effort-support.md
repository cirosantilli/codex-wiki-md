# Effort support

↑ **Parent:** [Support of a probability distribution](support-of-a-probability-distribution.md)

An effort support is the [support of a probability distribution](support-of-a-probability-distribution.md) used to randomize effort in a contest. The [all-pay indifference equation with random entry](all-pay-indifference-equation-with-random-entry.md) determines this support together with its [distribution function](cumulative-distribution-function.md).
