# Blocking in classical conditioning

↑ **Parent:** [Classical conditioning](classical-conditioning.md)

Reduced [learning](learning.md) about an added cue when another cue already predicts the outcome. Control conditions must match exposure and outcome history; blocking in [learning](learning.md) is distinct from blocking in experimental design.
