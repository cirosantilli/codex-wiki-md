# Positive alternating simplex

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial map](simplicial-map.md)

For a [simplicial map](simplicial-map.md) to the [standard simplicial decomposition of a sphere](standard-simplicial-decomposition-of-a-sphere.md), a $k$-simplex is positive alternating when its image has the displayed distinct labels. Reversing all signs makes it negative alternating. A collapsed or nonalternating simplex is neutral. Counting positive facets modulo two is the local ingredient in the [antipodal alternating-simplex parity lemma](antipodal-alternating-simplex-parity-lemma.md).
