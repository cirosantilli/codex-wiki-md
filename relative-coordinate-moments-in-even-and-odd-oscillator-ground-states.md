# Relative-coordinate moments in even and odd oscillator ground states

↑ **Parent:** [Fermi-Dirac statistics](fermi-dirac-statistics.md)

The lowest even [quantum harmonic oscillator](quantum-harmonic-oscillator-split.md) [quantum state](quantum-state.md) has density $\rho(y)=e^{-y^2/\tau^2}/(\sqrt\pi\tau)$, and the lowest odd [quantum state](quantum-state.md) has density $2y^2\rho(y)/\tau^2$. Since $\rho'=-2y\rho/\tau^2$, [integration by parts](integration-by-parts.md) gives $\mathbb E_{\mathrm F}[f]=\mathbb E_{\mathrm B}[f+yf']$ whenever the boundary terms vanish. For $f(y)=y^n$, this proves the displayed identity. Odd moments vanish in both [quantum states](quantum-state.md) by [parity](parity.md); even moments quantify the larger spatial spread in the odd [quantum state](quantum-state.md). In particular the relative-coordinate [variance](variance-split.md) is three times as large, and the odd density vanishes at particle coincidence.
