# Variable selection

↑ **Parent:** [Model selection](model-selection.md)

Variable selection chooses which predictor variables enter a statistical model. The [Lasso](lasso.md) produces exact zero slopes along its [Lasso regularization path](lasso-regularization-path.md); [ridge regression](ridge-regression.md) generally shrinks without excluding variables. Selection for prediction should use predictive [cross-validation](cross-validation.md) or another stated criterion, rather than equating a coefficient p-value with practical usefulness.
