# Polyomino

↑ **Parent:** [Combinatorics](combinatorics-split.md)

A finite connected union of unit squares on the square lattice, joined along whole sides. Counting conventions may identify shapes under [translation](translation-geometry.md), [rotation](rotation-mathematics.md), or [reflection](reflection-mathematics.md), so those conventions must be specified.
