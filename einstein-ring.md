# Einstein ring

↑ **Parent:** [Strong gravitational lensing](strong-gravitational-lensing.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einstein_ring)

An aligned source behind an axisymmetric [gravitational lens](gravitational-lens.md) produces a circular image at an [Einstein radius](einstein-radius.md). Its angular radius satisfies $\theta_E^2=4GM_{\rm projected}(<D_d\theta_E)D_{ds}/(c^2D_dD_s)$. Exact alignment gives a ring; displaced sources generally give arcs or distinct images rather than a complete ring.
