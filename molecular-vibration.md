# Molecular vibration

↑ **Parent:** [Molecule](molecule-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_vibration)

[Molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md) changes internal separations and [bond angles](bond-angle.md), unlike the fixed internal [molecular geometry](molecular-geometry.md) of a [rigid rotor](rigid-rotor.md). Its coupling to [molecular rotation](molecular-rotation.md) makes measured [rotational constants](rotational-constant.md) depend on the vibrational [quantum state](quantum-state.md).

A [diatomic molecule](diatomic-molecule.md) has one internal stretching coordinate: its two [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md) approach and separate about an equilibrium [bond length](bond-length.md). The [diatomic bond stretch](diatomic-bond-stretch.md) turns this motion into a particularly useful model. The local [potential energy](potential-energy.md) curvature supplies the restoring [force](force-split.md), while the [reduced mass](reduced-mass.md) accounts for both moving partners. Thus the same [chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md) can vibrate more slowly after an [isotope](isotope.md) substitution even when its electronic structure is nearly unchanged.

This separation makes [infrared spectroscopy](infrared-spectroscopy.md) informative: transition [frequencies](frequency.md) constrain [chemical bond](chemical-bond-split.md) stiffness, and [isotope scaling of diatomic vibration](isotope-scaling-of-diatomic-vibration.md) tests the mass dependence. The [University of Florida HCl and DCl FTIR experiment](university-of-florida-hcl-and-dcl-ftir-experiment.md) illustrates the measurement. Detectability also requires [vibrational infrared activity](vibrational-infrared-activity.md); having vibrational [energy eigenvalues](energy-eigenvalue-split.md) alone does not guarantee an [infrared](infrared.md) absorption line.
