# Correspondence bias

↑ **Parent:** [Attribution theory](attribution-theory.md)

A tendency to infer personal characteristics corresponding to observed conduct more strongly than is warranted after relevant situational constraints are considered. Establishing [correspondence bias](correspondence-bias.md) requires a justified benchmark for what the behavior reveals under those constraints. It is not a claim that every [dispositional attribution](dispositional-attribution.md) is an error.
