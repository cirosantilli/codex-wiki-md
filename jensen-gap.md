# Jensen gap

↑ **Parent:** [Jensen's inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md)

The [Jensen gap](jensen-gap.md) is the nonnegative difference $\mathbb E[f(X)]-f(\mathbb E X)$ in [Jensen's inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md), under its [integrability](integrability.md) assumptions. It quantifies how much applying a [convex function](convex-function.md) before averaging differs from applying it afterward. For $f(x)=x^2$ it is the [variance](variance-split.md) of $X$; for an [affine function](affine-function.md) it is zero. For general [convex functions](convex-function.md), it depends on both the [function](function-split.md) and the distribution of $X$, and is not determined by the [variance](variance-split.md) alone.
