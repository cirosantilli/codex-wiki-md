# Proca time-ordering contact term

↑ **Parent:** [Proca propagator](proca-propagator.md)

For a free massive [Proca field](proca-field.md) in the mostly-plus [Minkowski metric](minkowski-metric.md), the covariant inverse of the quadratic [action](action.md) and the canonical [time-ordered product](time-ordered-product.md) of vector potentials differ locally. With [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) $e^{iq\cdot(x-y)}$, the covariant [Proca propagator](proca-propagator.md) is $-i(\eta_{ab}+q_aq_b/m^2)/(q^2+m^2-i0)$, while the literal canonical [two-point correlation function](two-point-correlation-function.md) subtracts $i\delta_a^0\delta_b^0/m^2$ in momentum space. The time component is nondynamical; its physical-mode numerator is $|\boldsymbol q|^2/m^2$, rather than $-1+(q^0)^2/m^2$. The difference is an instantaneous [Dirac delta function](dirac-delta-function.md), not another propagating state. Both expressions agree away from coincidence. This distinction matters when identifying a covariant [Green function](green-s-function.md) with a canonical correlator in a constrained field theory.
