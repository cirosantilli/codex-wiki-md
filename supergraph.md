# Supergraph

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md)

A supergraph is a [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md) formulated with [superfield](superfield.md) propagators and vertices in [superspace](superspace.md). Performing its spinor-derivative algebra organizes perturbative corrections into [superspace integration](superspace-integration.md) terms and provides a proof of the local [superpotential](superpotential.md) [non-renormalization theorem](non-renormalization-theorem.md).
