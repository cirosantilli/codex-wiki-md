# Ekman number

↑ **Parent:** [Geophysical fluid dynamics](geophysical-fluid-dynamics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ekman_number)

The ratio of viscous force to [Coriolis force](coriolis-force.md) in a rotating fluid at length scale $L$. This convention uses rotation frequency $2\Omega$; conventions omitting the factor two are also common. For small $E$, bulk viscosity is weak but no-slip boundaries can form [Ekman layers](ekman-layer.md) of thickness of order $L\sqrt E$, with relatively larger boundary shear stress.
