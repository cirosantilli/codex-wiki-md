<h1 id="bott-chern-cohomology-of-a-compact-kahler-manifold">Bott-Chern cohomology of a compact Kähler manifold</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Bott-Chern cohomology](bott-chern-cohomology.md)

For a compact [Kähler manifold](kahler-manifold.md), the natural map $H_{BC}^{p,q}\to H_{\bar\partial}^{p,q}$ is an isomorphism. The [Kähler Laplacian identity](kahler-laplacian-identity.md) makes the harmonic representative of every [Dolbeault cohomology](dolbeault-cohomology.md) class $d$-closed, giving surjectivity, and the [ddbar lemma](ddbar-lemma.md) gives injectivity.
