<h1 id="vapor-solid-growth">Vapor–solid growth</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Nanowire](nanowire.md)

Growth by direct incorporation of vapour-derived species into a solid, without the defining liquid reservoir of [vapor–liquid–solid growth](vapor-liquid-solid-growth.md). It can thicken a nanowire sidewall during tip growth.
