# Antiparallel beta sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Beta sheet](beta-sheet.md)

An [antiparallel beta sheet](antiparallel-beta-sheet.md) has neighboring [beta strands](beta-strand.md) running in opposite N→C sequence directions. This specifies strand orientation rather than whether the [beta strands](beta-strand.md) belong to one [polypeptide chain](polypeptide.md) or several.
