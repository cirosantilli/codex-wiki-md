# Geometric sequences with accumulating ratios span ell-one

↑ **Parent:** [Absolutely summable sequence space](absolutely-summable-sequence-space.md)

Suppose the distinct complex numbers $\beta_k$ satisfy $|\beta_k|\leq\delta<1$. The [geometric sequences](geometric-progression.md) in the displayed formula belong to the [absolutely summable sequence space](absolutely-summable-sequence-space.md) and have [dense](dense-set.md) linear span. Otherwise the [Hahn-Banach theorem](hahn-banach-theorem.md) gives a nonzero annihilating functional, represented by a [bounded sequence](bounded-sequence.md) $b_j$. Its [power series](power-series.md) $F(z)=\sum_{j\geq1}b_jz^{j-1}$ is holomorphic in the unit disk and vanishes at every $\beta_k$. Compactness supplies an accumulation point inside that disk. The [identity theorem](identity-theorem.md) forces $F=0$ and hence all its coefficients zero, a contradiction. This is a useful form of [density by annihilators](density-by-annihilators.md) that converts a Banach-space problem into uniqueness of an analytic function.
