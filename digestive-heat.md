# Digestive heat

↑ **Parent:** [History of medicine](history-of-medicine.md)

Historical explanatory power thought to transform [food](food.md) into bodily nourishment by [digestion](digestion.md). In [Aretaeus of Cappadocia](aretaeus-of-cappadocia.md), weakness of this power explains the [coeliac affection of Aretaeus](coeliac-affection-of-aretaeus.md); this is not the modern mechanism of [coeliac disease](coeliac-disease.md).
