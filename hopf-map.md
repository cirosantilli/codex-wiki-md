# Hopf map

↑ **Parent:** [Hopf fibration](hopf-fibration.md)

The complex [Hopf fibration](hopf-fibration.md) on the unit sphere of $\mathbb C^2$ sends $(z_0,z_1)$ to its complex line in $\mathbb{CP}^1\cong S^2$. Its fibres are circles. With compatible orientations, its [Hopf invariant](hopf-invariant.md) is one and it generates $\pi_3(S^2)\cong\mathbb Z$.
