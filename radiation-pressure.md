# Radiation pressure

↑ **Parent:** [Pressure](pressure-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiation_pressure)

[Radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md) is the normal [momentum flux](momentum-flux.md) transferred by [electromagnetic radiation](electromagnetic-radiation-split.md) to a surface. A [photon](photon.md) travelling in [vacuum](vacuum.md) carries [momentum](momentum-split.md) $p_\gamma=E_\gamma/c=h\nu/c$, where $E_\gamma$ is its [energy](energy-split.md), $\nu$ its [frequency](frequency.md), $h$ the [Planck constant](planck-constant.md) and $c$ the [speed of light](speed-of-light.md). [Photon absorption](photon-absorption.md) transfers that [momentum](momentum-split.md); [specular reflection](specular-reflection.md) reverses its normal component. This lets [light](light.md) exert a [force](force-split.md) even when almost no [energy](energy-split.md) becomes [heat](heat-split.md) in the receiver.

Two useful formulas describe different preparations. A directed beam normally incident on a stationary perfect absorber gives [pressure](pressure-split.md) $I/c$, and on a stationary perfect mirror gives $2I/c$. Here $I$ is [light intensity](light-intensity.md), the incident [power](power.md) per beam cross-sectional [area](surface-area.md) perpendicular to propagation, in $\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$. An [isotropic](isotropy.md) [photon gas](photon-gas.md) has [pressure](pressure-split.md) $u/3$, where $u=U/V$ is its radiation [internal energy](internal-energy-split.md) per [volume](volume-thermodynamics.md), in $\mathrm{J\,m^{-3}}$. Stored [energy density](energy-density.md) and travelling [energy flux](energy-flux.md) must be distinguished: an [isotropic](isotropy.md) field can have zero net [energy flux](energy-flux.md) and positive [radiation pressure](radiation-pressure.md).

The physical appeal is that the same [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) connects a single [photon](photon.md) collision, a mirror's measurable [force](force-split.md), and a radiation-filled cavity's [equation of state](equation-of-state.md). The directional calculation below complements [radiation pressure on a perfectly absorbing surface](radiation-pressure-on-a-perfectly-absorbing-surface.md); the equilibrium spectrum and its [temperature](temperature-split.md) dependence belong to [blackbody radiation](black-body-radiation.md) and the [photon gas](photon-gas.md).
