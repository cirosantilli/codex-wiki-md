# Inhibition constant

↑ **Parent:** [Reversible enzyme inhibition](reversible-enzyme-inhibition.md)

An [inhibition constant](inhibition-constant.md) sets an inhibitor [concentration](concentration.md) scale in an [enzyme kinetics](enzyme-kinetics.md) model. In the rapid-binding construction under [reversible enzyme inhibition](reversible-enzyme-inhibition.md), $K_i=[E]I/[EI]$ and $K_i'=[ES]I/[ESI]$ are [dissociation constants](dissociation-constant.md). Smaller values favour the corresponding bound population at fixed free inhibitor [concentration](concentration.md). More generally, fitted [inhibition constants](inhibition-constant.md) need not be true binding [dissociation constants](dissociation-constant.md); a mechanism must justify that interpretation.[Symbolism and Terminology in Enzyme Kinetics, section 6.4, final paragraphs: operational inhibition constants versus explicitly defined dissociation constants](symbolism-and-terminology-in-enzyme-kinetics-recommendation-1981-by-nomenclature-committee-of-the-international-union-of-biochemistry.md)

For example, choosing $I=K_i$ makes $[EI]=[E]$ in this model, but does not imply that half of all [enzyme](enzyme-split.md) is $EI$: $ES$ and $ESI$ also contribute to the total population.
