# Annual versus accumulated radiative forcing

↑ **Parent:** [Radiative forcing](radiative-forcing.md)

For $F(C)-F(C_0)=a\ln(C/C_0)$, a further annual [concentration](concentration.md) increment produces $a\ln[(C+\Delta C)/C]$, whereas the accumulated forcing from the original baseline is $a\ln(C/C_0)$. The former can decrease as $C$ rises while the latter increases. Maintaining a short-lived compensating [aerosol](aerosol.md) burden also requires replacement, not only compensation of the next increment.
