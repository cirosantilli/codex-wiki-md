# Ice ablation

↑ **Parent:** [Glaciology](glaciology.md)

Ice ablation removes glacier or ice-sheet material by melting, sublimation, or mechanical loss. A negative net [ice accumulation](ice-accumulation.md) rate removes ice only where ice is present; an initially bare region cannot acquire negative thickness.
