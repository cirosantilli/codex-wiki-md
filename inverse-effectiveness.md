# Inverse effectiveness

↑ **Parent:** [Multisensory response enhancement](multisensory-response-enhancement.md)

The tendency for relative multisensory enhancement to be larger when component stimuli evoke weak responses. Absolute gain and percentage gain must be distinguished: $100(R_{AV}-R_{\max})/R_{\max}$ can increase merely because the denominator becomes small. A ratio is undefined at a zero denominator and does not directly measure information or behavioral benefit.
