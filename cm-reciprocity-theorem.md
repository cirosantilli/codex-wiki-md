# CM reciprocity theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Complex multiplication](complex-multiplication.md)

For a complex CM torus $\mathbb C/\mathfrak a$ and an idele $s$ of its CM field, the automorphism corresponding to $s$ by [Artin reciprocity](artin-reciprocity-law.md) takes its lattice to $s^{-1}\mathfrak a$ and acts on torsion through the adelic scalar $s^{-1}$. When the curve is defined over a larger field, use the idelic norm to the CM field. Comparison with the original curve gives a scalar in the CM field, producing the [Hecke character of a CM elliptic curve](hecke-character-of-a-cm-elliptic-curve.md).
