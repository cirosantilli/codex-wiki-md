# Global phase of a controlled quantum gate

↑ **Parent:** [Controlled unitary gate](controlled-unitary-gate.md)

Multiplying a target [unitary operator](unitary-operator.md) by $e^{i\phi}$ changes the controlled operation by a [relative quantum phase](relative-quantum-phase.md) on its control-one branch. Substitution in $C(V)=|0\rangle\langle0|\otimes I+|1\rangle\langle1|\otimes V$ proves the displayed identity. In particular, $C(-I)=Z\otimes I$ rather than the [identity operator](identity-operator.md). Preparing the control at $|+\rangle$ and applying a final [Hadamard gate](hadamard-gate.md) distinguishes $C(I)$ from $C(-I)$ with certainty, independently of the target [quantum state](quantum-state.md). The [global phase of an uncontrolled quantum gate is unobservable](global-phase-of-an-uncontrolled-quantum-gate-is-unobservable.md), but a specified [controlled unitary gate](controlled-unitary-gate.md) provides a branch relative to which that phase can be measured.
