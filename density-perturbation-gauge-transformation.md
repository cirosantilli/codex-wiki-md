# Density perturbation gauge transformation

↑ **Parent:** [Gauge transformation in cosmological perturbation theory](gauge-transformation-in-cosmological-perturbation-theory.md)

Density is a [scalar field](scalar-field.md). Under a first-order time shift $\widetilde\tau=\tau+T$, its background changes by $\bar\rho'T$, so its perturbation changes by the negative of that amount. Spatial shifts act only at second order on a homogeneous background density.
