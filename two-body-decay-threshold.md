# Two-body decay threshold

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic two-body decay](relativistic-two-body-decay.md)

In the rest frame of the parent [particle](particle-physics.md), [four-momentum conservation](four-momentum-conservation.md) requires $M=E_1+E_2\ge m_1+m_2$. For two equal masses the daughter [momentum](momentum-split.md) magnitude is $\tfrac12\sqrt{M^2-4m^2}$; the [two-body decay phase space](two-body-decay-phase-space.md) closes at $M=2m$. For a finite constant [decay amplitude](decay-amplitude.md), the [decay width](decay-width.md) tends to zero at this threshold.
