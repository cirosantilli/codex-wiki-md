# Projective image of the square symmetry group

↑ **Parent:** [Projective representation](projective-representation.md)

Let $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ and $B=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$. They generate the [dihedral group](dihedral-group.md) of a square, realized by $\{\pm I,\pm A,\pm B,\pm AB\}$. Its image in the [projective linear group](projective-linear-group.md) has four elements, since opposite [matrices](matrix.md) become equal. The projective classes of $A,B$ commute because $AB=-BA$, and each squares to the identity. Sending $[A^aB^b]$ to $(a,b)$ gives the [Klein four-group](klein-four-group.md). This is an example in which a nontrivial scalar commutator disappears after passing to a [projective representation](projective-representation.md).
