# Lipid mixing assay

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane fusion](membrane-fusion.md)

A readout of lipid redistribution between membrane populations, for example through [fluorescence dequenching](fluorescence-dequenching.md). It must be distinguished from aqueous [content mixing](content-mixing-assay.md) and leakage.
