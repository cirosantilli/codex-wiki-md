# Electrostatic screening

↑ **Parent:** [Electrostatics](electrostatics-split.md)

Mobile [electric charges](electric-charge-split.md) redistribute in response to an imposed [electric potential](electric-potential.md), partly compensating its field. The induced density response and the Poisson equation must be solved self-consistently. Classical dilute mobile ions lead to the [Debye–Hückel approximation](debye-huckel-approximation.md); a degenerate electron system instead has a response set by its electronic compressibility and interactions. A high charge response can pin a partly occupied [Landau level](landau-level.md) near the [Fermi energy](fermi-energy.md), whereas an incompressible gap suppresses that response. [electrostatic screening](electrostatic-screening.md) is not a claim that every material perfectly excludes every static field.
