# Dark-matter decay radiation

↑ **Parent:** [Dark matter](dark-matter.md)

If a [dark matter](dark-matter.md) particle of mass $m_\chi$ radiates power $\epsilon$ independently of its neighbours, optically thin isotropic emission has volume emissivity $j=\epsilon\rho/m_\chi$. Its [surface brightness](surface-brightness.md) is $I=(4\pi)^{-1}\int j\,dz$ and therefore traces the mass column rather than its square.
