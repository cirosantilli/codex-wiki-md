# Moving Gumbel cutoff in an inverse-power tail

↑ **Parent:** [Gumbel distribution](gumbel-distribution.md)

For fixed $a>0$ and $r>1$, the factor $\exp(-\lambda e^{-x})$ is a translated [Gumbel distribution](gumbel-distribution.md) cumulative function, with transition near $x=L$. Integration by parts transforms the [integral](integral.md) to an exponentially small boundary term plus $(r-1)^{-1}\int_0^{\lambda e^{-a}}e^{-u}(L-\log u)^{1-r}du$. Expanding the latter against its finite logarithmic moments gives the displayed two terms because $\int_0^\infty e^{-u}\log u\,du=-\gamma$, with $\gamma$ [Euler's constant](euler-s-constant.md). The smooth cutoff shifts the first correction even though replacing it by a step gives the leading tail.
