# Root of a complex number

↑ **Parent:** [Polar form of a complex number](polar-form-of-a-complex-number.md)

For $z=re^{i\theta}\ne0$ and a positive integer $n$, all solutions of $w^n=z$ are $r^{1/n}e^{i(\theta+2\pi k)/n}$ for $k=0,\ldots,n-1$. They differ by multiplication by [roots of unity](root-of-unity.md) and are distinct. If $z=0$, the sole root is zero.
