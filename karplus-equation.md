# Karplus equation

↑ **Parent:** [Nuclear magnetic resonance](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karplus_equation)

A [Karplus equation](karplus-equation.md) relates a vicinal scalar coupling to molecular torsion, often represented as $J=A\cos^2\phi+B\cos\phi+C$. Parameters depend on the chemical environment. A mobile molecule gives a population average of couplings, which is generally not the coupling evaluated at its mean angle.
