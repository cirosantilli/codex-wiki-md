# Bogomolny degree bound for the O3 sigma model

↑ **Parent:** [Degree charge of an O3 sigma-model lump](degree-charge-of-an-o3-sigma-model-lump.md)

For $E=\tfrac12\int(|\partial_1\mathbf n|^2+|\partial_2\mathbf n|^2)$, completing the square gives $E=\tfrac12\int|\partial_1\mathbf n\pm\mathbf n\times\partial_2\mathbf n|^2\pm4\pi Q$, hence $E\geq4\pi|Q|$. Saturation yields the [Bogomolny equations](bogomolny-equations.md) of the [sigma-model lump](sigma-model-lump.md). With the oriented [stereographic projection](stereographic-projection.md) $\mathbf n=(2\operatorname{Re}w,2\operatorname{Im}w,1-|w|^2)/(1+|w|^2)$, holomorphic rational maps have positive degree and saturate the bound; antiholomorphic maps have negative degree.
