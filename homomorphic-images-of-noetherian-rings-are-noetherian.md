# Homomorphic images of Noetherian rings are Noetherian

↑ **Parent:** [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md)

Let $\phi:R\to S$ be a [surjective](surjective-function.md) [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) and let $R$ be a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md). For every [ideal](ideal.md) $J$ of $S$, its inverse image is a finitely generated [ideal](ideal.md) of $R$. If $\phi^{-1}(J)=(a_1,\ldots,a_r)$, then $J=(\phi(a_1),\ldots,\phi(a_r))$. Thus $S$ is a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md). In particular, every [quotient ring](quotient-ring.md) of a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md) is a [Noetherian ring](noetherian-ring.md). For a [ring homomorphism](ring-homomorphism.md) that is not [surjective](surjective-function.md), apply the same argument with its image as codomain.
