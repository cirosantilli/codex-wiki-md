# Seismic wavelet

↑ **Parent:** [Seismology](seismology.md)

A localized seismic waveform used to describe a source or reflected pulse. Its phase and sign determine how an impedance-driven [seismic reflection polarity](seismic-reflection-polarity.md) appears in a recorded trace.
