# Conformal quadrilateral

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann surfaces](riemann-surfaces-split.md)

A [Jordan domain](jordan-domain.md) together with four distinct boundary points in cyclic order, regarded up to a [conformal map](conformal-map.md) preserving those marked points. Such a domain maps to a rectangle with its marked points at the four corners. The opposite boundary-arc pairs specify two dual curve-joining problems whose [extremal lengths](extremal-length.md) are reciprocal.
