# Homothety of complex lattices

↑ **Parent:** [Complex lattice](complex-lattice.md)

Two [complex lattices](complex-lattice.md) are homothetic when multiplication by one nonzero complex number takes one onto the other. Changes between oriented integral bases have determinant one, so normalized period ratios classify these classes by $SL_2(\mathbb Z)\backslash\mathbb H$. The [lattice Eisenstein sums](lattice-eisenstein-sum.md) scale as $G_k(a\Lambda)=a^{-k}G_k(\Lambda)$.
