# Navier slip boundary condition

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

A Navier slip boundary condition relates fluid velocity relative to a stationary flat wall to the wall-normal tangential velocity gradient, $u_b=\ell_s u_z$, with positive [slip length](slip-length.md) $\ell_s$. For a [Newtonian fluid](newtonian-fluid.md), it is equivalent to linear resisting wall traction of magnitude $\mu u_b/\ell_s$. The normal points from the wall into the fluid; this fixes the sign convention.
