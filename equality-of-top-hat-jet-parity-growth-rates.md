# Equality of top-hat jet parity growth rates

↑ **Parent:** [Equal-density top-hat planar-jet dispersion relation](equal-density-top-hat-planar-jet-dispersion-relation.md)

For a [top-hat planar jet](top-hat-planar-jet.md) in stationary fluid of the same [mass density](density.md), odd and even [velocity potentials](velocity-potential.md) have the same [temporal growth rate](growth-rate.md). Put $s=\tanh(|k|L)$. Their [dispersion relations](dispersion-relation.md) use $s$ and $1/s$, but $\sqrt{s}/(1+s)=\sqrt{1/s}/(1+1/s)$. Their real [phase velocities](phase-velocity.md) are respectively $Vs/(1+s)$ and $V/(1+s)$. The common [growth rate](growth-rate.md) is strictly below $|kV|/2$ for finite $|k|L>0$, approaching the isolated [vortex sheet](vortex-sheet.md) value when the sheets decouple.
