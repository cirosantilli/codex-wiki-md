# Harmonic sine mode in a half-strip

↑ **Parent:** [Dirichlet problem](dirichlet-problem.md)

For $a>0$, a [harmonic function](harmonic-function.md) on $x\ge0$, $0\le y\le a$ with zero data on the horizontal sides, sine data $\sin(n\pi y/a)$ at the end, and decay at infinity is given by the displayed expression for every integer $n$. The second derivatives cancel since the decay exponent squared equals the transverse [wavenumber](wavenumber.md) squared. The absolute value is necessary for negative integers; $n=0$ gives the zero solution. The solution is bounded and decays uniformly in $y$.
