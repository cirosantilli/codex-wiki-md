# Translation-character proof of Bloch theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Bloch's theorem](bloch-s-theorem.md)

The lattice translations commute with one another and with a lattice-periodic Hamiltonian. They can therefore be diagonalized simultaneously within each energy eigenspace. Every unitary character of a [Bravais lattice](bravais-lattice.md) has the form $r\mapsto e^{-ik\cdot r}$, with $k$ defined modulo the [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md). Hence $T_r\psi_k=e^{-ik\cdot r}\psi_k$, so $u_k(x)=e^{-ik\cdot x}\psi_k(x)$ is lattice-periodic.
