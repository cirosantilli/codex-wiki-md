# Two-layer sedimentation with a compression shock

↑ **Parent:** [Kinematic sedimentation](kinematic-sedimentation.md)

With upper concentration $a$ and lower concentration $2a$, the clearing, internal and deposit fronts have speeds $-u_s(1-a)$, $-u_s(1-3a)$ and $2u_sa$. If the lower two merge, the resulting deposit/upper-suspension front has speed $u_sa$. Final deposit thickness is $a(H+z_m)$ by [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md).
