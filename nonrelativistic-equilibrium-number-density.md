# Nonrelativistic equilibrium number density

↑ **Parent:** [Canonical ensemble](canonical-ensemble.md)

In the dilute Maxwell--Boltzmann regime, a species has number density proportional to $g(mk_BT)^{3/2}e^{(\mu-mc^2)/(k_BT)}$.
