# Setting-independent heralding preserves Bell locality

↑ **Parent:** [Local hidden-variable theory](local-hidden-variable-theory.md)

If a successful preparation flag is fixed before freely selected [Bell inequality](bell-inequality.md) settings, conditioning a [local hidden-variable theory](local-hidden-variable-theory.md) on that flag only replaces its hidden-variable distribution by the conditional distribution. The later response functions remain local and the distribution is independent of the later settings, so the [CHSH inequality](chsh-inequality.md) still holds. Setting-dependent rejection during the Bell measurements does not satisfy this argument.
