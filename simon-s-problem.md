<h1 id="simon-s-problem">Simon's problem</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hidden subgroup problem](hidden-subgroup-problem.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon's_problem)

Simon's problem asks for the hidden string $p\in\{0,1\}^n$ promised by $f(x)=f(y)$ exactly when $x\mathbin\oplus y\in\{0^n,p\}$. It is the [hidden subgroup problem](hidden-subgroup-problem.md) on $(\mathbb Z_2)^n$ with hidden subgroup $\{0^n,p\}$.
