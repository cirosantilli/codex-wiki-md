# Tensorization of a transport-entropy inequality

↑ **Parent:** [Transport-entropy inequality](transport-entropy-inequality.md)

If each coordinate law satisfies the same convex [transport-entropy inequality](transport-entropy-inequality.md), then the product law satisfies the sum-cost version. Sequentially couple conditional coordinates, apply [Jensen inequality](jensen-s-inequality.md), and use the [chain rule for relative entropy](chain-rule-for-relative-entropy.md).
