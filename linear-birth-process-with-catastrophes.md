# Linear birth process with catastrophes

↑ **Parent:** [Linear birth process with immigration](linear-birth-process-with-immigration.md)

Add independent rate-$\mu$ resets to zero to a [linear birth process with immigration](linear-birth-process-with-immigration.md). The resulting irreducible [continuous-time Markov chain](continuous-time-markov-chain.md) is nonexplosive by domination of births and finiteness of Poisson reset counts. When $\mu=\lambda$, an excursion from one reaches $n$ with probability $2/(n+1)$, so its [jump chain](jump-chain.md) is recurrent with infinite expected jump count. The elapsed return-cycle time instead has mean

$$
\lambda^{-1}+\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{2}{\lambda(n+1)(n+2)}=2/\lambda.
$$

Thus the continuous-time chain is positive recurrent while its jump chain is null recurrent. Its stationary probabilities are $\pi_0=1/2$ and $\pi_n=1/[(n+1)(n+2)]$ for $n\ge1$.
