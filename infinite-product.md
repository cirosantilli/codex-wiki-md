# Infinite product

↑ **Parent:** [Series (mathematics)](series-mathematics.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_product)

An infinite product is the [limit](limit-of-a-function.md) of the finite partial products $\prod_{n=1}^N a_n$. If $a_n=1+u_n$, every factor is nonzero, and $\sum_n|u_n|<\infty$, then the product converges to a nonzero value.
