# Correlated diphoton mass resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Diphoton invariant mass](diphoton-invariant-mass.md)

Logarithmic differentiation of the diphoton [mass](mass.md) gives $dM/M=(dE_1/E_1+dE_2/E_2)/2$ at fixed angle. If $s_i$ are fractional [energy](energy-split.md) [standard deviations](standard-deviation.md) and $\rho$ their [correlation coefficient](pearson-correlation-coefficient.md), [covariance](covariance.md) propagation gives the displayed formula. A common multiplicative [calibration](calibration-differential-geometry.md) error transfers directly to the reconstructed [mass](mass.md); independent errors combine differently. Angular errors, if present, add the corresponding derivative and [covariance](covariance.md) terms.
