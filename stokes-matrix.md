# Stokes matrix

↑ **Parent:** [Stokes phenomenon](stokes-phenomenon.md)

Two [sectorial fundamental solutions](sectorial-fundamental-solution.md) of the same linear system differ on their overlap by a constant invertible right factor, since $(Y_j^{-1}Y_{j+1})'=0$. With a common formal normalization these factors encode the exponentially small ambiguities invisible to the [asymptotic expansion](asymptotic-expansion.md). They are called [Stokes matrices](stokes-matrix.md). Equivalently their inverses act on column vectors of solution coefficients; the convention must be specified when ordering products.
