# Point-force radiation from a fluid-loaded sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Acoustic wave on a tensioned massive membrane](acoustic-wave-on-a-tensioned-massive-membrane.md)

A harmonic line [force](force.md) localized at one point of a two-dimensional sheet gives $\widehat\eta=F/D$ and $\Delta=\gamma D$ in the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md). The outgoing upper [pressure](pressure.md) is the displayed amplitude. Dividing by $c_0^2$ converts it to [acoustic density perturbation](acoustic-density-perturbation.md). The [method of steepest descent](method-of-steepest-descent.md) gives cylindrical sound from the [saddle point](saddle-point.md), while crossed zeros of the [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation.md) give guided or leaky modal residues. Retaining $k_0=\omega/c_0$ is essential for dimensionally consistent spreading and prefactors.
