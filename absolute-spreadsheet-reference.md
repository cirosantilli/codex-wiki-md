# Absolute spreadsheet reference

↑ **Parent:** [Spreadsheet cell reference](spreadsheet-cell-reference.md)

An [absolute spreadsheet reference](absolute-spreadsheet-reference.md), such as `$F$1`, fixes both coordinates when its [spreadsheet formula](spreadsheet-formula.md) is copied. In the [spreadsheet tabular model](spreadsheet-tabular-model.md), every copied total reads the same tax rate. Absolute addressing retains the [spreadsheet cell reference](spreadsheet-cell-reference.md), not the value: changing the [input](input-computer-science.md) in `F1` changes the computed totals even though the references remain `$F$1`.
