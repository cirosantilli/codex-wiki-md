# Implication elimination rule

↑ **Parent:** [Natural deduction](natural-deduction.md)

From $A\to B$ and $A$, implication elimination derives $B$. Under the [Curry-Howard correspondence](curry-howard-correspondence.md), this is application: from [lambda terms](lambda-term.md) $f:A\to B$ and $a:A$ form $f\,a:B$.
