# Iwasawa-module rank

↑ **Parent:** [Iwasawa algebra of a Zp-extension](iwasawa-algebra-of-a-zp-extension.md)

For a finitely generated [module](module-mathematics.md) over the [integral domain](integral-domain.md) $\Lambda$, its Iwasawa-module rank counts free summands up to [pseudo-isomorphism](pseudo-isomorphism.md). Rank zero is equivalent to being a [torsion module](torsion-module.md). A positive rank $r$ forces the $\mathbb Z_p$-ranks of finite-layer [coinvariant modules](coinvariant-module.md) to grow like $r p^n+O(1)$.
