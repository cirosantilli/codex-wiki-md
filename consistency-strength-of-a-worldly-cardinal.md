# Consistency strength of a worldly cardinal

↑ **Parent:** [Consistency-strength preorder](consistency-strength-preorder.md)

Let $T=\mathrm{ZFC}+\text{“there is a worldly cardinal”}$ and $T^*=\mathrm{ZFC}+\operatorname{Con}(\mathrm{ZFC})$. A worldly $\kappa$ makes $V_\kappa$ a model of ZFC, and [arithmetic absoluteness for a rank-initial model](arithmetic-absoluteness-for-a-rank-initial-model.md) makes it a model of $T^*$, so $T$ proves $\operatorname{Con}(T^*)$. If $T^*$ proved $\operatorname{Con}(T)$, then the stronger theory $T$ would prove its own consistency, contradicting the [Gödel second incompleteness theorem](godel-second-incompleteness-theorem.md). Thus, assuming consistency, $T^*<_{\mathrm{Cons}}T$.
