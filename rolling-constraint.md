# Rolling constraint

↑ **Parent:** [Rolling without slipping](rolling-without-slipping.md)

For a [rigid body](rigid-body-dynamics-split.md) rolling on a stationary support, the instantaneous contact point $P$ has zero [velocity](velocity.md): $\mathbf v_P=\mathbf v_G+\boldsymbol\omega\times\overrightarrow{GP}=0$. For a wheel of radius $R$ moving horizontally and a counterclockwise [angle](angle.md) $\phi$, this gives $\dot x+R\dot\phi=0$, hence $x+R\phi$ is constant. Maintaining this [mechanical constraint](constraint-mechanics.md) generally requires sufficient [static friction](static-friction.md) and a nonnegative [normal force](normal-force.md).
