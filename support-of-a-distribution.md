# Support of a distribution

↑ **Parent:** [Distribution theory](distribution-theory-split.md)

The support of a [distribution](distribution-mathematical-analysis.md) is the complement of the largest [open set](open-set.md) on which its pairing vanishes for every supported [test function](test-function.md). A [partition of unity](partition-of-unity.md) shows that the union of such open sets still has this vanishing property. For example, the [Dirac delta distribution](dirac-delta-function.md) and all its derivatives have support $\{0\}$.
