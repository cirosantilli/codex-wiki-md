# Measure realization of an Iwasawa algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Iwasawa algebra](iwasawa-algebra.md)

A [p-adic measure](p-adic-measure.md) gives the compatible finite-quotient elements $\sum_{gU}\mu(gU)[gU]\in I[G/U]$. The transition maps sum coefficients over fibers. Conversely compatible coefficients define values on cosets, then all clopen sets, and hence integration. Thus the inverse limit [Iwasawa algebra](iwasawa-algebra.md) is the ring of measures, with multiplication given by [convolution of p-adic measures](convolution-of-p-adic-measures.md). Completeness of $I$ suffices even when its residue field is infinite.
