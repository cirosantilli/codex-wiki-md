# Sharpless asymmetric epoxidation

↑ **Parent:** [Epoxidation](epoxidation.md)

Enantioselective [oxygen](oxygen.md) transfer to an allylic [alcohol](alcohol.md) using a [titanium](titanium.md)–tartrate complex and a [hydroperoxide](hydroperoxide.md). With the [alkene](alkene.md) horizontal and its hydroxymethyl substituent at lower right, L-(+)-tartrate directs delivery from the back face and D-(−)-tartrate from the front face. Rotate a structure in its plane to apply the mnemonic; reflecting it changes its [stereochemistry](stereochemistry-split.md).
