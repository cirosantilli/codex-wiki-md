# Graphene monovacancy

↑ **Parent:** [Graphene vacancy defect](graphene-vacancy-defect.md)

A [graphene vacancy defect](graphene-vacancy-defect.md) with one carbon removed. A common reconstruction has a pentagon and nine-membered ring with residual under-coordination. The reconstructed geometry need not retain the symmetry of the unrelaxed missing-atom site.
