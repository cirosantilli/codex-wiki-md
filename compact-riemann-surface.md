# Compact Riemann surface

↑ **Parent:** [Riemann surfaces](riemann-surfaces.md)

A [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md) which is compact as a topological space. For a connected one of genus $g$, the [Riemann-Roch theorem](riemann-roch-theorem.md) gives $g$ independent [holomorphic differential forms](holomorphic-differential-form.md). The [Riemann bilinear relations for a compact surface](riemann-bilinear-relations-for-a-compact-surface.md) make their normalized periods a full lattice in $\mathbb C^g$, and the [Abel-Jacobi map of a compact Riemann surface](abel-jacobi-map-of-a-compact-riemann-surface.md) turns this lattice into a complex torus encoding [divisor classes](divisor-class.md).
