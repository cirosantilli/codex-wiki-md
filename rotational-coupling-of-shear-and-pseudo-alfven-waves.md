<h1 id="rotational-coupling-of-shear-and-pseudo-alfven-waves">Rotational coupling of shear and pseudo-Alfvén waves</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Rotating-frame magnetohydrodynamic momentum equation](rotating-frame-magnetohydrodynamic-momentum-equation.md)

With aligned uniform [magnetic field](magnetic-field-split.md) and rotation, pressure projection couples the two incompressible transverse [wave polarization](polarization-waves.md) states at rate $\omega_I\sim2\Omega|k_\parallel|/k_\perp$. Each nonrotating [Alfvén wave](alfven-wave.md) has rate $\omega_A=|k_\parallel|v_A$. Their ratio is independent of the anisotropy. Leading finite-timescale decoupling therefore needs $k_\perp v_A\gg2\Omega$, with rotational coupling also slow relative to any retained nonlinear evolution. At finite amplitude the usual anisotropic reduction still permits passive [advection](advection.md) of pseudo-Alfvénic fluctuations by the Alfvénic subsystem; removing rotational mixing does not eliminate that one-way interaction. Because the two Alfvén polarizations are degenerate, weak coupling can still mix them over the longer time $\omega_I^{-1}$.
