# Dynamical equation

↑ **Parent:** [Dynamical systems](dynamical-systems-split.md)

An equation specifying how a system changes with [time](time-in-physics.md), such as an [ordinary differential equation](ordinary-differential-equation.md) for [position](position.md) and [velocity](velocity.md) or the [Schrödinger equation](schrodinger-equation.md) for a [wave function](wave-function-split.md). Its solution depends on its specified [initial conditions](initial-condition.md).
