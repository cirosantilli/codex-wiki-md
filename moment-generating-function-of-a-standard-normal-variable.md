# Moment-generating function of a standard normal variable

↑ **Parent:** [Standard normal distribution](standard-normal-distribution.md)

Completing the square in the [standard normal density](standard-normal-density.md) gives $\mathbb E[e^{tZ}]=e^{t^2/2}$. Differentiating at zero yields $\mathbb E[Z]=0$, $\mathbb E[Z^2]=1$, and $\mathbb E[Z^4]=3$.
