# Coset norm of a modular form

↑ **Parent:** [Modular form on a finite-index subgroup](modular-form-on-a-finite-index-subgroup.md)

For a finite-index inclusion of modular groups and a form with trivial multiplier, multiply its [slash operator for modular forms](slash-operator-for-modular-forms.md) translates over left coset representatives. Right multiplication permutes the factors, producing a form of weight $k[\Gamma':\Gamma]$ for the larger group. [modular cusp](cusp-of-a-modular-group.md) [cusp widths](width-of-a-cusp.md) determine the orders contributed by the factors; the product is nonzero if the original form is nonzero.
