# Polynomial growth

↑ **Parent:** [Power law](power-law.md)

A function has polynomial growth when $|f(x)|\leq C(1+|x|)^N$ for some constants $C,N$. Such growth is slow enough for a [locally integrable function](locally-integrable-function.md) to define a [tempered distribution](tempered-distribution.md).
