# Classical mixture of coherent states

↑ **Parent:** [Coherent state](coherent-state.md)

A normalized nonnegative probability measure over [coherent states](coherent-state.md) produces a classical mixture. The measure may contain [Dirac delta distributions](dirac-delta-function.md), so a pure [coherent state](coherent-state.md) is included. Conditioning on $\alpha$ gives [Poisson distribution](poisson-distribution.md) [photon statistics](photon-statistics.md); hence $\operatorname{Var}n=\langle n\rangle+\operatorname{Var}(|\alpha|^2)\ge\langle n\rangle$. Each [optical quadrature](optical-quadrature.md) has variance at least its vacuum value because mixing adds nonnegative variance of conditional means. These inequalities provide simple [nonclassicality witnesses](nonclassicality-witnesses.md).
