# CP rate cancellation in baryogenesis

↑ **Parent:** [Sakharov conditions](sakharov-conditions.md)

In a symmetric initial population of particles and antiparticles, exact [CP symmetry](cp-symmetry.md) equates conjugate decay rates after summing final momenta and helicities. Since [parity](parity.md) does not reverse baryon number, this equality prevents a total baryon asymmetry even if [charge conjugation](charge-conjugation.md) alone is violated. Different same-helicity rates can cancel against the opposite-helicity rates.
