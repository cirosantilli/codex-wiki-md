# Dense permanent approximation

↑ **Parent:** [Permanent (mathematics)](permanent-mathematics.md)

If every row and column of an $n$-by-$n$ zero-one [matrix](matrix.md) has more than $n/2$ ones, its [permanent of a matrix](permanent-mathematics.md) has a [fully polynomial randomized approximation scheme](fully-polynomial-randomized-approximation-scheme.md). The [short augmenting paths in a dense bipartite graph](short-augmenting-paths-in-a-dense-bipartite-graph.md) bound makes the leading coefficient dominate the [matching generating function](matching-generating-function.md) at polynomially large activity. The [weighted matching Markov chain](weighted-matching-markov-chain.md) samples polynomially bounded activities in polynomial [mixing time](mixing-time-of-a-markov-chain.md). Estimate successive [annealing ratios for a matching generating function](annealing-ratios-for-a-matching-generating-function.md) and divide by the final activity to the power $n$.
