# Premelting

↑ **Parent:** [Phase boundary](phase-boundary.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premelting)

Premelting is the persistence of a liquid layer at a surface or interface below the bulk [melting](melting.md) [temperature](temperature.md). Molecular [disjoining pressure](disjoining-pressure.md) and phase equilibrium can stabilize the film. Its hydraulic connection to a reservoir permits [frost heave](frost-heave.md) when water freezes onto the base of loaded [ice](ice.md).
