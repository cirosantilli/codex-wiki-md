# Selectable marker

↑ **Parent:** [Genetic marker](genetic-marker.md)

A [selectable marker](selectable-marker.md) is a [gene](gene.md) or other inherited feature that permits recovery of carriers under specified growth conditions. Selection for a marker does not identify every other [nucleotide sequence](nucleic-acid-sequence.md) in its carrier.
