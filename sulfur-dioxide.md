# Sulfur dioxide

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

The [molecule](molecule-split.md) $\mathrm{SO_2}$ is emitted by volcanism and combustion and can be oxidized into [sulfuric acid](sulfuric-acid.md)/[sulfate](sulfate.md). Direct gas deposition competes with gas-phase and aqueous [oxidation](oxidation.md). The precursor-gas lifetime and the resulting particle lifetime are different quantities.
