# Triangle group

↑ **Parent:** [Group](group-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangle_group)

A [triangle group](triangle-group.md) is generated by [reflections](reflection-mathematics.md) in the sides of a triangular [fundamental domain](fundamental-domain.md) in [spherical geometry](spherical-geometry.md), [Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry.md) or [hyperbolic geometry](hyperbolic-geometry.md). The angles $\pi/p,\pi/q,\pi/r$ determine the geometry through the sign of $1/p+1/q+1/r-1$. Its orientation-preserving [subgroup](subgroup.md) has [group presentation](group-presentation.md) $\langle x,y,z\mid x^p=y^q=z^r=xyz=1\rangle$.
