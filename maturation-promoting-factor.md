# Maturation-promoting factor

↑ **Parent:** [Cell cycle](cell-cycle-split.md)

[Maturation-promoting factor](maturation-promoting-factor.md) is the cyclin–CDK activity that can drive meiotic maturation and mitotic events. A mitotic cyclin–CDK1 complex connects changing cyclin availability to phosphorylation-dependent cell-cycle transitions.
