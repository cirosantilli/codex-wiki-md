# Isocyanate

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

An [isocyanate](isocyanate.md) bears an $\mathrm{R{-}N=C=O}$ group. A [molecule](molecule.md) bearing two such groups can react with a [diol](diol.md) to form [polyurethane](polyurethane.md) connections.
