# Black-hole energy extraction

↑ **Parent:** [Black-hole thermodynamics](black-hole-thermodynamics.md)

Black-hole energy extraction transfers part of a black hole's mass, charge, or rotational energy to its exterior. A reversible limiting process preserves the horizon area and saturates the maximum extraction permitted by the [First law of black-hole mechanics](first-law-of-black-hole-mechanics.md) and [Hawking's area theorem](hawking-s-area-theorem.md).
