# Law of one price

↑ **Parent:** [Arbitrage](arbitrage.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_one_price)

The [law of one price](law-of-one-price.md) requires traded assets or portfolios with the same terminal payoff to have the same earlier price. In a model where every discounted traded price is a true [martingale](martingale-split.md) under a common equivalent measure, it follows by taking the [conditional expectation](conditional-expectation.md) of that terminal payoff. With only [local martingale](local-martingale.md) prices and a lower-bound restriction on [admissible trading strategies](admissible-trading-strategy.md), it can fail: the seemingly profitable short position may have unbounded possible losses before maturity.
