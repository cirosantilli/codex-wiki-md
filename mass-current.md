# Mass current

↑ **Parent:** [Density](density.md)

The flux of [mass](mass.md) carried by a continuum moving with [velocity](velocity.md) $\mathbf v$. In the nonrelativistic limit, $\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot\mathbf j_m=0$ is local [mass conservation](mass-conservation.md). A stationary mass current can produce a stationary gravitomagnetic field without emitting [gravitational radiation](gravitational-wave-split.md).
