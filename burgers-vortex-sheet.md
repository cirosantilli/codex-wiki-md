# Burgers vortex sheet

↑ **Parent:** [Burgers vortex](burgers-vortex.md)

A planar strained [shear layer](shear-layer.md) is a [Burgers vortex sheet](burgers-vortex-sheet.md). For strain $u_x=-\alpha x$, $u_z=\alpha z$ and transverse velocity $u_y=U\operatorname{erf}(x/\delta_s)$, viscous balance gives $\delta_s^2=2\nu/\alpha$. At fixed velocity jump its excess [viscous dissipation](viscous-dissipation.md) per sheet area scales as $U^2\sqrt{\nu\alpha}$, which vanishes as [kinematic viscosity](kinematic-viscosity.md) tends to zero.
