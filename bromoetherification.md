# Bromoetherification

↑ **Parent:** [Organic reaction](organic-reaction.md)

Addition of bromine and an [oxygen](oxygen.md) [nucleophile](nucleophile.md) across an [alkene](alkene.md) to form a bromoether. Intramolecular attack on a [bromonium ion](bromonium-ion.md) can close a ring; tether length, charge distribution and anti opening determine the resulting connectivity.
