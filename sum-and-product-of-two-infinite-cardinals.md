# Sum and product of two infinite cardinals

↑ **Parent:** [Infinite cardinal arithmetic](infinite-cardinal-arithmetic.md)

For infinite cardinals $\kappa$ and $\lambda$, the [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md) and the [square of an infinite cardinal](square-of-an-infinite-cardinal.md) give

$$
\kappa+\lambda=\kappa\lambda=\max\{\kappa,\lambda\}.
$$

The maximum is a lower bound for both operations, while both are bounded above by respectively $\mu+\mu$ and $\mu^2$, where $\mu=\max\{\kappa,\lambda\}$.
