# Covering-law model

↑ **Parent:** [Scientific explanation](scientific-explanation.md)

An approach to [scientific explanation](scientific-explanation.md) that subsumes a phenomenon under general laws and specified circumstances. Its deductive form is the [deductive-nomological model](deductive-nomological-model.md); statistical forms permit probabilistic support rather than entailment.
