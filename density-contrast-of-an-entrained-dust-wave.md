# Density contrast of an entrained dust wave

↑ **Parent:** [Resonant dust entrainment by an epicyclic gas wave](resonant-dust-entrainment-by-an-epicyclic-gas-wave.md)

Let $\Sigma_{d0}$ be the uniform background dust [surface density of a disk](surface-density-of-a-disk.md) and $\delta_d=(\Sigma_d-\Sigma_{d0})/\Sigma_{d0}$ its fractional perturbation. Here $k$ is the radial [wavenumber](wavenumber.md), $\Omega$ is the epicyclic frequency and $u$ is a real velocity amplitude. For that uniform background and late velocity $v_x=u\cos(kx-\Omega t)$, linear dust [continuity equation](continuity-equation.md) gives $\partial_t\delta_d=-\partial_xv_x=ku\sin(kx-\Omega t)$. Integrating yields the displayed formula. The coherent travelling-wave fractional excess is $|ku|/\Omega$, with validity requiring this number much smaller than one. The static term $C(x)$ records initial density and transient displacement: drag damps velocity, but supplies no density diffusion. The long-wavelength gas wave has the same fractional density amplitude, so entrainment alone does not produce an additional dust-to-gas ratio enhancement at this order.
