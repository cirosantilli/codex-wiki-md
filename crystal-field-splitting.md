# Crystal field splitting

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal field theory](crystal-field-theory.md)

Separation of [metal](metal.md) d-orbital energies by the electrostatic field of surrounding [coordination ligands](ligand-in-coordination-chemistry.md). An octahedral field puts the three $t_{2g}$ orbitals below the two $e_g$ orbitals.
