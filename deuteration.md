# Deuteration

↑ **Parent:** [Isotope labelling](isotope-labelling.md)

Replacement of hydrogen with deuterium. In [protein](protein-split.md) [NMR](nuclear-magnetic-resonance-split.md), suitable [deuteration](deuteration.md) reduces proton dipolar relaxation but also removes directly observable proton signals unless selected sites are retained or reintroduced.
