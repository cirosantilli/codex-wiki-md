# Standardized mean difference

↑ **Parent:** [Covariate balance](covariate-balance.md)

A standardized mean difference divides a group mean difference by a pooled standard deviation.

This gives a dimensionless [effect size](effect-size.md), so mean differences can be compared across measurement scales.
