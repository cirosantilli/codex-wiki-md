# Adenosine monophosphate

↑ **Parent:** [Nucleotide](nucleotide.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adenosine_monophosphate)

[AMP](adenosine-monophosphate.md) is [adenosine](adenosine.md) carrying one [phosphate](phosphate.md) group at the [ribose](ribose.md)'s $5'$ position. The count distinguishes [AMP](adenosine-monophosphate.md) from [ADP](adenosine-diphosphate.md) with two and [ATP](adenosine-triphosphate.md) with three; [adenosine](adenosine.md) itself has none. See [Cooper, The Cell: ATP, ADP, and AMP](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK9903/).
