# Lubrication resistance

↑ **Parent:** [Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md)

[Lubrication theory](lubrication-theory.md) predicts large viscous resistance when fluid must be squeezed through a narrow gap. For normal approach of smooth curved surfaces with curvature scale $a$ and minimum separation $h\ll a$, the resisting [force](force.md) scales as $\mu a^2U/h$. Under a bounded normal [force](force.md) this gives $|\dot h|=O(h)$, so ideal smooth surfaces do not make contact in finite time. Tangential and rolling motions have different resistance laws; the normal squeeze law should not be applied to them.
