# Euler characteristic from coset cycle counts

↑ **Parent:** [Orbifold Euler characteristic](orbifold-euler-characteristic.md)

An $n$-sheeted surface cover of an orbifold of underlying [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md) $g_0$ with $r$ cone points has the displayed ordinary [Euler characteristic](euler-characteristic.md), where $c_i$ is the number of cycles of the $i$th cone [monodromy permutation](monodromy-permutation.md). The ramification deficit over that point is $n-c_i$, so the formula follows by counting lifted vertices, edges and faces, or by the [Riemann-Hurwitz formula](riemann-hurwitz-formula.md). If every cone cycle has its full prescribed length, this equals $n\chi_{\mathrm{orb}}$.
