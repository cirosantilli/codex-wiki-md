# Negative-energy minimum-separation upper bound

↑ **Parent:** [Minimum-separation binding-energy bounds](minimum-separation-binding-energy-bounds.md)

With $E=T-U<0$ and $T\geq0$, the positive binding [energy](energy.md) satisfies $U\geq|E|$. The [minimum-separation binding-energy bounds](minimum-separation-binding-energy-bounds.md) imply $r\leq A_0/|E|$. At least one pair stays close; this does not bound every particle's distance or exclude a [negative-energy gravitational collision](negative-energy-gravitational-collision.md).
