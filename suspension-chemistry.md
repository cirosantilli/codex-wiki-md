# Suspension (chemistry)

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suspension_(chemistry))

A particle suspension is a dispersed population of solid particles carried by a fluid. Its dynamics couple particle transport, [drag force](drag-physics.md), gravity, and the compensating motion of the carrier fluid.
