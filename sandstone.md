# Sandstone

↑ **Parent:** [Sedimentary rock](sedimentary-rock.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandstone)

A [sedimentary rock](sedimentary-rock.md) composed predominantly of sand-sized grains. Cementation, [porosity](porosity.md), [pore fluid](pore-fluid.md) and [effective stress](effective-stress.md) strongly affect its [seismic velocity](seismic-velocity.md).
