# Factorial contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Factorial design](factorial-design.md)

In a two-level [factorial design](factorial-design.md), a factor subset defines a sign column. The coefficient of this column is its response [contrast](contrast-statistics.md) divided by the number of runs when the columns are [orthogonal](orthogonal-vectors.md). A singleton describes a [main effect](main-effect.md) and a pair describes a [two-factor interaction](two-factor-interaction.md). On the full cube, distinct columns have zero [inner product](inner-product.md): summing over any coordinate in their [symmetric difference](symmetric-difference.md) cancels the terms.
