# Stochastic domination of probability measures

↑ **Parent:** [Coupling](coupling.md)

On a finite partially ordered state space, $\mu\preceq\nu$ means $\int f\,d\mu\leq\int f\,d\nu$ for every real-valued [order-preserving function](order-preserving-function.md) $f$. Equivalently, every increasing subset has no larger [probability](probability.md) under $\mu$ than under $\nu$. A coupling supported on pairs $(X,Y)$ with $X\leq Y$ proves this relation. This concerns order of [probability](probability.md) laws, rather than stochastic asymptotic order notation.
