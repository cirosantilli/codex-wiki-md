# Linear inviscid damping in unbounded Couette flow

↑ **Parent:** [Inviscid Couette continuous spectrum](inviscid-couette-continuous-spectrum.md)

In unbounded [Couette flow](couette-flow.md) with velocity $(y,0)$, linear [vorticity](vorticity.md) is transported exactly as $\omega(x,y,t)=\omega_0(x-yt,y)$. For a nonzero streamwise [Fourier mode](fourier-mode.md) $\alpha$ and sufficiently localized smooth initial data, inversion by the [one-dimensional modified Helmholtz Green function](one-dimensional-modified-helmholtz-green-function.md) gives the displayed pointwise decay of the [streamfunction](stream-function.md) and [velocity](velocity.md), although [vorticity](vorticity.md) does not decay in magnitude. The mechanism is phase mixing: shearing produces increasingly rapid transverse oscillations, whose elliptic velocity reconstruction suppresses them. Smoothness and decay of the initial [streamfunction](stream-function.md) without derivative control do not alone imply these algebraic rates.
