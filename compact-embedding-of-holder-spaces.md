<h1 id="compact-embedding-of-holder-spaces">Compact embedding of Hölder spaces</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Hölder space](holder-space.md)

On a bounded regular domain, a bounded subset of $C^{k,\alpha}$ is relatively compact in $C^{l,\beta}$ whenever $k+\alpha>l+\beta$. This is an [Arzelà-Ascoli theorem](arzela-ascoli-theorem.md) argument applied successively to the derivatives.
