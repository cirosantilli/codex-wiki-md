# Positive trace index for a compact simple Lie algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Trace form of a Lie algebra representation](trace-form-of-a-lie-algebra-representation.md)

For a nontrivial finite-dimensional [anti-Hermitian](skew-hermitian-matrix.md) [Lie algebra representation](lie-algebra-representation.md) of a [compact Lie algebra](compact-lie-algebra.md) that is simple, its [Trace form of a Lie algebra representation](trace-form-of-a-lie-algebra-representation.md) is a positive multiple of the [Killing form](killing-form.md). Define $T$ by $H(X,Y)=\kappa(TX,Y)$. Invariance makes $T$ commute with the [Adjoint representation of a Lie algebra](adjoint-representation-of-a-lie-algebra.md). Symmetry makes $T$ self-adjoint for $-\kappa$, so its [eigenspaces](eigenspace.md) are ideals. Simplicity implies $T=\mu I$. The kernel of the representation is an ideal and hence vanishes; therefore $H(X,X)=-\operatorname{Tr}(d(X)^\dagger d(X))<0$ for nonzero $X$, proving $\mu>0$.
