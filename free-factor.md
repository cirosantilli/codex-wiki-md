# Free factor

↑ **Parent:** [Free product](free-product.md)

A subgroup $A$ is a free factor of $G$ if $G$ is isomorphic to a [free product](free-product.md) $A*B$ by an isomorphism restricting to the inclusion of $A$, for some subgroup $B$. The [normal form theorem for a free product](normal-form-theorem-for-a-free-product.md) ensures that both factors embed. A free factor need not be a [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md).
