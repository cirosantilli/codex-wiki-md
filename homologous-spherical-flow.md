# Homologous spherical flow

↑ **Parent:** [Fluid mechanics](fluid-mechanics-split.md)

A homologous spherical flow has velocity proportional to radius. Its [velocity gradient](velocity-gradient.md) is $q(t)I$ and its [velocity divergence](velocity-divergence.md) is $3q(t)$.
