# Russian roulette (Monte Carlo)

↑ **Parent:** [Monte Carlo method](monte-carlo-method-split.md)

Unbiased random termination of a sampled contribution: retain it with probability $q>0$ and multiply surviving contributions by $1/q$. In [path tracing](path-tracing.md) this controls path length without the systematic truncation of a fixed bounce limit, provided the continuation probabilities and weights are handled consistently.
