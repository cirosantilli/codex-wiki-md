# Triangle replacement for self-avoiding walks

↑ **Parent:** [Even-length walk generating function on a bipartite graph](even-length-walk-generating-function-on-a-bipartite-graph.md)

Replace every degree-three [vertex of a graph](vertex-graph-theory.md) in one class of a [bipartite graph](bipartite-graph.md) by a triangle with one port for each incident [edge](edge-of-a-graph.md). A [self-avoiding walk](self-avoiding-walk.md) whose endpoints remain in the other class cannot make two completed passages through the same triangle: each passage needs two unused ports and only three exist. Each old two-edge passage has exactly two replacements, of lengths three and four. Therefore the restricted [generating functions](generating-function.md) satisfy $Z_H^0(x)=Z_G^0(\sqrt{x^3+x^4})$. If the old radius is $1/\mu$, the new radius obeys $\rho^3+\rho^4=\mu^{-2}$.
