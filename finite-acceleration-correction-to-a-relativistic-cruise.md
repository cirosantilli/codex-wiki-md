# Finite-acceleration correction to a relativistic cruise

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic motion](hyperbolic-motion.md)

Replace instantaneous acceleration and braking by legs of constant proper-acceleration magnitude $a$, retaining the same cruise [rapidity](rapidity.md) $\eta$ and fixed endpoint separation. Each leg takes [proper time](proper-time.md) $c\eta/a$ and displaces $(c^2/a)(\cosh\eta-1)$ in the endpoint [rest frame](rest-frame.md). Subtracting the proper cruise time displaced by both legs gives the displayed correction. A cruise segment exists only if the total endpoint separation exceeds twice this acceleration distance.
