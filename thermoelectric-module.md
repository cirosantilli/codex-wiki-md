# Thermoelectric module

↑ **Parent:** [Thermoelectric effect](thermoelectric-effect-split.md)

A [thermoelectric module](thermoelectric-module.md) connects thermoelectric material pairs between thermal faces. Electrical series connections and thermal parallel connections combine their transport responses for [thermoelectric cooling](thermoelectric-cooling.md) or electrical generation.
