# Superstring theory

↑ **Parent:** [String theory](string-theory-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superstring_theory)

String theories with worldsheet fermionic degrees of freedom and the corresponding supersymmetric consistency conditions. In the [RNS string](spinning-string.md) description, consistent supersymmetric [GSO projections](gso-projection.md) produce spacetime [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) and remove the NS tachyon. The flat critical dimension is ten. [Worldsheet supersymmetry](worldsheet-supersymmetry.md) alone does not guarantee spacetime [supersymmetry](supersymmetry-split.md) without a suitable projection.
