# Transmission electron microscopy contrast

↑ **Parent:** [Transmission electron microscopy](transmission-electron-microscopy.md)

[Transmission electron microscopy contrast](transmission-electron-microscopy-contrast.md) is a difference in recorded signal between specimen regions under specified illumination, angular acceptance and detector settings. For a positive reference signal $S_{\mathrm{ref}}$, one possible signed contrast convention is

$$
C=\frac{S-S_{\mathrm{ref}}}{S_{\mathrm{ref}}}.
$$

Negative $C$ means darker than the reference; it does not specify a surface height or [chemical composition](chemical-composition.md). The definition must accompany a numerical contrast value, since other conventions exist. A conspicuous feature need not establish fine [spatial resolution](spatial-resolution.md): detecting a small signal difference and distinguishing two nearby features are different measurement questions.
