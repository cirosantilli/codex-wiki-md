# Clock-to-Q delay

↑ **Parent:** [Flip-flop](flip-flop.md)

The time from a triggering [clock edge](clock-edge.md) to the corresponding settled output of a [flip-flop](flip-flop.md), under specified loading. The time available between successive active [clock edges](clock-edge.md) must accommodate this delay, the [combinational critical path](combinational-critical-path.md), the receiving [flip-flop](flip-flop.md)'s [setup time](setup-time.md), and any clock uncertainty.
