# Breit-Rabi formula

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperfine interaction](hyperfine-interaction.md)

For electronic $J=1/2$, nuclear [spin](spin.md) $I$ and conserved [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) component $m$, define $\Delta_{\rm hf}=A(I+1/2)$ and the dimensionless field $x$ shown in the title. The hyperfine-plus-magnetic energies are

$$
E_\pm(m)=-\frac{\Delta_{\rm hf}}{2(2I+1)}+g_I\mu_BmB
\pm\frac{\Delta_{\rm hf}}2\sqrt{1+\frac{4mx}{2I+1}+x^2}.
$$

The factors $g_I,g_J$ use the same [Hamiltonian](hamiltonian.md) convention and Bohr-magneton units. The two stretched states require the branch consistent with their unique spin configuration. The formula follows by diagonalizing each two-dimensional fixed-$m$ block; it connects weak-field coupled-spin levels to decoupled high-field states. The name refers to [Gregory Breit](gregory-breit.md) and [Isidor Isaac Rabi](isidor-isaac-rabi.md).[equations (26)–(28) and reference 37](rubidium-87-d-line-data-by-daniel-a-steck.md)
