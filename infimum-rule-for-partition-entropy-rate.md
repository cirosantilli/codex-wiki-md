# Infimum rule for partition entropy rate

↑ **Parent:** [Entropy rate of a measurable partition](entropy-rate-of-a-measurable-partition.md)

For a measure-preserving action of $\mathbb Z$ and a finite partition $\xi$,

$$
h_\mu(T,\xi)=\inf_{\varnothing\ne F\subseteq\mathbb Z_{\geq0}\text{ finite}}
\frac1{|F|}H_\mu\left(\bigvee_{n\in F}T^{-n}\xi\right).
$$

The reverse inequality to the interval formula follows from [Shearer's inequality](shearer-s-inequality.md) applied to translates of $F$ along long intervals; the uncovered boundary has sublinear size.
