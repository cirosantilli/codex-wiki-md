# Laser spiking

↑ **Parent:** [Laser relaxation oscillation](laser-relaxation-oscillation.md)

Large startup excursions of a [laser](laser-split.md) can accumulate population above threshold and then release it through a short stimulated-emission pulse. Inversion depletion and pump recovery can produce a damped sequence of spikes. This is a nonlinear transient, distinguished from small-signal [laser relaxation oscillations](laser-relaxation-oscillation.md) and from externally imposed [Q-switching](q-switching.md).
