# Eye tracking

↑ **Parent:** [Psychophysics](psychophysics.md)

Measurement of gaze direction or fixations using a specified recording method and calibration procedure. Prespecified regions of interest, usable-data criteria and time windows are needed to connect gaze records to a developmental [hypothesis](hypothesis.md).
