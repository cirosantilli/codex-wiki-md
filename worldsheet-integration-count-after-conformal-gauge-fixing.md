# Worldsheet integration count after conformal gauge fixing

↑ **Parent:** [Closed-string scattering amplitude](closed-string-scattering-amplitude.md)

For a stable $n$-point [closed-string scattering amplitude](closed-string-scattering-amplitude.md) on a [genus](genus-of-a-surface.md)-$g$ surface, let $d_g$ count complex unmarked [worldsheet moduli](worldsheet-moduli.md) and $k_g$ count complex dimensions of the [worldsheet conformal Killing group](worldsheet-conformal-killing-group.md). Integrating the $n$ vertex positions and moduli, then fixing $k_g$ positions, leaves $d_g+n-k_g$ complex integrations. The [punctured Riemann surface moduli dimension](punctured-riemann-surface-moduli-dimension.md) identifies this as $3g-3+n$. [Faddeev-Popov ghost fields](faddeev-popov-ghost.md) and antighost insertions implement the removed zero modes and the surviving modulus measure; they do not add independently integrated worldsheet coordinates.
