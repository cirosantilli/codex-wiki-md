# Gaussian generating functional for a Dirac field

↑ **Parent:** [Grassmann Gaussian integral](grassmann-gaussian-integral.md)

For a free [Dirac field](dirac-field.md) with action $\int\bar\psi K\psi$ and independent odd sources coupled as $\bar\psi J+\bar J\psi$, translation invariance of the [Berezin integral](berezin-integral.md) and completion of the square give $Z[J,\bar J]=Z[0,0]\exp(-i\bar J K_F^{-1}J)$. Here $K_F^{-1}$ has [Feynman i-epsilon prescription](feynman-i-epsilon-prescription.md) boundary conditions, and each bilinear includes its spacetime integrations. A left [Grassmann derivative](grassmann-derivative.md) with respect to $\bar J$, followed by a right [Grassmann derivative](grassmann-derivative.md) with respect to $J$, has normalized value $-iK_F^{-1}$ at zero sources. Multiplying by $-1$ removes the two insertion factors $i^2$ and yields the [Dirac propagator](dirac-propagator.md) $S_F=iK_F^{-1}$. Keeping source order fixed prevents a spurious [fermionic sign](fermionic-sign.md).
