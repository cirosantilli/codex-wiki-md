# Frequency estimation

↑ **Parent:** [Statistical inference](statistical-inference-split.md)

[Frequency estimation](frequency-estimation.md) infers a periodic signal's [frequency](frequency.md) from observed samples. It may use a peak of the [discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform-split.md), or fit a sinusoidal model with amplitude, [phase](phase-waves.md), and [frequency](frequency.md) as unknown parameters. Its attainable precision depends on noise, observation duration, [sampling rate](sampling-rate.md), and the estimator. [Fourier frequency resolution](fourier-frequency-resolution.md) alone does not specify that precision.
