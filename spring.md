# Spring

↑ **Parent:** [Hooke's law](hooke-s-law.md)

A linear [spring](spring.md) stores [elastic energy](elastic-energy.md) $kx^2/2$ when displaced by $x$ from its relaxed position. The external [force](force-split.md) maintaining that [displacement](displacement.md) is $kx$; its [restoring force](restoring-force.md) on the moving attachment has the opposite sign. Its [spring constant](spring-constant.md) $k$ has units [force](force-split.md) per length.
