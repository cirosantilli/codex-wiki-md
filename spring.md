# Spring

↑ **Parent:** [Hooke's law](hooke-s-law.md)

A linear [spring](spring.md) stores elastic energy $kx^2/2$ when displaced by $x$ from its relaxed position. The external force maintaining that displacement is $kx$; its restoring force on the moving attachment has the opposite sign. Its stiffness $k$ has units force per length.
