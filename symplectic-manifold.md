# Symplectic manifold

↑ **Parent:** [Symplectic geometry](symplectic-geometry-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_manifold)

A symplectic manifold is an even-dimensional [smooth manifold](smooth-manifold.md) $M$ equipped with a closed [nondegenerate](nondegenerate-bilinear-form.md) differential two-form $\omega$.
