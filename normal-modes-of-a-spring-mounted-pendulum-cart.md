# Normal modes of a spring-mounted pendulum cart

↑ **Parent:** [Small oscillation](small-oscillation.md)

A cart of [mass](mass.md) $m$ moves horizontally on a [spring](spring.md) of stiffness $k$, carrying a [simple pendulum](simple-pendulum-split.md) of bob [mass](mass.md) $M$ and length $L$. Let $x$ be cart displacement and $\phi$ the small angle from downward vertical. The quadratic [Lagrangian](lagrangian.md) is

$$
\mathcal L=\frac12(m+M)\dot x^2+ML\dot x\dot\phi+\frac12ML^2\dot\phi^2-\frac12kx^2-\frac12MgL\phi^2.
$$

The kinetic cross term comes from the bob's horizontal [velocity](velocity.md) $\dot x+L\dot\phi$ to first order. The [generalized eigenvalue problem](generalized-eigenvalue-problem.md) has mass matrix $\begin{pmatrix}m+M&ML\\ML&ML^2\end{pmatrix}$ and restoring matrix $\operatorname{diag}(k,MgL)$. Their characteristic [determinant](determinant.md) gives the displayed polynomial in $\omega^2$. For $m=M=L=g=1,k=2$, the squared [angular frequencies](angular-frequency.md) are $2\mp\sqrt2$, with [mode shapes](mode-shape.md) $(x,\phi)=(1,\pm\sqrt2)$. The bob and cart move in phase in the lower [normal mode](normal-mode.md) and in opposite phases in the higher [normal mode](normal-mode.md).
