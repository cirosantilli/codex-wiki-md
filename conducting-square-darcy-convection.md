# Conducting-square Darcy convection

↑ **Parent:** [Darcy-Bénard convection](darcy-benard-convection.md)

In a unit square with prescribed conductive boundary [temperatures](temperature.md) and impermeable boundaries, the [stream function](stream-function.md) convention $\mathbf u=(-\psi_z,0,\psi_x)$ gives linear equations $\Delta\psi=R\theta_x$, $\theta_t=\psi_x+\Delta\theta$, with $\psi=\theta=0$ on all sides. The [velocity](velocity.md) equation follows by taking the vertical-plane [curl](curl.md) of [Darcy law](darcy-law.md), and the [temperature](temperature.md) equation by linearizing the [heat equation](heat-equation.md) about $1-z$. Unlike a laterally unbounded Darcy layer, the conducting side boundaries produce a two-dimensional real marginal [eigenspace](eigenspace.md) at $R_c=8\pi^2$.
