# Collisional excitation

↑ **Parent:** [Atomic physics](atomic-physics-split.md)

A collision can transfer kinetic energy to an atom or ion and excite a bound [electron](electron.md). Subsequent radiative de-excitation removes that energy from an optically thin gas. The thermal excitation rate is strongly reduced when the transition energy exceeds $k_BT$, giving the threshold behavior of [atomic line cooling](atomic-line-cooling.md).
