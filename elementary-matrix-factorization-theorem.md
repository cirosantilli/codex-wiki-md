# Elementary-matrix factorization theorem

↑ **Parent:** [Elementary matrix](elementary-matrix.md)

Every invertible square [matrix](matrix.md) is a product of [elementary matrices](elementary-matrix.md). [Gaussian elimination](gaussian-elimination.md) reduces $A$ to the [identity matrix](identity-matrix.md) by elementary row operations, giving $F_r\cdots F_1A=I$. Reverse their order and invert them to write $A=F_1^{-1}\cdots F_r^{-1}$. Each inverse is again elementary. Conversely every elementary matrix is invertible, so every such product is invertible. The identity may be represented by the empty product.
