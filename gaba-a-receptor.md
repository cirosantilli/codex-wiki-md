# GABA-A receptor

↑ **Parent:** [Ionotropic receptor](ionotropic-receptor.md)

A [GABA-A receptor](gaba-a-receptor.md) is an ionotropic GABA receptor whose pore primarily supports anion conductance. Its effect on membrane voltage depends on the relevant ion gradients as well as opening probability.
