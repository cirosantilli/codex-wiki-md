# Massless semileptonic pseudoscalar decay rate

↑ **Parent:** [Decay width](decay-width.md)

For a [pseudoscalar meson](pseudoscalar-meson.md) of mass $M$ decaying into a [pseudoscalar meson](pseudoscalar-meson.md) of mass $m$ and two massless leptons through a [weak charged current](charged-current.md), the [transverse massless leptonic current](transverse-massless-leptonic-current.md) selects $f_+(s)$. With hadronic current normalized as $(p+k)^\mu f_+(s)+(p-k)^\mu f_-(s)$ and flavour coefficient $V$,

$$
\frac{d\Gamma}{ds}=\frac{G_F^2|V|^2}{192\pi^3M^3}\lambda(s,M^2,m^2)^{3/2}|f_+(s)|^2,\qquad 0\leq s\leq(M-m)^2.
$$

The [Källén function](kallen-function.md) has mass dimension four, and the coefficient has mass dimension $-7$. This [decay rate](decay-width.md) assumes the stated form-factor normalization and no additional isospin factor. It follows by the [integrated massless leptonic tensor](integrated-massless-leptonic-tensor.md) and the pion momentum change of variable in the parent's [centre-of-momentum frame](center-of-momentum-frame.md).
