# Hydraulic jump head loss

↑ **Parent:** [Hydraulic jump](hydraulic-jump.md)

In a stationary rectangular-channel [hydraulic jump](hydraulic-jump.md), orient the discharge $q=hu>0$ from side 1 to side 2. [Momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) gives $q^2=gh_1h_2(h_1+h_2)/2$ for unequal depths. The specific mechanical [energy](energy-split.md) is $B=u^2/2+gh$, so

$$
B_1-B_2=\frac{g(h_2-h_1)^3}{4h_1h_2}.
$$

Dividing by $g$ gives the displayed loss of head, measured as a length. The mechanical [energy flux](energy-flux.md) lost is $\rho q(B_1-B_2)$, so a dissipative jump must have $h_2>h_1$. With $R=h_2/h_1>1$, the squared [Froude numbers](froude-number.md) are $R(1+R)/2>1$ upstream and $(1+R)/(2R^2)<1$ downstream. Thus the [shallow water equations](shallow-water-equations.md) connect [supercritical flow](supercritical-flow.md) to [subcritical flow](subcritical-flow.md) through the admissible jump.
