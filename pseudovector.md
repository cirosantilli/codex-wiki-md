# Pseudovector

↑ **Parent:** [Vector](vector.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudovector)

An axial vector transforms as $\mathbf a'=(\det Q)Q\mathbf a$ under an [orthogonal matrix](orthogonal-matrix.md) $Q$. It agrees with a [polar vector](polar-vector.md) under a proper [rotation matrix](rotation-matrix.md) and has the additional determinant sign under an orientation-reversing transformation. [Angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) and [angular velocity](angular-velocity.md) are axial [vectors](vector.md).
