# Secular approximation for scalar-coupled spins

↑ **Parent:** [Weak coupling condition for two NMR spins](weak-coupling-condition-for-two-nmr-spins.md)

The leading-order averaging of rapidly oscillating exchange terms in the [interaction picture](interaction-picture.md) of two nuclear [Zeeman energy](zeeman-energy.md) terms. For $\Delta=\omega_1-\omega_2$,

$$
V_{\mathrm{int}}(t)=J\left(e^{i\Delta t}|01\rangle\langle10|+e^{-i\Delta t}|10\rangle\langle01|\right).
$$

Their first-order time integrals are bounded by $2|J/\Delta|$, so they contribute little when $|\Delta|\gg|J|$, while the stationary [Ising coupling](ising-coupling-of-two-qubits.md) $JZZ/2$ survives. Higher orders give frequency shifts of order $J^2/\Delta$. This approximation is controlled by the difference of resonance frequencies, not their common magnitude.
