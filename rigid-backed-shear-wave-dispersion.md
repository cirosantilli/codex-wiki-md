# Rigid-backed shear-wave dispersion

↑ **Parent:** [Guided shear-horizontal mode](guided-shear-horizontal-mode.md)

A layer with [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) $\mu_1$ and [wave speed](wave-speed.md) $c_1$ lies between a rigid support and a [half-space](half-space.md) with [shear modulus](shear-modulus.md) $\mu_2$ and [wave speed](wave-speed.md) $c_2>c_1$. A trapped [antiplane shear](antiplane-shear.md) [normal mode](normal-mode.md) has layer amplitude $A\sin(my)$ and exterior amplitude $A\sin(mh)e^{-\ell(y-h)}$, where $m=k\sqrt{c^2/c_1^2-1}$ and $\ell=k\sqrt{1-c^2/c_2^2}$. Continuity of [displacement field](displacement-field-mechanics.md) and [traction](traction.md) gives the displayed [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md). It requires $c_1<c<c_2$ and differs from a [Love wave](love-wave.md) with a free boundary.
