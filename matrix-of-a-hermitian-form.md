# Matrix of a Hermitian form

↑ **Parent:** [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md)

For a basis $(v_i)$, the matrix $A$ of a [Hermitian form](hermitian-form.md) has entries $A_{ij}=H(v_i,v_j)$. With the convention that $H$ is linear in its first argument, coordinate columns $x,y$ satisfy $H(x,y)=x^TA\overline y$.
