# Floating-point rounding

↑ **Parent:** [Floating-point arithmetic](floating-point-arithmetic-split.md)

Replacement of an exact real arithmetic result by a representable [floating-point number](floating-point-number.md) under a specified rounding rule. Repeated products can depend on evaluation order, so a recursive rewrite intended to reproduce a floating-point loop should preserve its sequence of accumulator operations. An exact algebraic invariant does not by itself establish equality of rounded machine results.
