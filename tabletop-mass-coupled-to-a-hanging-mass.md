# Tabletop mass coupled to a hanging mass

↑ **Parent:** [Lagrangian mechanics](lagrangian-mechanics-split.md)

A tabletop [mass](mass.md) $m$ joined through a hole by a [massless inextensible string](massless-inextensible-string.md) of length $l$ to a hanging [mass](mass.md) $M$ has radial inertia $M+m$ and conserved [angular momentum](angular-momentum.md) $J=mr^2\dot\theta$. The radial [energy](energy-split.md) is $\tfrac12(M+m)\dot r^2+U_J(r)$, with $0<r<l$. Its [effective potential](effective-potential.md) diverges at the hole for $J\ne0$ and has a unique minimum at $r_0^3=J^2/(mMg)$. Since $U_J''(r_0)=3Mg/r_0$, small radial oscillations at fixed $J$ have squared angular frequency $3Mg/[(M+m)r_0]$.
