# Adem relations

↑ **Parent:** [Steenrod algebra](steenrod-algebra.md)

These mod-two identities rewrite nonadmissible products of [Steenrod squares](steenrod-square.md). For example, $\operatorname{Sq}^1\operatorname{Sq}^1=0$, $\operatorname{Sq}^1\operatorname{Sq}^2=\operatorname{Sq}^3$, and $\operatorname{Sq}^1\operatorname{Sq}^3=0$. In general the sum ranges over $0\leq t\leq\lfloor a/2\rfloor$ and coefficients are reduced modulo two.
