# Noetherian modules are Hopfian

↑ **Parent:** [Noetherian module](noetherian-module.md)

For a surjective [module endomorphism](module-endomorphism.md) of a [Noetherian module](noetherian-module.md), the chain $\ker f\subseteq\ker f^2\subseteq\cdots$ stabilizes at some $N$. If $f(x)=0$, choose $y$ with $f^N(y)=x$. Then $y\in\ker f^{N+1}=\ker f^N$, giving $x=0$. Thus the endomorphism is also injective and is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md).
