# Foehn wind

↑ **Parent:** [Meteorology](meteorology.md)

A relatively warm, dry downslope wind on the lee side of a mountain. Condensation and condensate removal during ascent can produce [precipitation-driven foehn warming](precipitation-driven-foehn-warming.md); [isentropic foehn drawdown](isentropic-foehn-drawdown.md), turbulent mixing and radiative warming can also contribute. Rainfall is therefore not a necessary condition for every foehn event. The atmosphere's upstream stratification, moisture and flow over or around the mountain determine the contributing mechanisms.[Four mechanisms of lee-side warming and drying](foehn-effect-by-met-office.md)
