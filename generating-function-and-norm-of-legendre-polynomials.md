# Generating function and norm of Legendre polynomials

↑ **Parent:** [Legendre polynomial](legendre-polynomial.md)

With $P_n(1)=1$, the generating function satisfies $\partial_x(x^2\partial_xG)+\partial_t((1-t^2)\partial_tG)=0$. Expanding in [Legendre polynomials](legendre-polynomial.md) gives $x^2a_n''+2xa_n'-n(n+1)a_n=0$. Boundedness at zero removes $x^{-n-1}$, and the value at $t=1$ makes $a_n=x^n$. Orthogonality then gives $\int_{-1}^1G^2dt=\sum_nk_nx^{2n}$. The integral is $x^{-1}\log((1+x)/(1-x))$, so $k_n=2/(2n+1)$. Uniform analyticity on compact subdiscs $|x|<1$ justifies the generating expansion and termwise calculations.
