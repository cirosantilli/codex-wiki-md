# Square root of one minus z squared

↑ **Parent:** [Square root](square-root.md)

On $\mathbb C\setminus(({-\infty},-1]\cup[1,\infty))$, choose the [holomorphic](holomorphic-function-split.md) branch $f(z)=\sqrt{1-z}\sqrt{1+z}$, taking each factor's principal [square root](square-root.md). This gives $f(x)>0$ for $-1<x<1$. Across the [branch cuts](branch-cut.md), its limits are $f(x+i0)=-i\sqrt{x^2-1}$ and $f(x-i0)=+i\sqrt{x^2-1}$ for $x>1$, and $f(x+i0)=+i\sqrt{x^2-1}$ and $f(x-i0)=-i\sqrt{x^2-1}$ for $x<-1$. The endpoints are [branch points](branch-point.md); analytic continuation around a single endpoint changes the square-root sign.
