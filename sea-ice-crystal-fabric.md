# Sea-ice crystal fabric

↑ **Parent:** [Sea ice](sea-ice.md)

The [sea-ice crystal fabric](sea-ice-crystal-fabric.md) describes grain shapes and crystallographic orientations. Calm-water [congelation ice](congelation-ice.md) tends to develop vertical columns with predominantly horizontal crystal c-axes, whereas accumulated [frazil ice](frazil-ice.md) produces a granular texture with much less systematic orientation.
