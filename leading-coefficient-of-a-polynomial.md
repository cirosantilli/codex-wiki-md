# Leading coefficient of a polynomial

↑ **Parent:** [Polynomial](polynomial-split.md)

The leading coefficient of a nonzero [polynomial](polynomial-split.md) $a_nx^n+\cdots+a_0$, with $a_n\ne0$, is $a_n$; its [degree of a polynomial](degree-of-a-polynomial.md) is $n$. A [monic polynomial](monic-polynomial.md) has leading coefficient one. For [polynomials](polynomial-split.md) over a [field](field.md), a product has [leading coefficient](leading-coefficient-of-a-polynomial.md) equal to the product of the two [leading coefficients](leading-coefficient-of-a-polynomial.md), which prevents cancellation of their highest-degree terms.
