# Diffraction envelope

↑ **Parent:** [Fraunhofer diffraction](fraunhofer-diffraction.md)

A [diffraction envelope](diffraction-envelope.md) is the angular factor imposed by the shape and width of each opening in an [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) pattern. For two equally illuminated rectangular slits of width $b$ and separation $s$, [Fraunhofer diffraction](fraunhofer-diffraction.md) gives $I(\theta)=4I_s\operatorname{sinc}^2(\pi b\sin\theta/\lambda)\cos^2(\pi s\sin\theta/\lambda)$, where $\operatorname{sinc}u=\sin u/u$ and $I_s$ is one slit's on-axis [light intensity](light-intensity.md). The first factor limits the observable angular range even when the rapid [interference](interference-wave-propagation.md) fringes remain perfectly coherent.
