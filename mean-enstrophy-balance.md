# Mean enstrophy balance

↑ **Parent:** [Enstrophy](enstrophy.md)

For unforced homogeneous [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), the [vorticity equation](vorticity-equation.md) gives $\dot Z=\langle\omega_i\omega_jS_{ij}\rangle-\nu\langle|\nabla\times\boldsymbol\omega|^2\rangle$. [Curl](curl.md) and full-gradient squared averages agree because the [vorticity](vorticity.md) is divergence free and boundary divergences average to zero. In established high-Reynolds-number [turbulence](turbulence-split.md), slow integral-time evolution makes production and destruction nearly balance.
