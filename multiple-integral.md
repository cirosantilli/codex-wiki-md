# Multiple integral

↑ **Parent:** [Integral](integral.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_integral)

A multiple integral accumulates a [function](function-split.md) over a region with several coordinates. The [Fubini theorem](fubini-s-theorem.md) and [Tonelli theorem](tonelli-theorem.md) give conditions under which it equals successive one-dimensional [integrals](integral.md). A [change of variables formula](change-of-variables-formula.md) expresses it in new coordinates using the absolute [Jacobian determinant](jacobian-determinant.md).
