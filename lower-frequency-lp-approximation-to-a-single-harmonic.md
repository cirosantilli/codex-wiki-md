# Lower-frequency Lp approximation to a single harmonic

↑ **Parent:** [Best approximation in a normed space](best-approximation-in-a-normed-space.md)

For $1<p<\infty$ and $n\ge1$, existence follows from finite-dimensional [compactness](compact-space.md) and uniqueness from [strictly convex normed space](strictly-convex-normed-space.md) geometry. Translation by $2\pi/n$ and negative translation by $\pi/n$ preserve the target and the error [norm](norm.md). Uniqueness forces the minimizing [trigonometric polynomial](trigonometric-polynomial.md) to be invariant under the first operation and the negative of itself under the second. Invariance removes every nonzero frequency below $n$; negative invariance removes the remaining constant.
