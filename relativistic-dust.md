# Relativistic dust

↑ **Parent:** [Perfect fluid in general relativity](perfect-fluid-in-general-relativity.md)

[Relativistic dust](relativistic-dust.md) is a continuum of noninteracting massive particles with one local [four-velocity](four-velocity.md) $u^\mu$ and negligible [pressure](pressure-split.md). With signature $(+---)$, $u^\mu u_\mu=c^2$ and rest [mass density](density.md) $\rho$, its [stress-energy tensor](stress-energy-tensor-split.md) has the displayed form. Contracting [stress-energy conservation](stress-energy-conservation.md) with $u_\nu$ and using [metric compatibility](metric-compatibility.md) gives $c^2\nabla_\mu(\rho u^\mu)=0$. Substituting back yields $u^\mu\nabla_\mu u^\nu=0$ wherever $\rho>0$: each particle follows a [geodesic](geodesic.md). The cosmological [pressureless matter](pressureless-matter.md) model is a homogeneous application.
