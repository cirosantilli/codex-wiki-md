# Surjectivity of a continuous bounded-displacement map

↑ **Parent:** [Brouwer fixed-point theorem](brouwer-fixed-point-theorem.md)

If a [continuous map](continuous-map.md) $g:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n$ has $\|g(x)-x\|\leq K$, then it is onto. For a target $y$, choose $R\geq K+\|y\|$, $R>0$, and map the [unit ball](unit-ball.md) to itself by $f(x)=x-(g(Rx)-y)/R$. A [fixed point](fixed-point.md) gives $g(Rx)=y$.
