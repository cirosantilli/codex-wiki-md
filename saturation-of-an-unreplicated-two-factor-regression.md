# Saturation of an unreplicated two-factor regression

↑ **Parent:** [Two-way analysis of variance](two-way-analysis-of-variance.md)

A [two-way analysis of variance](two-way-analysis-of-variance.md) with the full [interaction](interaction-statistics.md) and one observation in each observed factor cell has one independently varying fitted cell mean per observation. A cell-indicator [design matrix](design-matrix.md) has full row [matrix rank](matrix-rank.md), even when some combinations are missing, so the model is a [saturated statistical model](saturated-statistical-model.md). Its [residual sum of squares](residual-sum-of-squares.md) is zero and its [residual degrees of freedom](residual-degrees-of-freedom.md) are zero. Missing combinations make some coefficients nonidentifiable; they do not restore a pure-error estimate. Standard errors and an ordinary residual-based [F-test](f-test.md) for interactions therefore cannot be recovered merely by fitting this larger model.
