# Supermartingale convergence with summable adapted drift

↑ **Parent:** [Almost sure supermartingale convergence theorem](almost-sure-supermartingale-convergence-theorem.md)

Suppose $X_n\geq0$ is [integrable](integrability.md) and adapted, and $Y_n\geq0$ is finite and adapted. Use the [stopped predictable budget for adapted increments](stopped-predictable-budget-for-adapted-increments.md) with an integer budget $a$. The process $X_{n\wedge\tau_a}+a-U_{n\wedge\tau_a}$ is an [integrable](integrability.md) nonnegative [supermartingale](supermartingale.md), because its budget decrement cancels the localized drift. It has a finite almost-sure limit. On the event that the total drift is smaller than $a$, the [stopping time](stopping-time.md) is infinite, so $X_n$ itself converges. Taking the [countable](countable-set.md) union over integer budgets proves finite [almost sure convergence](almost-sure-convergence.md). No [expectation](expected-value.md) of the total drift is needed.
