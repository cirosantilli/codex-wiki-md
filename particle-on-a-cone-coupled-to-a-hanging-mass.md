# Particle on a cone coupled to a hanging mass

↑ **Parent:** [Constraint (mechanics)](constraint-mechanics.md)

A particle of [mass](mass.md) $m$ constrained to the [cone](cone-geometry.md) $z=-r$ is joined through the apex by a light inextensible [ideal string](massless-inextensible-string.md) to a hanging particle of [mass](mass.md) $\sqrt2m$. The [cylindrical radius](cylindrical-radius.md) is $r>0$ and [gravitational acceleration](gravitational-acceleration.md) is $g$. If the total [ideal string](massless-inextensible-string.md) length is $s_0$, the second particle has vertical coordinate $z_2=-s_0+\sqrt2r$. The [ideal string](massless-inextensible-string.md) and [normal reaction](normal-force.md) exert no azimuthal [force](force-split.md), so [conservation of angular momentum](conservation-of-angular-momentum.md) gives $r^2\dot\theta=h$.

The first particle has [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $m\dot r^2+mh^2/(2r^2)$, while the second has [kinetic energy](kinetic-energy.md) $\sqrt2m\dot r^2$. Their combined [gravitational potential energy](gravitational-energy.md) is $mgr$ plus a constant. Hence [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md) gives the displayed equation, with [effective potential](effective-potential.md) $U(r)=gr+h^2/(2r^2)$. For $h\ne0$, $U$ is strictly convex and has its unique [minimum](minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md) at $r=(h^2/g)^{1/3}$. The energy level has two radial [turning points](turning-point.md) unless the motion is a [circular orbit](circular-orbit.md) at that [minimum](minimum-of-a-subset-of-a-total-order.md).

The model maintains a [mechanical constraint](constraint-mechanics.md) on the [surface](topological-surface-split.md). For unilateral contact with one side of the [cone](cone-geometry.md), a [normal reaction](normal-force.md) computed with a fixed outward sign must also have the physically allowed sign. A finite length of [ideal string](massless-inextensible-string.md) additionally restricts $r<s_0/\sqrt2$ before the hanging particle reaches the hole.
