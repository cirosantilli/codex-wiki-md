# Spin coherent-state path integral

↑ **Parent:** [Spin coherent state](spin-coherent-state.md)

A spin coherent-state path integral inserts group-averaged resolutions of the identity between short time steps. Its kinetic term is the [Spin coherent-state Berry phase](spin-coherent-state-berry-phase.md), equal to $S$ times the oriented solid angle swept on the sphere.
