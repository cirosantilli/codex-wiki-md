# Dirichlet scalar vacuum energy density

↑ **Parent:** [Vacuum energy density](vacuum-energy-density.md)

On a one-dimensional interval with Dirichlet boundary conditions, $k_n=n\pi/L$ and $\omega_n^2=k_n^2+m^2$. The massive-field vacuum energy density can depend on position even though its integral is the oscillator sum $\sum_n\omega_n/2$. All terms require a common ultraviolet regulator when vacuum energies in different geometries are compared.
