# Spring-loaded centrifugal governor

↑ **Parent:** [Centrifugal governor](centrifugal-governor.md)

Consider two equal balls of [mass](mass.md) $m$, four light rods each of length $L$, a fixed upper pivot and a light sliding lower collar. Each upper and lower rod makes angle $\theta$ with the spindle. A [spring](spring.md) of [spring constant](spring-constant.md) $k$ is uncompressed at $\theta=0$ and is compressed by collar motion. Its compression is $\delta=2L(1-\cos\theta)$. Collar balance gives $2T_l\cos\theta=k\delta$, while vertical ball balance gives $(T_u-T_l)\cos\theta=mg$. Radial [Newton's second law](newton-s-second-law.md) gives $(T_u+T_l)\sin\theta=m\omega^2L\sin\theta$. Eliminating the rod [tensions](tension.md) proves the displayed relation for nonzero $\theta$.
