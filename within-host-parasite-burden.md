# Within-host parasite burden

↑ **Parent:** [Macroparasite](macroparasite.md)

The [within-host parasite burden](within-host-parasite-burden.md) is the number of [parasites](parasite.md), or a specified parasite-load measure, carried by one [host](host-biology.md). A population's [mean](expected-value.md) burden and its infected-host [prevalence](prevalence.md) are different summaries of the burden distribution.
