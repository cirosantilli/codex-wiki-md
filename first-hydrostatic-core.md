# First hydrostatic core

↑ **Parent:** [Protostar](protostar.md)

A transient pressure-supported stage of a collapsing cloud after rising [optical depth](optical-depth.md) inhibits cooling. Continued [accretion](accretion.md) and changes of the molecular equation of state can lead to further collapse. It is distinct from the later compact stellar core.
