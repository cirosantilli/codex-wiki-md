# Measurement resolution

↑ **Parent:** [Metrology](metrology.md)

[Measurement resolution](measurement-resolution.md) is the smallest change in an instrument indication that its reading method can distinguish. It contributes to [measurement uncertainty](measurement-uncertainty.md), but does not by itself establish accuracy or remove [systematic error](systematic-error.md). The [manometer height-to-pressure calibration](manometer-height-to-pressure-calibration.md) shows how a height scale affects the inferred [pressure](pressure-split.md).
