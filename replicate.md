# Replicate

↑ **Parent:** [Replication in experimental design](replication-in-experimental-design.md)

A [replicate](replicate.md) is an instance of a [treatment](treatment.md) on a distinct [experimental unit](experimental-unit.md). Independent [replicates](replicate.md) permit estimation of response variation between [experimental units](experimental-unit.md); subsamples within one [experimental unit](experimental-unit.md) do not supply additional independent [treatment](treatment.md) [replication](replication-in-experimental-design.md). In the pot example, another pot receiving A supplies another A [replicate](replicate.md), while another [leaf](leaf.md) from an existing A pot supplies another [observational unit](observational-unit.md).
