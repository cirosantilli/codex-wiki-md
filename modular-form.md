# Modular form

↑ **Parent:** [Modular function](modular-function-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_form)

A modular form is a [holomorphic function](holomorphic-function.md) on the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md) that transforms with a fixed weight under a [congruence subgroup](congruence-subgroup.md) and is [holomorphic at every cusp](holomorphic-at-a-cusp.md).
