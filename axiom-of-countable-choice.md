# Axiom of countable choice

↑ **Parent:** [Axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md)

The axiom of countable choice allows one to choose an element from each member of a countable family of nonempty [sets](set-split.md). It is a restricted form of the [axiom of choice](axiom-of-choice.md). In the usual proof that a [countable union of countable sets](countable-union-of-countable-sets.md) is countable, it suffices to choose one enumerating [injection](injective-function.md) for each set; if those enumerations are already supplied, that proof requires no further choice.
