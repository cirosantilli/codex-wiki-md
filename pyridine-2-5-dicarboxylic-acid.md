<h1 id="pyridine-2-5-dicarboxylic-acid">Pyridine-2,5-dicarboxylic acid</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Chemical compound](chemical-compound.md)

A [pyridine](pyridine.md) ring bearing [carboxylic acid](carboxylic-acid.md) substituents at positions 2 and 5. Removing both acidic protons gives a dianion with a neighbouring N,O [chelation](chelation.md) site and a remote [carboxylate](carboxylate.md) site.
