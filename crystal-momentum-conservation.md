# Crystal momentum conservation

↑ **Parent:** [Crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md)

A lattice-translation-invariant interaction conserves total [crystal momentum](crystal-momentum.md) modulo a [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) [vector](vector.md) $G$. On a periodic chain of $M$ sites with unit spacing, the [discrete Fourier transform](discrete-fourier-transform-split.md) supplies $\sum_{j=1}^Me^{i(k_1+k_2-k_3-k_4)j}=M\delta_{k_1+k_2,k_3+k_4\pmod{2\pi}}$. This [Fourier orthogonality](fourier-orthogonality.md) proves the selection rule directly; it permits transfer by a [reciprocal lattice](reciprocal-lattice.md) [vector](vector.md) rather than requiring equality of unrestricted momenta.
