# Quantum step-reflection phase

↑ **Parent:** [Quantum reflection probability](quantum-reflection-probability.md)

A particle incident below a semi-infinite step has a unit-modulus [quantum reflection amplitude](quantum-reflection-amplitude.md) but a nontrivial phase. Continuity of the oscillating and evanescent solutions yields the displayed coefficient, for the incident phase origin at the interface. Its decaying exterior wave has zero net [probability current](probability-current.md). The phase depends on the chosen scattering convention; the unit reflection [probability](probability.md) does not.
