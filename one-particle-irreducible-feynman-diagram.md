# One-particle-irreducible Feynman diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Connected Feynman diagram](connected-feynman-diagram.md)

A one-particle-irreducible Feynman diagram is a connected [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md) that remains connected after any one internal line is cut. The vertices of the [quantum effective action](effective-action.md) generate precisely these diagrams.

These [Feynman diagrams](feynman-diagram.md) generate the vertices of the [quantum effective action](effective-action.md).
