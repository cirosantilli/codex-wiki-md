# Symmetric-part contraction criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Asymptotic stability](asymptotic-stability.md)

For $\dot e=Ae$, differentiation gives $d\|e\|^2/dt=e^T(A+A^T)e$. A uniform negative-definiteness bound with $\kappa>0$ yields exponential contraction by direct integration of this differential inequality. This is a sufficient criterion for [asymptotic stability](asymptotic-stability.md), stronger than requiring all [eigenvalues](eigenvalue.md) of $A$ to have negative real parts. A merely semidefinite symmetric part needs additional analysis.
