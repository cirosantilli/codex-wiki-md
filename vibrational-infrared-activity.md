# Vibrational infrared activity

↑ **Parent:** [Molecular vibration](molecular-vibration.md)

In the ideal [electric-dipole interaction](electric-dipole-interaction.md), a vibrational transition needs a nonzero [matrix element](matrix-element.md) of the [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md). For a [diatomic bond stretch](diatomic-bond-stretch.md), expand the body-axis dipole component as $d(q)=d_e+d'_eq+\cdots$. A changing [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md), rather than a permanent value $d_e$ alone, permits the harmonic fundamental. Keeping only the linear term and harmonic [wavefunctions](wave-function-split.md) gives $\Delta v=\pm1$.[Lecture 14, introductory recap and infrared-activity section: dipole change and adjacent-level selection](14-harmonic-oscillators-and-ir-spectroscopy-ucd-chem-110a-by-delmar-s-larsen.md)

The derivation is sharper than a picture of a shaking [molecule](molecule-split.md). [Orthogonality](orthogonal-vectors.md) removes the constant term for $v'\ne v$, and [creation and annihilation operators](creation-and-annihilation-operators.md) give

$$
\langle v'|q|v\rangle=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2\mu\omega}}\left(\sqrt{v+1}\,\delta_{v',v+1}+\sqrt v\,\delta_{v',v-1}\right).
$$

Here $\delta$ is the [Kronecker delta](kronecker-delta.md). Thus absorption raises $v$ by one when the dipole derivative and angular factor are nonzero, and its [photon](photon.md) satisfies

$$
h\nu_\gamma=E_{v+1}-E_v=\hbar\omega,\qquad
\widetilde\nu_\gamma=\frac{E_{v+1}-E_v}{hc}=\widetilde\nu_{\rm harm}.
$$

A nonzero vibrational [matrix element](matrix-element.md) is only one factor: rotational [electric-dipole selection rules](electric-dipole-selection-rule.md), initial populations, and spectral resolution also constrain visibility.

For an isolated [diatomic molecule](diatomic-molecule.md) with identical [atomic nuclei](atomic-nucleus-split.md), such as [molecular hydrogen](molecular-hydrogen.md) in its symmetric electronic [ground state](ground-state.md), stretching preserves zero [electric dipole moment](electric-dipole-moment.md). By symmetry, this homonuclear stretch is infrared inactive in the ideal dipole model, although it still vibrates. Other interactions or environments can permit detection.[Lecture 14, introductory recap: dipole-change criterion; the identical-nuclei case follows here by symmetry](14-harmonic-oscillators-and-ir-spectroscopy-ucd-chem-110a-by-delmar-s-larsen.md)
