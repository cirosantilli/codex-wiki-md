# Giant star

↑ **Parent:** [Star](star.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_star)

An evolved [star](star.md) with substantially larger [stellar radius](stellar-radius.md) and [luminosity](luminosity.md) than a [main sequence](main-sequence.md) star of similar [effective temperature](effective-temperature.md). A [red giant](red-giant.md) is a cool example; giant does not mean merely any star of large [mass](mass.md).
