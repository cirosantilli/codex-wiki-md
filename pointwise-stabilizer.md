# Pointwise stabilizer

↑ **Parent:** [Stabilizer subgroup](stabilizer-subgroup.md)

For a [group action](group-action.md), the pointwise stabilizer of a set $S$ is the [subgroup](subgroup.md) fixing each of its points individually. It is the intersection of their [stabilizer subgroups](stabilizer-subgroup.md). This is stronger than merely mapping the set to itself.
