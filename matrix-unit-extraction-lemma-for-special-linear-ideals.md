# Matrix-unit extraction lemma for special linear ideals

↑ **Parent:** [Special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md)

Over a [field](field.md) of [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) different from two, any [Lie algebra ideal](ideal-of-a-lie-algebra.md) in $\mathfrak{sl}_n$ containing a nonscalar [matrix](matrix.md) is the whole [special linear Lie algebra](special-linear-lie-algebra.md). Indeed, if $A_{ji}\ne0$ for $i\ne j$, then $[E_{ij},[E_{ij},A]]=-2A_{ji}E_{ij}$ extracts a [matrix unit](matrix-unit.md). For a nonscalar diagonal [matrix](matrix.md), $[E_{ij},A]=(A_{jj}-A_{ii})E_{ij}$ extracts one instead. Its bracket with $E_{ji}$ produces $H=E_{ii}-E_{jj}$, and $[H,E_{ji}]=-2E_{ji}$ extracts the reverse unit. Brackets $[E_{ij},E_{jk}]=E_{ik}$ and $[E_{ki},E_{ij}]=E_{kj}$ spread these units to every row and column; opposite pairs span all trace-zero diagonal [matrices](matrix.md). Thus only ideals consisting of scalar [matrices](matrix.md) can be proper. This proof works even when restrictions of distinct [roots of a root system](root-of-a-root-system.md) to a [Cartan subalgebra](cartan-subalgebra.md) coincide in positive [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md).
