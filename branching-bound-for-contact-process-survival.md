# Branching bound for contact-process survival

↑ **Parent:** [Contact process](contact-process.md)

With recovery rate one, the number of infected sites in a nearest-neighbour [contact process](contact-process.md) has death rate equal to its size and birth rate at most $2d\lambda$ times its size. It is dominated by a linear birth-death [branching process](branching-process.md) with these per-particle rates. In particular, from one infection its expected size is at most $e^{(2d\lambda-1)t}$, so its survival probability is zero when $2d\lambda<1$. This proves the displayed lower bound on the critical rate.
