# Long-wave and short-wave branches at marginal Toomre stability

↑ **Parent:** [Toomre parameter](toomre-parameter.md)

At fluid [Toomre parameter](toomre-parameter.md) $Q=1$, the local spiral-wave [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) becomes $n^2(\Omega_P-\Omega)^2=(a_0|k|-\kappa)^2$. The minus-sign, longer-wavelength root is nonnegative only between the two [Lindblad resonance](lindblad-resonance.md) conditions. The plus-sign, shorter-wavelength root remains positive outside that interval. Thus this [dispersion relation](dispersion-relation-split.md) alone does not prove that every [density](density.md) wave is confined between [Lindblad resonances](lindblad-resonance.md); an additional physical restriction is needed.
