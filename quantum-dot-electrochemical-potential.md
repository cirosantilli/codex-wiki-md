# Quantum-dot electrochemical potential

↑ **Parent:** [Constant interaction model](constant-interaction-model.md)

With $U(N)=\sum_{i=1}^N\epsilon_i+(Ne-Q_g)^2/(2C_\Sigma)$, the addition [electrochemical potential](electrochemical-potential.md) is $\mu_N=\epsilon_N+(N-1/2)e^2/C_\Sigma-eQ_g/C_\Sigma$. Gate and lead voltages enter $Q_g$. Adjacent addition potentials differ by an orbital spacing plus $e^2/C_\Sigma$.
