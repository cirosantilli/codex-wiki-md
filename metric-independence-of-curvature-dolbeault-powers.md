# Metric independence of curvature Dolbeault powers

↑ **Parent:** [Curvature Dolbeault class](curvature-dolbeault-class.md)

Composition of [endomorphisms](endomorphism.md) and [wedge product of differential forms](wedge-product-of-differential-forms.md) give the class $[\Theta^k]\in H^k(M,\Omega_M^k\otimes\operatorname{End}E)$. Even total degree of [vector-bundle curvature](curvature-form.md) and the noncommutative telescoping identity give $\Theta_1^k-\Theta_0^k=\bar\partial\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}\Theta_1^j a\Theta_0^{k-1-j}$ whenever $\Theta_1-\Theta_0=\bar\partial a$. Thus all powers are metric independent; no commutativity of [endomorphisms](endomorphism.md) is assumed.
