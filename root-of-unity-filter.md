# Root-of-unity filter

↑ **Parent:** [Root of unity](root-of-unity.md)

For $\omega=e^{2\pi i/N}$,

$$
\sum_{y=0}^{N-1}\omega^{my}
=\begin{cases}N,&N\mid m,\\0,&N\nmid m.\end{cases}
$$

This is the finite [geometric series](geometric-series.md) identity and expresses orthogonality of the characters of $\mathbb Z_N$.
