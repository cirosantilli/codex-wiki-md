# Relevance of independent self-sums

↑ **Parent:** [C-relevant pair of random variables](c-relevant-pair-of-random-variables.md)

If $(U,V)$ is $C$-relevant to $(X,Y)$ and $U_1,U_2,V_1,V_2$ are mutually independent copies, then $(U_1+U_2,V_1+V_2)$ is $2C$-relevant to $(X,Y)$. The [entropy submodularity for three independent sums](entropy-submodularity-for-three-independent-sums.md) implies

$$
d_R(U_1+U_2;X)
\leq\frac12\bigl(2d_R(U;X)+d_R(U;U)\bigr),
$$

and similarly for $V$. The [Entropic Ruzsa triangle inequality](entropic-ruzsa-triangle-inequality.md) bounds

$$
d_R(U;U)\leq2d_R(U;X),
\qquad
d_R(V;V)\leq2d_R(V;Y),
$$

which proves the claim after addition.
