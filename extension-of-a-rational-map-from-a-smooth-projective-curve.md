# Extension of a rational map from a smooth projective curve

↑ **Parent:** [Smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md)

Every [rational map](rational-map-of-projective-varieties.md) from a [smooth projective curve](smooth-projective-curve.md) to a [projective variety](projective-variety.md) extends uniquely to a [morphism](morphism-of-algebraic-varieties.md). At a missing point, the [discrete valuation ring](discrete-valuation-ring.md) of the curve lets one divide homogeneous coordinates by their smallest valuation, leaving regular coordinates of which at least one is a unit.
