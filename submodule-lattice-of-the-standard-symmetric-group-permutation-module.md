# Submodule lattice of the standard symmetric-group permutation module

↑ **Parent:** [Young permutation module](young-permutation-module.md)

For $n\geq3$, let $S_n$ permute the coordinates of $M=F^n$. Write $T$ for the constant vectors and $U=S^{(n-1,1)}$ for the augmentation [submodule](submodule.md). These and zero and $M$ are all the [submodules](submodule.md). Indeed a vector with two unequal coordinates yields a nonzero multiple of $e_i-e_j$ by subtracting its [image](image-of-a-function.md) under $(i,j)$; the [orbit](orbit-dynamical-system.md) of such a difference spans $U$. A [submodule](submodule.md) without such a vector lies in $T$. If the [characteristic](characteristic-of-a-field.md) does not divide $n$, then $M=T\oplus U$ and $U$ is simple. If it divides $n$, the lattice is the chain $0<T<U<M$, with $U/T$ simple of [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) $n-2$ and trivial end factors. The direct-sum [projection](projection-linear-algebra.md) in the first case is $x\mapsto x-(\sum_i x_i)\mathbf1/n$.
