# Compton y parameter

↑ **Parent:** [Kompaneets equation](kompaneets-equation.md)

The [Compton y parameter](compton-y-parameter.md) is the [Thomson scattering](thomson-scattering.md) [optical depth](optical-depth.md) weighted by the dimensionless [electron](electron.md) [temperature](temperature-split.md). It measures the integrated electron [pressure](pressure-split.md) along a [line of sight](line-of-sight.md). When hot, nonrelativistic electrons scatter much colder [photons](photon.md), the first-order radiation energy gain is $4y$ times the incident energy. The spectrum is redistributed while the total [photon number density](photon-number-density.md) is conserved locally by scattering.
