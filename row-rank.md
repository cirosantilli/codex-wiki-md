# Row rank

↑ **Parent:** [Matrix rank](matrix-rank.md)

The [row rank](row-rank.md) of a [matrix](matrix.md) is the dimension of the [vector space](vector-space-split.md) spanned by its rows. [Elementary row operations](elementary-row-operation.md) preserve this space; multiplication on the right by an [invertible matrix](invertible-matrix.md) preserves all [linear relations](linear-relation.md) among the rows. In [row echelon form](row-echelon-form.md) the nonzero rows are [linearly independent](linear-independence.md), so the row rank is the number of pivots. The pivot columns are also [linearly independent](linear-independence.md) and span the [column space](column-space.md), proving equality with the [column rank](column-rank.md).
