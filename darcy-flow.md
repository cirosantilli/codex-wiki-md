# Darcy flow

↑ **Parent:** [Darcy law](darcy-law.md)

Darcy flow is slow flow through a [porous medium](porous-medium.md) whose averaged flux obeys the [Darcy law](darcy-law.md). The [Darcy velocity](darcy-velocity.md) is the fluid volume flux per total cross-sectional area; it differs from the velocity averaged only over pore space.
