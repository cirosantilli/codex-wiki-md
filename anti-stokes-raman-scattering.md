# Anti-Stokes Raman scattering

↑ **Parent:** [Raman scattering](raman-scattering.md)

In [Anti-Stokes Raman scattering](anti-stokes-raman-scattering.md), matter loses internal [energy](energy-split.md) and the outgoing [photon](photon.md) gains it. The harmonic transition $v\to v-1$ requires $v\geq1$, so an initially excited vibrational population must be available. At low [temperature](temperature-split.md), high-[frequency](frequency.md) vibrational excitations are scarce and the corresponding anti-Stokes line is weak. The extra [photon](photon.md) [energy](energy-split.md) comes from the molecule; there is no conflict with [conservation of energy](conservation-of-energy.md).
