# Infective individual

↑ **Parent:** [SIR model](sir-model.md)

An [infective individual](infective-individual.md) is currently capable of transmitting the modeled infection. This is distinct from a [susceptible individual](susceptible-individual.md) and, when incubation is modeled, from an exposed individual not yet infectious.
