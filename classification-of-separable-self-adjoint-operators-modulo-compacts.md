# Classification of separable self-adjoint operators modulo compacts

↑ **Parent:** [Weyl-von Neumann theorem](weyl-von-neumann-theorem.md)

Bounded [self-adjoint operators](self-adjoint-operator.md) on separable infinite-dimensional complex [Hilbert spaces](hilbert-space-split.md) are unitarily equivalent modulo [compact operators](compact-operator-split.md) exactly when they have the same [essential spectrum in the Calkin algebra](essential-spectrum-in-the-calkin-algebra.md). The [Weyl-von Neumann theorem](weyl-von-neumann-theorem.md) diagonalizes each modulo a compact error. Their diagonal cluster sets are the essential spectra; [cluster-set matching of bounded sequences](cluster-set-matching-of-bounded-sequences.md) pairs the two diagonal lists with differences tending to zero, giving a compact diagonal difference. The separability assumption is essential: projections with countable and uncountable range dimensions can have the same essential spectrum but cannot differ by a [compact operator](compact-operator-split.md) after unitary conjugation.
