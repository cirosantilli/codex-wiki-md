# Spinor bundle

↑ **Parent:** [Spin manifold](spin-manifold.md)

A chosen [spin structure](spin-structure.md) on a [spin manifold](spin-manifold.md) defines the complex spinor bundle associated to the spin representation. In dimension $2m$ its [vector bundle rank](rank-of-a-vector-bundle.md) is $2^m$. Its [chirality matrix](chirality-matrix.md) is $\Gamma=i^mc(e^1)\cdots c(e^{2m})$ in the negative [Clifford multiplication](clifford-multiplication.md) convention. It satisfies $\Gamma^2=1$ and anticommutes with each $c(e^i)$, giving $S=S^+\oplus S^-$ and an odd [Dirac operator](dirac-operator.md). In odd dimension there is no such canonical chiral splitting of the irreducible spinor bundle.
