# Discreteness of principal ideles

↑ **Parent:** [Principal idele](principal-idele.md)

The diagonal multiplicative group of a [number field](number-field.md) is discrete in its [idele group](idele-group.md). Require local units at every finite place and an archimedean neighbourhood of one. A diagonal element $x$ meeting these restrictions has integral $x-1$. If $x\ne1$, its nonzero integer [field norm](field-norm.md) cannot have absolute value less than one, while sufficiently small archimedean differences would force exactly that.
