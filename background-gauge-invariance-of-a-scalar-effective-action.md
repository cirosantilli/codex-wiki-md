# Background gauge invariance of a scalar effective action

↑ **Parent:** [Effective action](effective-action.md)

For real scalar multiplets with antisymmetric generators $t_a$, transforming a source $J$ in the same representation leaves $J\cdot\phi$ invariant. With an invariant [functional measure](functional-measure.md), local changes of integration variables imply the [Ward identity](ward-identity.md) for the [quantum effective action](effective-action.md). In the convention $Z=e^{iW}$, $\varphi=W_J$, $\Gamma=-W+\int J\varphi$, it is

$$
\left[(t_a\varphi)\cdot\frac{\delta}{\delta\varphi}+\partial_\mu\frac{\delta}{\delta A_{\mu a}}+f_{abc}A_{\mu b}\frac{\delta}{\delta A_{\mu c}}\right]\Gamma=0.
$$

The [Legendre transform](convex-conjugate.md) gives $\Gamma_\varphi=J$ and $\Gamma_A|_\varphi=-W_A|_J$. Its classical limit is $\Gamma=-S_0$, not $S_0$, for the source term $+J\phi$. Differentiating twice in $\varphi$ produces two contact terms and the momentum-space identity $p_{1\mu}\tau^\mu_{a,ij}=i(t_a)_{ik}\tau_{kj}-\tau_{ik}i(t_a)_{kj}$ with the appropriate shifted two-point momenta. Changing the overall convention for $\Gamma$ changes both two-point and mixed vertices together; changing just one violates the identity. A [gauge anomaly](gauge-anomaly.md) would invalidate the assumed invariant-measure argument.
