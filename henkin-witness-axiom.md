# Henkin witness axiom

↑ **Parent:** [Henkin witness property](henkin-witness-property.md)

A [Henkin witness axiom](henkin-witness-axiom.md) names a witness to an [existential sentence](existential-sentence.md) using a fresh [constant symbol](constant-symbol.md). Freshness is part of the consistency-preservation argument in the [Henkin construction](henkin-construction.md).
