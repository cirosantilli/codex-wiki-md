# Normal closure

↑ **Parent:** [Normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_closure)

The normal closure of a subset $S\subseteq G$ is the smallest [normal subgroup](normal-subgroup.md) containing $S$, equivalently the subgroup generated by all conjugates $gsg^{-1}$ with $g\in G$ and $s\in S$.
