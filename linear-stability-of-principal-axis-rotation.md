# Linear stability of principal-axis rotation

↑ **Parent:** [Intermediate axis theorem](intermediate-axis-theorem.md)

Linearize the [Euler equations for a torque-free rigid body](euler-equations-for-a-torque-free-rigid-body.md) about rotation with angular speed $\Omega$ along the third principal axis. The transverse perturbations obey

$$
\dot\omega_1=\frac{I_2-I_3}{I_1}\Omega\omega_2,
\qquad
\dot\omega_2=\frac{I_3-I_1}{I_2}\Omega\omega_1,
$$

and hence

$$
\ddot\omega_1
=-\Omega^2\frac{(I_3-I_2)(I_3-I_1)}{I_1I_2}\omega_1.
$$

Cyclic permutation gives the corresponding equations about the other two axes. For $I_1<I_2<I_3$, the squared frequency is positive about axes one and three, whereas the intermediate-axis equation has a positive exponential-growth coefficient.
