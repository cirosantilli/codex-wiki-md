# Line source

↑ **Parent:** [Point source](point-source.md)

In a planar [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md), a [line source](line-source.md) injects [volume flux](volumetric-flow-rate.md) $Q$ per unit length perpendicular to the plane. Its radial [velocity](velocity.md) is $u_r=Q/(2\pi r)$, since integrating the outward [velocity](velocity.md) around a [circle](circle.md) gives $2\pi r u_r=Q$. Its [complex potential of a two-dimensional potential flow](complex-potential-of-a-two-dimensional-potential-flow.md) is $w=Q\log(z-b)/(2\pi)$ and its [complex velocity](complex-velocity.md) is $w'=Q/[2\pi(z-b)]$. Here $Q>0$ means outward flow; a [line sink](line-sink.md) has negative $Q$. Away from $b$, this is an [irrotational flow](irrotational-flow.md) as well as an [incompressible flow](incompressible-flow.md).
