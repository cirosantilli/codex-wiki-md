# Quark-flow diagram

↑ **Parent:** [Feynman diagram](feynman-diagram.md)

A schematic tracking of valence [quark](quark.md) and [antiquark](antiquark.md) flavor through a hadronic process. It can explain flavor conservation and required pair creation without computing the nonperturbative [hadronization](hadronization.md) amplitude. A complete elementary perturbative diagram contains additional interaction and color information.
