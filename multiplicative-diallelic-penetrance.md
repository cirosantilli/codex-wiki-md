# Multiplicative diallelic penetrance

↑ **Parent:** [Penetrance](penetrance.md)

With [Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium](hardy-weinberg-principle.md), let $G$ count copies of the risk [allele](allele.md). The [penetrance](penetrance.md) $p\theta^G$ must lie in $[0,1]$ for every possible [genotype](genotype.md). Writing $\alpha=1-\phi+\phi\theta$ and $\beta=1-\phi+\phi\theta^2$ gives $\mathbb E(q_G)=p\alpha^2$ and $\mathbb E(q_G^2)=p^2\beta^2$.
