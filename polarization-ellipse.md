# Polarization ellipse

↑ **Parent:** [Elliptical polarization](elliptical-polarization.md)

The [polarization ellipse](polarization-ellipse.md) is the curve traced by the transverse [electric field](electric-field-split.md) of a monochromatic wave at a fixed point. Its orientation and signed ratio of minor to major semiaxis specify a [pure polarization](pure-polarization.md). A zero minor semiaxis gives [linear polarization](linear-polarization.md), and equal semiaxes give [circular polarization](circular-polarization.md).
