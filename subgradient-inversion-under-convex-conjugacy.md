# Subgradient inversion under convex conjugacy

↑ **Parent:** [Subdifferential](subdifferential.md)

For a proper lower-semicontinuous convex functional on a real [Hilbert space](hilbert-space-split.md), $p\in\partial J(u)$ means $\langle v,p\rangle-J(v)\leq\langle u,p\rangle-J(u)$ for all $v$. Taking the supremum gives equality in the [Fenchel–Young inequality](fenchel-young-inequality.md): $J(u)+J^*(p)=\langle u,p\rangle$. Apply the same argument to $J^*$ and use the [Fenchel-Moreau theorem](fenchel-moreau-theorem.md) $J^{**}=J$ to obtain the reciprocal [subgradient](subgradient.md) condition.
