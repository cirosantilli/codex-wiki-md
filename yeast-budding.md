# Yeast budding

↑ **Parent:** [Budding yeast](budding-yeast.md)

Formation and growth of a daughter-cell bud. A visible bud is evidence of initial [polarized cell growth](polarized-cell-growth.md) but does not establish normal [cytokinesis](cytokinesis.md) or long-term proliferative capacity.
