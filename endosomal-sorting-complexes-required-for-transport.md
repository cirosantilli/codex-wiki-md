# Endosomal sorting complexes required for transport

↑ **Parent:** [Membrane transport](membrane-transport.md)

[ESCRT](endosomal-sorting-complexes-required-for-transport.md) components help sort membrane cargo and remodel membranes, including formation of internal vesicles in an [endosome](endosome.md). Ubiquitin can be a cargo-selection signal, but recognition and the membrane-remodelling steps are distinct operations.
