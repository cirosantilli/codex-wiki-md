# Rigid quiver representation

↑ **Parent:** [Representation of a quiver](representation-of-a-quiver.md)

A finite-dimensional [quiver representation](representation-of-a-quiver.md) is rigid when $\operatorname{Ext}^1_Q(X,X)=0$. Its [extension complex of quiver representations](extension-complex-of-quiver-representations.md) then has surjective differential. Equivalently, it has an open orbit under base change. Rigidity constrains arrow ranks and path identities.
