# Poincare metric on a Riemann surface

↑ **Parent:** [Hyperbolic metric](hyperbolic-metric.md)

A connected [Riemann surface](riemann-surfaces.md) whose [universal cover](universal-cover.md) is the [unit disc](unit-disc.md) inherits the complete curvature-minus-one metric displayed on its cover. [Möbius transformations](mobius-transformation.md) preserving the disc are isometries, so [deck transformations](deck-transformation.md) preserve the metric and it descends to the surface. This uniformization notion must be distinguished from the potential-theoretic existence of a Green function. [Holomorphic maps](holomorphic-map.md) between such surfaces contract their metric by lifting to [Schwarz-Pick theorem](schwarz-pick-theorem.md) on the covers.
