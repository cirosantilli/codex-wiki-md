# Strongly universal hash family

↑ **Parent:** [Universal hash family](universal-hash-family.md)

For every distinct pair of inputs and every specified pair of output values, the displayed joint probability holds. Thus outputs at distinct inputs are independent and uniform. Summing over equal output values gives the [universal hash family](universal-hash-family.md) collision bound with equality.
