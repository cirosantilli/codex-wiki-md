# Energy obstruction for a cubic planar oscillator

↑ **Parent:** [Poincaré index](poincare-index.md)

For $\dot x=y+ax-bx^3$, $\dot y=x^3-x$, the function $H=y^2/2+x^2/2-x^4/4$ obeys $\dot H=(x-x^3)(ax-bx^3)$. Every nonconstant [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md) lies in $|x|<1$: at a maximum above one or a minimum below minus one, $\ddot x=x^3-x$ has the wrong sign; equality would force the solution to be an [equilibrium](equilibrium-point-of-a-dynamical-system.md) by uniqueness. If $a\ne0$ and $b/a<1$, then $\dot H=a x^2(1-x^2)(1-(b/a)x^2)$ has the strict sign of $a$ except at $x=0$, preventing a nonconstant [periodic orbit](periodic-orbit.md).
