# Tensor product of fields

↑ **Parent:** [Algebra](algebra-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tensor_product_of_fields)

The [tensor product of fields](tensor-product-of-fields.md) $L\otimes_K M$ is a $K$-algebra, not necessarily a field. If $L$ and $M$ sit in one overfield, the map $l\otimes m\mapsto lm$ has an image that generates their compositum as a field. When the extensions are algebraic, this image is already a field. Injectivity of this multiplication map is equivalent to [linear disjointness](linear-disjointness.md).
