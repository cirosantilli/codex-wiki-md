# Strong product of graphs

↑ **Parent:** [Graph](graph-split.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_product_of_graphs)

Its vertices are pairs of vertices. Two distinct pairs are adjacent exactly when, in each coordinate, their entries are equal or adjacent in the corresponding [graph](graph-split.md). Cartesian products of independent sets are independent, giving supermultiplicativity of the [independence number](independence-number.md).
