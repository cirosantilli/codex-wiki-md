# Two-isogeny descent

↑ **Parent:** [Kummer map of an elliptic curve](kummer-map-of-an-elliptic-curve.md)

Here descent is an arithmetic calculation with square classes and an [isogeny of elliptic curves](isogeny-of-elliptic-curves.md), rather than the gluing of objects along a covering.

For $E:y^2=x^3+ax^2+bx$ and its two-isogenous curve $E':y^2=x^3-2ax^2+(a^2-4b)x$, square-class maps on $E(\mathbb Q)$ and $E'(\mathbb Q)$ determine the Mordell-Weil rank.
