# Absence of characters on an operator algebra with isomorphic complementary summands

↑ **Parent:** [Character space of an algebra](character-space-of-an-algebra.md)

If $X=Y\oplus Z$ and the Banach spaces $Y$ and $Z$ are isomorphic, then the operator algebra $\mathcal B(X)$ has no [characters](character-of-an-algebra.md). The two complementary projections have unequal character values, while mutually inverse off-diagonal maps force those values to be equal.
