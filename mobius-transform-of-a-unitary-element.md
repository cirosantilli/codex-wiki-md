<h1 id="mobius-transform-of-a-unitary-element">Möbius transform of a unitary element</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Unitary element of a C-star algebra](unitary-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md)

For a [Unitary element of a C-star algebra](unitary-element-of-a-c-star-algebra.md) $u$, the displayed element is also unitary. Its denominator has a multiplicative inverse by the [Neumann series](neumann-series.md), since $\|\overline\lambda u\|<1$. The spectrum of $u$ lies on the [unit circle](complex-unit-circle.md), where $|t+\lambda|=|1+\overline\lambda t|$. The resulting [continuous function](continuous-function.md) $f(t)=(t+\lambda)/(1+\overline\lambda t)$ therefore has $\overline f f=f\overline f=1$ on the spectrum. The [continuous functional calculus](continuous-functional-calculus.md) preserves products and the involution, giving $f(u)^*f(u)=f(u)f(u)^*=1$. The complex conjugate in the denominator is necessary for the unit-modulus calculation.
