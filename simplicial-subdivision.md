# Simplicial subdivision

↑ **Parent:** [Simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md)

A simplicial subdivision replaces the [simplexes](simplex.md) of a [simplicial complex](simplicial-complex.md) by finitely many smaller [simplexes](simplex.md) with the same underlying space, compatibly on common faces. A [piecewise linear ball](piecewise-linear-ball.md) can be parametrized by a map linear on the [simplexes](simplex.md) after appropriate subdivisions of its domain and target.
