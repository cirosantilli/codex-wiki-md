# Nuclear alpha-decay recoil correction

↑ **Parent:** [Alpha decay](alpha-decay.md)

In two-body [alpha decay](alpha-decay.md) of a parent at rest, [momentum conservation](momentum-conservation.md) makes daughter and alpha momenta equal and opposite. The nonrelativistic kinetic [energies](energy-split.md) therefore satisfy $T_d/T_\alpha=m_\alpha/M_d$, giving

$$
T_\alpha=Q_\alpha\frac{M_d}{M_d+m_\alpha},\qquad T_d=Q_\alpha\frac{m_\alpha}{M_d+m_\alpha}.
$$

An excited daughter reduces the available [Q value](q-value.md). To leading recoil order, an alpha line separation $\Delta T_\alpha$ corresponds to excitation $E_x=(1+m_\alpha/M_d)\Delta T_\alpha$. Thus ratios of excitation energies inferred from line separations do not need the common recoil factor. These formulas assume kinetic energies small compared with rest [masses](mass.md); an exact [relativistic two-body decay](relativistic-two-body-decay.md) calculation can replace them when necessary.
