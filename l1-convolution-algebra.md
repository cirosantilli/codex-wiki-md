# L1 convolution algebra

↑ **Parent:** [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md)

With left [Haar measure](haar-measure.md), $(f*g)(x)=\int_G f(y)g(y^{-1}x)\,dm(y)$ makes $L^1(G)$ a [Banach algebra](banach-algebra-split.md), since $\|f*g\|_1\leq\|f\|_1\|g\|_1$. For an Abelian group it is commutative, and the involution is $f^*(x)=\overline{f(x^{-1})}$. Its nonzero [characters of an algebra](character-of-an-algebra.md) are exactly integration against inverses of continuous unitary group characters.
