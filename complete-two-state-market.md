# Complete two-state market

↑ **Parent:** [Complete market](complete-market.md)

Two assets with linearly independent payoff vectors across two positive-probability states span all terminal payoffs. The state-price equations have a unique solution. A positive solution gives the [state-price density](state-price-density.md) after division by physical state probabilities. [Arrow state prices](arrow-debreu-state-price.md) sum to the riskless [discount factor](discount-factor.md), while normalized [Arrow state prices](arrow-debreu-state-price.md) are [risk-neutral probabilities](risk-neutral-probability.md); these three quantities must not be confused.
