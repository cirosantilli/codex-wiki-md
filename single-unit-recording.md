# Single-unit recording

↑ **Parent:** [Neuroscientific methods](neuroscientific-methods.md)

Measurement of the activity attributed to one [neuron](neuron-split.md), commonly through extracellular electrodes and spike isolation. It permits trial-resolved tests of stimulus tuning and interactions. Failure to evoke spikes with one stimulus alone does not exclude a modulatory input from that modality.
