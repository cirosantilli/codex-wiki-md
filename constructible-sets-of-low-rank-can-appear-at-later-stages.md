# Constructible sets of low rank can appear at later stages

↑ **Parent:** [Constructible hierarchy](constructible-hierarchy.md)

The equality $L\cap V_\alpha=L_\alpha$ can fail even at $\alpha=\omega+1$. Inside the [constructible universe](constructible-universe.md), $L_\omega=V_\omega$ is countable, so its [definable power set](definable-power-set-split.md) $L_{\omega+1}$ is countable. The [constructible power set](constructible-power-set.md) of $\omega$ is uncountable there by the [Cantor theorem](cantor-s-theorem.md). A real outside $L_{\omega+1}$ remains a constructible subset of $\omega$, hence belongs to $V_{\omega+1}$. The cardinal comparison must be made inside $L$, since its reals can be externally countable.
