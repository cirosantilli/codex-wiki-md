# Time-rescaled spike-train diagnostics

↑ **Parent:** [Spike train](spike-train.md)

For a correctly specified conditional rate, transformed interspike intervals have independent unit-exponential distributions, apart from observation-boundary censoring. Their transforms $1-e^{-z_i}$ should be uniform. Distributional checks and serial-correlation checks assess a fitted spike model on held-out data. A history-dependent model must include past spikes in its conditional rate; stimulus-only [Poisson spike-train likelihood fitting](poisson-spike-train-likelihood-fitting.md) cannot automatically capture refractoriness.
