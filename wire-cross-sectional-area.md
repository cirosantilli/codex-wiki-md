# Wire cross-sectional area

↑ **Parent:** [Current density](current-density.md)

The [wire cross-sectional area](wire-cross-sectional-area.md) is the [area](surface-area.md) of a slice perpendicular to the wire's axis. For a round wire of radius $a$, it is $A=\pi a^2$. It enters both the uniform-flow formula $I=jA$ for [current density](current-density.md) and the formula $R=L/(\sigma A)$ for the [electrical resistance](electrical-resistance.md) of a uniform [Ohmic conductor](ohmic-conductor.md).
