# Uniform convergence on compacts in probability under an absolutely continuous measure change

↑ **Parent:** [Uniform convergence on compacts in probability](uniform-convergence-on-compacts-in-probability.md)

If $Q\ll P$, [uniform convergence on compacts in probability](uniform-convergence-on-compacts-in-probability.md) under $P$ implies the same convergence under $Q$. Indeed, let $Z=dQ/dP$ be the [Radon-Nikodym derivative](radon-nikodym-derivative.md). For every event $E$,

$$
Q(E)\leq K P(E)+\mathbb E_P[Z\mathbf1_{\{Z>K\}}].
$$

Integrability makes the second term arbitrarily small for large $K$. Thus $P(E_n)\to0$ implies $Q(E_n)\to0$; apply this to each event where the supremum on $[0,T]$ exceeds a fixed tolerance. Only [absolute continuity of measures](absolute-continuity-of-measures.md) is needed, not equivalence.
