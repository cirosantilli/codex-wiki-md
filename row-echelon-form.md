# Row echelon form

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian elimination](gaussian-elimination.md)

A [matrix](matrix.md) is in row echelon form if zero rows follow all nonzero rows, each nonzero row has a first nonzero entry (pivot) to the right of the pivot in the preceding row, and entries below each pivot vanish. [Elementary row operations](elementary-row-operation.md) bring every [matrix](matrix.md) to this form: choose a nonzero entry in the earliest nonzero column, swap its row to the top, eliminate entries below it, and continue on the remaining submatrix. The nonzero rows are independent by their successively positioned pivots; the pivot columns are independent and span the [column space](column-space.md). Thus the number of pivots equals both [row rank](row-rank.md) and [column rank](column-rank.md).
