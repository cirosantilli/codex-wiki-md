# P wave

↑ **Parent:** [Elastic wave](elastic-wave.md)  
ⓦ [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P_wave)

A P-wave is longitudinal, carries nonzero dilatation, and has speed $c_P=\sqrt{(\lambda+2\mu)/\rho}$ in an isotropic solid.
