# Membrane leaflet

↑ **Parent:** [Lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md)

A [membrane leaflet](membrane-leaflet.md) is one of the two layers forming a [lipid bilayer](lipid-bilayer.md). In a [phospholipid](phospholipid.md)-based [cell membrane](cell-membrane.md), the [hydrophilic](hydrophilicity.md) regions face the adjacent [water](water.md), and the [hydrophobic](hydrophobicity.md) regions face the interior shared by the two [membrane leaflets](membrane-leaflet.md).
