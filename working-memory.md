# Working memory

↑ **Parent:** [Memory in animals](memory-in-animals.md)

Temporary retention and use of information for ongoing [cognition](cognition.md) and action. Its explanation must account for capacity, precision, interference, prioritization and updating. [Persistent neural activity](persistent-neural-activity.md) and [activity-silent working memory](activity-silent-working-memory.md) describe candidate mechanisms or states rather than interchangeable definitions of the behavioral capacity.

Temporary maintenance and manipulation of information in service of an ongoing task. Retaining intermediate [arithmetic](arithmetic-split.md) results or integrating a sentence with earlier text can tax [working memory](working-memory.md). A simple immediate span and a task requiring storage plus processing need not measure the same demand. Improvements in structurally similar [memory](memory-in-animals.md) tests are not sufficient evidence for improved [academic achievement](academic-achievement.md).[Developmental Science 16, 915–925; three-arm randomised trial, Results and Discussion](does-working-memory-training-lead-to-generalized-improvements-in-children-with-low-working-memory-by-darren-l-dunning-and-others.md)
