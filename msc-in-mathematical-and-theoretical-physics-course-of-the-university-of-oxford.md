# MSc in mathematical and theoretical physics course of the University of Oxford

↑ **Parent:** [Master's course of the University of Oxford](master-s-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md)

[Official course description](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/courses/msc-mathematical-and-theoretical-physics).

Ten assessment units in total, with free choice of lecture courses and an optional one- or two-unit dissertation. [Groups and representations course of the University of Oxford](groups-and-representations-course-of-the-university-of-oxford.md) is a shared examined subject. [Official joint-course paper archive](https://mmathphys.physics.ox.ac.uk/past-papers). Optional: either 30 pages for one unit or 60 pages for two units. This differs from the compulsory dissertations on the OMMS and MMSC.
