# Doob h-transform

↑ **Parent:** [Markov process](markov-process-split.md)

For a killed [Markov process](markov-process-split.md) and a positive harmonic function $h$, its Doob h-transform weights the path law through a stopping time $\tau$ by $h(X_\tau)/h(X_0)$, with localization when necessary. It describes conditioning on a boundary behaviour of probability zero. In the [complex upper half-plane](upper-half-plane-complex-analysis.md), $h(z)=\operatorname{Im}z$ conditions killed [planar Brownian motion](planar-brownian-motion.md) to remain in that half-plane.
