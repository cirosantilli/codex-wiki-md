# Standard polytabloid basis

↑ **Parent:** [Specht module](specht-module.md)

The [polytabloids](polytabloid.md) $e_t$ indexed by [standard Young tableaux](standard-young-tableau.md) of shape $\lambda$ form a basis of $S^\lambda$ over every [field](field.md), and a basis of its integral lattice over $\mathbb Z$. Column antisymmetry and [Garnir relations](garnir-relation.md) straighten any [polytabloid](polytabloid.md) into this set. Ordering leading [tabloids](tabloid.md) by the rows containing successive entries makes the corresponding coefficient matrix triangular with diagonal entries one, proving independence. In particular the [dimension](dimension-vector-space.md) is the standard-tableau count in every characteristic.
