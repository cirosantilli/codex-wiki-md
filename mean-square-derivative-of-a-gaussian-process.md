# Mean-square derivative of a Gaussian process

↑ **Parent:** [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md)

A [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) has a mean-square derivative at $t$ when its difference quotients converge in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md). The [mean-square convergence](convergence-in-l2.md) need not give differentiable sample paths. For a centered [stationary process](stationary-process.md) with twice continuously differentiable [covariance function](covariance-function.md) $K(t-s)$, the difference quotients are Cauchy in [L2 space](l2-space-is-a-hilbert-space.md), since their [covariance](covariance.md) is a rectangular average of $-K''$. Their limits form a [Gaussian process](gaussian-process.md) with [covariance function](covariance-function.md) $\mathbb E[Y(s)Y(t)]=-K''(s-t)$.
