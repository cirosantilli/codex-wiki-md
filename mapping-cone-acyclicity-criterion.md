# Mapping cone acyclicity criterion

↑ **Parent:** [Mapping cone (homological algebra)](mapping-cone-homological-algebra.md)

For a [chain map](chain-map.md) $f:C\to D$, use $M_i=C_{i-1}\oplus D_i$ and $d(x,y)=(d_Cx,(-1)^if(x)+d_Dy)$. The [chain map](chain-map.md) identity makes $d^2=0$. The [short exact sequence of chain complexes](short-exact-sequence-of-chain-complexes.md) $0\to D\to M\to C_{*-1}\to0$ has [connecting homomorphism](connecting-homomorphism.md) $(-1)^if_*$ in degree $i$. Its [long exact sequence in homology](long-exact-sequence-in-homology.md) shows that the [homology](homology-split.md) of $M$ vanishes precisely when every $f_*$ is an [isomorphism](isomorphism.md).
