# Fiber product of groups

↑ **Parent:** [Group extension](group-extension.md)

For homomorphisms $E\to H$ and $G\to H$, their fiber product is

$$
E\times_HG=\{(e,g)\in E\times G:\pi(e)=f(g)\}.
$$

It is a subgroup of $E\times G$. Pulling a [group extension](group-extension.md) back along $G\to H$ uses this fiber product as its middle group.
