# A-type over a structure

↑ **Parent:** [Partial type](partial-type.md)

For $A\subseteq M$, an A-type over [first-order structure](first-order-structure.md) $M$ is a set of formulas in a fixed finite tuple of variables, with parameters in $A$, consistent with the complete theory of $M$ expanded by constants for $A$. Every finite subset is realized in $M$. A [complete type](complete-type.md) decides all formulas in its variables over those parameters.
