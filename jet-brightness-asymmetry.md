# Jet brightness asymmetry

↑ **Parent:** [Relativistic beaming](relativistic-beaming.md)

An approaching [jet](jet-particle-physics.md) can be enhanced and its receding counterpart suppressed by different [Doppler factors](doppler-factor.md). A discrete moving emitter with rest-frame power-law spectrum $F_\nu\propto\nu^{-s}$ has the usual boost $\delta_D^{3+s}$, whereas a steady continuous-jet convention changes the exponent. Absorption and environmental asymmetry can also affect the contrast.
