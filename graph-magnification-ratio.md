# Graph magnification ratio

↑ **Parent:** [Plünnecke graph](plunnecke-graph.md)

This is the smallest ratio of the size of a $k$-step image to the size of its nonempty starting set in the bottom layer. For a [Plünnecke graph](plunnecke-graph.md), the roots $D_k^{1/k}$ are nonincreasing. The minimum, rather than just the image of the whole layer, is essential.
