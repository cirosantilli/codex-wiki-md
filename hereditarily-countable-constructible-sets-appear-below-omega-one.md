# Hereditarily countable constructible sets appear below omega-one

↑ **Parent:** [Constructible universe](constructible-universe.md)

Inside the [constructible universe](constructible-universe.md), every [hereditarily countable set](hereditarily-countable-set.md) appears in a countable level of the [constructible hierarchy](constructible-hierarchy.md). Take a countable [elementary substructure](elementary-substructure.md) of a sufficiently large $L_\eta$ containing its [transitive closure](transitive-closure.md) pointwise. The [condensation lemma for the constructible universe](condensation-lemma-for-the-constructible-universe.md) identifies the collapse with $L_\beta$ for countable $\beta$, and the collapse fixes the original set. Conversely, a countable-indexed level is countable inside the [constructible universe](constructible-universe.md).
