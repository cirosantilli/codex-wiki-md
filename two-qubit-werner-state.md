# Two-qubit Werner state

↑ **Parent:** [Density matrix](density-matrix.md)

This collective-unitary-invariant two-qubit state has singlet weight $p$ and equal weights on the three triplet [Bell states](bell-state-split.md). With $P_s$ the [spin singlet state](spin-singlet-state.md) projector, its [partial transpose](partial-transpose.md) has eigenvalues $(1-2p)/2$ once and $(1+2p)/6$ three times. It is entangled exactly when $p>1/2$. At $p=1/2$, it is the equally weighted mixture of six antiparallel product states along the three Pauli axes. In the interval $1/4\leq p\leq1/2$, convex mixing of this boundary state with $I/4$ explicitly proves separability. The weight $p$ here is the singlet probability, not the alternative coefficient multiplying a pure singlet in a white-noise mixture.
